## Problems

1. IMPRECISE. Origins: "He blames part of his agitation on what he calls his usual narcotic impulses." Transcript (FPSP): "Partly due to standard narcotic impulses". "what he calls" signals his wording. Fix: "what he calls his standard narcotic impulses." FR to match ("ses pulsions narcotiques habituelles" is fine as a translation, but keep the attribution).

2. IMPRECISE. Key facts: "Ross and Fordham bring the wounded Nastas to his room in Blackwater and ask him to find out what Nastas knows about Dutch." Transcript (Bear One): only Fordham asks ("Now, MacDougal, we need information from this fellow about Dutch van der Linde"); John also helps carry Nastas in. Fix: "Ross, Fordham and John bring the wounded Nastas to his room in Blackwater, and Fordham asks him to find out what Nastas knows about Dutch."

3. IMPRECISE (+ COPIED from the wiki). At Home: "Under Nastas's look, he corrects himself". Transcript: "Nastas: (grumbles)"; the "angry glare" is the character page's own wording ("after an angry glare from Nastas"). Fix: "When Nastas grumbles, he corrects himself". Same sentence: "while MacDougal planned the escape" -> transcript "I improvised an escape plan"; fix "while he himself improvised the escape plan" (same wording as nastas_A).

4. IMPRECISE. At Home: "Dutch spots John through the binoculars and knocks him out with a shot." Dutch does not use binoculars; John does. Transcript/nastas_A: John watches the camp through a scout's binoculars, Dutch spots him and fires, the bullet hits the binoculars, John falls backwards unconscious. Fix: "John watches Dutch's camp through a scout's binoculars until Dutch spots him and fires; the bullet hits the binoculars and John falls backwards, knocked out."

5. IMPRECISE (cross-fiche inconsistency). FPSP bear: "According to the mission page, he opens fire himself and the bear turns on them, so John has to kill it." Transcript: "(Marston and MacDougal open fire at the bear, killing it)"; nastas_A (fact-checked): "MacDougal opens fire, and he and John kill it." Transcript beats mission page. Fix: "MacDougal opens fire; according to the mission page the bear then turns hostile, and he and John kill it."

6. IMPRECISE. FPSP: "John objects that one ambush says nothing about an entire people." Transcript: "We got ambushed by Dutch's gang. It's a little soon to be drawing conclusions for an entire people." Fix: "John objects that they were ambushed by Dutch's gang and that it is too soon to draw conclusions about an entire people."

7. IMPRECISE. Prodigal: "The professor says he is not cut out for field work". Transcript: "I'm not cut out for this." ("field work" is from FPSP: "enough of field work for today"). Fix: "says he is not cut out for this".

8. IMPRECISE. Prodigal: "then leads him through the neighboring room, where a couple protests, and up onto the roof. There, one of Dutch's men ... holds MacDougal at gunpoint". Transcript: MacDougal says "we can get to the roof this way" and is seized there; mission page: "Go outside and climb the ladder onto the roof. MacDougal will be held captive by Nashoba". Fix: "John takes him through the neighboring room, where a couple protests, and MacDougal heads for the roof. According to the mission page, John climbs a ladder to reach him and finds one of Dutch's men, Nashoba, holding him at gunpoint; John shoots him."

9. IMPRECISE. Names: "Dutch van der Linde never names him: he asks John to send out \"that academic\"." Transcript (Prodigal): Dutch also says "You and your friend there the professor". Fix: "Dutch van der Linde never uses his name: he calls him \"your friend there the professor\" and asks John to send out \"that academic\"." (Check half2 Quotes does not also quote these.)

10. UNSUPPORTED. Personality: "He talks fast". No transcript, guide or wiki text says so (searched en.txt, fr.txt, GOTY quote, four transcripts). Fix: delete "talks fast"; keep "repeats himself" ("good day, good day", "but, but, but") and the stammer ("A-A-A-Are", "Q-Q-Q-Quick", "I...
