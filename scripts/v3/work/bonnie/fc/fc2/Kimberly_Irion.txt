==Involvement in the ''Red Dead'' series==
Within the ''[[Red Dead]] ''series, Irion portrays the rancher [[Bonnie MacFarlane]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]] ''and its ''[[Undead Nightmare]]'' [[DLC]], as well as voicing a multiplayer variant of the character in the ''[[Liars and Cheats]]'' DLC.

She later reprised the role in ''[[Red Dead Online]]'' as a minor mission giver.
