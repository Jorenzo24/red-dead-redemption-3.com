## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **The raft fight.** EN wiki History: "John dispatches their assailants while Irish cowers"; mission infobox caption
   "while Irish cowers". Fiche (L49, L83): he shoots back and calls out where the shooters are. **Fiche right**:
   the mission page's own trivia says "This is the only mission Irish actually fights in. He will use a Cattleman
   Revolver", and the transcript has him calling positions ("Watch the top of that cliff! They're shootin' down on us!",
   WSBTIP l.93).
2. **Father killed by a Welshman.** EN wiki (Background "possibly by one"; trivia "slit by a Welshman, as said in A
   Frenchman..."). Transcript (AFWI dialogues l.168): "Never trust a Welshman, me Pa always told me, and he got his
   throat slit, so he should know." The killer is implied, never stated. **Fiche right** ("without saying by whom").
3. **Whiskey quote.** Wiki: "They say God invented whisky..."; transcript WSBTIP l.53: "They said God invented whiskey".
   **Fiche right** (L52).
4. **Lily of the West.** Character page "precious Flora", transcript "faithless Flora". Fiche gives both (L175). OK.
5. **The 1914 notice (FR wiki).** FR: died "dans une explosion". Ledger No. 213 (`fc/ireddead_Ledger_No213_text.txt`):
   "accidental discharge of a gun in a Thieves' Landing outhouse". **Fiche right** (L44). FR also has John escort Irish
   "avec une diligence" to the raft: wrong (they meet at Brittlebrush Trawl, WSBTIP).
6. **Absent from Fort Mercer.** Wiki: "nowhere to be found until just before the mission is finished"; mission page
   trivia: he is seen in the opening cutscene. Fiche states both (L38, L176). OK.
7. **Multiplayer pack.** Infobox says Liars and Cheats; one gallery caption says Myths and Mavericks. Fiche follows
   Liars and Cheats with Rockstar dates (L170). Leave as is.
8. **Internal inconsistency (not a wiki difference).** L30 says Blackwater Ledger No. 56 itself names "Leander Holland,
   known to friends by the name of French" (confirmed, `fc/Blackwater_Ledger_No_56.txt` l.29); L66 says only "the wiki
   gives French's name as Leander Holland" and attributes Alwyn Lloyd to "a notice ... according to the Red Dead Wiki".
   **L30 is right**: fix L66 (both names are in the Ledger, which is a primary in-game text).
9. **Age 14 vs "a few years back".** Wiki: emigrated at 14 and met French and Welsh on that boat. Transcript (AFWI l.158):
   "We all met on the boat over a few years back". The fiche keeps the two in separate sentences with the 14 attributed
   to the wiki (L24/L25): fine, but never merge them into one claim.
