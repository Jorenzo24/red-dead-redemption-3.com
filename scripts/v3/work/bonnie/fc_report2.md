## Problems

1. DUPLICATE (Quotes vs In her own words). Quotes: "There's certain things in this country a woman could do much better, if you ask me." The same line is quoted in Personality > In her own words. OWNERSHIP: Quotes does not repeat those lines. Fix: cut it from Quotes.
2. DUPLICATE (Quotes vs In her own words). Quotes: "The outlaw with a code? ... There's nothing more depressing than a man who's found a way to think the bad into good." The last sentence is already quoted in In her own words. Fix: cut it from Quotes, or keep only "The outlaw with a code? How wonderfully romantic! The reluctant murderer, the noble criminal." in Quotes.
3. DUPLICATE (Quotes vs In her own words). Quotes: "I didn't teach him anything. I knew better than to try to change a man." In her own words quotes "I knew better than to try to change a man." Fix: cut it from Quotes.
4. IMPRECISE (Amos entry, also the Missions table). "Bonnie overrules him to save the herd and calls him a stupid man." / Table: "Overrules Amos and brings the herd home". Transcript A Tempest Looms: Bonnie argues with him ("you stupid man"), and it is JOHN who ends it: "Doesn't sound like we're left with much of a choice, then. Come on, Amos, round up your men." Biography (half1) has it right ("John sides with her and tells Amos to gather his men"). Fix: "Bonnie argues that losing the herd would ruin them all and calls him a stupid man; John then tells Amos to round up his men." Table: "Argues with Amos, then brings the herd home through the storm with John."
5. UNSUPPORTED (Skills). "...and directs the ranch hands through a thunderstorm in 'A Tempest Looms'." The transcript and mission page show her giving instructions to John only; John is the one who tells Amos to round up the men. Fix: "...and brings the herd home with John through a thunderstorm in 'A Tempest Looms'."
6. WRONG (Trivia). "'The Burning' is the only mission of Bonnie's strand that does not unlock a Stranger side mission." This is The Burning page's trivia, but the Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane page lists no Stranger unlock either (its "next" field gives only "The Assault on Fort Mercer", and it has no unlockables section), and The Outlaw's Return and Old Friends, New Problems unlock none. Fix: cut the item (or limit it to "Of her first seven missions, only..." which is also not what the page says; cutting is cleaner).
7. IMPRECISE (Amos entry). "When John asks why she never married, she names Amos as an example of the local men, who are a little 'countrified'." Two scenes are merged: "the local men" is from "Women and Cattle" ("Clearly you haven't met too many of the local men"); "Amos, he's a little... countrified" is from "Wild Horses, Tamed Passions". It also repeats Origins (half1), which already says she finds Amos countrified. Fix: drop the sentence here (Origins owns it), or "In 'Wild Horses, Tamed Passions', explaining why she never married, she calls Amos a little 'countrified'."
8. IMPRECISE (Other media > Red Dead Online and Red Dead Redemption 2). "In Red Dead Redemption 2 she is only mentioned, through the letter of the shipwreck survivor." The Biography (half1) also has the 1907 epilogue mention (locals, family away). Fix: "In Red Dead Redemption 2 she is only mentioned: in the shipwreck survivor's letter and, in 1907, as away from the ranch (see Biography)."
9. DUPLICATE (Drew entry vs Origins). The 30 years and coming from the East, "buried more of his children than he has raised", debts "terrible", losing the ranch "would kill him", "frailer than he looks" and the cheap governesses are all in Biography > Origins (half1, paragraphs 1, 3, 4). OWNERSHIP: backstory told in a mission belongs to A Origins, B gets one clause. Fix: keep in the Drew entry only the relationship-specific items (he is all she has in "The Burning", Chuck Kelley, Undead Nightmare pointer) plus "(see Origins)".
10. DUPLICATE (Patrick entry and MacFarlane brothers entry vs Origins). Ten years, banker in New York per his last letter, unnamed by her, residents' name Patrick, "man of the ranch", six brothers / five dead, penny dreadfuls, and the graves with years: all already in Origins. Fix: keep the new details here (she does not want his help, fears for his soul, the man-in-a-tie paraphrase; the causes of death for Owen) and replace the rest with "(see Origins)"; or move the causes of death into Origins and cut the brothers entry to one line.
11. DUPLICATE (Abigail entry vs Biography Act III). "Abigail, who cannot read, has John read it aloud", "Bonnie asks how she is after her ordeal", the two women siding against John and "Abigail tells John she likes Bonnie" are told in Biography (half1, Old Friends paragraphs). Fix: keep one clause ("they meet for the first time in 'Old Friends, New Problems' (see Biography)") plus the only new items ("I've been through worse"; "I've never felt so outnumbered").
12. DUPLICATE (John entry vs Biography). "an unusual start to a friendship", "she does not think he is a bad man", "a woman in a man's world" (Wild Horses) and the family-history conversation are already told in Biography (This is Armadillo, Wild Horses paragraphs). B's docstring says these lines are owned by the John entry, but A paraphrases them. Fix: in the John entry keep the quoted fragments; in A cut "calls the way they met an unusual start to a friendship" and "a woman in a man's world" (or vice versa), one place each.
13. DUPLICATE (quote fragment). John entry quotes "trigger itch"; Quotes has "Well, with your trigger itch and my feminine intuition, we should make quite a team." Same words in quotation marks twice. Fix: paraphrase in the John entry ("about his readiness to shoot").
14. DUPLICATE (Bill, Leigh Johnson, Norman Deek, Nathanial Johnston, Jack, suitor entries vs Biography). Each retells an event the Biography already has: "worst bandit in the county", "settled their differences" (Bill); advice to see the marshal and the plans heard from Johnson (Leigh); Deek held in Armadillo, exchange, shot by his own side (Deek); medicine paid in Armadillo (Johnston); the 1914 cabin and Jack never seeing her (Jack); the letter's apology / father / fortune (suitor). Fix: one pointer clause each; keep only lines not in A ("that nice Mr. Williamson", "vulnerable in different ways", "be polite to him", "must take after his father").
15. DUPLICATE (Skills vs Biography). "accepting her missions raises the player's honor" is in Biography > Red Dead Online ("they raise the player's honor"); "lets him keep the stallion ... as a thank-you from the family" is in Biography > Wild Horses. Fix: keep in Skills, cut from A (OWNERSHIP: Skills owns gameplay), or the reverse; one place.
16. DUPLICATE (Trivia vs Skills). "Artwork shows her holding a Winchester Repeater, a weapon she never uses in the game." is a repeat of Skills > Weapons and horses. Fix: cut the trivia item.
17. DUPLICATE (Trivia vs Skills / Biography). "'New Friends, Old Problems' rewards John with a Repeater Carbine, a horse (Cleveland Bay ... War Horse) and the first level of Dead Eye. 'Wild Horses, Tamed Passions' gives him the lasso and the Kentucky Saddler, and 'Paternal Pride' a Winchester Repeater." Carbine, horse, lasso and Saddler are in Skills; the Winchester in Paternal Pride is in Biography > Undead Nightmare. Fix: keep only the new fact: move "and the first level of Dead Eye" into the Skills sentence and cut the trivia item.
18. DUPLICATE (Trivia vs Skills). "Winning or losing the race ... makes no difference: the reward is 20 Fame either way." Skills already says "winning brings no bonus". Fix: put "20 Fame either way" in Skills and cut the trivia item.
19. UNSUPPORTED (Reception, quotation level). "Sara Humphreys described Bonnie as 'insightful and resourceful' rather than 'a woman masquerading as a male figure', while noting that she defers to simple domestic tasks..." The quoted words come from Wikipedia's summary (the article itself is paywalled on MUSE and was not read; Wikipedia's citation also covers the domestic-tasks point jointly). Fix: attribute the summary: "According to Wikipedia's summary of her 2012 article in Western American Literature, Sara Humphreys describes Bonnie as..." (same for Margini).
20. COPIED (Development > Name). Draft: "Hanson designed the ranch, renamed it MacFarlane's Ranch and gave the rancher's daughter the name Bonnie MacFarlane shortly before he left the studio." Wiki: "He renamed the ranch he designed 'MacFarlane's Ranch' and named the rancher's daughter Bonnie MacFarlane just before he resigned." Same clauses in the same order. Fix: "The same unsourced account says Hanson, who had designed the ranch, chose both its name and hers before leaving Rockstar San Diego."
21. IMPRECISE (Missions intro vs table). "She returns in two missions of the Homestead chapter" while the table labels both "Act III: West Elizabeth". Fix: use the site's act labels in both: "She returns in two missions of Act III."
22. IMPRECISE (consistency). Relationship heading "Nathanial Johnston" (wiki spelling) vs "Doctor Nathaniel Johnston" in the Biography (half1) and in 8 of the 11 occurrences in the fact-checked parts. Fix: use "Nathaniel Johnston" everywhere.
23. STYLE (John entry). "She teases him constantly ... and he answers with dry jokes." "constantly" and "dry jokes" are characterizations. Fix: "She teases him about his 'trigger itch', his riddles and his smell; he answers with jokes of his own."
24. IMPRECISE (Skills). "She drives the ranch wagon, which she hands over to John on the way to Armadillo." She hands him the reins before they leave ("You can take the reins... Hop on up"), not on the way. Fix: "...which she lets John drive to Armadillo."
