So are Palmer (resigns or is killed) and the Man from Blackwater facts (Hillcoat, Fox on 29 May 2010, 5USA on 5 Jun 2010, the PRA and Ridgewood scenes reused, the Blackwater bank flashback with Bill and Javier). De Longis checks out: born 23 Mar 1950 in Glendale, actor, stuntman and fight choreographer, Blade, Jal Culluh, whip training for Pfeiffer and Ford, credited for RDR and Undead Nightmare.
