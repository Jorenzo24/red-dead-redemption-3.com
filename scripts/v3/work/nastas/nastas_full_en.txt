
Nastas is a Native American informant for the Bureau of Investigation in Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911. He grew up on a reservation and went to school there. His tip sends Edgar Ross, Archer Fordham and John Marston to the wreck of the Serendipity in search of Dutch van der Linde, where they find him beaten and tied up, and walk into an ambush. Ross leaves him with the anthropologist Harold MacDougal in Blackwater.
Nastas then guides John towards Dutch's hideout at Cochinay, and carries him down the mountain after Dutch's shot knocks him out. He sets up a meeting between MacDougal and some of Dutch's men at Bearclaw Camp, where one of them, Enepay, shoots him dead. He is voiced by Benjamin Byron Davis, who also plays Dutch.
Key facts
In "Bear One Another's Burdens", John and Fordham find him tied up on the wreck of the Serendipity; John carries him to the agents' car through Dutch's men.
In "At Home with Dutch", he leads John and MacDougal to Cochinay, is wounded in a mine, then carries the unconscious John back down.
He argues with MacDougal over the buffalo, the forests and the fate of his people.
In "For Purely Scientific Purposes", Enepay calls him a treacherous snake and kills him at Bearclaw Camp; MacDougal later signs a notice of his death in the Blackwater Ledger.
In Undead Nightmare, which is not canon, he returns as one of the undead and attacks MacDougal.

Biography
Origins
Nastas is a Native American who lives in West Elizabeth in 1911. The games give no surname, no birth date and no tribe for him; the notice published after his death calls him "Nastas (Surname Unknown), of Indian descent". What is known of his past comes from one exchange in Harold MacDougal's office. When the professor addresses him in mock metaphors, Nastas answers that he grew up on a reservation and went to school. According to the Red Dead Wiki, that schooling was a classical English education at a boarding school. Nastas also says that Dutch van der Linde has drawn a following of young men from the reservation, and that some of his own friends have joined Dutch.
By the time John Marston reaches Blackwater, Nastas is an informant for the Bureau of Investigation, which Edgar Ross and Archer Fordham run from the city. The games never show how he came to work for them. In the wreck of the Serendipity, Fordham calls him "our informant", and the mission page of "Bear One Another's Burdens" says he is the source who gave the agents Dutch's supposed location.
Red Dead Redemption (1911)
In 1911, the Bureau holds John Marston's wife and son until he has delivered the members of his old gang. After Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella, the last target is Dutch van der Linde, who has formed a new gang in West Elizabeth. Nastas appears in three missions of Red Dead Redemption, all at the start of the third act: "Bear One Another's Burdens", "At Home with Dutch" and "For Purely Scientific Purposes". He is killed in the last of them.
Act III: West Elizabeth
In "Bear One Another's Burdens", Ross tells John in Blackwater that the Bureau knows where Dutch is. Fordham says Dutch is believed to be holed up with a group of renegades near the wreck of the Serendipity, a riverboat. The three men drive there in the agents' automobile, Fordham at the wheel. Ross stays with the car on the cliff while John and Fordham go down on foot. The wreck is quiet, and Fordham says the informant had better not have lied to them. John follows the shouts to an upper deck and finds a man tied up, with a bag over his head according to the mission page. Fordham recognizes Nastas, who warns them that it is a trap. Dutch's men then surround them.
Fordham judges that Nastas cannot walk and orders John to carry him. John fights his way back to the car with Nastas on his shoulder, shooting with one hand. Ross has him laid on the back seat and asks who "this savage" is; Fordham answers that he is the informant and that he speaks English. On the road back, the engine fails and riders from Dutch's gang attack. Ross says they must get the man to a doctor, and holds the riders off with John while Fordham repairs the car. In Blackwater, Ross decides to take "the Indian" to Harold MacDougal, an anthropologist who helps the agents deal with the Native population, to see what he can learn from him. John tells MacDougal that Nastas has been shot in the leg and badly beaten, and Fordham promises to send a doctor.
"At Home with Dutch" opens in MacDougal's office, where the professor has just told John that the blood of natives and of white men looks exactly the same under his microscope. Nastas walks in. MacDougal speaks to him in mock metaphors, then turns to John and explains that "savages" must be addressed simply; Nastas answers that he grew up on a reservation and went to school. He says he can show them where the bandits they are looking for are hiding: Dutch has drawn young men from the reservation, who are turning to bad things, and he is based in the hills at Cochinay. Nastas knows an unguarded way in, brings the horses round and leads the ride.
If the player has not yet unlocked the Manifest Destiny achievement, the three riders pass hunters shooting buffalo on the plains. MacDougal admires the animals; Nastas says the white man will soon wipe them out through overhunting, and that his people hunt to eat, not for sport. MacDougal replies that extinction is a myth refuted by Darwin and that hunting will make the buffalo stronger. In the forest of Tall Trees, Nastas tells MacDougal to enjoy the trees while he can, because they will soon be cut down, and complains that the land is no longer respected. When the professor asks him to talk through this "primal emotion", Nastas tells him not to worry about it.
At the foot of the cliffs, MacDougal says he has no head for heights and rides back to Blackwater. Nastas and John leave the horses and climb. A wooden scaffold breaks under John, and Nastas tells him to find another route while he looks around. The two meet again and Nastas leads John into an old mine shaft. A miner attacks them inside; according to the mission page he cuts Nastas in the arm before Nastas kills him. A cart loaded with dynamite then rolls into the tunnel, and the mission page says three men are waiting outside when the pair escape. Nastas says he is badly hurt. He refuses to slow John down and tells him to go on, saying he will catch up or meet him on the way down.
John climbs alone, silently kills or hogties the scout Helaku and takes his binoculars. Through them he watches Dutch's camp at Cochinay, until Dutch spots him and fires; the bullet hits the binoculars and John falls backwards, knocked out. He wakes in MacDougal's office in Blackwater. MacDougal first says that Nastas and he carried John down, then admits that Nastas heard the shots, climbed up to rescue John and carried him down, while he himself only improvised the escape plan. Nastas says that friends of his are with Dutch. MacDougal wants to reason with the Native members of the gang and save them from disaster; Nastas answers that his people have already lived through many disasters and that all this land was once theirs.
MacDougal answers that his people have brought them civilization, and tells the story of a Yale acquaintance whose father once shot eighteen Natives in an afternoon in Wyoming. Nastas tells him that for a wise man he is very stupid. John leaves the two men to work out right from wrong. MacDougal calls Nastas simple-minded, then backs down as Nastas steps towards him.
In "For Purely Scientific Purposes", MacDougal tells John that Nastas has set up a meeting, which the professor calls a pow-wow between Natives and whites, academics and criminals. Nastas, waiting with the horses, explains that some of Dutch's men have agreed to meet the professor at the Bear Claw cabin, in Tall Trees, and says they are curious to hear what a white man has concluded about their culture from the comfort of his hotel room. He advises MacDougal against asking them for skin samples from the soles of their feet. MacDougal says he is relieved to have the two men with him, Nastas above all. On the way, a grizzly bear crosses the road; MacDougal opens fire, and he and John kill it. Nastas remarks that the bear showed no aggression and would probably have moved on if they had not shot first.
Three of Dutch's men are waiting in the cabin: Enepay, Kosumi and Hinto, as the mission page names them. MacDougal says they come in peace. Enepay answers that such words mean nothing from people like them, who have left his people living like animals in the dirt, and orders them to raise their hands; Kosumi demands their weapons. Nastas protests that this is not what was agreed. Enepay calls him a treacherous snake and shoots him in the head, killing him. John kills Enepay, Kosumi and Hinto, then fights off the rest of the gang around the cabin and escorts MacDougal back to Blackwater through further attacks. On the ride, MacDougal says that his studies, Nastas and the blood samples had led him to believe acculturation was possible, and that he could not have been more wrong.
Issue No. 59 of the Blackwater Ledger goes on sale after the next mission of the strand, "The Prodigal Son Returns (To Yale)". Its Miscellany column carries a notice signed by the professor: "Nastas (Surname Unknown), of Indian descent, has passed away at Bear Claw Cabin in Tall Trees. May you find God, my dear friend. Prof. Harold MacDougal Esq." The French-language Red Dead Wiki dates this tribute to 1914; the issue number places it in 1911.
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
Undead Nightmare, an expansion released in October 2010, is not part of the series' canon. Nastas appears in one of its missions, "Curious Tales from Blackwater", as one of the undead. John finds MacDougal alone in a deserted Blackwater, back from Yale to continue his research. The professor goes down an alley to fetch his bag; according to the wiki's Undead Nightmare page, he says he left his bags with Nastas. An undead Nastas attacks him there and devours him. The mission then asks the player to kill or avoid Nastas, MacDougal and the other undead. According to the mission page, if the player spares them, the two can be met again later in the town or near the cemetery.
The Red Dead Wiki notes that the undead Nastas bears a bite wound on the neck rather than the head wound of the main story. Its page on the expansion lists him among the changes from the canon: in Undead Nightmare, Nastas survived the attack at the cabin and was then infected.
Personality
A Rockstar character card for Red Dead Redemption, reproduced on the Red Dead Wiki, sums him up in one line: "A Native American unsure about the joys of life on a reservation." In the game, he speaks careful, formal English. He calls the men he works with "sir", "Professor" and "Mr. Marston". He says little, and his answers are short.
He corrects Harold MacDougal's assumptions about him calmly, and loses patience only once, when he tells the professor that he is a very stupid man for a wise one (see Quotes). He speaks about the land, the forests and the buffalo whenever the subject comes up, and blames the white man for their loss. He also takes physical risks for others: he leads the climb, fights in the mine and goes back up the mountain for John (see Biography).
In his own words
When MacDougal greets him with mock metaphors, in "At Home with Dutch": "No, sir. I grew up on a reservation and attended school." On the buffalo hunters met on the way to Cochinay: "But we hunt to eat, not for sport. Soon there will be no buffalo left." Riding into Tall Trees: "Enjoy it while you still can. Soon you will have cut down all of these trees." Then: "There is no respect for the land anymore." Back in Blackwater, when MacDougal wants to save the Natives of Dutch's gang from disaster: "My people have already endured many disasters. Before, this was all our land."
Names
Nastas is the only name the game gives him. The notice of his death in the Blackwater Ledger lists him as "Nastas (Surname Unknown)" (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, Nastas is a Navajo name meaning "curve-like foxtail grass". The same wiki notes that John and Fordham pronounce it "Nosstas", while MacDougal sometimes says "Nastoss". The agents mostly call him the informant, and Ross refers to him as a savage and as "the Indian". Carrying him into MacDougal's office, John calls him a kid: "Kid got shot in the leg, beat up pretty good." Enepay calls him a "treacherous snake" before shooting him.
Appearance
He has shoulder-length black hair held by a red headband, and wears a turquoise earring and a turquoise necklace. He wears a pale grey jacket over a dark shirt. The Red Dead Wiki notes that his shoes look like moccasins, with stitching visible on the side.
Relationships
John Marston
John carries him out of the wreck of the Serendipity, and Nastas later carries John down from the cliffs at Cochinay (see Biography). Nastas addresses him as Mr. Marston and works with him in all three of his missions. When MacDougal talks nonsense about the pow-wow, John turns to Nastas for a straight answer, and Nastas gives it. Back in Blackwater, John thanks both men for the rescue, then leaves Nastas and the professor to their argument.
When Enepay shoots Nastas at Bearclaw Camp, John curses Dutch and kills the three men in the cabin (see Biography).
Harold MacDougal
Ross leaves the wounded Nastas with the professor, an anthropologist who advises the agents on the Natives of the region (see Biography). MacDougal treats him as a specimen of his theories: he speaks to him in metaphors, wonders whether his anger is a primal emotion and wants skin samples from the gang's feet. Nastas corrects him, contradicts his ideas on the buffalo and on the bear, and calls him a stupid man for a wise one (see Quotes). In the professor's own account, it is Nastas, not he, who carries John down from Cochinay.
On the way to Bearclaw Camp, MacDougal says he is relieved to have Nastas with him. After the ambush, he tells John that his studies, Nastas and the blood samples had led him to believe acculturation was possible, and that he was wrong. He signs the notice of Nastas's death in the Blackwater Ledger (see Biography). In Undead Nightmare, which is not canon, an undead Nastas attacks him in Blackwater (see Biography).
Edgar Ross
Nastas is an informant for the Bureau, and Ross acts on his tip about the Serendipity (see Biography). In the car, Ross does not know who he is: he asks whether he is a prisoner, then whether he speaks English. He then has "the Indian" taken to MacDougal, saying he cannot communicate with Natives himself.
Archer Fordham
Fordham knows him as "our informant". At the empty wreck, he says the informant had better not have lied to them, then recognizes Nastas tied up on the upper deck and orders John to carry him (see Biography). In the car, he tells Ross that Nastas speaks English.
Dutch van der Linde
The two men never share a scene. Nastas tells John and MacDougal that Dutch has drawn a following of young men from the reservation, that they are turning to bad things, and that friends of his are among them. Dutch's men beat him and leave him tied up on the Serendipity, where the agents walk into a trap, and other men of the gang kill him at Bearclaw Camp (see Biography). Benjamin Byron Davis voices both characters (see Development and performance).
Enepay, Kosumi and Hinto
Three Native members of Dutch's gang who wait for Nastas, MacDougal and John in the Bearclaw Camp cabin. Enepay calls Nastas a traitor and shoots him; John then kills all three (see Biography). Their names are those given by the mission page and the transcript.
Guide, weapons and horses
Guide and informant
The Bureau uses him as an informant on Dutch's new gang, and the agents act on his tip at the wreck of the Serendipity (see Biography). He knows West Elizabeth well: he leads John and MacDougal to Cochinay by a route he says is unguarded, finds a way up the cliffs and spots the mine shaft that leads through the mountain. Because friends of his ride with Dutch, he can also arrange a meeting with some of the gang's men (see Biography). He reads animals: he says the grizzly that crosses their path showed no sign of aggression and would have moved on if left alone (see Quotes). He went to school on the reservation and speaks fluent English: Ross still asks whether he speaks it, and MacDougal addresses him in metaphors until Nastas corrects him.
Weapons
The English-language Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver, a Double-barreled Shotgun and a Repeater Carbine; the French-language wiki lists only the Cattleman Revolver. In the mine of "At Home with Dutch", he kills the miner who attacks him; according to the mission page, the miner first cuts him in the arm. The wiki's "Murders Committed" list counts this miner as his only kill, in self-defense. According to the mission page, the miner uses the model of Quick Tom Candle, a multiplayer character. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Nastas also owns a carved flute of his own, which he plays well.
Horses
He brings the horses round for John and MacDougal in both of MacDougal's missions. According to the mission page of "At Home with Dutch", his horse is a Tobiano Pinto. The page of "For Purely Scientific Purposes" notes that his horse and MacDougal's are unusually fast in that mission, faster than an American Standardbred or a War Horse, which makes them hard to keep up with.
In the game
In "Bear One Another's Burdens", the mission fails if John assaults or kills Nastas or lets Dutch's men kill him. While John carries him, Nastas has his own health gauge and John can shoot with one hand only. "At Home with Dutch" and "For Purely Scientific Purposes" fail if John assaults, kills or abandons him or kills his horse. In the second, killing his horse after Nastas is dead still fails the mission, according to its page. According to the Red Dead Wiki, if John shoots an animal on the ride to Cochinay, Nastas calls him a bastard. His body can be looted after his death without any loss of honor or failure: the character page gives four to seven dollars, the mission page around seven.
Missions
Nastas appears in three story missions at the start of the third act of Red Dead Redemption, numbered 42, 44 and 45 in the story order. He gives none of them: the first belongs to Edgar Ross's strand, the next two to Harold MacDougal's. "Bear One Another's Burdens" becomes available after Abraham Reyes's "An Appointed Time" and is the first mission set in West Elizabeth; it opens MacDougal's strand, which continues with "At Home with Dutch" and "For Purely Scientific Purposes". In all three, assaulting or killing Nastas fails the mission; in the first, so does letting Dutch's men kill him. He also appears in one mission of Undead Nightmare.

Development and performance
Casting
Wikipedia credits Benjamin Byron Davis with two roles in Red Dead Redemption (2010): Dutch van der Linde and Nastas. The English and French Red Dead Wikis also give him as the voice of Nastas. The character he voices is the informant who helps the Bureau hunt down Dutch, the gang leader he also plays. On June 14, 2020, Game Rant (Sean Mackey) reported the double role, noticed by a Reddit user, and called it one of the game's Easter eggs. The article quotes no statement from Davis on Nastas, and none was found. Davis returned as Dutch in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018); Nastas does not appear in that game.
Promotion and cut content
Rockstar presented Nastas with a one-line character card (see Personality); the Red Dead Wiki's copy of it was uploaded on June 23, 2010. According to the wiki's page on the game's cut content, the files contain an earlier version of Nastas under the name "NativeAmericanContact", with the same belt and shirt; its high-resolution textures were deleted.
Reception
Before release
On February 26, 2010, Game Informer (Jeff Cork) wrote that Rockstar San Diego had filled the game with "nearly every stock Western character". He singled out Nastas as a possible exception: "Nastas could prove to be an interesting character if he's truly conflicted about his place in the world."
Studies of the game's Native Americans
In a chapter of the collection Undead in the West II (Scarecrow Press, 2013), M. Melissa Elston discusses Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare. Wikipedia summarizes her reading of Nastas as an example of what the writer Gerald Vizenor calls "manifest manners", a false version of the Native experience presented as the truth. On April 17, 2014, Phill Alexander, writing for NYMGamer, described Nastas as "a sort of guide-meets-confidant". He wrote that Nastas "shows some classic Native American traits", being quiet, not combative and close to nature, "but he is not presented to the gamer as a cartoon".
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
Nastas became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Myths and Mavericks Bonus Pack. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rockstar first hinted at this free pack on June 6, 2011, set a September release on July 27 and gave the date and contents on September 1. It was released free on September 13, 2011 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360. It added eight characters to the Revolution section of the Outfitter: Drew MacFarlane, Eli, Javier Escuella, Jonah, Landon Ricketts, Nastas, Uncle and Vincente de Santa. Like the other characters of the pack, he has only seven taunts, a few of which are quoted above. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, do not include the multiplayer mode.
Promotional material
Rockstar published a promotional screenshot of Nastas for Red Dead Redemption. For Myths and Mavericks, a promotional screenshot shows a Gang Shootout in Gaptooth Breach with Uncle, Landon Ricketts and Nastas. For Undead Nightmare, a promotional artwork shows him as one of the undead.
Later games
Nastas does not appear in Red Dead Redemption 2 or Red Dead Online, both set before 1911.
Trivia
According to the Red Dead Wiki, "Nastas" is a Navajo name meaning "curve-like foxtail grass". The games never name his tribe.
The title "Bear One Another's Burdens" quotes the Epistle to the Galatians (6:2), as its mission page notes. In the mission, John carries the wounded Nastas on his shoulder.
According to the mission page of "At Home with Dutch", if John shoots an animal on the ride to Cochinay, Nastas calls him a bastard.
According to the same page, the miner who attacks Nastas in the cave uses the model of Quick Tom Candle, a character of the multiplayer mode.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Nastas's body can be searched after his death, without loss of honor and without failing the mission; the character page gives four to seven dollars, the mission page about seven. The mission page adds that killing his horse after his death still fails the mission, and that his horse and MacDougal's are faster than the American Standardbred and the War Horse in this mission.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Nastas, Hassun and Chogan are the only Native American characters in the game who are not hostile to the player.
The French-language Red Dead Wiki notes that he is wounded in every mission in which he appears: shot and beaten on the wreck, hurt in the mine, then killed at the cabin.
