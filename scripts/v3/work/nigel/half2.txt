Relationships
John Marston
John saves Dickens's life in "Old Swindler Blues", then works for him in exchange for his help against Fort Mercer (see Biography). John makes no secret of his contempt: in "Liars, Cheats and Other Proud Americans" he reminds him, "What you are, dear boy, is a man whose life I've saved twice now", and threatens to shoot him if he does not help. Dickens answers that John has "the exterior of a violent man, but the soul of an angel". When John says the gang stole from the rich to give to the poor, Dickens mocks him: "A Robin Hood with spurs. How romantic!"
On the way to Rathskeller Fork, Dickens proposes: "We should consider a more permanent partnership." John answers: "This partnership ends as soon as I have Bill Williamson." They part on good terms at Fort Mercer and again at Brittlebrush Trawl. In Blackwater, John gets him released, calling him "a harmless old fraud. The kind of man that built this country" (see Biography). In Undead Nightmare, which is not canon, they meet again at Fort Mercer (see Biography).
Seth Briars
Dickens sends John to "my old friend, Seth", whom he says is most often found at Coot's Chapel. Back at Ridgewood Farm he calls him "Seth of the dead" and defends grave robbing, which he says was valued as an art in ancient Egypt. John replies: "More deeply disturbed. I can see why you two get along." Seth's part is to open the gate of Fort Mercer from inside (see Biography).
Irish
Dickens presents Irish as another old friend, usually found in Armadillo or a nearby town on some "bacchanalian revel", who can supply the firepower. John sums up: "Great, an alcoholic arms dealer." Dickens: "What could be better?" Irish provides the Gatling gun for Fort Mercer, and Dickens is chatting with him at Brittlebrush Trawl when John arrives for the crossing to Mexico (see Biography).
Leigh Johnson
When Eli reports Dickens missing in "Justice in Pike's Basin", the marshal is unimpressed that the town worries more about a snake oil salesman than about the gang they have just wiped out (his verdict on the elixir is under Trade and the elixir). In "Spare the Rod, Spoil the Bandit" he concludes: "He's certainly a character, that West Dickens." Before the assault on Fort Mercer, Dickens tells the marshals that when the shooting starts, they should "start awarding each other medals", then corrects himself.
Eli
Johnson's deputy is a customer. He reports Dickens missing to the marshal, saying many townspeople miss him, and he drinks a bottle of the elixir at the start of "Spare the Rod, Spoil the Bandit". When John mocks the tonic, Eli defends it: "It's a medical breakthrough from the East; the result of years of research." At Fort Mercer, he calls for the gate to be opened to let Dickens in.
Jonah
The other deputy does not follow him. In "Spare the Rod, Spoil the Bandit" he says: "I can't understand a goddamn word he says." At Fort Mercer he answers Dickens's medals remark with a puzzled "Huh?".
Bill Williamson
Dickens never meets Bill on screen. He builds his plan around him: "Together we can conquer, if not the world, then certainly Bill Williamson." He also warns John that "Folks around here don't see you as any different than Bill Williamson." Bill has already fled to Mexico when Fort Mercer falls (see Biography).
Edgar Ross
Ross is with John when a police officer brings Dickens into the Blackwater building in "Bear One Another's Burdens". At John's request he tells the officer to let him go, calling him sarcastically "the man's a hero" (see Biography).
Bonnie MacFarlane
They share no scene. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Dickens says he once tried to sell his elixir at MacFarlane's Ranch and was chased off by Bonnie herself.
Nathanial Johnston
The Armadillo doctor treats Dickens's wounds after "Old Swindler Blues". When John arrives in the next mission, Dickens is leaving the office, telling the doctor that miracle cures are no laughing matter and wishing him good day.
Aquila
This man in the Ridgewood Farm crowd calls Dickens a fraud and challenges his volunteer, John, to a series of tests (see Biography).
T. Bernadette Moncrief
In Red Dead Redemption 2, this Annesburg woman writes to the gunsmiths that she ran "Nigel Dickens" off with a sawed-off shotgun after his medicine hooked her parents (see Biography).
His family
The games show no relative. According to the Red Dead Wiki, which quotes conversations with John, his mother worked in a theater, and he had a son who died soon after birth (see Biography).
Trade and the elixir
The sales pitch
His shows follow the same script in Cholla Springs and Plainview: he gathers a crowd, then lists the ailments his tonic cures, from rheumatism, lumbago and neuralgia to toothache, sprains, dislocations and "ravaged nerves". He presents it as the result of years of medical research and as wisdom from the East. He then calls a volunteer from the crowd to drink it and perform feats, a part he gives John at Ridgewood Farm while claiming the man is "entirely unknown to me" (see Biography). His price, according to John, is "2 dollars an ounce"; Dickens offers him "1.95 an ounce" for 100 ounces or more. His four missions with John, two shows and two races, are listed in the Missions table.
The elixir
The bottles carry several names: West Dickens' Elixir, Dr. West Dickens' Own Patent Tonic, and N.W. Dickens' Elixir according to the Red Dead Wiki. The Blackwater Ledger article "Wonder Tonic From The East, Doctors Worried About Future" reports users' claims: a one-legged woman in Plainview grew back her limb, and an old man near death ran to a brothel. The same article lists the ailments the distributor says it cures, among them dysentery, paralysis, malaria and rickets. Marshal Leigh Johnson calls Dickens "the narcotic and bat piss salesman who cons housewives out of their money with promises of eternal youth". After drinking it, John says: "That tonic I drank in Ridgewood went through me like a dose of salts."
In the game, John drinks the elixir in "You Shall Not Give False Testimony, Except for Profit", and this sequence unlocks the second level of Dead Eye, in which targets are marked automatically. According to the Red Dead Wiki, bottles of it stand on a shelf in the house at Tanner's Reach and in the poker room of the Armadillo saloon. In Undead Nightmare, which is not canon, the elixir becomes an item the player can use, Undead Bait, made from one Wild Feverfew and one Prairie Poppy (see Biography for how it is found to attract the undead).
The wagon
He travels in a painted wagon bearing his name, which serves as his shop and stage. John drives it in "Old Swindler Blues" and to Ridgewood Farm; on the escape from Plainview, Dickens takes the reins and John rides shotgun. The race winnings pay for armor plate on it, and Dickens calls it his Trojan horse. In "The Assault on Fort Mercer", its side panels open to reveal John and the machine gun inside. Several of his missions fail if the horses pulling the wagon are killed. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he never rides a horse himself in the game.
Weapons
The Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver as his weapon. In the transcripts of his missions he never fires a shot: at Fort Mercer, his part is to talk to the outlaws and give the signal by rapping on the side of the wagon.
Missions
Dickens has a strand of five story missions in the first act of Red Dead Redemption, all in New Austin. The first, "Old Swindler Blues", becomes available after Leigh Johnson's "Justice in Pike's Basin"; the second also requires Bonnie MacFarlane's "Women and Cattle" and, once completed, opens the strand of Seth Briars. "Liars, Cheats and Other Proud Americans" opens the strand of Irish. "Liars, Cheats and Other Proud Americans" and "The Sport of Kings, and Liars" are two of the three races of the story, with Bonnie's "Obstacles in Our Path"; racing is not offered again in single player. His strand ends with "The Assault on Fort Mercer", given by Leigh Johnson. In Undead Nightmare, he gives two missions.

Development and performance
Casting
Nigel West Dickens appears in Red Dead Redemption and its expansion Undead Nightmare, both released in 2010. He is voiced by Don Creech, credited in the role by Wikipedia and by the Red Dead Wiki. Creech is an American actor born in New York; the Red Dead Wiki gives 30 October 1948 as his date of birth. According to Wikipedia, his other roles include Mr. Sweeney, the science teacher of the series Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide (2004 to 2007), and William Stryker in the film X-Men: First Class (2011).
Production
According to IGN's retrospective "Red Dead Redemption 10 Years On: How Rockstar Won the West", published by Luke Reilly on 22 May 2020 and cited by Wikipedia, the first scene recorded for the game was John Marston's meeting with Dickens. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the game files contain a cut cutscene with the code name fbi03, in which Edgar Ross briefs John with Archer Fordham present; the Dickens scene of "Bear One Another's Burdens" would then have followed.
Reception
Character lists
On 4 November 2016, Dennis Patrick of Gameranx included Dickens among the characters he wanted to see again in Red Dead Redemption 2, describing him as "a man that John Marston hated to work with but managed to come in handy from time-to-time". In a list of the ten best characters of the Red Dead Redemption franchise, published by Sportskeeda on 13 August 2023, Aniket Mukherjee ranked him sixth, between Leigh Johnson (seventh) and Bill Williamson (fifth). The author wrote that his "extravagant schemes and outrageous adventures bring lighthearted humor" to the game's more serious tone.
Other media and legacy
The Man from Blackwater (2010)
Dickens appears in Red Dead Redemption: The Man from Blackwater, a short film made with the game's engine, directed by John Hillcoat and broadcast by Fox on May 29, 2010. The film follows the first act, in which John teams up with Leigh Johnson, Dickens and Seth Briars to take Fort Mercer, and reuses scenes from "Old Swindler Blues" and "The Assault on Fort Mercer" among others.
Multiplayer
Dickens became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Liars and Cheats Pack, detailed by Rockstar on August 27, 2010 and released on September 21, 2010. Players select him in the Redemption section of the Outfitter, alongside John, Abigail, Jack, Bonnie MacFarlane, Leigh Johnson, Luisa Fortuna and Seth Briars. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, do not include the multiplayer mode.
Red Dead Redemption 2
Red Dead Redemption 2 does not show Dickens. He is named in a customer's letter in the Wheeler, Rawson and Co. catalogue and in a 1907 newspaper article (see Biography).
Trivia
Red Dead Redemption
In the Red Dead Wiki transcript of "Old Swindler Blues", Dickens names the Bollard Twins and Walton's Gang when he recognizes John. According to the character page of the same wiki, he only mentions a gang when John wears the Walton's Gang Outfit or the Bollard Twins Outfit.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, when he is low on health during "Old Swindler Blues", Dickens can say "I'm coming home, Daddy."
The mission page of "You Shall Not Give False Testimony, Except for Profit" notes that its title parodies the commandment against bearing false witness (Exodus 20:16 and Deuteronomy 5:20). According to the same page, Dickens can run over a local with his stagecoach on the way into the farm. After the mission, he stays on his wagon; if John threatens him, he runs away or fires back, but he cannot be killed.
The title "Can a Swindler Change His Spots?" refers to Jeremiah 13:23, about the leopard that cannot change its spots. According to the mission page, most of the townspeople Dickens has swindled in that mission belong to the Williamson gang. If the player waits at Cueva Seca at the end of the mission, Dickens urinates on the ground, and keeps going even if knocked over.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Dickens, Agustin Allende and Luisa Fortuna are the only major characters of Red Dead Redemption who never ride a horse.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Dickens says in conversation that he once tried to sell his elixir at MacFarlane's Ranch and was chased away by Bonnie MacFarlane herself; the wiki does not say in which mission.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, a man named Wilton Glover says the tonic makes one's chestnuts bleed.
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
In Undead Nightmare, the elixir becomes the Undead Bait; according to the Red Dead Wiki, it is the only way for the player to obtain the tonic. It can be crafted from one Wild Feverfew and one Prairie Poppy. Landon Ricketts combines it with dynamite to make Boom Bait.
Among the writings on the walls of Blackwater in Undead Nightmare is the sentence "I know a cure for all ailments", which Dickens says to John in "You Shall Not Give False Testimony, Except for Profit".
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Dickens carries a small box that he handles with care and that rattles. The wiki also says that he and Seth are the only uninfected major characters of the expansion who never fight the undead.
Completing "A Cure for Most of What Ails You" and Seth's "Get Back in That Hole, Partner" unlocks the achievement "Judge A Man By The...".
