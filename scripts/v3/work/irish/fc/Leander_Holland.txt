'''Leander Holland''', a.k.a. "'''French'''," is a [[Characters in Redemption#Minor Characters|minor character]] featured in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

==History==
===Background===
French is a small-time career criminal, and is characterized as unkempt, aggressive, and witless. According to [[Irish]], he met French along with [[Alwyn Lloyd]] (a.k.a. "Welsh") on the boat to America, and were 'thick as thieves', literally.

===Events of ''Red Dead Redemption''===
French and his accomplice, Welsh, are in the middle of interrogating Irish by drowning him in a water trough. They accuse Irish of stealing their horses, and demand retribution. When they are interrupted by [[John Marston]], Irish claims he is innocent and that French was the one who stole the horses, although this is never proven. Sent by [[Nigel West Dickens]] to inquire with Irish about weaponry to [[The Assault on Fort Mercer|assault Fort Mercer]], Marston is forced to kill both Welsh and French in self defense and save Irish.

==Trivia==
*Just like Welsh and Irish, his nickname is a direct reference to his heritage.
*Ironically enough, his last name is Holland, a region divided in two provinces located in the Netherlands, another European country.
**However, the surname Holland is of English/Scottish origin, not French (the French surname equivalent would be Hollande).
*While it is stated that Irish met French on the boat over from Europe, French's accent seems to be a mixture of a southern and a Cajun. However, John remarks after killing him and Welsh, to Irish, "He didn't sound very French."
**Given Leander's non-French surname, and no Francophone accent, it is possible that either Leander's backstory is a fabrication or that Leander has lived in the United States long enough to have lost his accent, and potentially changed his name, like from Hollande to Holland.
*He is dressed similar to Irish, with the addition of a hat and a few other items.
*Surprisingly, French can be disarmed, and even shot non-fatally. If disarmed, he will scream something along the lines of "I'm going to cut you like a pig, you son of a bitch!" but will not chase the player. He can, however, attempt to throw a punch at the player if they get close.
*French seems to keep his [[revolver]] tucked at front of his pants rather than in the holster. The same goes for [[Irish]].
*Before completing "A Frenchman, a Welshman and an Irishman", Holland can be seen standing inside the Armadillo saloon. Strangely, the player is able to walk straight through him.
