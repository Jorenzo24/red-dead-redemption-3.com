## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **"Sold" vs "gave" Jack to Bronte.** Wiki EN: "she reveals that she sold Jack". Fiche: "her sons gave the boy to Angelo Bronte" (L2, L39, L72, L82). **Fiche is right**: transcript gsw.txt "My sons gave him to Angelo Bronte"; mission page Blood Feuds says "gave". The money angle is kept correctly as Arthur's line in ch. 4 (L42). Keep.
2. **Who handed Jack over.** Wiki EN: "she reveals that she sold Jack" (Catherine herself). Fiche: her sons. **Fiche is right** (same transcript line).
3. **Number of sons killed.** Wiki FR: "ses deux fils sont tués". Fiche: three sons die (Bartholomew outside, Gareth and Gerald upstairs). **Fiche is right**: mission page Deaths list (Bartholomew by Dutch, Gareth and Gerald by Arthur) and Arthur's "reckon they're all dead" (gsw.txt). Wiki EN also says "all of her sons".
4. **Internal inconsistency (not a wiki difference, but found while comparing).** L38 says Gareth and Gerald "are killed in the attack on the room", then L39 says "Dutch shoots one of her sons in front of her". The mission page resolves it: one of the two is barely alive and Dutch finishes him. Fix L38/L39 as in section 1 #2.
5. **Descent.** Wiki: British; fiche: English (L25). Wiki wording wins since the fiche attributes it to the wiki.
6. **First meeting location.** Wiki EN: "first seen at the Braithwaite manor"; fiche: she meets them "in front of the house" (L33). Consistent (mission page: at Braithwaite Manor); no change.
