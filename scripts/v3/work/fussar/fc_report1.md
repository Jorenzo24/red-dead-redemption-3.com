## Problems

1. **DUPLICATE (FIX_RULES 3)**. A Origins: "Detractors call Fussar a controversial figure who is flamboyant at times and cruel at others and has Guarma under a quasi-military rule." Personality (B) quotes the same words ("a controversial figure who is flamboyant at times and cruel at others", "under a quasi-military rule"). Fix: keep in Personality; in Origins paraphrase without quotation marks (e.g. "The same article notes that critics see him as a controversial and sometimes cruel ruler who governs the island like a military post") or cut.

2. **DUPLICATE (FIX_RULES 3)**. A Origins: "His security force is reportedly feared across the island, with plantation workers often working 14 hour shifts to keep up." Same fact quoted in B "The sugar plantation" (half2: "14-hour shifts", "reportedly feared across the island"). Fix: keep in B "The sugar plantation"; drop from Origins (or one paraphrased clause without quotes).

3. **DUPLICATE (FIX_RULES 3)**. A, chapter 5: "It reports that American business leaders 'speak warmly of Mr. Fussar as a kind and effective leader who brought peace and prosperity to the region, supported workers' rights, and took a particular interest in the lot of women on the island.'" Personality quotes "a kind and effective leader who brought peace and prosperity to the region" and "supported workers' rights". Fix: keep the quotation in Personality; in A paraphrase ("American business leaders praise him in the article") without quotation marks.

4. **DUPLICATE**. A Origins: "Fussar's overseer, Levi Simon, tells the castaways that they run the third most productive sugar plantation in the Northern Caribbean, and that his employer has many powerful friends." Same lines quoted in B "The sugar plantation" and "Soldiers, navy and guns" (half2), "powerful friends" a third time in C Quotes; the same Simon scene is also told again in A chapter 5 ("Simon presents himself as Fussar's senior overseer..."). Fix: keep the quotations in B; cut this sentence from Origins (chapter 5 already tells the scene).

5. **DUPLICATE**. A Origins: "the smuggler Hercule Fontaine calls him 'Colonel Fussar'. His seat is Aguasdulces, which Hercule calls Fussar's compound." Names (B) owns "Hercule calls him Colonel Fussar"; half2 "Governor and colonel" again says colonel "in Hercule's mouth" and quotes "Fussar's compound". Fix: Origins keeps "he holds the rank of colonel" and "his seat is Aguasdulces" without the attribution/quote; keep the Hercule wording in Names and in B "Governor and colonel".

6. **DUPLICATE**. A Origins: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, the soldiers stationed on Guarma belong to the Cuban military and serve under his command..." Repeated in half2 "Governor and colonel" ("The Red Dead Wiki places him at the head of the Cuban Military on the island"). Fix: keep one (B "Governor and colonel"), cut from Origins.

7. **DUPLICATE**. A Origins: "signed an exclusive purchase order with its governor, whom he called 'a great friend of America'" plus "two trips". Told again in Relationships > Leviticus Cornwall (two trips, purchase order, "a great friend of America" quoted) and B "The sugar plantation" ("Cornwall buys the island's sugar under an exclusive purchase order"). Fix: keep the full version in Origins (A owns the article as backstory) and reduce the Cornwall relationship to one clause ("see Biography") without re-quoting; or keep in Relationship and trim Origins. One quotation only.

8. **DUPLICATE (cross-half, rule 3)**. Personality quotes Leon ("A tyrant..."), Bronte ("comes here to whore and despoil himself") and Micah ("thorough, nasty and vindictive"); all three are quoted again verbatim in C Quotes (half2). Arthur's "This goddamn idiot" is fine (not in C). Fix: keep in Personality, drop the three from C Quotes.

9. **DUPLICATE**. Personality: "He questions a captive in front of his troops and turns to them for their verdict." Retells the Despot event already in Biography and in the Javier relationship. Fix: cut, or shorten to a pointer ("see Biography").

10. **IMPRECISE**. Personality: "On Guarma, Fussar addresses his soldiers in Spanish and speaks English to his prisoner and to the gang." Transcript l. 10760-10762: his lines to Javier "Now, that wasn't hard..." and "...I always loved... Mexicans" are in Spanish. Fix: "He speaks English to the gang and partly to Javier, and Spanish to his soldiers."

11. **IMPRECISE (internal inconsistency)**. A: "Arthur pushes a rifle lying on the floor over to the captain". Relationships > The ship's captain (half2) says "Arthur kicks him a rifle". Sources: mission page and wiki character page "kicks"; transcript stage note "tosses the rifle ... into the captain's hands". Fix: use one verb in both places ("kicks", per the mission page).

12. **IMPRECISE**. A Despot: "according to the Red Dead Wiki, he never gives the gang away." Wiki: "he refuses to speak"; javier_A: "he tells them nothing". "Never gives the gang away" overreaches (Fussar learns their identity anyway, Hercule l. 10849). Fix: "according to the Red Dead Wiki, he tells them nothing", as in javier_A.

13. **IMPRECISE**. A Hell Hath No Fury: "A warship appears in the bay and lands soldiers below the fort." Neither the transcript nor the mission page says the warship lands the soldiers: soldiers come down the beach and Hercule reports "boats coming from both sides" while the ship shells the coast. Fix: "A warship appears offshore and shells the coast while soldiers come ashore on the beach below the fort."

14. **IMPRECISE**. Key facts: "Leviticus Cornwall, who called him a great friend of America in the newspaper article 'Guarma Island Sugar Boom'." Cornwall does not speak in the article; the article reports that he "had called" Fussar so. Fix: "...who, according to the newspaper article 'Guarma Island Sugar Boom', called him a great friend of America."

15. **IMPRECISE**. Names: "His full name, Alberto Fussar, is given by Bronte at the party and by the newspapers." Levi Simon ("Senior Overseer for Alberto Fussar", l. 10495) and Hercule ("I'll fight Alberto Fussar every day I can", l. 10946) also use the full name. Fix: add them, or "is used by Bronte, Simon, Hercule and the newspapers".

16. **COPIED**. A Origins: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, those who oppose the governor are tortured and put to death, and the harshness of his rule is what set off the rebellion on the island." Wiki: "Anyone opposing him would be tortured and executed. Fussar's strict and harsh governance led to a rebellion on Guarma." Same structure, synonym swap. Fix: reword more freely, e.g. "The Red Dead Wiki adds that opponents of the regime face torture and execution, and links the island's rebellion to his rule."

17. **STYLE**. A chapter 5: "Fussar is first a name." Literary opener, no fact. Fix: cut, or "Fussar does not appear in the first Guarma missions."

18. **STYLE**. A Despot: "Meanwhile his soldiers threaten Javier with broken kneecaps to make him talk, and Javier keeps repeating that he knows nothing; according to the Red Dead Wiki, he never gives the gang away." Three facts and a wiki attribution in one sentence. Fix: split into two sentences (and apply fix 12).
