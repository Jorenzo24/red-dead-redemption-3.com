Relationships
Rains Fall
Rains Fall, chief of the Wapiti, is his father. According to his father's speech in "My Last Boy", Eagle Flies's elder brother was killed by a drunken soldier and his mother had her throat slit; he is the chief's last son. The two disagree about the Army from their first scene with Arthur: Rains Fall answers his prediction of war by saying the tribe cannot fight another war, and Eagle Flies tells Dutch that his father "has confused wisdom with weakness". After the horse raid he says that his father need not know anything ("he'd rather live in ignorance"), and when Arthur tells him his father sent them to Fort Wallace, he asks: "My father... he told you to come and kill guards?"
Rains Fall tells Arthur that his son is "easily led", that he is foolish but still his son, and that he fears he is a great disappointment to him. In the canoe after Fort Wallace, Eagle Flies says that there will be more trouble and that even his father may have to fight. His father's plea at Beaver Hollow, his answer, and his death in his father's tent are told in the Biography.
Arthur Morgan
Eagle Flies first sees Arthur with the gang's wagon train and at the mayor's party, then meets him outside the Saint Denis courthouse, where he takes him for a mercenary. After the refinery, Arthur thanks him for saving his life. When Arthur starts to tell Dutch what he and Charles did for him in "A Rage Unleashed", Eagle Flies finishes the sentence: "Pretended to be mercenaries... did me a great favor." Arthur warns Rains Fall that Dutch does not have his son's interests at heart, and goes to Fort Wallace with Charles despite the chief's refusal (see Biography).
At the oil fields Eagle Flies saves Arthur, whom Dutch has left behind, and is shot by Colonel Favours (see Biography). On the ride to the reservation Arthur tells Charles: "If it wasn't for Eagle Flies, I'd be...", and calls him "a brave man" ("Your friend here... my friend... he's a brave man."). On the way to rescue Abigail in "Our Best Selves", Arthur names him to Sadie among the people Dutch has led to their deaths: "Another angry fool he used, just like he did with the rest of us."
Charles Smith
On the way to Beaver Hollow after Guarma, Charles tells Arthur that he ran into Rains Fall and Eagle Flies while Arthur was away and has been helping on the reservation. In a rare chapter 4 camp scene, Charles says he will be away for a few days to help Eagle Flies free members of the tribe being sent to a reform school, and turns down Arthur's offer to come. In "A Rage Unleashed", Charles reminds him: "You know I told your father I will not fight over some horses." When Eagle Flies says his father need not know about the raid, Charles answers: "Your father would rather you did not do anything so foolish!" On the way to Fort Wallace he tells Arthur that Dutch "saw Eagle Flies burned hot, and he fanned the flames".
Charles plans and leads the rescue from Fort Wallace and takes him home (see Biography). In the canoe, when Eagle Flies repeats Dutch's idea that the soldiers only have the power you allow them to have, Charles answers: "Well, Dutch says a lot of things." On the bank he tells him: "Hey, friend... let me take you back to your father." At the oil fields Charles rides with the group that reaches him, stays at his side while Dutch goes for the bonds ("I'm staying with you"), and carries him into his father's tent with Arthur. Afterwards Charles stays with the tribe.
Dutch van der Linde
Dutch meets him at Beaver Hollow in "A Rage Unleashed". When Eagle Flies offers money, Dutch tells him: "Put your money away, son." On the ride he explains to Arthur why he is helping: "Some good honest conflict between the army and the Indians... could be just the distraction we need." On the bank he tells Eagle Flies "I like you, son" and rides off with him to return the horses. In "Favored Sons" he plans the ambush with him (see Biography), and in the canoe from Fort Wallace Eagle Flies repeats one of Dutch's ideas about the Army's power.
When Eagle Flies rides into Beaver Hollow to call the gang to the oil fields, Dutch tells him: "I love your courage, son. It is a thing of great beauty." On the way he admits to Arthur that the attack was partly his idea. At the refinery, Eagle Flies refuses to leave with the wounded: "Until I find all my men, I'm not leaving." Dutch answers "As you wish" and goes for the state bonds; the rest is told in the Biography.
Paytah
Paytah is his companion among the Wapiti. He waits with the canoes for the horse raid and boards the boat with him, and he is beside him in Cumberland Forest, where Eagle Flies goes to help him when he is wounded. At the oil fields Eagle Flies sees him in danger and greets him with "Paytah, you're alive." On the ride to the reservation the dying Eagle Flies calls his name; Paytah answers: "It's okay, brother, save your breath." He tells Arthur that Eagle Flies was "Fearless, always has been." The Red Dead Wiki credits Loren Anthony as Paytah's voice.
Henry Favours
The colonel commands the regiment at Fort Wallace. Eagle Flies calls him a liar and a murderer and holds him responsible for the seizure of the horses, the withholding of medicine and the removal of the young to reform schools (see In his own words). After the patrol ambush Eagle Flies is held in the fort, where he jokes about being tortured; Favours shoots him at the refinery (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, Favours had been paid by Leviticus Cornwall to drive the tribe off its land.
Leviticus Cornwall
Both of Eagle Flies's attacks target Cornwall's refinery, Cornwall Kerosene & Tar, at the Heartland Oil Fields: first to steal the Leland Oil Development Company report on the oil near Wapiti, then in the battle of "My Last Boy". When he rides into Beaver Hollow he says the Army tried to kill his people "for oil". The two never meet on screen; Cornwall is already dead when Eagle Flies first comes to Beaver Hollow ("With Cornwall gone", Dutch tells Arthur on the ride).
Evelyn Miller
The writer introduces Eagle Flies and his father to Arthur outside the Saint Denis courthouse and explains what is at stake (see Biography). When Miller reminds them of their appointment with a senator, Eagle Flies calls it "a waste of our time and his".
Sadie Adler
Sadie volunteers to ride to the oil fields and is part of the group that reaches Eagle Flies on the walkway; if Arthur does not pull the soldier off him, she does. After he is shot she offers to come with Arthur to take him to his father; Arthur sends her back.
Karen Jones
Karen and Javier escort Eagle Flies into Beaver Hollow in "A Rage Unleashed" (see Biography); she holds a double-barreled shotgun, without firing it, when he arrives.
Javier Escuella
Javier escorts him into camp with Karen in "A Rage Unleashed", and volunteers to ride to the oil fields in "My Last Boy". At the refinery Dutch tells Javier to get the others out ("Javier, you get them and you go.") while he goes for the bonds.
John Marston
John volunteers to follow Eagle Flies's war party in "My Last Boy" and, on the way, asks why the Wapiti would attack the oil fields. The gallery shows John at Eagle Flies's grave in 1907.
Angelo Bronte
From a balcony at the mayor's party in "The Gilded Cage", Bronte points out Eagle Flies and Rains Fall to Dutch and Arthur as "the redskins" handing a letter to the mayor, and mocks their hopes (see Biography).
Raids, weapons and horses
Missions and raids
The Red Dead Wiki lists Eagle Flies as the giver of "American Fathers II" and "Favored Sons" (the full list is in the Missions table). In his missions, abandoning him, attacking him or letting him die fails the mission. For the refinery job he supplies the plan: the name of the foreman, Danbury, the window of the office where the files are kept, the name of the report wanted (from the Leland Oil Development Company) and two ways in, under the fence or hidden in one of the wagons that keep rolling in. He tells Arthur to call him if there is a problem; according to the Red Dead Wiki, it is he who sets off the oil well explosion that lets Arthur get away. He also carries the payment.
In chapter 6 he organizes as much as he fights. For the horse raid he knows where the boat is moored near Van Horn and has a man, Paytah, waiting with canoes. On the clifftop in Cumberland Forest he keeps lookout with a spotter, tells Arthur and Dutch when to come back up, gives the signal and tells his men to wait for his lead; after the fight he has the soldiers searched for anything his father could use to strengthen the tribe's case. At the oil fields he fights across the walkways and through the train with the group that reached him.
Weapons and horses
The Red Dead Wiki lists four weapons for him: a bow, a Carbine Repeater, a Lancaster Repeater and a Springfield Rifle. After his rescue from Fort Wallace he is unarmed and asks for a gun in the canoe. His mounts, according to the same wiki, are a Splashed White American Paint, a Splashed White American Paint on a palomino base and a Tobiano American Paint; the wiki adds that his American Paint is a unique horse resembling the Splashed White coat. He escapes Fort Wallace on Taima, seated behind Charles, and after the oil fields Arthur carries him to the reservation on his own horse.
Missions
The English Red Dead Wiki lists eight missions of Red Dead Redemption 2 for Eagle Flies, from the gang's ride to Horseshoe Overlook in chapter 1 to the battle at the oil fields in chapter 6. The French edition also counts eight, but groups the two parts of "American Fathers" under one title and adds "Archeology for Beginners", in which he does not appear: his father only talks about him. He appears in no other game of the series. The second table lists the missions in which he is only mentioned.

Development and performance
Casting
Eagle Flies is played by Jeremiah Bitsui, a Navajo actor of Omaha descent born in Chinle, Arizona. Wikipedia credits Bitsui with both the voice and the motion capture of the character. Bitsui is best known as Victor, the henchman he played in Breaking Bad (2009-2011) and again in Better Call Saul (2017-2022). His other roles include a child part in Natural Born Killers (1994), the series Bosch (2019-2020) and Dark Winds (2022). Wikipedia's list of the game's characters states that he worked on Red Dead Redemption 2 for about three years, citing an interview he gave to The Colt Balok Show on October 31, 2018, a few days after the game's release.
Cut content
According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on cut content, the files hold an unfinished chapter 4 mission, labelled "ntv0", in which Arthur, Charles and Eagle Flies were to ambush an army convoy carrying Native American children to a boarding school. Its surviving animations show a Native American pleading for his life because he has a family, and a soldier who claims he only obeyed orders and tries to kill him. The opening dialogue between Arthur and Charles survives in a shorter form, as the rare chapter 4 camp scene described under Relationships. The same page notes that the game's trailer showed Eagle Flies with a different appearance in his final mission, and that unused eye models for him remain in the files.
Reception
Critics and scholars
In an IGN feature on the game's historical accuracy, posted on December 24, 2018, Jared Petty compared the Wapiti storyline to the seizure of the Black Hills in the 1870s and to the treatment of the Osage in the 1920s. He summarized Eagle Flies's arc in these terms: he "comes to believe that the situation is simply too impossible for peace to accomplish anything and declares war on the invading army forces, against the wishes of his father", coaxed by Dutch. Petty added that "Young warriors following their passionate, angry peer against the wishes of an authority figure is another common theme in American tribal culture", since chiefs often led by reputation rather than by legal power.
In an article published in 2025 in the journal American, British and Canadian Studies, Andrei Nae of the University of Bucharest counts Eagle Flies among the three Native American characters who matter to the game's story and play, with Rains Fall and Charles Smith. He writes that Eagle Flies "advocates for violent resistance and often engages in violent action against the government and capitalist interests", that Arthur's task is "to rein in the violent impulses of the young Native American", and that "The narrative arc of Eagle Flies is a cautionary tale warning against Native Americans using violence" in a game whose mechanics are defined by violence.
Other media and legacy
Eagle Flies appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2. The Red Dead Wiki lists no Red Dead Online appearance for him. Rockstar's character descriptions published on September 6, 2018 cover the members of the Van der Linde gang and include none for him. According to the mission page of "American Fathers II", the escape from the refinery, with Arthur climbing out of a window, being held up by guards and saved by an exploding oil well, featured in several pre-release promotional videos and trailers of the game.
Trivia
In "American Fathers II" and "A Rage Unleashed", attacking, killing or abandoning Eagle Flies fails the mission, according to the mission pages; in "American Fathers II", letting him be killed by the guards also fails it.
According to the mission page of "My Last Boy", sixteen braves ride into Beaver Hollow with Eagle Flies and Paytah.
In "My Last Boy", if Arthur does not pull the soldier off Eagle Flies on the walkway, Sadie does it instead (mission page).
If the player does not shoot Colonel Favours in time, Favours kills Arthur and the mission fails, with the message "Failed: You have died" (mission page). According to the wiki's page on Favours, looting the colonel's body after the cutscene makes Eagle Flies die on the spot and fails the mission.
The Gold Medal objectives of "My Last Boy" include saving Paytah, who struggles with a soldier near a train car after the Gatling gun train arrives.
According to the wiki's page on the reservation, Wapiti inhabitants are hostile to Arthur at first, then greet him once he has done a few missions for Rains Fall and Eagle Flies.
