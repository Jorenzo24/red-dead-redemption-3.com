Relationships
Eagle Flies
Eagle Flies is his younger son and, after the deaths of his elder son and his wife, his only remaining family (see Biography). In Saint Denis, Eagle Flies predicts war and calls Arthur a mercenary, while his father insists on trying every peaceful avenue. Rains Fall calls him "the impetuous Prince" in front of Arthur and tells Arthur that his son is "easily led". In "A Rage Unleashed", Eagle Flies says his father need not know about the raid and would rather live in ignorance.
After his capture, Rains Fall calls him foolish but says he loves him, and first refuses Arthur's offer to free him from Fort Wallace. At Beaver Hollow, Eagle Flies rejects his plea and rides off to the oil fields. He dies in his father's tent on the reservation (see Biography).
Arthur Morgan
When they meet in Saint Denis, Rains Fall tells Arthur he has already seen him on the gang's wagon train, crossing the river at Cumberland Falls, and upstairs at the mayor's party. In his tent, Arthur tells him he thinks he is dying, and the chief answers that he hopes he finds peace. On the ride of "Archeology for Beginners", Arthur can tell him about his son Isaac, and warns him that Dutch does not have Eagle Flies's best interests at heart. In "The King's Son", Rains Fall asks him about his father; Arthur answers that he has been dead a long time.
After that mission, Arthur writes in his journal that he once would have found such a man weak, and now sees him as "wise and thoughtful and sensible". On the way to Fort Wallace he tells Charles: "I like Rains Fall." In "My Last Boy", the chief turns to Arthur for help in front of the gang, which reveals their dealings to Dutch. In 1907 Rains Fall speaks of Arthur to John (see Biography).
Charles Smith
Charles helps the Wapiti on their reservation while Arthur is on Guarma, and he has promised Rains Fall to stay out of the tribe's conflict with the Army. Rains Fall tells Arthur that most of what he knows about Dutch comes from Charles. Charles brings the chief to Arthur at Beaver Hollow and persuades Arthur to come to the talks ("Do it for me"). Rains Fall later tells Charles of the Army's reprisals after the meeting: women taken, elders beaten. After Eagle Flies's death, Charles tells him: "I'm sorry, but we... we must pack and move." He stays behind to help the tribe.
Dutch van der Linde
Rains Fall knows Dutch only by reputation. He tells Arthur that Dutch "talks a lot" and that his son follows him. After Eagle Flies's capture, he says Dutch has made relations with the Army worse than at any point in the last five years, adding that he is sure Dutch means well but that "matters are more complex than he understands". At Beaver Hollow, Dutch praises Eagle Flies's courage while the chief begs his son to stay; when Rains Fall turns to Arthur, Dutch asks: "You helped this feller, Arthur?"
Henry Favours
The colonel commands the regiment at Fort Wallace. Rains Fall tells Arthur that Favours has already taken revenge for Eagle Flies's raid, two women having been assaulted by his men, and describes him to Arthur: "he walks an old line, he's obstinate and he hates Monroe". At the talks, Favours declares the treaty void and the reservation the property of the United States government; Rains Fall ends the meeting with "Good day, Colonel Favours." According to the Red Dead Wiki, Favours had agreed with Leviticus Cornwall to drive the tribe off its land. He shoots Eagle Flies at the oil fields (see Biography).
Lyndon Monroe
Captain Monroe was reassigned to the area from a regiment in the north, and Rains Fall says that news of the conflict has reached Washington. "He's a good man, he wants to help," the chief tells Arthur. Monroe brings him the news that the oil company has been approved to drill on the reservation. Rains Fall thanks Arthur for helping Monroe recover the vaccines, if the player did. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rains Fall is the only character to say Monroe's first name, once, in "The Fine Art of Conversation", and only if Arthur has not met Monroe before. His arrest at the talks is told under Biography.
Evelyn Miller
The writer introduces Rains Fall and Eagle Flies to Arthur outside the courthouse in Saint Denis. He says the planned removal of the tribe contravenes the peace treaty signed three years earlier, and that prospectors have filed reports with Leviticus Cornwall and the State Government claiming large oil reserves under the reservation. In "The Gilded Cage", Dutch tells Arthur that Miller is lobbying officials in Saint Denis on the chief's behalf.
Leviticus Cornwall
The oil on the reservation ties Cornwall to the tribe's troubles: according to Evelyn Miller, the prospectors' reports were filed with him, and the Leland Oil Development Company file on the oil near Wapiti, which Arthur takes in "American Fathers II", is kept in the foreman's office of the Cornwall Kerosene & Tar refinery. Rains Fall never meets him on screen.
Paytah
Paytah, Eagle Flies's companion, is one of the three riders on the hill in "Eastward Bound", according to the Red Dead Wiki. In "My Last Boy" he rides off with Eagle Flies despite the chief's plea. On the way back to the reservation with the dying Eagle Flies, he tells Arthur: "Better to die fighting than sick and weak."
Hosea Matthews
In "Eastward Bound", three riders watch the gang's wagons from a hill; the Red Dead Wiki identifies them as Rains Fall, Eagle Flies and Paytah. Hosea tells Arthur that the natives of the Heartlands lost all their land and were killed or herded onto reservations, and that he heard the Army there was particularly harsh about it.
Angelo Bronte
From a balcony at the mayor's party in "The Gilded Cage", Bronte points out Rains Fall and Eagle Flies to Dutch and Arthur as they hand a letter to the mayor, Henri Lemieux. He says he has no sympathy for them, since anyone stupid enough to be tricked by the Americans gets what they deserve.
John Marston
John is one of the gang members who volunteer to ride with the Wapiti in "My Last Boy". In 1907 he can meet Rains Fall at the Annesburg train station, where the chief recognizes him as one of Arthur's companions (see Biography).
Chief, remedies and horses
Chief and negotiator
As chief of the Wapiti, Rains Fall deals with the authorities on the tribe's behalf (the treaties he signed are told under Biography). In Saint Denis, with Evelyn Miller, he waits for an appointment with a city councillor and a senator, and he offers to pay Arthur "very handsomely" to obtain proof about the oil on the reservation. Captain Monroe reports to him on his talks with the mayor and the Bureau of Indian Affairs. He arranges the meeting with Colonel Favours himself, and asks for people from outside the tribe to be present, believing it will make lying harder for the Army.
Herbs and remedies
During the ride of "Archeology for Beginners", he stops twice to pick plants for Arthur's cough: English Mace, then ginseng. He puts them in Arthur's saddlebag with this advice: "Mix these together. They taste awful... but it'll help to keep your strength up." The mission page lists three English Mace among the rewards, and notes that English Mace and ginseng are two of the three ingredients of a Special Health Cure, the third being Yarrow. If no soldier is killed, he gives Arthur the Owl Feather Trinket, which can be obtained no other way. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the trinket slows the drain of the Health, Stamina and Dead Eye cores by 15 percent.
Horses
Rains Fall rides his own horse to the sacred site, to the gang's camp at Beaver Hollow and to the peace talks. The Red Dead Wiki lists three mounts for him: a Blue Roan Nokota, a Grullo Dun Mustang and a Tobiano American Paint.

Development and performance
Casting
Rains Fall appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). He is played by Graham Greene, an Oneida actor from the Six Nations of the Grand River reserve in Ontario, born on June 22, 1952. Wikipedia credits Greene with both the voice and the motion capture of the character. Greene was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for Kicking Bird in Dances with Wolves (1990). His other roles include Thunderheart (1992), Maverick (1994), The Green Mile (1999), Wind River (2017), the series 1883 (2022) and Reservation Dogs (2023). Insider Gaming noted in September 2025 that Rains Fall was his only video game role.
In a feature on the making of the game published by Vulture on October 14, 2018, Harold Goldberg called Greene "the most famous actor in RDR2" and wrote that the role, a small one, was close to the heart of the writer Dan Houser. Houser said: "He did such a brilliant job of bringing this chief character to life. The government is coming down on him hard. He's losing his rights as an independent king, and he's a gentle soul in that violent world. [Greene] bought [sic] this great depth to [the story]." In the same article, Houser explained that Rockstar no longer cast well-known actors, preferring performers whose voices players do not recognize.
Graham Greene died on September 1, 2025, at the age of 73, after a long illness. According to his manager, Gerry Jordan, quoted by Radio Canada International on September 2, 2025, he died in Stratford, Ontario, which CTV News reported the same day; some early reports had placed his death in a Toronto hospital. Gaming sites including Insider Gaming and RDR2.org marked his death by recalling the role of Rains Fall.
The Wapiti and the Lakota language
The Wapiti are a fictional people. Chris Plante wrote in Polygon's review, on October 25, 2018, that Rockstar had told him the tribe is "not modelled on a specific tribe". When Rains Fall speaks to his men on the way to the peace talks in "The Fine Art of Conversation", the subtitle labels his line as Lakota; the Red Dead Wiki notes that the game's captions identify the Wapiti's language as Lakota.
Cut content
According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on cut content, concept art shows that Rains Fall was first named Rains Come; the drawings are filed under the name of the artist Daren Bader. The sacred site he visits with Arthur in "Archeology for Beginners" was labeled Cohutta on a leaked map, a name the final game does not use. The game files also contain an unused official, Mr. Pattison, meant to appear on the Wapiti reservation, and a cut system in which Wapiti warriors would have acted as lawmen in the Ambarino region.
Reception
Critics and scholars
In Polygon's review, Chris Plante wrote that the game's mixing of real peoples, places and groups produced story beats ranging "from benign to confused to insensitive and harmful". After Greene's death, Andrew Highton of Insider Gaming wrote on September 2, 2025 that "Graham absolutely nails the role perfectly", and Ray Ampoloquio of RDR2.org described Rains Fall as "a father figure and moral compass for Arthur Morgan".
In an article published in 2025 in the journal American, British and Canadian Studies, Andrei Nae of the University of Bucharest counts Rains Fall among the three Native American characters who matter to the game's story and play, with Eagle Flies and Charles Smith. He contrasts the two Wapiti: "While the elder Rains Fall is in favour of diplomatic solutions (in spite of the untrustworthiness of the federal government), the young Eagle Flies, under the negative influence of Dutch van der Linde, advocates for violent resistance." He argues that the game, while marking "a significant shift" in its portrayal of Native Americans, casts them in "the stereotype of the noble savage who cannot help themselves and are in need of a white saviour".
Other media and legacy
Rains Fall appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2. He was not part of the series of character portraits and descriptions that Rockstar posted on September 6, 2018, and that The Hollywood Reporter reproduced the next day: that series covered the members of the Van der Linde gang. The Red Dead Wiki lists no Red Dead Online appearance for him. In Red Dead Online, the Wapiti Indian Reservation is empty and abandoned, as it is in 1907.
Trivia
When Rains Fall speaks a Native American language instead of English, the in-game captions identify it as Lakota. He uses it at the peace talks to tell his men that Arthur and Charles are friends.
The Owl Feather Trinket he gives Arthur slows the drain of all of Arthur's cores by 15 percent, according to the mission page.
The two herbs he picks during "Archeology for Beginners", English mace and ginseng, are two of the three ingredients of a Special Health Cure; only yarrow is missing.
The wolves that Arthur scares off on the ride with Rains Fall are, according to the mission page, the only wolves in the game that are not hostile to the player.
Before a game update, the sacred site was already burned when visited in free roam, even as early as chapter 2. Since the update, the site is intact if visited before the mission becomes available.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rains Fall is the only character to say Captain Monroe's first name, once, in "The Fine Art of Conversation", and only if Arthur has not met Monroe before.
In "My Last Boy", Rains Fall uses the word "implore" to try to stop Eagle Flies, with John present. John uses the same word at Fort Mercer in 1911 when he tries to make Bill Williamson give himself up. Neither plea works.
Meeting Rains Fall in the epilogue gives the player a sizable honor gain.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rains Fall looks visibly older in the 1907 credits scene, while at Annesburg station the same year he looks as he did in 1899.
