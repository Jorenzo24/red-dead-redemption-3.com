L8: Colter, an abandoned mining town in the Grizzlies West of Ambarino, the gang's camp in Chapter 1.
L9: Chapter 1: Colter is the first of the six chapters of Red Dead Redemption 2, followed by a two-part epilogue. It is set in May 1899 in the Grizzlies West, in the state of Ambarino, a few days after a failed ferry robbery in Blackwater. The chapter has six story missions, and two of them, "The Aftermath of Genesis" and "Old Friends", can be played in either order. The Red Dead Wiki describes it as the shortest chapter of the game.
L10: Full spoilers for the opening of Red Dead Redemption 2 follow.
L11: In-game chapter overview
L12: The game's pause menu summarizes the chapter with this text:
L13: "After a failed robbery, the gang have fled from Blackwater into the Grizzly Mountains, but a late spring storm has left them half frozen and starving. They hole up in an abandoned mining town to lick their wounds and await a break in the weather."
L14: The game itself opens on white text over a black screen: "By 1899, the age of outlaws and gunslingers was at an end. America was becoming a land of laws... Even the west had mostly been tamed. A few gangs still roamed but they were being hunted down and destroyed."
L15: Background: the Blackwater ferry robbery
L16: The robbery that sends the gang into the mountains is never shown. What the player learns about it comes from the characters during the chapter.
L17: In his speech at Colter, Dutch van der Linde names the people the gang has lost or cannot account for: Davey Callander, Jenny Kirk, Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander. He says of Sean and Mac: "they may be okay, we don't know."
L18: Javier Escuella, who was there, tells Arthur in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": "We had the money, it seemed fine, then suddenly they were everywhere." The attackers were Pinkerton agents.
L19: Javier also says that Dutch "killed a girl in a... bad way, but it was a bad situation." Arthur answers that it is not like him. The Red Dead Wiki names the young woman Heidi McCourt.
L20: According to Javier, Davey, Mac and John were all shot, and nobody knows what happened to Sean. He adds that the men on the boat "were only just holding on" by the time Arthur and the others reached the other side of town.
L21: Asked by Arthur, Dutch says the money was "stashed with the rest of the money in town" and that the gang cannot go back to collect it any time soon. In an optional answer, he says of the Pinkertons: "That many men? Oh, they knew we were coming."
L22: Simon Pearson, the camp cook, says he could not bring supplies when they ran. At the next camp, Susan Grimshaw tells Arthur that most of his belongings from Blackwater were saved, except his money.
L23: In "Eastward Bound", Hosea Matthews tells Arthur that the two of them kept telling Dutch the ferry job "didn't feel right", and that they had "a real lead in Blackwater". At the new camp he tells Dutch: "then Micah got you all excited about that ferry and here we are."
L24: Newspapers in the game later put the sum aboard the ferry at 150,000 dollars. The fates of Sean and Mac are revealed in Chapter 2.
L25: The missions of Chapter 1
L26: Dutch holds up a lantern in the blizzard during "Outlaws from the West".
L27: 1. Outlaws from the West
L28: The first mission is given by Dutch. It starts and ends at Colter and takes Arthur to the Adler Ranch.
L29: At night, the gang's wagons push through a snowstorm in the Grizzlies. Reverend Orville Swanson tells Dutch that Abigail says Davey is dying and that they must stop. Hosea remarks that it is May. Arthur Morgan, sent ahead by Dutch, rides back and reports an abandoned mining town nearby. He leads the wagons there. The title card "Chapter I: Colter" follows.
L30: Hosea opens the first cabin, revolver drawn. Abigail, Bill Williamson and Arthur carry in Davey Callander on a stretcher, already dead. Susan Grimshaw sends Mary-Beth to light the fire, Karen to fetch blankets and Pearson to check the food. Abigail says: "Davey's dead." Dutch announces that he sent John and Micah ahead to scout and that he and Arthur will ride out to find one of them. He then addresses the gang and asks Pearson and Susan to turn the place into a camp. He ends with: "Stay strong. Stay with me. We ain't done yet!"
L31: Outside, Arthur asks what really happened on the boat. Dutch answers: "We missed you, that's what happened." Charles Smith brings them two horses; Dutch tells him to get indoors and rest his hand. Arthur rides Charles's mare, Taima. On the trail, Dutch tells Arthur he is the only one he can rely on to stay strong. The player can then ask about the money or about whether Blackwater was a trap.
L32: They meet Micah Bell, who has found a homestead "blazing with light and noise", where it sounds like a party. Dutch tells him that Davey and Jenny did not make it. Micah has not seen John since the storm came in. Alone with Arthur, Micah complains that the gang has too many mouths to feed.
L33: At the homestead, Dutch sends Micah behind a wagon and Arthur behind a shed, and goes to the door alone. He poses as a lost traveler and asks for a few cans of food. Micah finds a body under a sheet in the wagon. One of the men recognizes Dutch and shouts that Colm will be furious. The men are Colm O'Driscoll's gang. Arthur or Micah fires first, and the three outlaws kill the O'Driscolls at the house.
L34: Dutch and Arthur search the cabin for food, medicine and ammunition. Arthur notes that there is a large price on Colm's head; Dutch answers that wanting Colm dead "is about the only thing me and Uncle Sam agree on." Arthur suggests moving the women and Jack to the house, but Dutch does not want the gang to split up.
L35: In the barn, an O'Driscoll who hid during the shootout jumps Arthur from the loft. Arthur beats him while Dutch watches. Under questioning, the man says the others are at an old mining camp southwest of the ranch, near a lake, and that they plan to rob a train and blow the tracks. Dutch leaves his fate to Arthur, who can kill him, knock him out or let him go. According to the mission page, a spared O'Driscoll reappears in "Old Friends", where he is killed. Arthur then calms a Tennessee Walker in the barn, and Dutch tells him to keep it.
L36: A woman screams inside the house. Micah has found her hiding in the cellar and insists she is one of the O'Driscolls. She throws a bottle at him, then holds him off with a knife from the table. Dutch tells Micah: "No, she ain't, Micah. Look at her." Micah flips the table, a lantern falls and the house catches fire. Dutch and Arthur push Micah away, and Dutch calms the woman and leads everyone outside. She says the men came three days earlier and killed her husband. Arthur helps her onto Dutch's horse and tells her: "We're bad men, but... we ain't them." On the ride back she gives her name: Sadie Adler. The house burns with her husband Jake's body still in the wagon.
L37: At Colter, Lenny Summers is on watch and calls the others out. Dutch tells Hosea that O'Driscolls are in the area, scouting a train; Hosea answers: "That's the last thing we need right now, Dutch." Dutch asks Tilly and Karen to look after Sadie and says he has not slept in three days. Susan hands out the rooms. Micah protests that Arthur gets a room to himself while he shares a bunk room with Bill and others, using a racial slur, and Hosea sends him to bed.
L38: The mission rewards Arthur with the Tennessee Walker from the barn, according to the mission page. It continues directly into the next mission.
L39: Dutch, Arthur and Micah ride through the snowstorm toward the Adler Ranch in "Outlaws from the West".
L40: 2. Enter, Pursued by a Memory
L41: The mission is given by Abigail, listed under her 1899 name, Abigail Roberts, on the mission page. It starts and ends at Colter and takes place in the Grizzlies West.
L42: In the main cabin, Abigail tells Arthur that John Marston has not been seen for two days. Arthur is reluctant and mocks John, calling him "little John". Hosea tells him to at least go and look, and asks Javier to ride out with him, calling them "the two best fit men we've got." Javier agrees, saying that John would look for him if things were reversed. He hands Arthur a sawed-off shotgun.
L43: John was last seen heading up the river. Arthur suggests he may have "kept riding north and never looked back", and adds that it would not be the first time; Javier says John would not leave like that. They find an abandoned campsite and follow horse tracks across a stream and up the mountain. On the way, Javier gives his account of Blackwater.
L44: On a flat stretch of the mountain they find John's horse, dead and mauled; Javier says John was riding it when they left Blackwater. Javier fires a shot in the air, and John's shouts answer from higher up. The two men leave their horses and climb on foot. If the player does not use provisions on the climb, Javier shares a bottle of Kentucky bourbon with Arthur, according to the mission page. They find John lying on a ledge, his face torn by wolves. John says: "Never thought I'd say this, but... it's good to see you, Arthur Morgan."
L45: Arthur carries John on his shoulder. Arthur tells John: "See, I told Dutch you weren't the right man for this." Three wolves appear on the slope. Arthur hands John to Javier, draws the wolves onto himself and kills them. On the ride down, more wolves attack and are shot. They ride in the water for a while to lose the scent. Arthur tells John they will need a better story for the scar.
L46: Back at Colter, Bill and Lenny pull John off Javier's horse so roughly that Javier calls them idiots and tells them to mind his leg. Abigail thanks the two men and tells John that this is "a new low, even by your standards." Hosea thanks them as well. Arthur asks how the gang will get out of this; Hosea, who has been talking with Leopold Strauss, says they will keep heading east when the weather breaks. Arthur is surprised at the idea of moving toward civilization. Hosea answers: "the west is where our problems are worse."
L47: The mission closes on Arthur writing in his journal: "We have been running for weeks." The reward is the sawed-off shotgun. After this mission, the player can walk around the camp, according to the mission page.
L48: 3. The Aftermath of Genesis
L49: The mission is given by Simon Pearson at Colter, where it starts and ends. It can be played before or after "Old Friends".
L50: In the camp's kitchen, Pearson tells Arthur they have a few cans of food and a rabbit for ten or twelve people. He starts a story about being stranded at sea for fifty days in the Navy, which Arthur refuses to hear. Pearson says he sent Lenny and Bill hunting and they found nothing. Charles cuts the argument short: "Enough of this. We'll go find something."
L51: Arthur points out Charles's injured hand. Charles says he burned it, a "stupid mistake", and expects it to heal in a day or two. Pearson throws Arthur a can of "Assorted Salted Offal" for the road. Since Charles cannot draw a bow, he gives his own to Arthur and explains that a gun would scare every animal for miles.
L52: On the ride, Charles says he still does not really know what happened on the boat; Arthur says Javier told him a bit, "but... it sure weren't good." Charles finds deer tracks near a stream and teaches Arthur to follow them on foot, stay low and aim for the neck or head. Arthur kills two deer, and the two men carry one each to their horses.
L53: On the way back, the dialogue depends on the order of play. If "Old Friends" is done, Charles notes that the gang has picked up two more mouths to feed, and Arthur says the O'Driscoll prisoner will get nothing but the can of salted offal. In both versions Charles says Sadie has "a wild look in her eye", and Arthur says she has lost her husband, her home and everything she had, and may have family somewhere. Arthur explains that the gang has been fighting the O'Driscolls over scores for years and that Colm and Dutch "go way back, and not in a good way."
L54: A grizzly bear blocks the trail. Charles refuses to hunt it, saying they have enough meat, and they go around it. According to the mission page, the bear cannot be killed in this mission. Arthur notes that Charles has been with them for five or six months and asks why he has not moved on. Charles says he rode alone for a long time and is done with it, and that Dutch treats him fair: "And for a feller with a black father and an Indian mother, that ain't normally the case." Asked in return, Arthur says he has been with Dutch for "Twenty years, something like that. Since I was a boy." Dutch taught him and John to read, and Arthur adds: "Dutch saved me, saved most of us."
L55: At Colter, Uncle is loitering at Pearson's table and leaves after trading jabs with Arthur. Pearson hands round a bottle of Navy rum. Charles leaves to rest his hand, and Arthur helps Pearson skin a deer. Pearson tells him pelts sell in almost any town, and asks him to bring anything else he catches.
L56: The reward is the bow. According to the mission page, the mission unlocks Eagle Eye, and if it is played after "Old Friends", it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".
L57: 4. Old Friends
L58: The mission is given by Bill Williamson in the men's cabin at Colter. It takes place at Ewing Basin, in the Grizzlies West, and ends at Colter. It can be played before or after "The Aftermath of Genesis".
L59: In the cabin, Micah mocks Bill and Lenny for their long faces. Bill says people miss the ones who fell. Lenny tells Micah there will be a party when he falls, and Bill laughs. Micah punches Bill in the face; Arthur, Javier and Lenny hold the two men apart. Dutch bursts in: "You fools punching each other... when Colm O'Driscoll's needing punching, hard."
L60: Arthur asks whether a raid is wise when the gang is barely back on its feet, and reminds Dutch that he always said revenge is a luxury they cannot afford. Dutch answers that the O'Driscolls "were talking about trains and detonators", and that Colm always had good information. He hands Arthur a Carbine Repeater and a lasso. Dutch leaves Hosea, Charles and Pearson to watch the camp, and rides out with Arthur, Micah, Bill, Javier and Lenny.
L61: They follow horse tracks southwest along the main trail. Dutch says: "Some things I can forgive, others I can forget. What he did to Annabelle, I can't do neither." Arthur reminds him that he killed Colm's brother. Dutch answers that he hopes the two will be reunited soon.
L62: From a ridge above the camp near the lake, Dutch posts Bill and Micah in a hidden position outside the camp and keeps Lenny and Javier on the high ground. Through binoculars, Dutch and Arthur watch Colm strike one of his men, who rides away; Colm leaves soon after. Dutch decides that Colm can wait. As they creep down, he tells Arthur that the gang needs "supplies, equipment, and a way out of here."
L63: In cover, Dutch lets Arthur decide who opens fire. The gang kills the O'Driscolls in the camp, then a second wave that comes out of the trees; Arthur chooses to advance or hold ground. Looking at the bodies, Dutch says Colm "doesn't give a damn about his men" and only cares about numbers. Bill searches a wagon, Micah a building and Arthur another, where he finds a box of dynamite. Micah hands Dutch a roll of paper "found on one of them": the plans for a train belonging to a "Mr. Leviticus Cornwall."
L64: On the ride back, Dutch tells the men he is proud that not a man is down: "Outlaws for life, fellers." He promises that John, Mac, Charles and Sean will ride with them again. When Arthur warns that Colm will come after them, Dutch says the gang allowed itself to get a step behind in Blackwater and that it will not happen again.
