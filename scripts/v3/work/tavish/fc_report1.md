## Problems

1. SPECULATION (intro). "Working for the other side, Arthur and Sean MacGuire burn his tobacco fields, and the Grays answer with an ambush in Rhodes". "Answer" makes the ambush a reprisal for the fire. Source: gsw.txt, Short Walk, Bill: "They think it was the Braithwaites"; the sheriff only says "we don't know what you been doing"; tavish_A docstring itself excludes "the Grays plot revenge". Fix: "...burn his tobacco fields, and the Grays later lure the gang into an ambush in Rhodes, at which Tavish is not present."

2. IMPRECISE (Key facts). "next to a historian's letter saying that the Gray ancestor was an informer for the Duke of Cumberland, not an exiled Jacobite." The letter never uses "Jacobite" about the ancestor or Mr. Gray's belief; it speaks of his "ostracizing and emigration" after 1745 and a "Scot in exile" (_m/Gray_Family_History_Letter.txt). "Not an exiled Jacobite" is the wiki's reading (Gray's Secret, Caliga Hall pages). Fix: "...saying that the Gray ancestor, Ross Gray, was a paid informer for the Duke of Cumberland." (Same wording to fix in the Ross Gray relationship entry, half2.)

3. IMPRECISE (facts grid, Family). "father Murdo Gray according to the Red Dead Wiki". The text (Origins, Murdo entry, Quotes) derives it from Beau's own line in "The Course of True Love I" (gsw.txt l. 6253, "grandson of old Murdo Gray"). Fix: "father Murdo Gray (Beau calls himself Murdo's grandson; the Red Dead Wiki lists Murdo as Tavish's father)", or simply drop the attribution and keep it consistent with Origins.

4. IMPRECISE (Origins, para 4). "The Grays have been feuding with the Braithwaites ... for longer than anyone in Rhodes can explain." Conflates two lines: Archibald says "for as long as I can remember" (gsw.txt l. 5837); "no one can even quite explain why" is Beau's (l. 6253) and is about why, not how long. Fix: "Archibald MacGregor says the two families have been fighting for as long as he can remember."

5. IMPRECISE (Chapter 3, para 1). "After the move to Clemens Point, near Rhodes, Dutch van der Linde decides to play the Grays and the Braithwaites against each other and rob them both." In "The New South" Dutch only sends Arthur to sniff around the Grays and Hosea to the Braithwaites (l. 5874-5877); the plan to "convince each family that we're on their side... and then we rob 'em both" is stated in "Advertising, The New American Art" (l. 6544). Fix: "After the move to Clemens Point, Dutch sends Arthur to find out about the Grays and Hosea about the Braithwaites; in "Advertising, The New American Art" he says the plan is to convince each family the gang is on its side, then rob both."

6. IMPRECISE (Origins, para 2). "Dutch explains that Sheriff Gray got the gang into Caliga Hall by speaking to his father". Arthur had already entered Caliga Hall in "The Course of True Love I"; Dutch's line answers "how the hell did we get an in at the Gray's place?" about John and Javier's meeting (l. 6543-6544). Fix: "...that Sheriff Gray got the gang its meeting at Caliga Hall by putting in a word with his father".

7. IMPRECISE (Horse Flesh, para 1). "He says the Grays cannot be seen to get too close to the Braithwaites". Transcript: "Problem is we can't be seen to get too close." No object; the next lines ("keep us away from this... publicly") suggest he means the theft, not the Braithwaites (l. 7448, 7462). Fix: "He says the Grays cannot be seen to get too close, and that they have gold to pay."

8. IMPRECISE (Horse Flesh, para 1, omission). The paragraph opens with John's railway story but skips what prompts it: Tavish first says the newcomers turn up in town, help everybody, and trouble follows them everywhere, and asks who they are (l. 7436). This is his only on-screen suspicion of the gang and matters for the variant paragraph. Fix: add "Tavish asks who they are, saying that trouble follows them wherever they go;" before John's answer.

9. IMPRECISE (Variant paragraph). "if the player dismounts and speaks to Tavish before leaving". The mission page says "at the beginning of the mission" (_m/Horse_Flesh_for_Dinner.txt, trivia). Fix: "if the player dismounts and speaks to Tavish at the start of the mission".

10. IMPRECISE (Short Walk). "Sheriff Leigh Gray tells the gang that the family knows what it has been doing". "It" reads as the family. Transcript: "You're so dumb to think we don't know what you been doing." (l. 7909). Fix: "Sheriff Leigh Gray tells the gang that the Grays know what it has been up to".

11. IMPRECISE (Death). "next to a small marble table holding a coffee pot and a cup." The screenshots show a metal pot; nothing marks it as coffee rather than tea (fc/img_*Grays_Secret*). Fix: "a metal pot and a cup".

12. IMPRECISE (Personality). "accuses them of ruining the county and killing his uncle (see Quotes)." The Quotes block does not contain the "hag" line (it states that Tavish's words about himself and his family are under Personality, and part B paraphrases the slurs). Broken pointer. Fix: drop "(see Quotes)" (same broken pointer in the Catherine Braithwaite relationship entry, half2).

13. IMPRECISE (In his own words). ""We've got gold, Yankee... we've got gold." (to John, who asks what the family wants)". John only says "And?" after "Problem is we can't be seen to get too close." (l. 7449). Fix: "(to John)".

14. SPECULATION (Names). "Sean says that "Old Man Gray" has doubled security; the game does not name him further." Nothing identifies "Old Man Gray" as Tavish, and Sean reports hearsay ("They was saying Old Man Gray's doubled security", l. 7596). tavish_A's docstring explicitly excludes the identification. The closing clause is also unclear (STYLE). Fix: remove the sentence from Names, or write: "On the way to Caliga Hall in "The Fine Joys of Tobacco", Sean repeats that "Old Man Gray" has doubled security; the game does not say which Gray he means."

15. COPIED (Death). "His body can be found on a porch of the main house at Caliga Hall" vs en.txt: "His body can be found on the back porch of the main building at Caliga Hall". Fix: "Arthur can find his body on a porch of the main house, a point of interest called "Gray's Secret"."

16. COPIED (1907). Guards will still patrol the grounds at night." Fix: "The wiki adds that the estate is still in the family's hands and still worked by day and guarded by night." (and see DUPLICATE 20).

Utilising slave labor, the Grays made a fortune from tobacco and cotton production. Following the Civil War, however, the fortune slowly declined due to the abolition of slavery."). Fix: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, the family's wealth came from tobacco and cotton worked by slaves before the Civil War, and shrank once slavery was abolished."
