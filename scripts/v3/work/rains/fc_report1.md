## Problems

1. IMPRECISE (intro). Draft: "Charles Smith helps his people and stays with them after the gang breaks apart." Source: transcript l. 13296-13300 and My_Last_Boy.txt: Charles leaves the gang at the end of "My Last Boy" to stay with the Wapiti, while the gang is still together at Beaver Hollow. Fix: "Charles Smith helps his people and leaves the gang to stay with them after the battle."

2. IMPRECISE (intro). Draft: "he lobbies officials in Saint Denis". Source: transcript l. 9314 (Dutch: Miller is "lobbying officials in Saint Denis on their behalf"); American_Fathers_I.txt l. 18. The lobbying is Evelyn Miller's; Rains Fall goes with him to the appointments. Fix: "he seeks help from officials in Saint Denis with the writer Evelyn Miller".

3. IMPRECISE (key facts). Draft: "Eagle Flies dies in his tent after the battle." "his" reads as Eagle Flies's tent. Source: My_Last_Boy.txt ("Rains Fall's tent"), HOUSE_FACTS l. 63. Fix: "Eagle Flies dies in his father's tent on the reservation after the battle."

4. IMPRECISE (facts grid, Family). Draft: "an elder son and his wife, killed by soldiers by his account". Reads as the elder son's wife; and his own account names a drunken soldier only for the son, not for the wife (transcript l. 13111: "My wife had her throat slit"; the soldier for the wife is wiki-only, en.txt Background). Fix: "his wife and an elder son (killed; a drunken soldier killed the son, by his account)".

5. STYLE / IMPRECISE (Origins). Draft: "The games give no birth date and no age for Rains Fall." He appears in one game only (en.txt infobox, rains_full_en.txt l. 147). Fix: "The game gives no birth date and no age for Rains Fall."

6. DUPLICATE (Origins vs Archeology paragraph vs intro vs C Quotes). Draft Origins: "Rains Fall tells Arthur Morgan in 1899 that he signed three treaties himself and that each of them was broken." The same line is paraphrased again in the Archeology paragraph ("his three treaties have been broken"), stated in the intro, and quoted verbatim in C Quotes (rains_full_en.txt l. 123, owned by C per FIX_RULES 4-5). Fix: cut the Origins sentence and attach the wiki's reading to the next sentence ("The Red Dead Wiki reads his three treaties as two broken and a third that placed the tribe on the reservation"); keep the Archeology paraphrase (event) and the C quote.

7. IMPRECISE (Archeology). Draft: "and now the young men will want a war they cannot win." Transcript l. 12406: "Now my people are gonna want a war. A war they can't win." Fix: "and now his people will want a war they cannot win."

8. DUPLICATE (quotes inside the Biography). Draft: "whom he calls \"the impetuous Prince\"" (Archeology) and "introduces him to Rains Fall, \"a great chief\"" (American Fathers I) and "Rains Fall ends the talks with \"Good day, Colonel Favours\"". OWNERSHIP: A always paraphrases; "the impetuous Prince" is quoted in the Eagle Flies relationship (l. 62), "a great chief" in Names (l. 57), "Good day, Colonel Favours." in the Favours relationship (l. 72). Fix: A paraphrases ("whom he mockingly calls an impetuous prince"; "introduces him as a great chief" without marks, or "introduces him to Rains Fall and his son"; "Rains Fall ends the talks by bidding Favours good day and stands to leave").

9. IMPRECISE (Favored Sons paragraph, order). Draft: "Charles later tells Arthur that, according to Rains Fall, the talks with Favours were followed by reprisals: women taken and elders beaten." placed before "In \"The King's Son\"...". Source: transcript l. 13020-13021: Charles says it on the ride to Fort Wallace in "The King's Son". Fix: move into the King's Son paragraph ("On the way to Fort Wallace, Charles tells Arthur that, according to Rains Fall, ..."). Also DUPLICATE with the Charles relationship (l. 66 "Rains Fall later tells Charles of the Army's reprisals after the meeting: women taken, elders beaten"): keep the Biography, cut it from the relationship.

10. UNSUPPORTED (epilogue). Draft: "Rains Fall himself confirms the destination in the epilogue: he tells John Marston that his people are no longer really a tribe, only a group of families, and that they are now in Canada." No transcript exists for the encounter; the line comes only from the Red Dead Wiki quote list (en.txt Quotes). FIX_RULES 3 requires attribution. Fix: "According to the Red Dead Wiki's quote list, he tells John Marston in the epilogue that his people are no longer really a tribe, only a group of families, and that they are now in Canada."

11. DUPLICATE (epilogue, two adjacent paragraphs). Draft: "...and that they are now in Canada." then "According to the wiki, he tells John that the Wapiti were forced to flee to Canada and that he has come back for a while to mourn Eagle Flies". The flight to Canada is told twice in a row (plus the opening sentence "The Wapiti leave the reservation after the battle" and the article). Fix: in the second paragraph keep only "According to the wiki, he has come back for a while to mourn Eagle Flies, whose death he has not got over, and he also speaks of Arthur."

12. COPIED (epilogue). Draft: "The chief recognizes John as an associate of Arthur. ... John offers his condolences, and Rains Fall boards a train back to Canada." Wiki (en.txt Epilogue): "who recognises Marston as an associate of Arthur ... After John gives his condolences on the loss of his son, Rains Fall then boards a train to return to Canada." Fix: "He knows John as one of Arthur's companions. After John's condolences, he takes the train back to Canada."
