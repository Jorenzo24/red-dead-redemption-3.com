# House facts: shared-event decisions already settled (do not contradict without a transcript)

Flat list, collected from CLAUDE.md "Décisions structurantes" (v3 entries, gang medium tier, Colm, Ross, Milton,
Cornwall) and from the FIX_RULES files of Bill, Abigail, Jack, Javier and Uncle (Oct 2026). Each line was decided
after an adversarial fact-check. When a new fiche settles another shared point, ADD it here in the same PR.
Hierarchy everywhere: transcripts > mission pages > character pages of the wiki.

## Method decisions
- The Red Dead Wiki (Fandom) is the structural base: cover everything it says that is not speculative, then add (Wikipedia, interviews, reception). Length >= the wiki page.
- Wiki-only game content is allowed if not contradicted, attributed ("according to the Red Dead Wiki"); quotation marks only for words verified verbatim in a transcript or quote list.
- The wiki's character pages are often wrong where mission pages are right (Arthur, Sadie pages): mission pages win.
- No "Appearance" section: outfits become gallery captions. "Personality" is short (~450 words), never a second biography.
- ONE "Relationships" section (.rel component, portrait + text). Relationship portraits = the linked character's live portrait.jpeg resized to 600 px.
- Never a forward link: a fiche links only characters already live at its publishDate.
- A sentence about a choice of the player says so ("player's choice", "if captured", "depending on the player").
- Real names, birth years, ages: only from a source; otherwise "Not given in the games" (+ attributed wiki estimate if useful).

## RDR2 1899, by chapter
- Chapter numbers checked: "Eastward Bound" = chapter 1; "The Battle of Shady Belle" = chapter 3; "That's Murfree Country" = chapter 5.
- "Wolves, Dogs and Sons": Jack asks "is he really your uncle?" first, then "why do we call him Uncle?". The "whip him" line is John's, not Uncle's.
- Saint Denis ship: the Antenor; NO destination ever (not Cuba, not the Caribbean). Removed from Arthur, Charles, Micah, Abigail, Javier.
- Ch. 1, Colter: Davey arrives dead at Colter. John's scars come from the wolves in chapter 1.
- Ch. 1: Kieran is captured in "Old Friends" (chapter 1), not chapter 3.
- Ch. 1: the Cornwall train robbery ("Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?") is chapter 1; the plans come from a dead O'Driscoll at Ewing Basin ("Old Friends"); Cornwall is not on the train (portraits only).
- Ch. 1, "Eastward Bound": Bill and Charles drive the wagon; Javier rides alongside on his horse.
- Cornwall train: the wiki mission page says Javier is hurt missing the jump; the transcript is silent: "according to the mission page" or "left behind" (consistent with bill_A: "Javier misses").
- Cornwall's hiring of the Pinkertons is never shown: given through the lines that link the two (Milton, Hosea, Arthur).
- Ch. 2: Milton's first appearance is "A Fisher of Men", CHAPTER 2 (Dakota River near Horseshoe Overlook), not chapter 3. Ross beside Milton: river, Clemens Point, bank (wiki), Annesburg; NOT at Lakay or Van Horn.
- Ch. 2: "Polite Society" = Arthur, Uncle, Karen, Mary-Beth, Tilly (not Hosea).
- Coach robbery with Tilly (Bill): Tilly is offended, Bill opens fire, the shooting makes Tilly ride off.
- Canada robbery: Uncle brings it up in "High and Low Finance"; Arthur says Uncle was a poor lookout. Arthur alone mentions the Tucson job.
- Colm: the parley, Arthur's capture and escape all happen in "Blessed are the Peacemakers" (Lone Mule Stead); the hanging = "Goodbye, Dear Friend" (chapter 6).
- Death of Colm's brother: two attributed versions (Dutch, transcript: he kills the brother, then Colm kills Annabelle; wiki page "Old Friends": reverse order), told ONCE (Dutch relationship). No source links Milton to Colm ("the law").
- The Sean/Kieran pronunciation scene is at Clemens Point.
- Ch. 3: Sean is rescued in "The First Shall Be Last" (not "Who is Not Without Sin"). Sean is killed in an ambush by the Grays (not a bounty hunter); the shooter is not named.
- Ch. 3: Trelawny is freed in "The New South" once Arthur brings Anders Anderson back (no on-screen promise by the sheriff). "He ain't Trelawny" is said on the way TO the bear hunt ("Exit, Pursued by a Bruised Ego"); the Sean line is optional, like the fishing lines. Trelawny says he is from Cornwall (to Uncle). After chapter 6 he is only heard at his Saint Denis apartment.
- Ch. 3: Shady Belle was cleared of Lemoyne Raiders (not O'Driscolls).
- Ch. 3, Shady Belle / Clemens Point: Milton and Ross walk into camp; Milton offers three days to disappear, conditional on Dutch surrendering; the gang refuses. NEVER "amnesty" or "pardon".
- "An Honest Mistake": the guards Lowe and Cooperforth die in the barn (Bill knocks the bucket/lantern over); Charles does not kill Lowe at the wagon. Uncle's tip is for a wagon he thinks unguarded; it turns out to be Cornwall's. Arthur saving Uncle is optional (Gold Medal objective).
- Karen's drinking starts in the Clemens Point chapter (Camp Events), NOT after Sean's death. Only in-game age clue: "Mid twenties, blonde" (Arthur, "Polite Society").
- Karen slaps Molly at Clemens Point (Camp Events; Molly's wiki page says Shady Belle).
- Molly's mirror: the reward is a cigar, chapter 3 only. The line "none of them will live seven years" does not exist.
- Hosea: Abigail hid the explosive alone (Camp Events + wiki).
- Strauss is shot in the leg (Camp Events, chapter 3).
- Ch. 4: John is arrested in Saint Denis; it is Micah who is shipwrecked with Arthur, Dutch, Bill and Javier.
- Ch. 4: Abigail tells Arthur that Jack has disappeared after "A Short Walk in a Pretty Town" (Rhodes ambush), not after "Advertising". Abigail is a co-giver of "Advertising, The New American Art".
- Ch. 4, "My Last Boy": Bill is with Dutch's group, not at the warehouse; the battle is at the Heartland Oil Fields (Cornwall = the company).
- Cornwall Kerosene & Tar is in the Heartlands, not Annesburg. Cornwall is killed by Dutch in "Just a Social Call" (chapter 6, Annesburg docks), not "Our Best Selves". No scene of Cornwall has a transcript: his lines come from the wiki quote list.
- Fleeting Joy: Bill also helps Arthur and Sadie.
- Mount Hagen: a large sum of money and gold, most of it from the ferry robbery. At Mount Hagen, Charles stays in cover, then shoots Sadie's attacker.
- Ch. 5, Guarma: Fussar IS killed by Arthur at the cannon.
- Ch. 5: Molly is killed at the END of chapter 5, at Beaver Hollow (not Shady Belle). In the low-honor silver ending, "Not Molly... not Miss Grimshaw... not even Colm" comes AFTER Dutch arrives and stops Micah.
- Molly CLAIMS to come from a wealthy Dublin family (the guide says so: attribute).
- Ch. 6: Susan is killed by Micah in chapter 6 and buried by Charles at Roanoke Ridge.
- Ch. 6: Eagle Flies dies on the reservation. It is Taima, Eagle Flies seated behind Charles, used for the escape; Karen and Javier bring Eagle Flies to Beaver Hollow.
- The machete death of an O'Driscoll is in "Horsemen, Apocalypses". "Horsemen, Apocalypses" comes before "Urban Pleasures".
- Ch. 6: Abigail is grabbed OUTSIDE camp by Milton's men (transcript), not taken from camp, not by Milton in person. Tilly hides Jack at that moment (not during the Beaver Hollow raid).
- Milton is killed at Van Horn, not Beaver Hollow. Mac Callander: "a mercy killing" according to Milton. Milton's last line: "You're losing your strength, Mr. Morgan."
- Strauss leaves the gang at Beaver Hollow, thrown out by Arthur OR leaving on his own depending on the player. He dies in Pinkerton custody between 1899 and 1907; it is CHARLES (1907 epilogue), not Dutch, who reports it.
- Beaver Hollow standoff -> Arthur entry + table (Javier fiche); "Our Best Selves" -> Dutch entry + table.
- Uncle, Pearson, Mary-Beth (and Karen per sources) leaving: unsupported as a shown departure; say Dutch announces they have gone.
- Arthur drives Susan to the Radleys.
- Chapter 6 = four endings (choice x honor).
- Molly's death: no "on the night she dies" detail.

## Epilogue 1907
- Micah's death is a shared shot: Dutch fires, John finishes him. Never "Dutch kills Micah". Ending credits 1907: Ross and Fordham examine Micah's body.
- Tilly's 1907 letter is signed "Yours, Tilly"; she presents herself as Tilly Pierre.
- Mary-Beth's 1907 meeting is at Valentine station (not tied to a precise epilogue part). Trelawny talks about his book to Karen, not Mary-Beth.
- Swanson's Emerald station scene has NO transcript: only two attested lines and the train departure.
- Sadie sends ONE telegram, addressed "JIM MILTON", "IF YOURE THE JM I KNOW"; she does not mock the alias (the mission page says they talk about the past). Not a letter.
- Wedding: the mission page version ("on the hill", "American Venom") or no place.
- Uncle's saloon encounter in 1907 raises the player's honor (not Arthur's).
- "Uncle Dutch will visit" 1907 scene is CUT CONTENT (D only).
- Squatter at Beecher's Hope: a man ("he").
- Jack: on the road from 1899 to 1907; Beecher's Hope 1907 to 1914. His "age twelve" line to the ranch hand has no transcript: attributed to the wiki, same wording everywhere.
- Pearson served "briefly" in the navy (never "navy cook"). Actor: Jim Santangeli.
- Epilogue endings: Mary-Beth novelist ("Leslie Dupont"), Pearson's Rhodes store, Swanson pastor in New York.

## Red Dead Redemption (1911-1914)
- Dutch lets himself fall from the cliff (not shot by John).
- Javier's 1911 fate = player's choice everywhere: killed by John OR captured and handed to the Bureau. Capture-branch lines labelled "if captured". Never "his last words"; never "hanged". John's "they're all dead" in "Old Friends, New Problems" plays in both branches (wagon ride to the MacFarlane ranch).
- Reyes is not freed at El Presidio during Javier's capture.
- Allende and Bill are killed at Escalera ("An Appointed Time"), where Luisa dies too (not El Presidio). Bill dies "near Escalera" (on the road, not in the town). At Fort Mercer, Jonah reports Bill's flight to Mexico.
- Bill: no canon birth date (1866 dropped; wiki estimate 1869 to 1872, attributed). Bill is NOT in Red Dead Online (only "The Dedham" outfit is inspired by him). Charles is never named among "traitors".
- Dutch is NOT in Red Dead Online.
- Bonnie meets Abigail at her home in "Old Friends, New Problems" (corn delivery).
- Jack first appears on screen in RDR1 in "The Outlaw's Return" (Act III, Beecher's Hope). Bill mentions him in "An Appointed Time". Never "does not appear in the first three acts".
- Uncle's death ("The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed"): Uncle fights off the first wave with John before being hit; Abigail and Jack escape on ONE horse.
- Ross's weapon: rifle in "A Fisher of Men" (1899), shotgun in 1914; said once (Trivia).
- At Beecher's Hope (1911), Ross is at the barn firing squad, not at the head of the column. In "Remember My Family", Jack fires first.
- "This is your fault, Marston!" is Hopkins's line, not Ross's. Ross's line: "Oh trust me, it looks better in the report that way" ("that way", not "on").
- Edgar Ross: first name and Bureau head from the 1907 newspaper; Emily from the 1914 newspaper (Blackwater Ledger No. 213); Phillip from the transcript ("Philip").
- 1914 (Act III guide): a man at Blackwater station, Emily at the Lake Don Julio cabin, Phillip at his camp in Mexico.
- Abigail's grave: "Abigail Marston, 1877-1914, Always in our hearts"; no cause of death.
- A daughter of John and Abigail, dead before 1911, is attested only by John ("Women and Cattle": "I had a daughter, but she died."; "Father Abraham": "a daughter in heaven"). Word it "John mentions a daughter who died"; never her birth.
- Abigail and John: "around 1894" / "about five years before 1899"; Jack born "around 1895".
- Archer Fordham: presence at Beecher's Hope attributed to the wiki; gallery-1 caption = 1907.
- Fordham also appears in the RDR2 epilogue (watching Micah's body with Ross).

## Names, actors, real-world facts
- Jack's credited name (RDR1 manual): John "Jack" Marston Jr. Actors: Josh Blaylock (RDR1), Marissa Buccianti (1899), Ted Sutherland (1907); Rob Wiethoff did the motion capture for Jack's duel with Ross (mission page, attributed).
- Uncle has no real name. Three actors: Spider Madison (RDR1), John O'Creagh (died 30 Oct 2016; his songs remain), James McBride (RDR2). "Not exactly the most productive gang member" (official guide, verbatim). O'Creagh's Run is named after him (wiki + Wikipedia).
- Lenny's actor = Harron ATKINS (not "Alexander"). Kieran = Pico Alexander (voice only sourced, no motion capture claimed). Swanson = Sean Haberle. Bill = Steve J. Palmer. Pearson = Jim Santangeli. Colm motion capture = Andrew Berg (attributed to the wiki).
- Game Rant author of the Houser report = Sarah Fields (not Viraaj Bhatnagar). IGN interview: "reported on 13 October 2026".
- Rockstar's 6 Sep 2018 descriptions (THR, sources/thr_descriptions.txt) cover the Van der Linde gang only; none for Kieran, none for Cornwall.
- Arthur calls Lenny "Leonard" once ("Preaching Forgiveness as He Went"). "Son of former slaves" = wiki, attributed. Lenny did not join the gang as a teenager.
- Sean's father Darragh: name from the in-game newspaper clipping; the family link = wiki. Sean is in Red Dead Online (Blood Money missions, 13 Jul 2021).
- Susan: per the official guide, in the gang as long as Arthur.
- Javier: the Shady Belle wanted poster reads "SE BUSCA POR ASESINATO Y TRAICIÓN / Punta Orgullo, Nuevo Paraíso / VIVO O MUERTO / RECOMPENSA $1.000"; the Collector's guide says he killed "a high-ranking army officer over a woman" and cannot return until there is a change in power. No "former", no place tied to the killing.
- Micah: 1877 clipping (Micah Bell, 38, and his son, 17, suspected of the Briggs murders, Crawford County, Ohio); Amos's letter; 1907 papers (Blackwater Ledger No. 75). The place of Micah's advances to Abigail is disputed between wikis: no "Clemens Point".
- Charles: "Lone Wolf" ring name, "Sulky" nickname, Nokota from Falmouth = wiki-only, attributed.
- Hosea: Curzon Dobell has no Wikipedia article; Development sourced from Bristol Old Vic (May 2025).
- Tram station safe (Dutch): 15 dollars and a quarter each; the wiki Arthur page's "90 dollars" is dropped.
- Mission title: "Who is Not Without Sin". Mission names stay in English, as Rockstar titles them.
- Currency written consistently ("100 dollars").
