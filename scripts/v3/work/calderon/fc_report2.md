## Problems

1. WRONG. Las Hermanas (1911): "If John gives, nuns later approach him in random encounters to ask for donations; refusing costs honor." Source: I_Know_You.txt Part 2 says "Choosing not to donate money will result in a loss of honor", i.e. choosing to rob/not donate in the mission itself, not refusing the later random-encounter nuns. Fix: drop "refusing costs honor" (robbing costing honor is already in the table).

2. IMPRECISE. Father O'Leary: "she tells him that Dorkins was right about Arthur." Source gsw.txt l. 8751/8756: "Brother Dorkins was right about you" is said to Arthur; to the priest she says "See, Father… Brother Dorkins found him… I told you… Brother Dorkins was fantastic." Fix: "as Arthur leaves, she tells him that Brother Dorkins found Arthur and was fantastic." (Same error in half1 Biography, ch. 4: "tells the priest that Dorkins had been right about him".)

3. IMPRECISE. Strange Man: "an old nun is taking the money she raised from the monastery to the bank". Transcript (I_Know_You_dialogues.txt): "an old nun is traveling from the monastery, taking the money she raised to the bank. ... Road's full of thieves." Fix: "an old nun is travelling from the monastery to take the money she has raised to the bank". (half1 Biography says "from the convent": align.)

4. IMPRECISE. Quotes, context of "My husband died a long time ago...": "after Arthur speaks of his son, Mary and his parents". Arthur never names Mary ("I had a girl who loved me… I threw that away", gsw.txt l. 12651); the Mary Linton entry itself says "without naming her". Fix: "after Arthur speaks of his son, a girl who loved him and his parents".

5. IMPRECISE. Rains Fall: "They are the only two characters to whom Arthur speaks of his late son, Isaac". The wiki (en.txt Trivia) says "one of the few people ... besides Rains Fall"; arthur_F says the two he talks to "at length" and that he alludes to Isaac in "A Fisher of Men"; the fiche's own Trivia says "among the few". Fix: "They are the only two characters to whom Arthur tells of his son Isaac" (arthur_D wording: "tells the story only to") or "among the few"; keep one wording.

6. DUPLICATE. Trivia: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, she and Rains Fall are among the few characters with whom Arthur discusses his son." Same fact in the Rains Fall entry; FIX_RULES 6 keeps it in B Rains Fall. Fix: cut from Trivia.

7. DUPLICATE (+ COPIED). Trivia: "lawmen chasing a wanted Arthur may shoot her and Father O'Leary if he leads them nearby; two shots usually kill them, and their bodies can then be looted. Her loot is always Burdock Root." Already in Charity work and gameplay (Saint Denis). Wording is close to the wiki ("lawmen pursuing a wanted Arthur Morgan may attack ... if he leads them to the two NPCs ... usually die after two gunshots, after which their bodies become lootable"). FIX_RULES 6: cut from Trivia; in the gameplay paragraph, reword away from the wiki (e.g. "If Arthur is wanted and leads lawmen to her, they can kill her and the priest, according to the Red Dead Wiki; she dies from about two shots and can be searched, always carrying Burdock Root.").

8. DUPLICATE. Trivia: "When 'The Fine Art of Conversation' is replayed on PC, Swanson appears at Emerald Station by default..." Same in Charity work (Saint Denis). Fix: cut from Trivia (FIX_RULES 6).

9. DUPLICATE. Trivia: "If John robs her in 'I Know You', she carries well over 300 dollars, according to the mission page." Same in Las Hermanas (1911), attributed there to the Red Dead Wiki. Fix: cut from Trivia; in the gameplay section attribute to "the mission page" (that is where it is: I_Know_You.txt trivia).

10. DUPLICATE. Trivia: "according to the Red Dead Wiki a drunk man at Doyle's Tavern in Saint Denis calls her a 'Spanish nun'." FIX_RULES 2: "Spanish nun" only in B Names (already there in half1). Fix: end the Trivia item after the two Spanish phrases.

Fix: cut the subsection or reduce it to one sentence pointing to Names ("How the games link the two nuns is set out under Names.").

12. DUPLICATE. Other media: "She is not among the Red Dead Redemption 2 character descriptions Rockstar published on September 6, 2018." Already the first sentence of Personality (half1). Fix: keep in Personality (owner of official descriptions per OWNERSHIP), cut here.

13. DUPLICATE. Abraham Reyes: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, she calls Reyes a vain, ambitious and repugnant parasite, and a philanderer." Already in half1 Biography (UN), with "womanizer". Fix: keep once (Biography, using the wiki's word "philanderer"), cut from the Reyes entry or vice versa.

Mr. Morgan!"), not waiting for him. Also "Their last meeting is at Emerald Station, if his honor is high" omits the second condition ("Of Men and Angels" completed). 19. SPECULATION. Ayauhtéotl: "the Mother Superior is one of the few characters, with John, who can see her." Source: wiki Ayauhtéotl trivia, hedged ("may be able to become invisible", "seem to be the only people other than John who can see her"), a deduction from her having spoken to the nun. Also "The woman behind the plague" is interpretive (the wiki: "It appears that..."). Fix: cut the "can see her" clause; replace "The woman behind the plague" with "The woman who reveals herself as Ayauhtéotl at the end of Undead Nightmare".

20. IMPRECISE. Vincente de Santa: "where the undead De Santa rises from his grave as the last enemy". Mother_Superior_Blues.txt: "when the cemetery is almost empty, Vincente de Santa will rise as the 'boss' of the graveyard". Fix: "rises from his grave when the cemetery is nearly cleared".

21. IMPRECISE / attribution (FIX_RULES 3). Fix: "The character the Red Dead Wiki identifies with her first appears..." / "If the Red Dead Wiki's identification is followed, John meets her twelve years later...".

The player can then see undead prowling around in some shots of the cutscene."
