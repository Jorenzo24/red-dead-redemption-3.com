Arthur Morgan

Died 1899 Deceased Van der Linde gang Voiced by Roger Clark

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Arthur Morgan is the protagonist of Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). Rockstar presents him as "Dutch's most trusted enforcer", a member of the Van der Linde gang since his teens.

He is played by Roger Clark, whose performance won Best Performance at The Game Awards 2018.

Biography

Origins

According to the official guide, Arthur lost his parents young and joined Dutch van der Linde's gang at 14, becoming Dutch's first protégé alongside Hosea Matthews. With a waitress named Eliza, he had a son, Isaac; he sent them money until both were killed in a robbery. He was once engaged to Mary Gillis, now Mary Linton.

1899: the gang on the run

After a failed ferry robbery in Blackwater, the gang flees into the snowbound mountains of chapter 1, where Arthur and Javier rescue John Marston from wolves. In chapter 2, while collecting a debt for Leopold Strauss, Arthur beats a sick farmer, Thomas Downes, who coughs blood on him: this is how Arthur contracts tuberculosis.

In chapter 3, the gang's double game between the Grays and the Braithwaites ends with Jack Marston kidnapped and handed to Angelo Bronte. In chapter 4, Arthur brings Jack back; Dutch drowns Bronte, an act Arthur finds out of character. The Saint Denis bank robbery turns out to be a Pinkerton trap: Hosea and Lenny are killed and John is arrested. The survivors are shipwrecked on Guarma.

Illness and the break with Dutch

Back on the mainland, Arthur collapses in Saint Denis, and Dr. Joseph R. Barnes diagnoses tuberculosis. In chapter 6, he and Sadie Adler free John from Sisika Penitentiary against Dutch's wishes, in "Visiting Hours". Dutch then leaves Arthur behind at the oil fields in "My Last Boy", leaves John for dead after the army payroll robbery in "Our Best Selves", and refuses to rescue Abigail. Arthur breaks with him and learns that Micah Bell is the Pinkertons' informant.

Death: the four endings

In the final mission, "Red Dead Redemption", Arthur chooses on the mountain either to help John escape or to go back for the gang's money. That choice, combined with Arthur's honor, produces four endings:

  • Helping John, high honor: Arthur dies of his illness watching the sunrise.
  • Helping John, low honor: Micah shoots him in the head.
  • Going back for the money, high honor: after a knife fight with Micah, Arthur dies at sunrise.
  • Going back for the money, low honor: Micah stabs him to death.

In every version, Dutch walks away. In the 1907 epilogue, John, Sadie and Charles track Micah down on Mount Hagen, where Dutch and John kill him.

As a playable character
  • The player controls Arthur for the six chapters of the main story; the epilogue switches to John Marston.
  • An honor system tracks his actions. It changes some dialogue and scenes, and decides how his last scene plays out.
  • Arthur keeps a journal of sketches and notes, updated as the story advances.
  • His weight changes with how much the player makes him eat, and his tuberculosis visibly marks his face and body in the later chapters.
Development and performance

After the three protagonists of Grand Theft Auto V, Rockstar returned to a single lead. "Sticking with a single character felt more appropriate for the structure and narrative of a Western", Rockstar's Josh Bass told The Hollywood Reporter in September 2018.

Writer Dan Houser told GQ that, instead of the usual arc of a hero who grows stronger, Arthur is strong from the outset and "is going to be taken on a more intellectual roller coaster when his world view gets taken apart". He told Vulture that a second love interest for Arthur was written and then cut, as part of about five hours removed from the game. In a 2025 podcast interview, Houser said an early opening in which Arthur's baby died was also cut, because "it was too tough in some ways".

Arthur is played by Roger Clark, an Irish-American actor born in 1978 who grew up near Sligo, in Ireland. His first day on the project was in August 2013, and he described the role as "five years of work", mostly in performance capture rather than in a voice booth. He told The Hollywood Reporter that he drew on Toshiro Mifune and John Wayne for the character.

Reception

Roger Clark won Best Performance at The Game Awards 2018. He was nominated for Performer at the 2019 BAFTA Games Awards, and Arthur was nominated for Outstanding Achievement in Character at the 2019 D.I.C.E. Awards; both awards went to God of War.

Reviewing the game, IGN noted "an infectious authenticity" in Clark's voice, and Kotaku wrote that Clark "brings Arthur to life with uncommon confidence and consistency". In The New York Times, Peter Suderman described Arthur as "a bad man with a good heart, because his choices are, in fact, your own". In an April 2024 BAFTA public poll of more than 4,000 voters, Arthur was voted the 11th most iconic video game character.
