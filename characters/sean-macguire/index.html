Sean MacGuire

Died 1899 Deceased Van der Linde gang Irish

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Sean MacGuire is a young Irish thief in the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar describes him as coming from "a long line of criminals and political dissidents".

He is shot dead in Rhodes in 1899, in an ambush set up by the Gray family.

Biography

Origins

According to the official guide, Sean's father was wanted by the government; he fled to America with his son and was caught and killed there. Sean joined the gang after failing to steal Dutch's pocket watch in an alley.

Capture and return

Separated from the gang after the failed Blackwater robbery, Sean is captured by bounty hunters. In chapter 2, in "The First Shall Be Last", Arthur, Charles and Javier free him, with Josiah Trelawny creating a diversion. The camp throws a party for his return, during which he and Karen Jones begin an affair.

He then joins Arthur, John and Charles on the gang's train robbery, in "Pouring Forth Oil IV".

The Gray feud and his death

In chapter 3, the gang works both sides of the feud between the Grays and the Braithwaites. In "The Fine Joys of Tobacco", Sean and Arthur burn the Grays' tobacco fields for Catherine Braithwaite.

In "A Short Walk in a Pretty Town", the Grays offer the gang a job in Rhodes. It is a trap. As Sean walks down the street with Arthur, Bill and Micah, a sniper shoots him in the head and he dies instantly. After his death, Karen starts drinking heavily.

Development and performance

Sean is played by Michael "Mick" Mellamphy, who provided the voice and the motion capture. According to a cast panel at SacAnime in June 2019, Mellamphy was the second actor cast in the role, replacing another actor during production.

In January 2021, Mellamphy, Roger Clark (Arthur) and Penny O'Brien (Molly O'Shea) took part in an online panel organised by Origin Theatre's First Irish Festival, reported by TheGamer. He recalled asking Benjamin Byron Davis, who plays Dutch, whether Sean was really part of the gang, and being told: "Mick, my man. Sean's part of the gang." On the accent, he said: "There was just something very Dublin about [Sean] - working class, a bit of fun."

He also said that, as a player, he missed the party celebrating Sean's return, because he went hunting with Arthur and came back to a hungover camp the next morning.

Reception

In November 2018, Eurogamer published an article by Cian Maher titled "Why Red Dead Redemption 2's Sean MacGuire is the best Irish character in a video game yet". It ranks Sean above earlier Irish characters from Rockstar, including Irish in Red Dead Redemption and the McReary family in Grand Theft Auto IV, and praises the historical grounding of his background.
