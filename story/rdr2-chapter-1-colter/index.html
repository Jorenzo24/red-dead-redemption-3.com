Chapter 1: Colter

Red Dead Redemption 2 Chapter 1 of 6 Colter, Ambarino 1899

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 24, 2026

Chapter 1: Colter is the first of the six chapters of Red Dead Redemption 2, followed by a two-part epilogue. It is set in May 1899 in the Grizzlies West, in the state of Ambarino, a few days after a failed ferry robbery in Blackwater. The chapter has six story missions, and two of them, "The Aftermath of Genesis" and "Old Friends", can be played in either order. The Red Dead Wiki describes it as the shortest chapter of the game.

Full spoilers for the opening of Red Dead Redemption 2 follow.

In-game chapter overview

The game's pause menu summarizes the chapter with this text:

"After a failed robbery, the gang have fled from Blackwater into the Grizzly Mountains, but a late spring storm has left them half frozen and starving. They hole up in an abandoned mining town to lick their wounds and await a break in the weather."

The game itself opens on white text over a black screen: "By 1899, the age of outlaws and gunslingers was at an end. America was becoming a land of laws... Even the west had mostly been tamed. A few gangs still roamed but they were being hunted down and destroyed."

Background: the Blackwater ferry robbery

The robbery that sends the gang into the mountains is never shown. What the player learns about it comes from the characters during the chapter.

  • In his speech at Colter, Dutch van der Linde names the people the gang has lost or cannot account for: Davey Callander, Jenny Kirk, Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander. He says of Sean and Mac: "they may be okay, we don't know."
  • Javier Escuella, who was there, tells Arthur in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": "We had the money, it seemed fine, then suddenly they were everywhere." The attackers were Pinkerton agents.
  • Javier also says that Dutch "killed a girl in a... bad way, but it was a bad situation." Arthur answers that it is not like him. The Red Dead Wiki names the young woman Heidi McCourt.
  • According to Javier, Davey, Mac and John were all shot, and nobody knows what happened to Sean. He adds that the men on the boat "were only just holding on" by the time Arthur and the others reached the other side of town.
  • Asked by Arthur, Dutch says the money was "stashed with the rest of the money in town" and that the gang cannot go back to collect it any time soon. In an optional answer, he says of the Pinkertons: "That many men? Oh, they knew we were coming."
  • Simon Pearson, the camp cook, says he could not bring supplies when they ran. At the next camp, Susan Grimshaw tells Arthur that most of his belongings from Blackwater were saved, except his money.
  • In "Eastward Bound", Hosea Matthews tells Arthur that the two of them kept telling Dutch the ferry job "didn't feel right", and that they had "a real lead in Blackwater". At the new camp he tells Dutch: "then Micah got you all excited about that ferry and here we are."

Newspapers in the game later put the sum aboard the ferry at 150,000 dollars. The fates of Sean and Mac are revealed in Chapter 2.

The missions of Chapter 1

1. Outlaws from the West

The first mission is given by Dutch. It starts and ends at Colter and takes Arthur to the Adler Ranch.

At night, the gang's wagons push through a snowstorm in the Grizzlies. Reverend Orville Swanson tells Dutch that Abigail says Davey is dying and that they must stop. Hosea remarks that it is May. Arthur Morgan, sent ahead by Dutch, rides back and reports an abandoned mining town nearby. He leads the wagons there. The title card "Chapter I: Colter" follows.

Hosea opens the first cabin, revolver drawn. Abigail, Bill Williamson and Arthur carry in Davey Callander on a stretcher, already dead. Susan Grimshaw sends Mary-Beth to light the fire, Karen to fetch blankets and Pearson to check the food. Abigail says: "Davey's dead." Dutch announces that he sent John and Micah ahead to scout and that he and Arthur will ride out to find one of them. He then addresses the gang and asks Pearson and Susan to turn the place into a camp. He ends with: "Stay strong. Stay with me. We ain't done yet!"

Outside, Arthur asks what really happened on the boat. Dutch answers: "We missed you, that's what happened." Charles Smith brings them two horses; Dutch tells him to get indoors and rest his hand. Arthur rides Charles's mare, Taima. On the trail, Dutch tells Arthur he is the only one he can rely on to stay strong. The player can then ask about the money or about whether Blackwater was a trap.

They meet Micah Bell, who has found a homestead "blazing with light and noise", where it sounds like a party. Dutch tells him that Davey and Jenny did not make it. Micah has not seen John since the storm came in. Alone with Arthur, Micah complains that the gang has too many mouths to feed.

At the homestead, Dutch sends Micah behind a wagon and Arthur behind a shed, and goes to the door alone. He poses as a lost traveler and asks for a few cans of food. Micah finds a body under a sheet in the wagon. One of the men recognizes Dutch and shouts that Colm will be furious. The men are Colm O'Driscoll's gang. Arthur or Micah fires first, and the three outlaws kill the O'Driscolls at the house.

Dutch and Arthur search the cabin for food, medicine and ammunition. Arthur notes that there is a large price on Colm's head; Dutch answers that wanting Colm dead "is about the only thing me and Uncle Sam agree on." Arthur suggests moving the women and Jack to the house, but Dutch does not want the gang to split up.

In the barn, an O'Driscoll who hid during the shootout jumps Arthur from the loft. Arthur beats him while Dutch watches. Under questioning, the man says the others are at an old mining camp southwest of the ranch, near a lake, and that they plan to rob a train and blow the tracks. Dutch leaves his fate to Arthur, who can kill him, knock him out or let him go. According to the mission page, a spared O'Driscoll reappears in "Old Friends", where he is killed. Arthur then calms a Tennessee Walker in the barn, and Dutch tells him to keep it.

A woman screams inside the house. Micah has found her hiding in the cellar and insists she is one of the O'Driscolls. She throws a bottle at him, then holds him off with a knife from the table. Dutch tells Micah: "No, she ain't, Micah. Look at her." Micah flips the table, a lantern falls and the house catches fire. Dutch and Arthur push Micah away, and Dutch calms the woman and leads everyone outside. She says the men came three days earlier and killed her husband. Arthur helps her onto Dutch's horse and tells her: "We're bad men, but... we ain't them." On the ride back she gives her name: Sadie Adler. The house burns with her husband Jake's body still in the wagon.

At Colter, Lenny Summers is on watch and calls the others out. Dutch tells Hosea that O'Driscolls are in the area, scouting a train; Hosea answers: "That's the last thing we need right now, Dutch." Dutch asks Tilly and Karen to look after Sadie and says he has not slept in three days. Susan hands out the rooms. Micah protests that Arthur gets a room to himself while he shares a bunk room with Bill and others, using a racial slur, and Hosea sends him to bed.

The mission rewards Arthur with the Tennessee Walker from the barn, according to the mission page. It continues directly into the next mission.

2. Enter, Pursued by a Memory

The mission is given by Abigail, listed under her 1899 name, Abigail Roberts, on the mission page. It starts and ends at Colter and takes place in the Grizzlies West.

In the main cabin, Abigail tells Arthur that John Marston has not been seen for two days. Arthur is reluctant and mocks John, calling him "little John". Hosea tells him to at least go and look, and asks Javier to ride out with him, calling them "the two best fit men we've got." Javier agrees, saying that John would look for him if things were reversed. He hands Arthur a sawed-off shotgun.

John was last seen heading up the river. Arthur suggests he may have "kept riding north and never looked back", and adds that it would not be the first time; Javier says John would not leave like that. They find an abandoned campsite and follow horse tracks across a stream and up the mountain. On the way, Javier gives his account of Blackwater.

On a flat stretch of the mountain they find John's horse, dead and mauled; Javier says John was riding it when they left Blackwater. Javier fires a shot in the air, and John's shouts answer from higher up. The two men leave their horses and climb on foot. If the player does not use provisions on the climb, Javier shares a bottle of Kentucky bourbon with Arthur, according to the mission page. They find John lying on a ledge, his face torn by wolves. John says: "Never thought I'd say this, but... it's good to see you, Arthur Morgan."

Arthur carries John on his shoulder. Arthur tells John: "See, I told Dutch you weren't the right man for this." Three wolves appear on the slope. Arthur hands John to Javier, draws the wolves onto himself and kills them. On the ride down, more wolves attack and are shot. They ride in the water for a while to lose the scent. Arthur tells John they will need a better story for the scar.

Back at Colter, Bill and Lenny pull John off Javier's horse so roughly that Javier calls them idiots and tells them to mind his leg. Abigail thanks the two men and tells John that this is "a new low, even by your standards." Hosea thanks them as well. Arthur asks how the gang will get out of this; Hosea, who has been talking with Leopold Strauss, says they will keep heading east when the weather breaks. Arthur is surprised at the idea of moving toward civilization. Hosea answers: "the west is where our problems are worse."

The mission closes on Arthur writing in his journal: "We have been running for weeks." The reward is the sawed-off shotgun. After this mission, the player can walk around the camp, according to the mission page.

3. The Aftermath of Genesis

The mission is given by Simon Pearson at Colter, where it starts and ends. It can be played before or after "Old Friends".

In the camp's kitchen, Pearson tells Arthur they have a few cans of food and a rabbit for ten or twelve people. He starts a story about being stranded at sea for fifty days in the Navy, which Arthur refuses to hear. Pearson says he sent Lenny and Bill hunting and they found nothing. Charles cuts the argument short: "Enough of this. We'll go find something."

Arthur points out Charles's injured hand. Charles says he burned it, a "stupid mistake", and expects it to heal in a day or two. Pearson throws Arthur a can of "Assorted Salted Offal" for the road. Since Charles cannot draw a bow, he gives his own to Arthur and explains that a gun would scare every animal for miles.

On the ride, Charles says he still does not really know what happened on the boat; Arthur says Javier told him a bit, "but... it sure weren't good." Charles finds deer tracks near a stream and teaches Arthur to follow them on foot, stay low and aim for the neck or head. Arthur kills two deer, and the two men carry one each to their horses.

On the way back, the dialogue depends on the order of play. If "Old Friends" is done, Charles notes that the gang has picked up two more mouths to feed, and Arthur says the O'Driscoll prisoner will get nothing but the can of salted offal. In both versions Charles says Sadie has "a wild look in her eye", and Arthur says she has lost her husband, her home and everything she had, and may have family somewhere. Arthur explains that the gang has been fighting the O'Driscolls over scores for years and that Colm and Dutch "go way back, and not in a good way."

A grizzly bear blocks the trail. Charles refuses to hunt it, saying they have enough meat, and they go around it. According to the mission page, the bear cannot be killed in this mission. Arthur notes that Charles has been with them for five or six months and asks why he has not moved on. Charles says he rode alone for a long time and is done with it, and that Dutch treats him fair: "And for a feller with a black father and an Indian mother, that ain't normally the case." Asked in return, Arthur says he has been with Dutch for "Twenty years, something like that. Since I was a boy." Dutch taught him and John to read, and Arthur adds: "Dutch saved me, saved most of us."

At Colter, Uncle is loitering at Pearson's table and leaves after trading jabs with Arthur. Pearson hands round a bottle of Navy rum. Charles leaves to rest his hand, and Arthur helps Pearson skin a deer. Pearson tells him pelts sell in almost any town, and asks him to bring anything else he catches.

The reward is the bow. According to the mission page, the mission unlocks Eagle Eye, and if it is played after "Old Friends", it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".

4. Old Friends

The mission is given by Bill Williamson in the men's cabin at Colter. It takes place at Ewing Basin, in the Grizzlies West, and ends at Colter. It can be played before or after "The Aftermath of Genesis".

In the cabin, Micah mocks Bill and Lenny for their long faces. Bill says people miss the ones who fell. Lenny tells Micah there will be a party when he falls, and Bill laughs. Micah punches Bill in the face; Arthur, Javier and Lenny hold the two men apart. Dutch bursts in: "You fools punching each other... when Colm O'Driscoll's needing punching, hard."

Arthur asks whether a raid is wise when the gang is barely back on its feet, and reminds Dutch that he always said revenge is a luxury they cannot afford. Dutch answers that the O'Driscolls "were talking about trains and detonators", and that Colm always had good information. He hands Arthur a Carbine Repeater and a lasso. Dutch leaves Hosea, Charles and Pearson to watch the camp, and rides out with Arthur, Micah, Bill, Javier and Lenny.

They follow horse tracks southwest along the main trail. Dutch says: "Some things I can forgive, others I can forget. What he did to Annabelle, I can't do neither." Arthur reminds him that he killed Colm's brother. Dutch answers that he hopes the two will be reunited soon.

From a ridge above the camp near the lake, Dutch posts Bill and Micah in a hidden position outside the camp and keeps Lenny and Javier on the high ground. Through binoculars, Dutch and Arthur watch Colm strike one of his men, who rides away; Colm leaves soon after. Dutch decides that Colm can wait. As they creep down, he tells Arthur that the gang needs "supplies, equipment, and a way out of here."

In cover, Dutch lets Arthur decide who opens fire. The gang kills the O'Driscolls in the camp, then a second wave that comes out of the trees; Arthur chooses to advance or hold ground. Looking at the bodies, Dutch says Colm "doesn't give a damn about his men" and only cares about numbers. Bill searches a wagon, Micah a building and Arthur another, where he finds a box of dynamite. Micah hands Dutch a roll of paper "found on one of them": the plans for a train belonging to a "Mr. Leviticus Cornwall."

On the ride back, Dutch tells the men he is proud that not a man is down: "Outlaws for life, fellers." He promises that John, Mac, Charles and Sean will ride with them again. When Arthur warns that Colm will come after them, Dutch says the gang allowed itself to get a step behind in Blackwater and that it will not happen again.

Near the lake, Dutch spots a lone man he thinks was at the camp with Colm and tells Arthur to bring him back alive. Arthur chases him, ropes him off his horse and hogties him. On the ride to Colter, the prisoner first says he does not know his name, then gives it: Kieran Duffy. He says he has only been riding with the O'Driscolls for a couple of months. At camp, Dutch refuses to have him beaten, since that would only produce lies. He tells Bill and Uncle to tie him up somewhere safe and get him hungry first. Kieran protests that he is not an O'Driscoll and hates Colm; Dutch answers with sarcasm.

The rewards are the Carbine Repeater, the lasso and Dead Eye targeting, according to the mission page. If "Old Friends" is played second, it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".

5. Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?

The mission starts in John's cabin at Colter, where Dutch announces the job. It takes place in the Grizzlies West and, according to the mission page, ends at Granite Pass.

John lies in bed with Swanson beside him and tells Arthur: "I owe you." Dutch comes in and says it is time for the train. John asks to come and tries to get up: "I was always ugly, Dutch... it's just a scratch." Dutch tells him to lie still. Abigail brings Jack to see his father, and the couple trade insults in front of the boy.

Outside, Dutch sends Bill ahead to set the charge "at the water tower, just before the tunnel." Hosea objects: the weather is breaking, the gang could leave, and the plan was to lie low and head back west. He says: "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch." Dutch answers that everything the gang owns is in Blackwater. Hosea warns that "Leviticus Cornwall's no joke"; when Arthur asks who that is, Hosea describes Cornwall as "a big railway magnate, sugar dealer, oil man."

Dutch rides out with Arthur, Micah, Charles, Javier and Lenny. He explains that, according to the O'Driscolls' plans, the train will come north from Big Valley and will be hit after it crosses into the Grizzlies. Charles is to watch for outriders. Dutch takes the driver and engineer, Lenny and Javier the two front cars, and Arthur and Micah the private car at the back. Micah keeps asking about going back to Blackwater for the money; Charles says it would be crazy with the town full of Pinkertons, and Dutch ends the discussion. Dutch says that if the O'Driscolls are right, there will be a stack of railroad bonds on the train. As they leave the snow, Arthur says: "Out of the snow, finally."

At the ridge above the water tower, Dutch sends Arthur down to check on Bill. Bill has Arthur unspool the wire and set up the detonator by the rocks. When the train passes, Bill pushes the plunger and nothing happens. Arthur, Javier and Lenny run to the far end of the tunnel and jump onto the train from above. Javier misses and is left behind; the mission page says he is hurt in the fall. Lenny ends up hanging from the side of a car, and Arthur can pull him up; otherwise Lenny climbs up alone and thanks him sarcastically.

Inside the first car, Lenny kills a guard with his knife. Arthur chooses whether he or Lenny takes the next one, which starts the gunfight along the train. Lenny asks what Bill was doing; Arthur says he hooked up the wire himself, "but we won't mention that." Near the engine, the engineer comes out of the coal bin and hits Arthur with a shovel. Arthur overpowers him, throws him off the train and stops it. Guards pour out of the cars, and the rest of the gang rides in to help clear them.

Employees have locked themselves in the last car. One of them calls out: "I work for Leviticus Cornwall." Dutch counts down from five, has the gang fire on the car, then sends Arthur and Charles to blow the door with Bill's dynamite. The employees come out with their hands up; Dutch tells them: "We don't want to kill you... we just wanna rob your boss." Cornwall himself is not on the train.

Arthur, Lenny and Micah search the car, which Lenny compares to a palace. Micah opens the safe and finds only papers. Lenny reads them: railroad contracts, invoices, sugar imports from the Spanish West Indies and "Some fancy new boat he's ordered from Europe." Micah laughs that he will not rob another boat as long as he lives. Arthur finds the bonds in the car's cabinets. Dutch calls them bearer bonds and thinks they can be sold easily.

Dutch tells Arthur to get rid of the train and leaves the employees to him: "It's up to you." Arthur can kill them or put them back on the train, start the engine and jump off. The mission page says the choice only affects the player's honor. Dutch tells Arthur that the gang will be moving on when he gets back.

The reward is dynamite, according to the mission page. Two documents can be missed in the car: the Oil Company Letter to Cornwall and the Mining Company Letter to Cornwall. The mission continues directly into "Eastward Bound".

6. Eastward Bound

The last mission of the chapter is given by Dutch. It starts at Colter and ends at Horseshoe Overlook, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.

Arthur rides back from the train to find the camp loading the wagons. Dutch says the weather seems stable; Hosea reminds him they just robbed a Cornwall train. Dutch declares: "the worst is behind us." Hosea proposes Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine: "We'll be able to hide out there no problem... as long as we keep our noses clean." Charles and Abigail help John into a wagon. Dutch puts Arthur and Hosea in the same wagon, saying the two like to talk about "what's gone wrong with old Dutch."

As the convoy leaves the snow, Dutch sends Micah and Lenny ahead to check the road. Micah complains about being paired with "the boy", then tells Lenny he can buy him a whisky. After a stream crossing, a wheel comes off Arthur and Hosea's wagon. The wagon ahead, carrying Bill and Charles, with Javier riding alongside, stops. Hosea turns down Javier's help; Charles stays behind, and he and Hosea hold the wagon up while Arthur puts the wheel back on.

Three riders watch them from a cliff. Charles says: "If they wanted trouble... we wouldn't have seen them." The Red Dead Wiki identifies them as Rains Fall, Eagle Flies and Paytah, of the Wapiti tribe. Charles rides on in their wagon. Hosea explains that the native people of the Heartlands lost the land and were killed or moved to reservations, and that he heard the Army there was particularly harsh. Charles asks: "How do you rob and kill people pleasantly? We don't, in spite of Dutch's talk."

Arthur asks Charles about his tribe. Charles says he does not know if he has one. His father was a Black man who lived with his mother's people for a while; when they were forced off their lands, the three fled. Soldiers later took his mother, and they never saw her again. His father drank, and at around thirteen Charles struck out on his own. Hosea says it was about the age they found Arthur: "A wilder delinquent you never did see. But he learned fast."

Hosea describes Valentine as a livestock town of "Cowboys, outlaws, working girls." He tells Arthur that it "isn't like Dutch to lose his head like that." Arthur answers that things go wrong sometimes and that the three of them must have got it right far more often than wrong. Hosea gives Arthur yarrow and ginseng he has been preparing. At the entrance to the site, Javier waits by a rock, shows them the track and jumps onto the back of the wagon.

At the new camp, Dutch says: "Gentlemen, we have survived." Hosea blames Micah for the ferry job. Dutch answers that they have all made mistakes, but that he kept the gang together and "kept the nooses off our neck." Hosea says he does not have long and wants people safe before he goes. Dutch's plan is to make some money, go around the law, "be west of Uncle Sam" and buy land within a few months. Strauss leaves for the nearby town to "strike up a little business."

Dutch gathers the gang. He tells them they are safe but "far too poor", and that everyone must get to work. Hosea tells them to stay out of trouble: "Remember, we are itinerant workers." Dutch adds: "Uncle, Reverend Swanson... no more passengers." Pearson asks for real food every day, and Dutch sets out a box: "the camp gets its slice." Susan shows Arthur to his tent.

The title card "Chapter II: Horseshoe Overlook" appears, followed by "A couple of weeks later..." Arthur writes in his journal: "We got off the mountain and rode east into some pretty enough country called the Heartlands." According to the mission page, completing "Eastward Bound" opens free roam and gives Arthur a camera, five ginseng and five yarrow, and Horseshoe Overlook becomes the gang's camp.

Camp life at Colter

The Camp Events page of the Red Dead Wiki lists a small number of optional scenes at Colter. They can be seen once "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" is complete, until the second of "Old Friends" and "The Aftermath of Genesis" is finished.

  • By the fireplace, Dutch tells Hosea he feels Hosea doubts him; Hosea says he is only worried. Dutch asks Arthur if he doubts him too, and Arthur says he does not.
  • In the main cabin, Sadie sobs over her husband while Mary-Beth Gaskill comforts her. Arthur can offer Sadie food; she says she is not hungry.
  • Susan Grimshaw tells everyone to stay warm, that they will be off the mountain in a few days and that the gang must stick together. Tilly Jackson says she hopes she is right.
  • Swanson reads Isaiah 40:20-31 aloud. Karen Jones asks what it means; he says he has no idea, but it gives him comfort.
  • Abigail scolds the bedridden John for getting himself mauled by wolves. When Arthur approaches, John says "Oh God no", and Susan tells Arthur to leave him alone.
  • Dutch asks Hosea to send someone "sensible" to bury Sadie's husband. Arthur can tell Hosea the body is in a wagon in front of the house.
  • Dutch and Hosea argue about what comes next. Hosea brings up the Pinkertons and bounty hunters; Dutch says the plan is to lie low, make money and get out, but admits he does not know where to yet.
  • If "Old Friends" comes first, Kieran can be found tied up in the stable. He tries to scare Arthur with the O'Driscolls who will come looking for him.

The same page notes that Bill, Javier, Charles and Pearson have no camp events in this chapter. According to the Colter page of the wiki, Pearson's butcher stand sits beside the stable, and Arthur's health drains quickly if he strays too far from the camp during the chapter.

Characters in this chapter

Every member of the gang at Colter makes a first appearance in the game in this chapter. Arthur Morgan, the playable character, takes part in every mission. Dutch van der Linde leads the gang and Hosea Matthews argues against the train job. John Marston is found wounded by wolves and spends the rest of the chapter in bed, visited by Abigail and their son Jack.

The riders of the chapter are Micah Bell, Bill Williamson, Javier Escuella, Lenny Summers and Charles Smith, whose burned hand keeps him off the bow. The camp is run by Susan Grimshaw and fed by Simon Pearson. Also present are Orville Swanson, Leopold Strauss, Uncle, Karen Jones, Tilly Jackson, Mary-Beth Gaskill and Molly O'Shea, whom Susan asks to show Dutch to his room.

Two newcomers arrive at camp in this chapter: Sadie Adler, taken in after the raid on her ranch, and Kieran Duffy, held as a prisoner. Colm O'Driscoll is seen only through binoculars at Ewing Basin. Leviticus Cornwall is named but does not appear. Rains Fall is one of the three riders on the cliff in "Eastward Bound", according to the Red Dead Wiki.

Davey Callander arrives dead, and Jenny Kirk died before the gang reached Colter. Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander are missing, and Josiah Trelawny does not appear until a later chapter.

Trivia

  • Davey Callander's grave is in a small graveyard at Colter, and Jenny Kirk's lies north of Colter, near Spider Gorge, according to the Red Dead Wiki. Both count toward the "Paying Respects" trophy.
  • Hosea's line "If we don't stop soon, we'll all be dying" is the one Arthur remembers in "A Fork in the Road" with high honor. His plea "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch" returns during Arthur's last ride with high honor, according to the wiki's mission pages.
  • "Old Friends" unlocks Dead Eye targeting, and "The Aftermath of Genesis" unlocks Eagle Eye, according to the mission pages.
  • After "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", an avalanche blocks the mountainside north of the ravine where John was found, according to the mission page.
  • The title "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" plays on the stage direction "Exit, pursued by a bear" from Shakespeare's The Winter's Tale, as does the later mission "Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego", according to the wiki.
  • "Outlaws from the West" is the only mission in which a gang member's horse, Charles's mare Taima, is meant to be ridden by the player, according to the mission page. Arthur later thanks Charles for lending her.
  • The train robbery is reported under the headline "Train Robbed" in the Blackwater Ledger, the New Hanover Gazette and the Saint Denis Times. Arthur's journal lists the job twice under "Gang Savings", the second time after Hosea cashes in the bonds, according to the wiki.
  • Rains Fall later tells Arthur, in Saint Denis, that he saw him with the wagon train crossing the river at Cumberland Falls.
  • Colter is not revisited in the story after Chapter 1. In Red Dead Online, set in 1898, it is an O'Driscoll hideout, according to the wiki.

Key events of Chapter 1

  • May 1899: after the failed Blackwater ferry robbery, the gang flees into the Grizzlies West of Ambarino and shelters in the abandoned mining town of Colter. Davey Callander arrives dead; Jenny Kirk has already died; Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander are missing.
  • "Outlaws from the West": Dutch, Arthur and Micah kill a band of O'Driscolls at the Adler Ranch and bring back the widow Sadie Adler. Arthur keeps a Tennessee Walker from the barn.
  • "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": Arthur and Javier find John Marston on a mountain ledge, his face torn by wolves, and bring him back to camp.
  • "The Aftermath of Genesis": Charles teaches Arthur to hunt with a bow, and they bring two deer back to Pearson.
  • "Old Friends": the gang raids the O'Driscoll camp at Ewing Basin, takes dynamite and the plans for a Cornwall train, and Arthur captures Kieran Duffy.
  • "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?": the gang robs Leviticus Cornwall's train in the Grizzlies and takes railroad bonds, despite Hosea's objections.
  • "Eastward Bound": the gang leaves the snow for a new camp at Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.
