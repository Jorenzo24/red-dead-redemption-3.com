Relationships
Agustin Allende
His colonel, the governor of Nuevo Paraíso. De Santa presents him to John as a good and strong man who carries the weight of a million problems. He defends him each time John criticizes him, and says the colonel keeps control of every situation (see Quotes). Allende insults him in Spanish in front of John (see Names) and tells him he is not his shadow.
De Santa brings young women to the mansion for him (see Biography). Asked about it, he answers that the colonel needs recreation like anyone else and has no time to court women. At Sepulcro, he condemns a rebel to death in the name of Colonel Allende's government (see Biography). Both men are reported dead in the same issue of the Blackwater Ledger (see Biography).
John Marston
De Santa is John's first contact with the army in Nuevo Paraíso and sends him on every mission of his strand, promising that the colonel will find Bill and Javier in return (see Biography). He asks John why he hunts the two men; John says first that he is being made to do it, then that his wife and child have been taken from him. In front of Allende, De Santa calls John a friend of Mexico. He betrays him in Chuparosa (see Biography).
At Sepulcro, John beats him until he names a hiding place for Javier, then kills him or leaves him to the rebels (see Biography). If the rebels killed him, John later tells Reyes that De Santa was only following orders and that he was "dumb and loyal to his awful master".
Abraham Reyes
The rebel leader De Santa is fighting. He insults him at length on the way to Chuparosa (see Quotes), calls him a man born in a golden cradle who pretends to fight for the poor, and says that Reyes only makes speeches from a balcony. Later he calls him a coward who writes poems and songs about battles he never fought. Reyes's rebels save John from De Santa's firing squad in Chuparosa (see Biography).
When John tells Reyes, in "Must a Savior Die?", that he is only after two men, Reyes answers: "You have been spending too much time with Captain de Santa." John replies that he did not mean it that way. After Sepulcro, Reyes celebrates De Santa's death. If John shot him, Reyes says he is glad it was John's bullet after Chuparosa; if the rebels did, he asks why John did not kill him himself and adds: "A lot of young boys will sleep safer in their beds now."
Bill Williamson
One of the two outlaws John is hunting. De Santa asks about "Billy the cowboy and his Mexican friend" on their first ride. In "Cowards Die Many Times", he leads John to Chuparosa on the pretext that Bill and Javier have been captured there (see Biography).
Javier Escuella
In "Mexican Caesar", De Santa offers John, on the colonel's behalf, information on Javier's whereabouts (see Biography). At Sepulcro, beaten by John, he names Casa Madrugada as Javier's hiding place; soldiers are waiting there instead (see Biography).
Luisa Fortuna
Luisa learns that De Santa has been sent to oversee a massacre at Sepulcro and asks John to ride there with her men and kill him and Allende's other followers (see Biography). She calls him an animal and vermin, and stays behind at the rebel camp.
Espinoza
A fellow army captain and his rival. When John asks whether they share the same rank, De Santa says Espinoza leads nothing and calls him an angry dog the army lets out from time to time; at Torquemada he tells John to ignore that stupid ape. Espinoza, for his part, mocks him with the names given under Names. In "Mexican Caesar", he tells John that De Santa licks the colonel's boots, plays with his waiter friend and flirts with barmen, and that he never saw him fight at Torquemada.
On the same escort, Espinoza notes that his squad is made of new recruits and of the two men De Santa hates most (see Quotes). At the end, he asks John whether he wants to kill De Santa or leave it to him; John answers that De Santa is more useful to him alive. In "Cowards Die Many Times", De Santa blames Espinoza for that escort. John kills Espinoza in Chuparosa (see Biography).
Quique Montemayor
A waiter at Allende's mansion in Escalera; the Red Dead Wiki says he works for De Santa and identifies him as the waiter who brings De Santa his tequila in "Civilization, at Any Price". In "Mexican Caesar", he helps De Santa push two young women into the mansion and insults them in Spanish (see Biography). Espinoza's remarks about the waiter are under the Espinoza entry; a published reading of these scenes is under Reception.
Victor Melendez
The rebel who leads John to Sepulcro on Luisa's orders. He asks John whether the rebels may kill De Santa or whether John wants the pleasure himself. If John shoots, Victor says he is glad it was him; if the rebels shoot, he thanks John but says it really should have been him. He then says De Santa is responsible for hundreds, perhaps thousands, of innocent deaths (see Quotes), and that with Espinoza and De Santa dead, the army has no leaders left.
His parents
Neither is named in the game. What he says of his upbringing is told under Biography (Origins). On the ride to Chuparosa, he tells John that a woman can be a powerful force or a destructive force, and gives his mother as the example of both.
Herbert Moon
The Armadillo shopkeeper names him among the Mexican leaders he lumps together, in a line quoted by the Red Dead Wiki: "Sanchez, Allende, De Santa, Reyes; all the same! They just want Armadillo rail rides."
Weapons, horse and gameplay
The Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver and a Repeater Carbine as his weapons, and a Painted Quarter Horse as his mount; the French-language wiki gives him a Volcanic Pistol. In "The Demon Drink", he challenges John to a race to Tesoro Azul, which is one of the mission's objectives. In "Cowards Die Many Times", he runs a shooting contest on the wagon ride to Chuparosa: each man gets five shots at the local wildlife, and he promises 25 dollars to whoever kills the most. According to the mission page, the contest starts when John shoots an animal and can be won three times (the reward is in the Missions table).
In "The Demon Drink", "Empty Promises" and the first part of "Cowards Die Many Times", the mission fails if John assaults, kills or abandons him. At Sepulcro, John must kill his soldiers without hitting him, then lasso him, hogtie him and carry him to the cemetery entrance. According to the mission page, he runs slowly and can steal John's horse; a rider who cuts him off can herd him back like cattle. He may shoot at John or at the rebels, and draws a new revolver or rifle each time he is disarmed. If John starts a fistfight with him, the rebels shoot him, and the following dialogue plays as if John had killed him. The Red Dead Wiki adds that killing him counts as a civilian kill.
Missions
De Santa appears in six story missions of Red Dead Redemption, all in the second act, set in Nuevo Paraíso, and in one mission of Undead Nightmare. He gives the five missions of his own strand, all of which begin in Escalera. The sixth, in which he dies, belongs to the strand of Luisa Fortuna. Abraham Reyes mentions him in two other missions of the act.

Development and performance
Casting
Captain De Santa appears in Red Dead Redemption and in its expansion Undead Nightmare, both released in 2010. The Red Dead Wiki credits Hector Luis Bustamante as his voice, and the Wikipedia article on the actor lists the role, as "Captain Vincente De Santa", in both games. According to Wikipedia, Bustamante is a Colombian-American actor, born Héctor Luis Bustamante Durango in Medellín, whose family moved to the United States when he was 12; the Red Dead Wiki gives his date of birth as March 12, 1972. His television and film roles include the series The Shield and the film Hostage (2005). He won the 2009 Imagen Award for outstanding lead actor in a made-for-television movie, and Wikipedia names Little Girl Lost: The Delimar Vera Story (Lifetime Movie Network, 2008) as his best-known performance.
No interview in which Bustamante discusses Red Dead Redemption was found. No source consulted names a performer for De Santa's motion capture, and Wikipedia's article on the game does not mention the character. The Red Dead Wiki's page on the game's cut content lists nothing about him.
Reception
Villain rankings
De Santa does not have an entry in "Red Dead Redemption: Every Villain Ranked From Worst To Best", published by WhatCulture on February 7, 2022. In the entry on Allende, ranked 13th, Matt Thompson writes that "his underling, Captain Vincente de Santa has more of a character evolution after the betrayal", and regrets that the colonel was not given the same depth.
LGBTQ Video Game Archive
The LGBTQ Video Game Archive, a research project led by Adrienne Shaw of Temple University, published an entry titled "Quique Montemayor and Captain Vincente de Santa in Red Dead Redemption" on June 24, 2016. It files the pair under implicit gay and implicit bisexual representation. The entry says that the two men seem to be "openly in an intimate relationship", that several major characters comment on it, and that De Santa also flirts with women. It quotes, without naming the speaker, Reyes's line on young boys sleeping safely after De Santa's death, and the word mariconcito. Its listed sources are two Red Dead Wiki pages, a 2013 blog post and a 2010 IGN forum thread.
Academic study
In "The Mustang Diaries: A Study of Mexico in Red Dead Redemption 1", published in the Revista Panamericana de Comunicación (vol. 4, no. 2, 2022), Javier Aldape, of Texas A&M University, mentions him once, as "one of his subordinates, Captain Vicente De Santa", who tells John that Colonel Allende knows where Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella are.
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
De Santa became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Myths and Mavericks Bonus Pack. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rockstar first hinted at this free pack on June 6, 2011, set a September release on July 27 and gave the date and contents on September 1. It was released free on September 13, 2011 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360; according to the same wiki, it was the last downloadable content released for the game. It added eight characters to the Revolution section of the Outfitter: Drew MacFarlane, Eli, Javier Escuella, Jonah, Landon Ricketts, Nastas, Uncle and De Santa. His multiplayer lines are in Spanish (see Quotes).
According to its caption on the wiki, a promotional image of the pack shows De Santa in a Stronghold match at Torquemada with Javier Escuella, Drew MacFarlane and Eli. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, contain the story and Undead Nightmare but not the multiplayer mode, so the De Santa model is not available in them.
Later games
De Santa does not appear in Red Dead Redemption 2 or in Red Dead Online. The Red Dead Wiki lists him only in Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare.
Trivia
According to their mission pages, "Civilization, at Any Price", "The Demon Drink", "Empty Promises" and "Cowards Die Many Times" fail if the player assaults, kills or abandons De Santa. "Captain De Santa's Downfall" fails if he is killed before he is captured and made to talk.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, if the player kills De Santa, the game counts him as a civilian kill.
According to the mission page of "Civilization, at Any Price", the player can climb onto the train during the escort. De Santa then shouts at John to get off, but the mission does not fail, and he eventually stops.
According to the mission page of "The Demon Drink", if the player disarms Espinoza during the shootout at Tesoro Azul, Espinoza asks: "Did De Santa make you do this?"
According to the mission page of "Cowards Die Many Times", a round of the shooting contest is won by killing five animals with five bullets, and the contest can be played three times.
According to the mission page of "Captain De Santa's Downfall", De Santa can steal John's horse or shoot at John while he flees. If John rides in front of him, he turns back, so he can be herded like cattle. When disarmed, he keeps drawing a new revolver or rifle. If he is close to the rebels, he fights them and ignores John.
According to the same page, if the player starts a fistfight with De Santa after the interrogation, the rebels open fire on him, but the following dialogue plays as if John had killed him.
According to the mission page of "The Gates of El Presidio", when the mission is replayed, Reyes always speaks as if the rebels, not John, had killed De Santa.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, when De Santa speaks of the colonel, the subtitles write "Coronel", the Spanish word, instead of "Colonel".
The mission title "Cowards Die Many Times" comes from a line of Shakespeare's Julius Caesar, "Cowards die many times before their deaths", according to the mission page.
De Santa shares his surname with Michael De Santa, one of the three playable characters of Grand Theft Auto V (Rockstar, 2013). In that game, De Santa is a false name: the character was born Michael Townley.
