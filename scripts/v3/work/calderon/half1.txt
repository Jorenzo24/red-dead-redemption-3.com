
Sister Calderón is a Catholic nun in Saint Denis in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. She teaches street children to read with Brother Dorkins and collects money and food for the poor. Arthur Morgan recovers her stolen crucifix in chapter 4 and gives to her collection in an optional mission. If his honor is high, he meets her again at Emerald Station in chapter 6, as she leaves for a mission in Mexico; there he tells her that he is dying. Otherwise, Orville Swanson appears in her place.
In Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911, the mother superior of the Las Hermanas convent asks John Marston for alms, and in the non-canon Undead Nightmare she helps him against the plague. The Red Dead Wiki treats the two as one character. Irene DeBari voices her in Red Dead Redemption 2.
Key facts
She gives only her surname, Calderón; no game gives her a first name, a birth date or a nationality.
In "Brothers and Sisters, One and All" (chapter 4), Arthur gets back the crucifix her mother gave her.
In "Of Men and Angels", Arthur gives her food or 10 dollars for the poor; the mission must be done before "The Fine Art of Conversation".
At Emerald Station, with high honor, Arthur tells her he has tuberculosis and is afraid, before she boards a train for Mexico.
In Red Dead Redemption (1911), John can give 10 dollars to the mother superior of Las Hermanas, in Nuevo Paraíso, or rob her ("I Know You"); the Red Dead Wiki identifies her with the Saint Denis nun.

Biography
Origins
No game gives Sister Calderón a first name, a date of birth or a place of birth. In Red Dead Redemption 2 she is a Catholic nun in Saint Denis and gives her surname, Calderón, to Arthur Morgan in "Of Men and Angels". In Red Dead Redemption, set in 1911, the nun John Marston meets outside the Las Hermanas convent in Nuevo Paraíso is called only the Mother Superior. In Undead Nightmare, an expansion outside the series' canon, the Mother Superior of Las Hermanas gives her name as Calderón. The Red Dead Wiki treats the nun of 1899 and the Mother Superior of 1911 as a single character, whom it calls Mother Superior Calderón.
What is known of her past comes from her own words to Arthur. She says that she was married and that her husband died long ago. Her crucifix came from her mother: as a street boy runs off with it, she says her mother gave it to her when she was a girl; when Arthur returns it, she says her mother gave it to her when she was a novice, shortly before she died. She also tells Arthur that she was once like him. By her account, she did terrible things and could not stop, because she did not believe goodness existed, until the day she saw that love did exist; from then on, doing wrong seemed absurd to her. She gives no details of those acts.
In 1899 she works with Brother Dorkins, a monk, and teaches street children to read. According to the Red Dead Wiki, both are attached to the Church of the Holy Blessed Virgin in Saint Denis, led by Father O'Leary. She tells Arthur that Dorkins keeps asking to be sent overseas as a missionary, and that a Sister Ethel cooks for the poor they feed.
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 4: Saint Denis
Arthur first hears of her through Brother Dorkins. In the stranger mission "Help a Brother Out", he frees two captives for Dorkins, who tells him he often works at the old church on Gaspar Street. The follow-up, "Brothers and Sisters, One and All", opens there: the Sister and Dorkins are helping street children through a reading lesson. She coaxes a boy who is struggling with the letters, and gently tells Dorkins that his grammar corrections are confusing the child. Dorkins introduces Arthur, and she says Dorkins has told her about the good turn he did; Arthur says Dorkins talks a lot of nonsense.
One of the boys then grabs her crucifix and runs. Arthur gives chase; she calls after him not to hurt the child, and Dorkins says they will wait at the cathedral. Arthur finds the boy held by a man who accuses him of stealing his watch, makes the man let him go, and picks up the crucifix the boy drops. He then runs into Edith Downes, now working as a prostitute, who calls the police on him; he has to lose the lawmen before going to the cathedral.
He finds the Sister there, talking with a priest about the daily challenges of her work. She can hardly believe he has the cross back, asks after the boy, and hugs Arthur, calling him the most wonderful man; Arthur answers that Dorkins is greatly deceived about him. She explains what the crucifix means to her (see Origins) and, as Arthur leaves, tells the priest that Dorkins had been right about him.
Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow
"Of Men and Angels" becomes available only if "Brothers and Sisters, One and All" was completed, and only after "A Fork in the Road", the mission in which Arthur learns he is ill. It must be played before "The Fine Art of Conversation", after which it is no longer offered. Arthur finds the Sister outside the Saint Denis cathedral with a collection box, asking passers-by for money and food for the poor. She introduces herself by name, says she and Dorkins had hoped to see him again, and reports that Dorkins is frustrated with their order.
She asks how he is; he says never better, and she does not believe him. She tells him about her own past (see Origins). Arthur says he only hopes for a dignified exit, and that he is an outlaw, not a religious man. She laughs, says she will pray for him anyway, and tells him that religion is only a word, that people are what matter, and that good actions come first and the heart follows. She suggests he find food for the starving. Arthur gives either the food he is carrying or some money (10 dollars); both choices raise his honor. She calls him wonderful, adds with a joke that she will go on thinking badly of him if he prefers, and lets him go. The second part of the mission completes automatically.
Her last scene with Arthur closes "The Fine Art of Conversation". After Arthur and Charles Smith get Captain Lyndon Monroe away from the failed talks between Colonel Favours and Rains Fall and put him on a train at Emerald Station, Charles rides off and Arthur is left on the platform, coughing. If "Of Men and Angels" was completed and his honor is high, the Sister comes up to him; otherwise he meets Reverend Orville Swanson. She says she is on her way to Mexico, where she is finally being sent on a mission, and that Dorkins is very jealous.
Arthur starts coughing again and she helps him onto a bench. He tells her he is dying of tuberculosis, which he caught while beating a man for a few dollars, and that he has lived a bad life. She answers that everyone sins, and that the trouble is that Arthur does not know himself: each time they have met, he was helping someone. He tells her about the son he lost, the woman who loved him and whom he let go, the mother who died when he was a child and the father he watched die. She replies that her own husband died long ago. When he asks what he should do now, she tells him to be grateful that he finally sees his life clearly, and suggests he help someone.
Arthur says he still does not believe in anything; she laughs that she often does not either, until she meets someone like him. He admits that he is afraid. She tells him there is nothing to fear and urges him to act out of love; he promises to try. She boards the train, calling out her goodbyes. Arthur does not see her again. With high honor, her advice about seeing his life clearly is one of the lines that come back to him on his last ride, in "Red Dead Redemption", the final mission played as Arthur.
Red Dead Redemption (1911)
Act II: Nuevo Paraíso
In 1911 a Mother Superior runs the convent of Las Hermanas, in the Perdido region of Nuevo Paraíso. The Red Dead Wiki identifies her as Sister Calderón and describes Las Hermanas as the place she had been sent to twelve years earlier; the games do not say where her 1899 mission took her. John Marston meets her in the second part of the stranger mission "I Know You", which opens once he has completed the first part and the Irish mission "We Shall Be Together in Paradise". At a campfire on the road west of El Matadero, the Strange Man tells John that an old nun is carrying the money she raised from the convent to the bank, on a road full of thieves. He suggests John help her, or rob her himself.
Outside Las Hermanas, the Mother Superior is asking for alms for the poor. When John asks whether looking after the flock is not the Lord's job, she answers that the Lord has sent him to her, and that a few dollars would show people at their wits' end that someone cares. John can drop 10 dollars in her basket; she thanks him in Spanish and blesses him. If he gives, nuns later come up to him in random encounters to ask for donations. He can instead rob her, which costs him honor.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, outside the mission she can be found at Las Hermanas going about her daily work, and eating on the second floor of the church.
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
Undead Nightmare, an expansion released in 2010, is not part of the series' canon. In "Mother Superior Blues", John reaches the Las Hermanas train station as an undead is strangling the Mother Superior. She breaks free and shoots it in the head, then fears she has committed a mortal sin; John tells her it was self-defense. She explains that her nuns are trapped in the church with the undead and are less willing than she is to use violence, and asks John to clear the convent. He can kill the undead himself or hand ammunition to the survivors.
Once the convent is safe, John calls her sister, and she corrects him: she is the Mother Superior. She tells him that the bishop fell ill the week before and ate half of the choirboys. She has no idea what is afflicting the people, but believes it is a sickness of the soul, and asks John to bring her a freshly turned undead. John hogties one and brings it back. She pours holy water on it: the body burns briefly in a blue flame and seems to come back to life for a moment, then returns to its growling. She wonders whether her faith is strong enough. She then sends John to cleanse the cemetery of Sepulcro and gives him Holy Water, which becomes available to the player. At Sepulcro, John burns the coffins and kills the undead, among them Vincente de Santa, who rises from his grave.
When John returns, she tells him in the church that a woman has told her the curse is the result of something done by Abraham Reyes, the next president, and sends him to Escalera. The game's mission description calls the informant a nun passing through; according to the Red Dead Wiki, she is Ayauhtéotl, who appears at Las Hermanas dressed as a nun. The same wiki reports that the Mother Superior speaks of Reyes with contempt, as a vain and ambitious parasite and a womanizer, and adds that he seems to have the most half-witted friends, which leads John to ask whether she knows Bill Williamson. The second part of the mission, which requires the side missions "Filth and Other Entertainment" and "Biographies and Lies", consists of riding to Escalera and finding Reyes in the presidential mansion. John does not see the Mother Superior again.
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang only and do not include her. In Saint Denis she teaches street children to read with Brother Dorkins, and corrects both the children and Dorkins himself with a laugh. When a boy runs off with her crucifix, she calls after Arthur not to hurt him; when Arthur brings it back, she asks after the boy. She collects money and food for the poor outside the cathedral, and her conversations with Arthur turn on the idea that acts count for more than beliefs (see Quotes).
She does not preach. When Arthur says he is an outlaw and not a religious man, she laughs. She tells him she once did terrible things herself (see Biography), and at Emerald Station she admits that she often does not believe either. In Red Dead Redemption (1911) she asks John Marston for alms and answers his objections instead of letting him go (see Biography). In Undead Nightmare, outside the series' canon, she shoots an undead herself and then worries that she has committed a mortal sin, according to the mission page.

Names
Calderón is the only name the games give her. In "Of Men and Angels", Arthur greets her as "Sister" and she supplies the rest: "Calderón." No first name is ever given. Brother Dorkins introduces Arthur to her with "Sister, my friend, Mr. Morgan.", and Arthur only ever calls her Sister. In Red Dead Redemption, the Strange Man speaks of "an old nun" and John, after his donation, says "There you are, sister."; the dialogue reproduced by the Red Dead Wiki labels her Mother Superior. In Undead Nightmare, when John calls her sister, she corrects him: she is the Mother Superior, Mother Superior Calderón, according to the ireddead.com walkthrough and the expansion's Italian version. The Red Dead Wiki titles her page "Mother Superior Calderón" and also lists the name Sister Calderón. According to the same wiki, a drunk at Doyle's Tavern in Saint Denis refers to her as a Spanish nun.
Appearance
She is an elderly woman; the transcript of the station scene calls her an old nun from Saint Denis. She wears a Catholic nun's habit: a black tunic, a white coif and a black veil. In Undead Nightmare she keeps the habit and carries a rifle (see Charity work and gameplay).
