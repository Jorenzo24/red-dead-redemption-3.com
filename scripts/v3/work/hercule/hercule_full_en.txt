
Hercule Fontaine is a smuggler who leads a band of rebels against Alberto Fussar, the colonel who governs the Caribbean island of Guarma, in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. In chapter 5 his ambush frees Dutch van der Linde, Arthur Morgan, Micah Bell and Bill Williamson, shipwrecked on the island and marched in chains by Fussar's soldiers; Javier Escuella is captured during the escape. Hercule promises them a boat in return for their help. Fussar calls in a warship from Cuba, which Arthur sinks with Hercule's cannon at the fort of Cinco Torres. Hercule then joins the attack on Aguasdulces, where Arthur kills Fussar, and the gang sails away on the ship he found for them. The game does not show what becomes of him.
He is voiced by Guyviaud Joseph.
Key facts
A smuggler who moves goods on and off the islands behind Fussar's back and uses the old fort of Cinco Torres as a hideout with his men.
In "Welcome to the New World", his ambush frees the chained castaways; he promises them a boat if they help Leon Fuentes with a group of escaped workers.
In "Hell Hath No Fury", he tells Dutch that if Fussar is silenced, he can get them away before anyone arrives from America; Arthur then sinks Fussar's warship with Hercule's cannon.
In "Paradise Mercifully Departed", he leads the attack on Fussar's gun positions and spots Fussar in the tower that Arthur brings down with a second cannon.
His last line to the gang: the rebels will probably disappear back to Haiti and be gone by morning. His fate after Guarma is not shown.

Biography
Origins
Red Dead Redemption 2 gives no date or place of birth for Hercule Fontaine and says nothing about his life before 1899. When he meets the gang, he explains that he is not from Guarma. He describes himself as a man who moves goods onto and off the islands without the knowledge of Colonel Alberto Fussar, who governs Guarma. He and his men use the old Spanish fort of Cinco Torres as a hideout when they are on the island. He also keeps a supply of guns in an old building in the jungle, which the Red Dead Wiki names El Nido. According to the wiki, Hercule supplied the uprising against Fussar before 1899 and runs his rebel movement from Cinco Torres.
On the island, he works with the rebel Leon Fuentes and with Baptiste, who tells Arthur Morgan that he works for Hercule. The Red Dead Wiki lists Hercule and Leon as the two leaders of the Guarma rebels. The game never states Hercule's nationality outright. Levi Simon, Fussar's overseer, dismisses the island's insurgents as Haitian pirates, and at the end of the chapter Hercule says that his group will probably go back to Haiti. The Red Dead Wiki describes him as Haitian.
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 5: Guarma
At the end of chapter 4, after the failed robbery of the Saint Denis bank, Dutch van der Linde, Arthur, Micah Bell, Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella flee on a ship that sinks in a storm. They wash up on Guarma. In "Welcome to the New World", soldiers led by Levi Simon arrest the five castaways on the beach and chain them together. On the march, the column picks up a group of captured local rebels, among them Leon Fuentes, whom a guard accuses of insurrection.
Hercule's rebels open fire on the escort from the jungle. Arthur takes a gun from a dead guard and kills the soldiers while the others free themselves. When enemy reinforcements appear, Hercule tells everyone to follow him at once. Javier is shot in the leg and captured during the escape, and the others have to leave him behind. Hercule leads Dutch, Arthur, Micah and Bill through the jungle to an old building where he has guns hidden. They take rifles from the crates and drive off the soldiers who followed them. Arthur thanks him for getting them out; when Arthur comments on the island, Hercule replies that it is not his.
Once they are safe, Hercule asks the castaways what they are doing on Guarma. Dutch answers that they are running from something, and Hercule gives his name. Dutch introduces himself, Micah, Bill and Arthur. Hercule warns them that Fussar will do everything he can to find them. Leon describes Fussar as a tyrant who works the islanders to death, and Hercule adds that Leon wants the people to stand up to him, but that most of them are too afraid. From a lookout, Hercule shows the group Aguasdulces, Fussar's compound, where the cane cut on the plantations is processed. Leon explains that a group of workers escaped into the jungle the day before, and Hercule says that Fussar's men are now hunting them.
Dutch asks whether they can trust him. Hercule answers that they have no real choice, since he is the only one who can arrange a boat for them. In return, he asks them to help Leon find the escaped workers and then to meet him at the fort of Cinco Torres. Dutch promises to do everything they can if Hercule helps them get Javier back and leave the island. Hercule tells them that Javier, if he is still alive, will be held at Fussar's compound, and that a cave hidden below the cliffs leads straight there. He takes the four men to a place where they can rest, near La Capilla, which the mission page describes as the rebels' camp, then says goodbye and leaves with Leon.
Hercule does not appear in the next two missions, but both refer to him. In "Savagery Unleashed", Arthur and Leon save several of the escaped workers from the gallows. Baptiste then tells Arthur that Hercule is lining up a boat to take the gang away and that they should meet him at Cinco Torres; he gives Arthur some money, $450 according to the Red Dead Wiki. In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", Dutch has found the cave Hercule told them about and pays an old woman, Gloria, to guide them through it. On the way, Arthur asks whether Hercule is simply using them. Dutch thinks he almost certainly is, but considers him their best chance of leaving the island. Once Javier is freed, Dutch takes him away and tells Arthur they will meet again at Hercule's fort.
In "Hell Hath No Fury", Arthur reaches Cinco Torres as Dutch is arguing with Hercule about the promised boat. Hercule tells them that Fussar now knows who they are and what price is on their heads. If they can silence Fussar, he says, he can get them away before anyone has time to arrive from America. Dutch insists on the boat. Hercule answers that they have landed in the middle of a war: Fussar has called in the navy from Cuba, and no boat can leave for now. A rebel then calls him urgently in French. From the top of the fort, the group sees a warship approaching the coast. Hercule tells them they can fight or run; Dutch chooses to fight, and Hercule goes to get the fort's cannon ready.
Soldiers land on the beach below and the gang fires on them from the walls. Micah complains that this is not their fight and that Hercule is using them; Hercule asks him whether he wants to leave the island or not. Bill shouts that Hercule promised them a boat, and Hercule answers that he is getting one, but that they are trapped. Arthur helps Hercule push the cannon into position. When the soldiers reach the fort, Micah and Bill stay on the roof to guard the cannon, while Arthur, Dutch and Hercule go down to clear the beach under fire from the warship. Hercule tells Arthur to take the shotgun. One of the ship's shells falls among its own soldiers. As boats come in from both sides, Arthur gives Dutch a position to cover, and Hercule follows Arthur.
Once the beach is clear, Hercule says that something must be done about the warship and that two of them should go and work the cannon. Dutch stays below to hold off the soldiers, and Arthur goes back up with Hercule. On the way, Arthur asks how many men Fussar has; Hercule answers that numbers do not matter, because Fussar can always pay for more. He shoots a soldier who is heading for Dutch. On the roof, Arthur fires the cannon while Hercule reloads it and tells him to aim at the waterline. The warship sinks. Dutch praises the shooting. Hercule warns that the soldiers will be back, but announces that he has found a boat and that its captain should reach the dock at any moment. Dutch sends Micah to meet the captain, asks Bill to help him fetch Javier, and tells Hercule that it was a great pleasure to meet him.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Hercule engaged this captain to take the five gang members off the island in return for their help with the workers. At the start of "Paradise Mercifully Departed", Micah comes back from scouting: Fussar knows who they are, his men are holding the captain, and gun positions cover the harbor, so that any boat trying to sail out would be shot to pieces. When Dutch turns to him, Hercule says that he will fight Fussar every day he can. Bill stays on the ship to guard Javier, and Dutch tells Hercule that the others will follow him.
Hercule leads Dutch, Arthur and Micah through territory held by Fussar's soldiers. Arthur and Micah kill the two guards of the first gun position, plant dynamite and blow it up. Hercule warns of a soldier on a roof, takes the group on to a second set of guns, and points out more of Fussar's men coming through the fields while Micah rigs the charges. Once the guns are destroyed, Micah locates the captain in a blue building, and Dutch sends Arthur in while the others hold the area outside. Hercule is not shown in the cabin, where Levi Simon and then Fussar hold Arthur and Dutch at gunpoint. Arthur kicks a rifle lying on the floor over to the captain, who shoots Simon dead, and Fussar runs out.
On the run to the ship, Hercule says that he did not see where Fussar went. Fussar's reinforcements arrive, and near the port Hercule spots him at the top of a tower, where Fussar loads a cannon and opens fire on them. Hercule tells Arthur that they have to take him down and points him to another cannon further ahead on the left. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Hercule draws Fussar's fire so that Arthur can reach it. Arthur fires at the tower and destroys its top, killing Fussar. The mission page adds that the player can also kill Fussar by shooting him.
At the dock, the injured captain says that he can handle the ship. Dutch asks Hercule whether he and his men will be all right. Hercule answers that the rebels will do as the gang does and disappear, probably back to Haiti, and that they will be gone by morning. Dutch thanks him. The five gang members board with the captain and sail for America. This is Hercule's last appearance.
After Guarma
The game does not show what becomes of Hercule after the gang leaves. No later mission mentions him. A later issue of the newspapers (Blackwater Ledger No. 68, New Hanover Gazette No. 33, Saint Denis Times No. 49) reports Fussar's assassination in "Leader of Guarma Assassinated". The article says the killing was first blamed on a revolt of the plantation workers, then on several white men, while some blame socialist insurrectionists; it names no rebel leader. In the epilogue newspapers (Blackwater Ledger No. 74, New Hanover Gazette No. 39, Saint Denis Times No. 55), "Unrest in the Tropics" reports that American banks took control of the sugar industry on Guarma after Fussar's death, without mentioning the rebels. The Red Dead Wiki notes that the state of the rebellion on the island by 1907 is unclear.
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang and do not include him, and no official guide description of him was found. What is known of his character comes from his three missions. He asks the castaways what they are doing on the island before he gives his own name. He says plainly that he is not from Guarma and that he works behind Fussar's back. He sets the terms of his help at once: a boat for the gang, in return for help with Leon's escaped workers (see Biography).
Under fire at Cinco Torres he answers Bill's and Micah's complaints sharply, then follows Arthur's lead in the fighting on the beach. He knows that killing soldiers will not end Fussar's hold on the island. Once Fussar is dead, he does not claim the island for the rebels: he says they will disappear, like the gang, before morning.

Names
He introduces himself as Hercule Fontaine to Dutch after the escape. The gang and the rebels call him Hercule. In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", Arthur refers to him as "this Hercule feller" (see Relationships). Leon Fuentes describes Baptiste as "One of Hercule's men". The Red Dead Wiki lists no alias for him.
Appearance
He is a Black man with short, thick curly hair and a full black beard, and a small scar on his left cheek. He wears several strings of colored beads around his neck.
Relationships
Dutch van der Linde
Dutch gives his name and the names of Micah, Bill and Arthur after the escape, and asks whether Hercule can be trusted; Hercule answers that they have no choice. Dutch accepts the deal, a boat in return for their help. Later, he tells Arthur that Hercule is "Almost certainly" using them, but that he is their best chance of leaving the island.
At Cinco Torres, Dutch reminds Hercule that the gang has done what he asked and argues with him over the promised boat (see Biography). After the warship sinks, he tells him: "Hercule... it was a great pleasure to make your acquaintance." Before the attack on the gun positions he lets Hercule lead ("Hercule, we follow you."), and at the harbor he asks whether Hercule will be all right, then thanks him.
Arthur Morgan
At the old building where they fight off the soldiers, Arthur thanks Hercule for getting them out and remarks: "Nice island you got here." Hercule replies: "It's not my island." In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", Arthur asks Dutch: "Are you sure this Hercule feller ain't just using us?"
At Cinco Torres the two men fight side by side. Arthur helps Hercule push the cannon into position; on the beach, when Arthur sends Dutch to cover the left, Hercule says he will follow Arthur. Back on the roof, Arthur fires the cannon while Hercule brings the shot, and the warship sinks. At the harbor, Hercule points Arthur to a second cannon to bring down Fussar's tower (see Biography).
Micah Bell
During the battle at Cinco Torres, Micah asks: "Why the hell are we doing this? This ain't our fight!" Hercule answers: "Do you want to get off this island or not?", and Micah shouts: "He's using us!" Micah later guards the cannon with Bill, and joins Hercule, Dutch and Arthur in the raid on the gun positions, where he rigs the dynamite (see Biography).
Bill Williamson
When the soldiers close in on the fort, Bill shouts: "You said you were getting us a goddamn boat, Hercule!" Hercule answers: "I am! What do you want me to do? We are trapped up here!" Bill stays on the roof to guard the cannon, then on the ship to guard Javier while the others attack the gun positions (see Biography).
Javier Escuella
Javier is shot in the leg and captured as the group flees the ambush led by Hercule. Hercule tells Dutch that if he is still alive, he will be held at Fussar's compound, and points him to the cave that leads there (see Biography). Javier leaves Guarma on the same ship as the others.
Alberto Fussar
The colonel who governs Guarma. Hercule smuggles goods behind his back and fights him with the rebels. He calls him Colonel Fussar and Aguasdulces "Fussar's compound". At Cinco Torres he tells Dutch that Fussar knows who the gang are and has called in the navy from Cuba. Arthur kills Fussar with a cannon shot at the tower Hercule points out (see Biography). The two men never speak to each other on screen.
Leon Fuentes
A rebel chained with the castaways and freed in the ambush. With Hercule, he explains Fussar's rule to the gang; Hercule adds that Leon wants the people to rise against Fussar but that most are too afraid. Hercule asks the gang to help Leon with a group of escaped workers. The Red Dead Wiki describes Leon as a member of the rebel faction led by Hercule.
Baptiste
A one-armed rebel whom Arthur frees in "Savagery Unleashed". He introduces himself to Arthur with "I work with Hercule", tells him that Hercule is lining up a boat for the gang at Cinco Torres, and gives him money; the Red Dead Wiki puts the sum at 450 dollars and describes him as a smuggler working with Hercule.
The ship's captain
After the warship sinks, Hercule tells Dutch that he has found a boat and that its captain should reach the dock at any moment. Micah goes to meet him. Fussar's men seize the captain before the gang can sail, and the attack on Aguasdulces frees him (see Biography).
Levi Simon
Fussar's senior overseer, whose patrol chains the castaways on the beach. Before the ambush, he tells Dutch that a "Bunch of Haitian pirates" will not frighten Fussar and will soon be hanged in the streets; the game does not say whom he means. He is killed in the cabin standoff (see Biography). He and Hercule never share a scene.
Smuggling and fighting
Smuggling
Hercule describes himself as a smuggler who moves goods on and off the islands without Fussar's knowledge. The Red Dead Wiki lists his occupations as revolutionary and smuggler. He has weapons hidden on the island: during the escape in "Welcome to the New World" he leads the group to an old building where he says he has guns stashed, and the castaways arm themselves there. He and his men use the old fort of Cinco Torres as a hideout whenever they are on Guarma. He tells Dutch that he is the only one who can arrange a boat off the island, and in "Hell Hath No Fury" he finds a ship's captain for the gang (see Biography). He also knows the island's paths: he tells Dutch about the cave below the cliffs that leads to Fussar's compound.
Fighting
The Red Dead Wiki credits him with four rifles: a Bolt Action Rifle, a Carcano Rifle, a Springfield Rifle and a Rolling Block Rifle. He fights beside the gang in all three of his missions; the mission pages list him among those who kill soldiers in "Welcome to the New World", "Hell Hath No Fury" and "Paradise Mercifully Departed". At Cinco Torres he readies the fort's cannon, tells Arthur to take the shotgun before going down to the beach, shoots a soldier who is running at Dutch, and keeps the cannon supplied with shot while Arthur aims at the warship. In "Paradise Mercifully Departed" he leads Dutch, Arthur and Micah to Fussar's gun positions and spots Fussar in the tower (see Biography, and the Missions table).
In "Welcome to the New World", the mission fails if the player attacks or kills Hercule or Leon. The Red Dead Wiki notes that in "Hell Hath No Fury" he runs very fast on the way back up to the roof, along a path with sharp turns and drops, and advises players to follow him on the mini-map.
Languages
He speaks English with the gang and French with his men. In the transcript, a rebel calls him in French when the warship appears, Hercule swears in French during the fighting at Cinco Torres, and he sets off toward the gun positions with "En allez". On the run to the harbor in "Paradise Mercifully Departed", he calls the soldiers "connards".
Missions
The Red Dead Wiki lists three missions of Red Dead Redemption 2 for Hercule, all in chapter 5. He is the giver of "Hell Hath No Fury"; the other two missions are given by Dutch and by Bill. Two more Guarma missions mention him without showing him.

Development and performance
Casting
Hercule Fontaine appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), in chapter 5. He is voiced by Guyviaud Joseph: Wikipedia's list of the game's characters, which cites the game's credits, names him as the actor of the "Guarman rebellion leader Hercule Fontaine", and the Red Dead Wiki, in English and in French, gives the same credit. No interview in which Guyviaud Joseph discusses the role has been found, and no source names him for the character's motion capture. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the two other named rebels are voiced by Babs Olusanmokun (Baptiste) and Andhy Méndez (Leon Fuentes).
Cut content
The Red Dead Wiki's page on cut content lists an unused coat for Hercule. Although there are no horses on Guarma in the final game, the files contain mission-fail text for attacking Hercule's and Baptiste's horses, and vehicle records for a horse-drawn wagon in "Paradise Mercifully Departed". The same page describes a cut mission whose internal name is "Smuggler1", in which Arthur and Bill would have rescued the niece of the rebel Leon Fuentes and taken her off the island. A cut Guarma character, Senor Andreas Colon, wears trousers nearly identical to Hercule's.
Reception
Hercule has drawn little press attention. In a WhatCulture list of single-player expansions published on June 16, 2020, Dustin Crawford called him "an interesting character" and described him as a "Haitian-born freedom fighter leading a revolt" against Colonel Fussar. He suggested an expansion set on Guarma after the fall of Fussar's regime, noting that the Cuban Navy's involvement left room for international conflict.
Other media and legacy
Hercule appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2. He was not among the characters described in the details Rockstar gave The Hollywood Reporter in September 2018, which covered members of the Van der Linde gang. Guarma cannot be visited in Red Dead Online, and Hercule does not appear there. According to the Red Dead Wiki, players of the online mode can set a flag bearing the Guarma coat of arms as their camp flag after reaching rank 59 of the third Outlaw Pass.
Trivia
In "Welcome to the New World", the mission fails if the player attacks or kills Hercule or Leon Fuentes.
He switches to French several times during the fighting: a few exclamations, an order to move on and an insult aimed at the soldiers.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the second part of the score track "Welcome to the New World" plays when the castaways break free and fight the soldiers with Hercule's help.
In "Hell Hath No Fury", Hercule reloads the cannon between Arthur's shots, but according to the Red Dead Wiki the shells visible in the magazine never move.
The Red Dead Wiki notes that Hercule runs very fast on the way back up to the roof of Cinco Torres, along a path with sharp turns and drops, and that the player can easily lose sight of him.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the ship's captain can already be seen among the men following Hercule after the first fight of "Welcome to the New World", before the story introduces him.
The Red Dead Wiki mentions a random encounter on Guarma in which rebels are chased by Fussar's soldiers.
