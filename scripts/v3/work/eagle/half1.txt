
Eagle Flies is the son of Rains Fall, chief of the Wapiti, a fictional Native American people, in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. His elder brother and his mother were killed before the story begins. Where his father seeks a peaceful settlement over the tribe's reservation, Eagle Flies predicts war and wants to fight the Army. In 1899 he helps Arthur Morgan steal a report on the oil under the reservation from the Cornwall refinery, then turns to raids against the Army with the support of Dutch van der Linde, who sees in the conflict a distraction for the gang.
Captured after an ambush on an Army patrol, he is freed from Fort Wallace by Arthur and Charles Smith. He then leads an attack on the Heartland Oil Fields against his father's wishes, saves Arthur there and is shot by Colonel Henry Favours; he dies in his father's tent on the reservation. Eagle Flies is played by Jeremiah Bitsui.
Key facts
In "American Fathers I", he meets Arthur outside the Saint Denis courthouse with his father; in "American Fathers II" he guides Arthur into the Cornwall Kerosene & Tar refinery to steal the Leland Oil Development Company report, and pays him.
In "A Rage Unleashed", he asks the gang for help after the Army seizes the tribe's horses; with Dutch, Arthur, Charles and Paytah he takes them back from an Army boat near Van Horn.
In "Favored Sons", an ambush on an Army patrol in Cumberland Forest ends in a battle and his capture; in "The King's Son", Arthur and Charles free him from Fort Wallace.
In "My Last Boy", he ignores his father's plea and leads the attack on the Heartland Oil Fields; he saves Arthur, is shot by Colonel Favours, and dies in his father's tent on the reservation.
He is played by Jeremiah Bitsui, known for the role of Victor in Breaking Bad, who provided his voice and motion capture.

Biography
Origins
Red Dead Redemption 2 gives no birth date and no age for Eagle Flies. He is the son of Rains Fall, chief of the Wapiti, a Native American people living on a reservation in the Grizzlies East, in the state of Ambarino. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he is the chief's second son, and the tribe was moved onto the reservation at some point before 1899. His father gives the family's history himself in 1899: the elder son had his head smashed by a drunken soldier, and the chief's wife had her throat slit. The wiki states that both were killed by the United States Army. Its French edition places these deaths shortly before 1899; the English edition dates them only to some point before that year. By 1899, Eagle Flies is his father's only surviving child.
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 1: Colter
In "Eastward Bound", the gang leaves Colter for Horseshoe Overlook. A wheel comes off the wagon driven by Arthur Morgan and Hosea Matthews, and Charles Smith stays behind to help. While they refit it, three riders watch them from a cliff. Charles remarks that the riders would not have shown themselves if they wanted trouble, and Hosea says that white settlers treated the people of the region badly. The Red Dead Wiki identifies the three riders as Eagle Flies, his father and Paytah, a Wapiti warrior close to Eagle Flies. Rains Fall later confirms the sighting to Arthur in Saint Denis: he says he saw him with the wagon train crossing the river at Cumberland Falls.
Chapter 4: Saint Denis
In "The Gilded Cage", Eagle Flies and his father attend the garden party of Henri Lemieux, the mayor of Saint Denis. From a balcony, Angelo Bronte points out the guests to Dutch van der Linde and Arthur. He mocks the two Wapiti as they hand a letter to the mayor, saying that people stupid enough to be tricked by the Americans get what they deserve. The next morning, Dutch tells Arthur that the writer Evelyn Miller is in town lobbying officials for the chief, and suggests the gang might help.
In "American Fathers I", Miller stops Arthur outside the Saint Denis courthouse and introduces him to Rains Fall and his son. The chief explains that his people are to be moved again. Eagle Flies says this will mean war; his father answers that the Wapiti are now too weak to fight. Miller explains that prospectors have reported large oil reserves under the reservation to Leviticus Cornwall and to the state government, and that he needs proof. When Arthur asks how much the job pays, Eagle Flies remarks that all white men are mercenaries. Arthur laughs and answers that he is wanted in two states and that the government likes him no better than it likes them. Rains Fall promises to pay him well and sets a meeting with his son near Citadel Rock, west of the oil fields, a couple of days later. As the group heads to an appointment with officials, Eagle Flies calls it a waste of time; his father insists that they must try everything.
In "American Fathers II", Eagle Flies is the mission giver. He waits for Arthur on a hill above Cornwall Kerosene & Tar, the refinery of the Heartland Oil Fields. He tells him that the foreman, Danbury, keeps the files in an office above the refinery floor, and that they should include a report from the Leland Oil Development Company. He suggests crawling under the fence or hiding in one of the wagons that keep rolling in, and offers to come if Arthur calls; Arthur reminds him that the plan was to keep him out of it. Arthur rides in under a wagon's cover in the rain, forces Danbury to hand over the file, then slips while climbing out of the window and lands among guards.
An oil well explodes before the guards can take him. The mission page states that Eagle Flies set off the blast. He calls Arthur over, and the two men shoot their way out of the refinery and ride off. Once clear, Eagle Flies says he enjoyed watching some of the oil burn and teases Arthur, who had promised to get in and out quietly. Arthur hands over the papers and thanks him for saving his life. Eagle Flies admits he does not know what to hope for, says the documents may be of some use, and pays him; the mission page gives the reward as 125 dollars. After this mission, according to the wiki's Camp Events page, Charles tells Arthur that Eagle Flies has asked him to rescue some Native children from a reform school; Charles turns down Arthur's offer of help.
The press covers the conflict the same year. "Tensions High", an article of the Blackwater Ledger (No. 69), says that the Wapiti of the reservation remain warlike, "spurred on by their chief and his son", and that the officers of nearby Fort Wallace consider armed hostilities a distinct possibility.
Chapter 5: Guarma
While Arthur, Dutch and others are stranded on Guarma, Charles spends time near the reservation. In "That's Murfree Country", on the way to Beaver Hollow, he tells Arthur that he ran into Rains Fall and Eagle Flies and has been helping where he can, and that things are bad there.
Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow
In "A Rage Unleashed", Eagle Flies rides into Beaver Hollow escorted by Karen Jones and Javier Escuella. Arthur introduces him to Dutch; Eagle Flies explains that Arthur and Charles once did him a great favor by posing as mercenaries. Asked about his father, he says Rains Fall has confused wisdom with weakness. The Army regiment at Fort Wallace has seized the tribe's horses: without them his people cannot hunt and will starve, and he calls it another act of war. He offers money. Charles reminds him that he told his father he would not fight over some horses; Dutch, who made no such promise, tells him to keep his money and agrees on the spot. Arthur and Charles go along to keep things from getting out of hand.
On the ride, Eagle Flies describes Colonel Henry Favours as a vile man whose regiment enjoys persecuting the tribe: the young have been taken from the reservation and sent to reform schools, along with many women, and medicine and supplies are deliberately withheld from the sick elders. Near Van Horn they meet Paytah, who has canoes ready. At night the group rows to the Army boat, boards it and overpowers the soldiers. Dutch crashes the boat into the rocks, and the horses are swum ashore; Eagle Flies rounds up four and asks Arthur to bring in the rest. On the bank he says his father does not want to fight again. Dutch rides off with him to return the horses and plans some reconnaissance at Fort Wallace. When Charles points out that Rains Fall called fighting an impossible gamble, Eagle Flies replies that his father need not know.
Charles then asks Arthur to talk to Rains Fall. In "Archeology for Beginners", if the player accepts, the chief reproaches Arthur for the raid with his son, whom he calls an impetuous prince. He says he is a great disappointment to his son, who sees glory in death, and that his son is easily led. Arthur warns him that Dutch does not have Eagle Flies's interests at heart, and afterwards writes in his journal that he would like to stop Dutch from pushing the young man into something foolish. In "The Fine Art of Conversation", Rains Fall tells Arthur and Charles that his son has too much of a mind of his own. According to the mission page, if "Favored Sons" has just been played, Charles is angry on the way that Dutch got Eagle Flies arrested.
In "Favored Sons", Eagle Flies, Dutch, Paytah and a group of Wapiti warriors wait on a clifftop in Cumberland Forest. The plan is to fell trees with dynamite, trap an Army patrol in the valley, then tar and feather the soldiers; Eagle Flies assures Arthur that nobody will be killed. While Arthur and Dutch lay the charges, Eagle Flies keeps watch with his spotter, and the patrol turns out larger than expected. After the blast, he orders the trapped soldiers to drop their guns and says their humiliation of his people has gone on long enough. More soldiers arrive and the shooting starts. Eagle Flies remarks that his father will not be happy, then has the dead searched for anything that could help the tribe's case.
A cannon opens fire. Eagle Flies runs to Paytah, who has been hit, and finds him alive. When cavalry rides out from Fort Wallace, Arthur and Dutch flee on horseback without him; they escape by jumping from a cliff into a river. On the bank, Dutch says Eagle Flies must have been taken, and Arthur adds that he may be dead. The capture itself is not shown. The Red Dead Wiki says he was seized while trying to help the wounded Paytah, and that Paytah got away. In "The King's Son", Rains Fall tells Arthur that the Army holds his son and will hang him for treason.
In "The King's Son", Arthur decides to free him despite the chief's refusal. On the ride, Charles says Dutch saw that Eagle Flies was burning hot and fanned the flames. That night, Arthur and Charles get into Fort Wallace and find him strung up in a cell, trading insults with a guard; Charles kills the guard, and Arthur takes the keys and cuts him loose. Eagle Flies asks whether his father sent them to kill guards, and says sarcastically that he enjoys being tortured. The alarm sounds. He leads them to a hole in the wall he saw when he was brought in, but it has been repaired, and Arthur blows a new breach. Eagle Flies escapes on Charles's horse, Taima, seated behind him, then the three go down the river in the canoes Charles left there. Under fire, Eagle Flies vows revenge on the Army and asks for a gun.
On the shore, Eagle Flies says he heals fast and will be fine in a day or so. He expects Favours to answer the rescue with more trouble, and says even his father may have to fight. When Arthur suggests the tribe should simply run, he asks where they would go. He thanks Arthur, and Charles takes him back to his father on a horse left behind by a soldier.
In "My Last Boy", Eagle Flies rides into Beaver Hollow at the head of armed warriors in war paint. He tells Dutch and Arthur that the Army tried to kill his people for oil and asks the gang to ride with them against the factory. Rains Fall arrives behind them, tells his son about his own wars and his dead family, begs him not to die for pride and, as his chief, implores him. Eagle Flies answers that his father's words mean nothing to him and leaves with his men for the Cornwall refinery. Rains Fall then asks Arthur for help, which reveals to Dutch that Arthur has been seeing the chief. Arthur agrees, followed by Charles, then Dutch and most of the gang; Micah Bell and his two recruits stay behind. On the way, a wounded warrior tells them that Eagle Flies needs help.
At the Heartland Oil Fields, Charles spots Eagle Flies fighting on a walkway. Arthur, Charles and Sadie Adler ride down with a few Wapiti to reach him while Dutch attacks from another side. Arthur kills a soldier grappling with him. Eagle Flies thanks them, asks where his men are, and insists that every warrior they find must be saved; he joins Arthur's group and calls out when Paytah is in danger. Once both groups have regrouped and most of the soldiers are dead, Dutch sends the others away, but Eagle Flies refuses to leave until he has found all his men, and Charles stays with him. Dutch and Arthur go into the warehouse for the state bonds Dutch is after.
On the way out, a pipe bursts and sprays steam into Arthur's face. Soldiers close in, Arthur calls for help, and Dutch walks away. Eagle Flies bursts in and kills the soldiers, but as he helps Arthur up, Colonel Favours shoots him in the abdomen. Arthur kills Favours before he can fire again. Arthur scolds Eagle Flies for the risk he took; Eagle Flies answers that Arthur saved his life more than once and that giving his own in return is as it should be. Outside, Arthur accuses Dutch of leaving him to die. He puts Eagle Flies on his horse and rides to the reservation with Charles and Paytah. On the way Eagle Flies asks to go back for the bodies of his men, and Paytah tells him to save his breath.
At the reservation, Rains Fall asks for his son to be brought to him. Arthur and Charles carry Eagle Flies into his father's tent, where he dies after a last moment with Rains Fall. The Red Dead Wiki's page on Eagle Flies says he dies in his father's arms. Outside, Charles tells Arthur that the Wapiti must move and that he will stay to help them. Later in the chapter, on the way to rescue Abigail Marston in the "Red Dead Redemption" mission, Arthur lists to Sadie the people Dutch has used and lost, and adds Eagle Flies to the list as another angry fool Dutch manipulated.
Epilogue (1907)
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the Wapiti buried Eagle Flies near the reservation before leaving it. The English wiki places his grave on the western side of Donner Falls and says it can be found from the epilogue onward; the French edition places it east of the falls. A gallery caption on the English wiki shows John Marston visiting the grave in 1907. The 1907 newspaper article "Pump Jacks Sit Silent" (Blackwater Ledger No. 72) reports that the tribe went on the run after its attacks on the Army and the battle at the Cornwall factory, that many of its members were gunned down in Wyoming and that a few are believed to have reached Canada. The same article says the oil near the reservation proved minimal and that drilling has stopped.
During the epilogue, John Marston can meet Rains Fall at Annesburg station. This encounter has no transcript; according to the Red Dead Wiki, the chief has come back from Canada for a while to mourn his son, whose death he has not gotten over, before taking the train north again.
Epilogue 2: Beecher's Hope
The last scene of the ending credits that follow "American Venom" shows Rains Fall alone on a summit of the Grizzlies East. He watches an eagle fly away and wipes his eyes. The Red Dead Wiki's page on the credits ties the scene to the loss of his last son.
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of September 6, 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang and do not include Eagle Flies, and no official guide text on him has been found. In the game he is the Wapiti who argues for fighting the Army. At the courthouse in Saint Denis he predicts that a new removal will lead to war, and he calls the family's appointment with a senator a waste of time. His father sees him differently from himself: "My son thinks there is glory in death," Rains Fall tells Arthur, and he also says that his son "has a mind of his own... too much so". Eagle Flies sets a limit before the patrol ambush ("Nobody will be killed"), and in captivity and in battle his concern is for his men: at the oil fields he will not leave until he has found them all.
He is wary of the gang at first. When Arthur asks how much the job will pay, he tells his father: "Told you, they're all mercenaries." His view changes after the refinery: when Arthur introduces him to Dutch, he says that Arthur and Charles did him a great favor. In the Fort Wallace cell and in the canoe he answers with dry jokes, and he asks for a gun to help while still hurt. Arthur sums him up for Rains Fall in "The King's Son": "He's very brave... very angry. He's me."
In his own words
When Arthur asks after his father in "A Rage Unleashed": "Father has confused wisdom with weakness. His people, my people... we've suffered too much... been lied to too much... now, they've taken our horses." On the seizure: "Colonel Favours is a liar and a murderer... his people won't stop until we're all dead. Without horses, we cannot hunt... without hunting... we will starve. This is another act of war." On the ride, about Favours: "He's a vile man. He and his regiment take pleasure in persecuting us. All the young have been taken from our reservation, shipped off to reform schools."
To a guard outside his cell at Fort Wallace: "No, I think the snake was full up after eating your brain." When Arthur asks if he is all right: "Sure... I enjoy being tortured... clears the mind." In the canoe: "They'll pay for this. They'll wish they killed me when they had a chance." On the bank: "I'll be fine in a day or so. I heal fast." When Arthur suggests the tribe run away: "Where would we go?" At the oil fields: "Any men we see... we must save them." Wounded, on the ride back: "Our men... we must go back for the bodies..."
Names
Eagle Flies is the English form of his name; his name in his own language is never given. Evelyn Miller introduces him to Arthur as the chief's son. His father calls him "the impetuous Prince" in front of Arthur, "my last son" and "My only boy... my precious boy" at Beaver Hollow. Dutch and Arthur address him as "son", and on the ride to the reservation Arthur calls him "this boy" and "my friend". Paytah calls him "brother". The French-language Red Dead Wiki names him Aigle-Qui-Vole.
Appearance
He is a young man with black hair, whose age is never given; Rains Fall speaks of what he himself did "when I was your age". In Saint Denis and at the refinery he wears his hair tied back. When he rides into Beaver Hollow for the attack on the oil fields, his hair is loose and his face is painted for war (see the gallery).
