
Alberto Fussar is the governor of Guarma, a Caribbean island, in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. He holds the rank of colonel and runs the island's sugar plantation from his compound at Aguasdulces, with his overseer Levi Simon. He sells the island's sugar to Leviticus Cornwall under an exclusive purchase order. In chapter 4 he is a guest at the mayor's party in Saint Denis, where Angelo Bronte points him out to Dutch and Arthur. In chapter 5 the gang is shipwrecked on Guarma; his soldiers capture Javier Escuella, and he tries to keep the castaways on the island. Arthur kills him with a cannon shot as the gang fights its way to the ship.
He is the main antagonist of the Guarma chapter. He is played by Alfredo Narciso.
Key facts
He governs Guarma as a colonel and runs its sugar plantation; the rebel Leon Fuentes describes its workers as little more than slaves brought from other islands.
He sells his sugar to Leviticus Cornwall, who called him a great friend of America in the newspaper article "Guarma Island Sugar Boom".
In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", his soldiers drag the captured Javier into Aguasdulces, where he questions him before Dutch and Arthur free him.
He calls in a warship from Cuba and sets up gun batteries to keep the gang from leaving the island.
In "Paradise Mercifully Departed", he escapes the cabin standoff in which Levi Simon dies, then opens fire from a tower over the harbor; Arthur brings it down with a cannon shot.

Biography
Origins
The games give no date or place of birth for Alberto Fussar and say nothing of his early life or of how he came to power. By 1899 he governs Guarma, a Caribbean island that Dutch van der Linde places second to the east of Cuba, and he holds the rank of colonel: the smuggler Hercule Fontaine calls him "Colonel Fussar". His seat is Aguasdulces, which Hercule calls Fussar's compound. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the soldiers stationed on Guarma belong to the Cuban military and serve under his command, guarding the plantations and putting down the uprising.
Sugar is the island's business. Fussar's overseer, Levi Simon, tells the castaways that they run the third most productive sugar plantation in the Northern Caribbean, and that his employer has many powerful friends. The rebel Leon Fuentes describes the plantation workers as little more than slaves brought in from other islands, worked until they die so that Fussar grows rich. Hercule adds that Leon wants the population to rise against Fussar, but that most people are too frightened. According to the Red Dead Wiki, those who oppose the governor are tortured and put to death, and the harshness of his rule is what set off the rebellion on the island.
His sugar sells in the United States. The article "Guarma Island Sugar Boom" (Blackwater Ledger No. 67, New Hanover Gazette No. 32, Saint Denis Times No. 48), published in chapter 4, runs under the subheading "CUBAN IMPORTS ON THE RISE". It reports that Leviticus Cornwall made two trips to the region to tour sugar plantations and signed an exclusive purchase order with its governor, whom he called "a great friend of America". The same article gives the other side: "Detractors call Fussar a controversial figure who is flamboyant at times and cruel at others and has Guarma under a quasi-military rule." It adds that "His security force is reportedly feared across the island, with plantation workers often working 14 hour shifts to keep up."
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 4: Saint Denis
Fussar's first appearance is at the garden party given by Mayor Henri Lemieux at his mansion in Saint Denis, in "The Gilded Cage". Angelo Bronte, who has invited the gang, receives Dutch and Arthur Morgan on a balcony and runs through the guests for them. After the mayor, he picks out Fussar. Bronte says that Fussar owns a sugar plantation on the island and comes to the city to indulge himself, and Dutch laughs with him. Fussar says nothing in the scene. Bronte moves on to Hobart Crawley, to Rains Fall and Eagle Flies, and to the newspaperman Hector Fellowes.
At the end of chapter 4, after the failed robbery of the Saint Denis bank, Dutch, Arthur, Micah Bell, Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella escape on a ship that sinks in a storm. The five men wash up on Guarma, Fussar's island.
Chapter 5: Guarma
Fussar is first a name. In "Welcome to the New World", soldiers led by Levi Simon chain the castaways together on the beach. Simon presents himself as Fussar's senior overseer and speaks of labor troubles and of Haitian pirates who will soon be hanged in the streets. Dutch gives a false name and Simon promises to free the five men once he knows who they are. Hercule Fontaine's rebels ambush the column. Javier is shot in the leg and taken, while the others escape with Hercule, who warns them that Fussar will be desperate to find them. He points out Aguasdulces and the factory where the island's cane is processed. Fussar's men are hunting a group of workers who escaped into the jungle the day before; in "Savagery Unleashed", Arthur and Leon Fuentes save some of them from the gallows.
He appears in person in "A Kind and Benevolent Despot". On the way through the caves to Aguasdulces, Arthur asks whether Fussar was not one of the men Bronte showed them at the party in Saint Denis, and Dutch agrees that he knew the name. At the top of the ladder, the two men see Fussar arrive with his soldiers and Simon, dragging the captured Javier behind a donkey. Fussar asks Javier who he is; Javier answers that he comes from Mexico and has never met the other men. Fussar mocks him in front of his soldiers, one of whom punches Javier in the face, then leaves him with a parting jibe about Mexicans.
While Dutch and Arthur creep around the compound, Fussar can be heard addressing his men in Spanish. He tells them he has just been in America, describes how strikers are dealt with there, and holds it up as the order he wants on Guarma. Meanwhile his soldiers threaten Javier with broken kneecaps to make him talk, and Javier keeps repeating that he knows nothing; according to the Red Dead Wiki, he never gives the gang away. He is held in a cage. Dutch and Arthur kill the guards inside the refinery, spill the sugar from its sacks and set off the furnace. The building explodes, they take Javier from his cage and fight their way out through the cane fields. Fussar is not seen again in the mission.
In "Hell Hath No Fury", the gang joins Hercule at the old fort of Cinco Torres. Hercule tells Dutch that Fussar now knows who they are and what price is on their heads. If they can silence him, Hercule says, he can get them away before anyone arrives from America; but Fussar has called in the navy from Cuba, and no boat can leave for now. A warship appears in the bay and lands soldiers below the fort. When one of its shells falls among its own men, Dutch thanks Fussar aloud. Arthur asks how many men Fussar has; Hercule answers that the number does not matter, because Fussar has the money to hire more. Arthur sinks the warship with the fort's cannon, and Hercule announces that a boat is on its way.
At the start of "Paradise Mercifully Departed", Micah comes back from scouting with bad news: Fussar knows exactly who they are and wants to keep them on the island for a few more days. His men hold the ship's captain, and gun positions cover the harbor so that any boat trying to sail will be shot to pieces. Hercule declares that he will fight Fussar every day he can. Bill stays on the ship with the recovering Javier, while Dutch, Arthur, Micah and Hercule blow up the coastal guns with dynamite and fight their way into Aguasdulces, where the captain is held in a blue building.
Inside the cabin, Simon is lying in wait; he knocks Arthur to the floor and holds him at gunpoint. Dutch walks in and aims at Simon. Fussar then walks in, revolver drawn, and tells them they are going to die; Dutch draws his second revolver to cover him, and Arthur aims at Fussar from the floor. Fussar warns that the United States Navy is on its way. Dutch answers that this is exactly why Fussar will let them leave, or die where he stands. Arthur pushes a rifle lying on the floor over to the captain, who shoots Simon in the chest and kills him. Fussar fires a few shots and runs out of the cabin; moments later Dutch asks whether he has escaped.
The group runs for the ship through Fussar's soldiers. Near the port, Hercule spots Fussar at the top of a tower, where he loads a cannon and opens fire on them. Hercule sends Arthur to a second cannon on the ground, further ahead on the left; according to the Red Dead Wiki, Hercule draws Fussar's fire so that Arthur can reach it. Arthur fires at the tower and blows its top apart, killing Fussar. According to the mission page, Arthur can also kill him with a gunshot instead of the cannon. Arthur remarks that he got Fussar for Javier. The captain takes the five gang members on board, Hercule says the rebels will probably disappear back to Haiti, and the ship sails for America. Fussar dies in 1899.
Back in the United States, a later issue of the papers (Blackwater Ledger No. 68, New Hanover Gazette No. 33, Saint Denis Times No. 49) carries the article "Leader of Guarma Assassinated", headed "ALBERTO FUSSAR DEAD." and "BELOVED LEADER CUT DOWN." It states that "Reports conflict as to the details of his demise, though early assumptions are that it was part of an attempted coup." It reports that American business leaders "speak warmly of Mr. Fussar as a kind and effective leader who brought peace and prosperity to the region, supported workers' rights, and took a particular interest in the lot of women on the island." The killing was first blamed on a revolt of the plantation workers, but "reports have begun to reach the mainland that several white men were involved in the assassination", and some blame a band of socialist insurrectionists. Some locals, according to the article, say tensions had grown as working conditions worsened under American pressure for cheaper sugar.
After his death: the epilogue (1907)
Fussar is mentioned once more in the newspapers of the epilogue period (Blackwater Ledger No. 74, New Hanover Gazette No. 39, Saint Denis Times No. 55). The article "Unrest in the Tropics", headed "COST OF FOOD, FUEL ANGERS CITIZENS.", describes soaring prices and shortages from Barbados to Cuba. It recalls "the assassination of prominent sugar industry advocate and Guarman governor Alberto Fussar in 1899". It reports that, shortly afterwards, American banking institutions, JD McKnight and Co. among them, took control of the sugar industry on several islands, Guarma included. Fields that once fed the islanders were turned over to sugar cane, so that all food has to be imported at great expense. The foreign investors hire locals to guard their plantations, "subjecting workers to harsh conditions and penalties". According to the Red Dead Wiki, McKnight's company also took over many of Cornwall's businesses.
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang and do not include Fussar. The in-game newspaper article "Guarma Island Sugar Boom", published in chapter 4, reports that his detractors call him "a controversial figure who is flamboyant at times and cruel at others", and that he holds Guarma "under a quasi-military rule". After his death, "Leader of Guarma Assassinated" reports that American business leaders describe him as "a kind and effective leader who brought peace and prosperity to the region". The same article says they saw him as "a progressive leader and humanitarian" who supported workers' rights.
The people of Guarma describe him otherwise. Asked by Dutch who Fussar is, the rebel Leon Fuentes answers: "A tyrant, driven by nothing but power and greed. Our only purpose is to work ourselves to death... to make him rich." In Saint Denis, Angelo Bronte says the governor "comes here to whore and despoil himself". Before the last attack on Aguasdulces, Micah says: "I like the man's style... he's thorough, nasty and vindictive." When Fussar opens fire from the tower, Arthur calls him "This goddamn idiot".
On Guarma, Fussar addresses his soldiers in Spanish and speaks English to his prisoner and to the gang. He questions a captive in front of his troops and turns to them for their verdict. In a speech to his men, he holds up the United States as a model of order (see In his own words). In the cabin standoff, he is the one who breaks away and runs (see Biography).
In his own words
No transcript of his scenes is published on the Red Dead Wiki; the lines below are checked against a fan transcript of the game and against the wiki's quote list. His lines to the soldiers are spoken in Spanish and quoted here from the English subtitles. To Javier, who claims he never met the other castaways, he says: "Mr. Simon doesn't believe you... even the mule doesn't believe you." To his troops at Aguasdulces: "Men... I was just in America... do you know how they deal with striking workers there? Do you know? They shoot them! That is civilization. That is future. That is law and order and that is what we must bring to this place. And this is how we will deal with them here... our duty is to be fierce." A little later, in the same speech: "...cowards are the worst traitors of all, for they don't mean their betrayal, but it is betrayal all the same."
Names
His full name, Alberto Fussar, is given by Bronte at the party and by the newspapers. Hercule calls him Colonel Fussar. His overseer Levi Simon, Dutch and Micah say Mr. Fussar; Micah adds a sarcastic "Nice Mr. Fussar". The newspapers call him the governor of Guarma. The Red Dead Wiki lists Colonel Fussar and Mr. Fussar as his aliases.
Appearance
Fussar has dark hair combed straight back, a curled, waxed mustache and stubble. At the mayor's party he wears a uniform decorated with medals. On Guarma he wears a blue vest over a white shirt, with a watch chain and a sabre at his side.
