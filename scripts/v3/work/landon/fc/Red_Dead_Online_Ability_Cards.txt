'''Ability Cards''' are special cards in [[Red Dead Online|''Red Dead Online'']] that provide the [[Red Dead Online protagonist|''Red Dead Online'' protagonist]] with attributes that affect the damage they deal, and how it is dealt.

== Description ==
There are four slots available for abilities: 1 slot for Dead Eye, granting an ability activated during [[Dead Eye]], and 3 slots for Passive Abilities, which affect the player persistently. There is '''one Active slot''', and '''three Passive slots''' to fill.   

The Dead Eye slot unlocks at Rank 2. The three passives unlock at ranks 10, 20, and 40.    

Abilities can be found in the Abilities section of the Pause menu or accessed via the Weapon Wheel.

While ranking up, Ability Cards will unlock and become available to buy with cash. Similarly, upgrade the cards already owned by unlocking upgrades from gaining XP. Each card has three tiers to unlock, greatly enhancing the abilities whilst increasing in price. The 3rd tier gives the best stats for the card.

For all cards it costs '''$50''' to buy (except the player's first Dead Eye card), '''$350''' to upgrade to Level 2, and '''$500''' to upgrade to level 3.

== Passive Ability Cards  ==
There are three different types of Passive Ability Cards: Combat Abilities, Recover Abilities, and Defense Abilities. Passive Card effects are always working while in the player's loadout.

* The 1st Passive Ability Card slot unlocks at Rank 10
* The 2nd Passive Ability Card slot unlocks at Rank 20
* The 3rd Passive Ability Card slot unlocks at Rank 40
