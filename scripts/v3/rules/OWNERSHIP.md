# Ownership rules: each fact lives in ONE place (binding for drafters, fact-checkers and fixers)

Merged from the FIX_RULES files of Bill, Abigail, Jack, Javier and Uncle (Oct 2026). Part letters:
A = Biography; B = Personality (official descriptions, In his/her own words, Names, Appearance), Skills, Relationships;
C = Missions, Quotes, Trivia, Other media; D = base (facts grid, intro, essentials), Development and performance, Reception.

## Who owns what
- **Narrative events** (what happens in a mission) -> Biography (A). Relationships (B) refer to them in one short clause
  at most ("see Biography"), without retelling. The Missions table (C) keeps one line per mission.
- **Backstory told by the character himself in a mission** (Uncle's Ohio, wives, Africa; Javier's past) -> A Origins;
  B gets one clause at most.
- **Official descriptions** (Rockstar THR 6 Sep 2018, Official Guidebook, GOTY / Collector's guide) -> Personality (B) only,
  quoted there. A Origins does not quote them. C Marketing may say "Rockstar published character descriptions on
  6 Sep 2018" without quoting; D only points to them.
- **Names** (real names, aliases, nicknames, who uses them) -> B "Names" only (the facts grid may list them). A reputation
  quote about a name (e.g. Reyes on Javier) goes to Names.
- **Camp scenes** (Camp Events: Pearson punch, fish, Lenny "boy", Hosea revolver, Timbuktu, prayers, ...) -> the relevant
  Relationship entry (B). Not in Personality, not in Trivia; A keeps a pointer clause at most.
- **Quotes**: each line in exactly ONE place. Lines quoted in B "In his/her own words", Names or a relationship entry are
  NOT repeated in C Quotes (C picks other verified lines). Prefer Quotes (C) for mission lines and B for camp lines;
  the other place paraphrases without quotation marks. A always paraphrases.
- **Appearance**: 2-3 sentences in B (h4 inside Personality); no outfit catalogue (outfits = gallery captions). Physical
  details (height, scars, hat pin) live there and nowhere else.
- **Skills / gameplay** (companion activities, weapons, horses, bounty hunting, literacy, games played) -> B Skills;
  the Skills paragraph points to the Missions table rather than repeating it. The Skills block does not repeat the
  section name in its first heading.
- **Songs / music** -> one "Music" subsection only.
- **Trivia (C)** keeps only facts that appear nowhere else in the fiche. Before publishing, cut every trivia item the
  reports flag as told elsewhere.
- **Cut content** -> D (Development, cut-content paragraph) only, never in the biography.
- **Reception (D)** must not repeat Development's points (e.g. "seen as the main villain" once).
- **Undead Nightmare** (non-canon) -> its own h3 in A; never mixed into canon paragraphs, never linked to canon events.

## Lessons from the full-tier fiches
- Full and medium fiches generate many DUPLICATES between Development, Trivia, Quotes and Relationships: the fact-check
  must look for them explicitly (category DUPLICATE) and the fixer resolves them with the rules above.
- A fact shared with another fiche must be told the same way in both (same mission, same people, same wording of the
  hedge). If the new fiche proves the other one wrong, fix the other one too (EN + FR, HTML regenerated) and note it in
  the CLAUDE.md entry.
