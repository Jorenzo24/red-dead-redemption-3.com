
Colonel Agustin Allende is the governor of Nuevo Paraíso, a Mexican province, in Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911. He commands the army there in the name of President Ignacio Sanchez from his mansion in Escalera, and fights the rebellion led by Abraham Reyes. Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella live under his protection. Through his captain, Vincente de Santa, he promises to find them for John Marston in exchange for his help against the rebels, then has John led into a trap in Chuparosa.
John then joins Reyes. When the rebels take Escalera, Allende flees by stagecoach with Bill and offers him to John to save himself; both men die on the road, shot by John or by Reyes. He is voiced by Gary Carlos Cervantes.
Key facts
In "The Demon Drink", he meets John at his mansion in Escalera and sends him to fight the rebels at Tesoro Azul.
In "Mexican Caesar", De Santa offers John 20,000 pesos and Javier's whereabouts, on his behalf, to escort a munitions train.
In "Cowards Die Many Times", he tells John that Bill and Javier are prisoners in Chuparosa; it is a trap, and Reyes's rebels save John.
In "An Appointed Time", the rebels storm his mansion; he flees by stagecoach with Bill and dies on the road, shot by John or by Reyes.
The Blackwater Ledger reports his death under the headline "'Tyrannical' Governor Overthrown".

Biography
Origins
Red Dead Redemption gives no birth date for Agustin Allende in its dialogue. By 1911 he is a colonel of the Mexican Army and the governor of Nuevo Paraíso, the Mexican province across the border from New Austin. He serves the national government of General Ignacio Sanchez, who never appears on screen. He rules the province from Escalera, a town built in levels on a hillside. According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on the town, the market sits on the lowest level and Allende's mansion on the highest, with houses and shops in between.
Allende gives a few details on his family in "The Demon Drink". He tells John Marston that his people have lived and worked in the province for a hundred years. One of his uncles cultivated land in the region, and Javier Escuella's father, a drunk, worked on it as a laborer. The Red Dead Wiki gives another version of his background: it says he was born into a poor family. The wiki bases this on a remark by Abraham Reyes in "The Great Mexican Train Robbery" that does not appear in its own transcript of that mission. According to the same wiki, some townsfolk of Nuevo Paraíso also say that he was once a poor peasant.
The wiki also describes how he runs the province. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Allende takes land from the peasants and sells it to aristocrats, has those who complain beaten or killed, and makes the former owners work the same land for little or no pay. The wiki attributes this account to Luisa Fortuna in "Must a Savior Die?", but the line is missing from the transcript of that mission. The wiki adds that Javier Escuella works for Allende as a hired gun. Allende's men, for their part, describe their task as crushing a rebellion: by 1911, the rebels of Abraham Reyes are fighting the army across the province.
Red Dead Redemption (1911)
In 1911, agents of the Bureau of Investigation hold John Marston's wife and son to force him to hunt down his former companions Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella. Allende's name comes up before John even crosses the border. At the end of the first act, in "We Shall Be Together in Paradise", Irish suggests he try the provincial governor, a colonel based in Escalera whose Spanish name he cannot remember, and says he once played cards with him. In Chuparosa, in "The Gunslinger's Tragedy", Landon Ricketts tells John that Colonel Allende runs the region like a feudal king.
Act II: Nuevo Paraíso
John first approaches the government through Allende's officer, Captain Vincente de Santa. In "Civilization, at Any Price", John comes to the manor in Escalera and asks to speak to the commander. De Santa receives him instead. He calls Allende a good and strong man who carries the weight of a million problems, and dismisses what John has heard about the colonel as lies repeated like a parrot. He promises that the colonel will help John if John helps the army. The mission then has John escort a decoy train out of Chuparosa to lure the rebels into a trap.
John meets Allende in person at the start of "The Demon Drink". He finds him at his mansion in Escalera, berating De Santa in Spanish: he calls the captain a crybaby and accuses him of being ready to applaud the day his head ends up on a spike. When he notices John, he asks who he is. De Santa introduces him as the man who helped them defeat Reyes. Allende greets John as the bounty hunter, suggests he may have come to hunt him, since John's country loves to make trouble in his, and wonders aloud whether to have him dragged around town by a horse or thrown to the dogs. John answers that he is only here to bring two men to justice and that the colonel's politics are not his concern. Allende then asks De Santa, in Spanish, to find him more interesting company than the woman he brought him the night before.
John asks what Allende knows of the men he hunts. Allende tells him where Escuella comes from (see Origins) and says that men like Javier's father are natural allies for Reyes. He goes on about the local population: he says his family brought them civilization and God, that they despise his people for it, and that they need a ruler. When John says he is sorry to hear it, Allende replies that there is nothing to be sorry for: it is a fight between two forces, and force must be used to get one's way. He then asks John for a favor while he looks for the two men. John offers to help once the men are found. Allende answers that he is in no position to negotiate, and sends him to Tesoro Azul, where De Santa's men are fighting the rebels. As John leaves, Allende scolds De Santa for standing behind him.
On the ride, John remarks on the way Allende treats his own men and on his pressure to find young girls. De Santa answers that the colonel needs recreation like anyone else and has no time to court women. At Tesoro Azul, John, De Santa and Captain Espinoza kill the rebels in the village. Espinoza then comes back with a girl in tow, says he heard women crying in a house, and reminds the men that nobody touches them before Allende. When John asks whether all this was done to get women for Allende, Espinoza answers that it is only a bonus, and has him burn the houses with fire bottles. In "Empty Promises", De Santa takes John to Torquemada, an abandoned fort held by the rebels, under urgent orders from the colonel: to kill them all.
"Mexican Caesar" opens at the manor. De Santa and Quique Montemayor push two young women inside; one of them begs not to be forced. Allende greets the women in Spanish and says he loves patriots. He tells John that they will make a patriot of him yet. Asked about the two men, he says he has heard they ride with Reyes, and has De Santa explain the job: escort a train through rebel country, for 20,000 pesos and information on the whereabouts of Javier Escuella. During the conversation, a man chases a captive girl past them. John accepts. On the road, Espinoza tells John that he is Allende's right arm and one of the few men the colonel respects. He also says that Allende told him De Santa had praised him and asked for him to be given the assignment, which makes him suspect a trap. The convoy and the train are attacked by rebels, and John defends them with a Gatling gun until they reach the army's base camp. According to the mission page, Allende never pays the 20,000 pesos.
On the rebel side, Allende is described as the enemy. In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again", the army is after Luisa Fortuna, and Ricketts tells John that her looks are normally enough to interest Allende. In "The Mexican Wagon Train", Luisa reports that Allende is sending untried prisoners to their deaths, among them a writer named Castillo and a local official who refused to evict smallholders behind on their taxes. In "Must a Savior Die?", John rescues Abraham Reyes from a firing squad at El Presidio; the executioner tells Reyes, in Spanish, that he will pay for his treason and for wanting to overthrow Allende. Once freed, Reyes admits that he had taken John for the American working for Allende.
In "Cowards Die Many Times", De Santa brings John to Allende, who is fondling a woman on a bench on his patio. Allende tells the woman not to be so conventional, and says he will save her for later or kill her and her whole family, since they are probably rebels. He then welcomes John as a rare find, a gringo who is a friend of his country, and announces that the men John is hunting have been captured in Chuparosa. He tells John to ride there with De Santa and take possession of them, as his gift for John's help. John answers that he has a wife and son at home whom he misses. Allende replies that they all do, and tells De Santa to take care of him. On the way, John asks whether the colonel ever leaves his villa; De Santa answers that Allende is both military commander and governor of the province, and must be kept out of harm's way.
In Chuparosa, De Santa sends John into the church, where the prisoners are supposed to be held. Nobody is there. A soldier knocks John out, and De Santa's men prepare to execute him. Reyes and his rebels attack the town at that moment and save him, and John kills Captain Espinoza in the town hall. Bill and Javier were never in Chuparosa. The transcripts do not show who gave the order to kill John; according to the Red Dead Wiki, Allende had ordered De Santa to do it. After the battle, Reyes tells his rebels that they will fight on until local tyrants like Allende are gone, and that Allende will one day know justice. In "Father Abraham", John tells Luisa that Allende betrayed him and that he saw him do things that disgust even him.
In the same mission, Luisa tells John that her father was killed the day before. According to her, the army accused him of treason, cut out his heart and fed it to the dogs; she blames Allende for it, and says he then took the honor of two young girls. She adds that Allende knows the rebels are close to victory and has sent for reinforcements, and that Reyes asked in person for John to stop them before they reach Escalera. Luisa calls Allende pure evil and swears to cut his heart out one day. John and the rebels blow up the convoy.
Reyes gives John his own view of Allende in "The Great Mexican Train Robbery". He calls him a coward who beats the people he swore to protect and enjoys it, and describes him as a puppet of Sanchez, who controls everything he does. The army train that John and the rebels rob in Chuparosa carries supplies for Allende's forces, and Reyes says that Allende will pay a high price that day. In "Captain De Santa's Downfall", De Santa executes a rebel at El Sepulcro in the name of Allende's government, then dies there, shot by John or by the rebels depending on the player's choice. The rebel Victor tells John that Allende is scared and making mistakes, and that with Espinoza and De Santa dead, the army has no leaders and the rebels must move on Allende at once.
In "The Gates of El Presidio", Reyes tells John what his spies have learned: Bill and Javier paid Allende to hide them, and have been under his protection since John arrived in Mexico. On the wagon, Reyes says he often thinks of the day he will storm Allende's mansion in Escalera and sleep in his silk sheets. After the rebels take El Presidio, John catches Javier; depending on the player, he captures him or kills him. If Javier is captured, he tells John that Bill is not there, but with Allende.
"An Appointed Time" is the last mission of the second act. The army has executed rebels in Escalera and captured Reyes. John frees him after Luisa is shot dead by the soldiers and Major Raul Zubieta falls in a duel. The rebels then free prisoners from Allende's jail and storm the hill. John takes a machine gun while the rebels blow the gate open with gunpowder barrels, then holds the gate against soldiers coming up the slope. As the rebels chop down the door of the mansion, Allende calls out to Bill that they can still reach his stagecoach, and Bill tells him to go while he covers him. The two men flee in an armored stagecoach. According to the mission page, it is guarded by two riders, a gunner on the Gatling gun mounted at the back and a guard riding shotgun beside the driver. John and Reyes ride after them.
According to the mission page, the coach stops once John kills the driver or the horses. When Bill asks what he is doing, Allende answers that he is buying his freedom, calls him a dumb ape and orders him out of the wagon. He then comes out himself, calls on them not to shoot and offers Bill to John in exchange for his life, promising to leave the country. He pins Bill to the ground. While Bill and John trade insults, Allende says in Spanish, in the transcript's version, that he will sleep in the bed of the man he is speaking to that night; the transcript does not say whom he addresses. The player decides what happens next. John can shoot Allende, Bill or both; Reyes shoots whichever man John spares, and both men if John fires at neither. In the version given by the transcript, Reyes kills Allende, and Bill is shot by Reyes or by John as he reaches for Allende's gun. Allende dies on the road outside Escalera in 1911. Reyes then proclaims that Allende is dead and that Mexico is his.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the rebels then take over Escalera and replace the army as the law in the town. The Blackwater Ledger reports the news in issue No. 58, on sale after the mission, under the headline "'Tyrannical' Governor Overthrown. Is Sanchez Next?". The article opens: "The bloody rebellion in Mexico claimed its first high profile victim yesterday, with the killing of a state governor." It says Allende "was killed yesterday, following a fire fight with rebels", and adds: "Unsubstantiated reports suggest American mercenaries may have been fighting on both sides during the battle." It also describes the dead governor: "Colonel Allende, who had been implicated in several massacres and other atrocities in the region, was not a popular governor and locals cheered when they heard the news of his demise." Reyes, quoted in the same article, calls it a great victory that will lead all the way to the Presidential Palace in Mexico City. Two issues later, the paper reports the fall of Sanchez's government and Reyes's rise to power.
Personality
The Red Dead Wiki quotes the Game of the Year Edition guide of Red Dead Redemption: "This gentleman is a 53-year-old, sadistic, military leader of a local Mexican province. The province is in the midst of a rebellion. Many years ago, Allende was an idealist, but he's become a vicious cynic with a love of torture and sexual abuse. Takes pleasure in all manner of vices. He is intelligent, corrupt, and brutal."
On screen, he insults his officers in Spanish, in front of John. At their first meeting, he threatens to have John dragged around town by a horse or set upon by dogs, then gives him orders. He receives him on the terrace of his mansion in Escalera, with young women brought to him by his soldiers. He speaks of the people of the province with contempt. He is never seen near a fight: John remarks on it to De Santa, who answers that the governor must be protected and kept out of harm's way.
In his own words
To John, at their first meeting in "The Demon Drink": "Ah, perhaps you come to hunt me, eh? Your country loves to make trouble in mine." On his family and the province, in the same scene: "My people have lived and worked here for a hundred years. We brought civilization, and these people, these fucking monkeys, despise us." When John asks to be given the two outlaws first: "You are in no position to negotiate." Welcoming John back in "Cowards Die Many Times": "You know, you are a rare find. A gringo who is also a friend of our country."
Names
John, Reyes and the newspapers call him Colonel Allende or Allende; his soldiers say el Coronel. De Santa calls him "His Excellency, El Coronel" in "Mexican Caesar". Reyes calls him "una marioneta", a puppet, and "a perrito for General Sánchez", which he explains as a little dog that licks the general's feet. According to the captions of the Red Dead Wiki, two rebel posters call him the lapdog and the puppet of Sanchez. Irish, who claims to have played Three Card Stud with him, remembers only "Colonel something or other...some Spanish name". Wikipedia spells his first name Agustín.
Appearance
He is a man of about fifty with slicked-back black hair, a receding hairline, a curled handlebar mustache and a tuft of beard under the lip. He wears a black army uniform with a gold-embroidered collar, gold fringed epaulettes and three medals on the chest. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he and Major Raul Zubieta are the only soldiers in the game with medals on their uniforms.
