## Problems
1. IMPRECISE (Hosea entry). Draft: "plays cribbage with her and vouches for Arthur". Source: gsw l. 22863-22867. Hosea answers with a joke ("Some say he is as dumb as he looks... he's not quite that dumb"), not a recommendation. Fix: "and answers her jibe about Arthur with one of his own".
2. IMPRECISE (Dutch entry). Draft: "kills one of her sons in front of her and drags her outside". Source: gsw l. 24680-24694. Dutch drags her off the stairs, then "puts Catherine on his shoulder and carries her out". The Biography says "carries". Fix: "drags her from the stairs and carries her outside".
3. IMPRECISE (Leigh Gray entry). Draft: "the sheriff of Rhodes, a Gray, sent the gang to take her moonshine". Source: the Biography and the American Distillation page. Sheriff Gray deputized Dutch, Arthur and Bill to shut down a Braithwaite still, and Archibald handed them the liquor. Fix: "She knows that Sheriff Leigh Gray was behind the raid on her still, which left the gang with her moonshine (see In her own words)."
4. IMPRECISE (Bartholomew entry). Draft: "Her son greets Hosea and Arthur with a shotgun until she tells him to pay them, and insults Hosea at the cribbage meeting." Source: gsw l. 18400 and 22887 label the speaker only "Braithwaite". The shotgun and the lowered gun come from the wiki Bartholomew page alone, and "Pay the man" is addressed to her men in general. The Biography hedges the shotgun ("according to the Red Dead Wiki"). Fix: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, he is the son who points a shotgun at Hosea and Arthur until she has them paid, and the one who insults Hosea at the cribbage meeting."
5. IMPRECISE (Quotes 1 and 2). Draft: "Bartholomew Braithwaite to Hosea, who suggests selling the moonshine elsewhere" and "Bartholomew Braithwaite to Hosea, who asks how his mother will pay". Source: the transcript labels both lines "Braithwaite". Only the wiki quote list names Bartholomew. The third quote is hedged, these two are not. Fix: "A Braithwaite son (Bartholomew according to the Red Dead Wiki) to Hosea, ..." in both.
6. IMPRECISE (Gertrude entry). Draft: "Penelope tells Arthur that the family kept her there rather than send her to a sanatorium." Source: Gertrude page. This exchange is optional (only if Arthur takes Penelope to the outhouse), it is wiki-only, and the Biography hedges it. Here it is stated as a plain fact. Fix: "If Arthur stops at the outhouse with Penelope, she explains..." with wiki attribution (see also DUPLICATE 9).
7. UNSUPPORTED (Manor paragraph). Draft: "Inside, she receives her visitors in the living room". Source: the transcript says only "Ah, please come in, sir. This way, sir" (l. 22847). The Manor page lists a living room but does not place the cribbage scene there. Fix: "Inside the manor she receives Hosea and Sean for cribbage" (or cut, see DUPLICATE 6).
8. IMPRECISE (Reception, WhatCulture). Draft: "She comes after the Murfree Brood (fifth) and ahead of Colm O'Driscoll (seventh)." Source: wc_9/10/11.html. The list counts down from worst to best (15 to 1), so on the page she comes after Colm and before the Murfree Brood. Fix: "She is placed between Colm O'Driscoll (seventh) and the Murfree Brood (fifth)."
9. IMPRECISE (Trivia, Gareth). Draft: "a later update placed Bartholomew at the front of the house". Source: the Gareth page says only "This has since been fixed". No update is named. Fix: "this was later fixed, and Bartholomew now appears at the front of the house" (and see DUPLICATE 3).
10. IMPRECISE (Gallery caption 1). Draft: "Catherine Braithwaite at Braithwaite Manor." Source: wiki file caption and image (purple dress, front door, son with gun). This is her first meeting with Hosea and Arthur. Fix: "Catherine Braithwaite at her front door with her sons, in "Advertising, The New American Art"." Caption 2 ("Catherine inside the burning manor.") is CORRECT. Better alt text: "Catherine Braithwaite in a nightgown on the floor of her burning hall, Dutch on the stairs above". Cross-chunk note: the Appearance line (half1) mentions only the purple dress. During the raid she wears a cream nightgown (wiki "DutchPull" images), so add one clause there or leave it to the caption.
11. DUPLICATE (Quotes, Archibald). Draft: "Not to mention the fact that Catherine Braithwaite has a rather... expensive interest in thoroughbred horses to maintain." The same line is already quoted in Personality ("a rather... expensive interest in thoroughbred horses"). OWNERSHIP: other characters' descriptions are quoted in B only. Fix: cut from Quotes.
12. DUPLICATE (Quotes, Tavish). Draft: "That hag and her inbred sons... they've ruined this county. They killed my uncle, you know." Personality already quotes "that hag and her inbred sons". The Tavish entry and the Biography (Horse Flesh) paraphrase the uncle claim, so it appears 4 times. Fix: cut from Quotes. Keep the Personality quote, the Biography paraphrase and one short clause in the Tavish entry.
13. DUPLICATE (subtitle label Gareth/Bartholomew). It is told in the Biography (Blood Feuds paragraph), the Bartholomew entry ("the subtitles of that scene label him Gareth"), the Quotes source line ("subtitled Gareth") and Trivia (long item). Keep it in the Biography plus the short Quotes attribution. Cut the Bartholomew sentence and the Trivia item: OWNERSHIP says trivia only for facts found nowhere else.
14. DUPLICATE (horse brand). "According to the Red Dead Wiki, the horses the gang steals carry the Braithwaite insignia on their hindquarters" (estate, Horses) = "The horses stolen in "Horse Flesh for Dinner" carry the Braithwaite brand on their hindquarters, according to the mission page" (Trivia). Keep it in Horses with "according to the mission page", and cut the Trivia item.
15. DUPLICATE (Gertrude). The Gertrude entry repeats the Biography: Beau's line, the outhouse, Penelope and the sanatorium, the name only in the credits, the remains in 1907. Trivia "Only Arthur can record it: John ... cannot mark it" repeats the entry's "a point of interest only Arthur can discover". Fix: in the entry, keep Beau's line plus "see Biography". In Trivia, keep only the POI name "Braithwaites' Secret" and the Arthur/John detail. Drop "only Arthur can discover" from the entry.
16. DUPLICATE (Manor paragraph). 17. DUPLICATE (Moonshine paragraph). Archibald's stills "all over Lemoyne", the Raiders, "a dollar a bottle" and the letter calling Bronte her most valued customer are all already in the Biography (Origins and Advertising). Fix: shorten to one sentence with "see Biography", or move the Archibald facts here and cut them from Origins.
18. DUPLICATE (Kieran entry). Hosea's "Kieran saw a couple of fellers..." and the Shady Belle apology are both told in the Biography (A Short Walk paragraph). OWNERSHIP: camp scenes belong to Relationships. Keep the apology here and cut the apology sentence from the Biography.
19. DUPLICATE (Penelope entry). "she mentions what happened to her aunt and cousins and says the survivors ... are angrier than ever" repeats the Biography's chapter 6 paragraph. Fix: keep it in the Biography. In the entry, say "she elopes with Beau with Arthur's help (see Biography)".
20. DUPLICATE (Red Dead Online moonshine). The Bartholomew entry "He also appears in Red Dead Online, in the moonshine business storyline" and Origins ("the family has taken over the moonshine trade in Lemoyne with Danny-Lee Caton...") overlap with Other media (Moonshiners). Also, Origins mixes Red Dead Online facts into the canon backstory. Keep it in Other media. Cut it from Origins and reduce the Bartholomew sentence to "(see Other media)".
