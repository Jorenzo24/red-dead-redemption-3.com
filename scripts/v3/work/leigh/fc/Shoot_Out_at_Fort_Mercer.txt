{{Infobox Newspaper article|Title=Shoot Out at Fort Mercer|Image=|Newspaper=[[Blackwater Ledger No. 56]]|Last=|Next=}}'''Shoot Out at Fort Mercer''' is one of the [[Newspapers|newspaper articles]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

== Text ==
SHOOT OUT AT FORT MERCER
----Lawmen, gunmen and [[John Marston|outlaws]] were involved in a [[The Assault on Fort Mercer|bloody battle]] recently at the abandoned military base, [[Fort Mercer]] in [[Río Bravo|Rio Bravo]], [[New Austin]]. At the time of going to press events were still a little unclear, but it seems that this was the end of the [[Williamson gang]] that had terrified New Austin in recent months, not perhaps of [[Bill Williamson|Mr. Williamson]] himself, whose body has not be found and who is believed to have fled to Mexico. [[Leigh Johnson|Marshall Leigh Johnson]] who led the raid was commended for his bravery.
