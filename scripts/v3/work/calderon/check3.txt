Development and performance
Voice
In Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), Sister Calderón is voiced by Irene DeBari, according to the game's cast listings (English Voice-Over Wiki, GTABase) and the Red Dead Wiki. Wikipedia describes DeBari as an American television actress whose career began in 1967. She played Rosa Calletano, the wife of Lieutenant Ray Calletano, in Hill Street Blues, credited at the time as Irena Du Barry; she has also been credited as Irene De Bari and Irene Rosetti.
For Red Dead Redemption (2010) and Undead Nightmare, the English and French Red Dead Wikis also credit Irene DeBari, in a single field covering every game. The cast list of the 2010 game consulted for this page has no entry for the Mother Superior; it credits a role named "Nun" to Begonya Plaza, whom the Red Dead Wiki gives as the voice of the game's ordinary nuns. No source found for this page says whether the role also involved motion capture.
Cut content
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Red Dead Redemption was to give the Mother Superior a stranger mission of her own, known as rcm18, in which the player would find four missing relics and bring them back to her. The mission is not in the game, but its dialogue files remain in the game files. An unused item, the Sacred Relic, was intended for it.
For Red Dead Redemption 2, the same wiki reports that lines were planned for when the player hogties her with the lasso; this dialogue was cut from the final game. It also notes that she is the only nun in Red Dead Redemption 2, and that unused generic nun assets remain in the game files.
Reception
The Emerald Station scene
Game Rant devoted a feature to her meeting with Arthur, written by Christian Harrison and published on 6 March 2021 under the title "One Life-Changing Moment in Red Dead 2 Happens at a Train Station, and That's No Coincidence". The article retells the scene, in which Arthur is honest with himself for the first time about the life he has led. It compares it with the meeting that replaces it when Arthur's honor is low, calling Swanson's advice "a stark and nihilistic contradiction to Arthur's interaction with Mother Superior Calderón".
On 6 July 2026, RDR2.org reported, in an article by Ray Ampoloquio, that a high school graduate of the class of 2026 had built her commencement speech around the line the nun gives Arthur at the station. The site called the scene "one of its most quietly powerful" and "a small, unshowy exchange" in a game better known for bank robberies and shootouts.
Other media and legacy
According to the Red Dead Wiki, which treats the two nuns as one character, Calderón first appears in Red Dead Redemption (2010), as the Mother Superior of Las Hermanas, in the main game and in the Undead Nightmare expansion, and returns eight years later in its prequel, Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), as the Saint Denis nun, in a story set twelve years earlier. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, include the story and Undead Nightmare, and therefore her scenes.
The Red Dead Wiki lists her in no other game, Red Dead Online included.
Trivia
According to the Red Dead Wiki, she is the only minor or side character to appear in Red Dead Redemption, Red Dead Redemption 2 and Undead Nightmare.
She slips Spanish into her speech in both games: "Por favor" during the reading lesson in Saint Denis, and her thanks to John at Las Hermanas.
The title "Of Men and Angels" comes from the first verse of 1 Corinthians 13, according to the mission page.
The gold medal objective of "Brothers and Sisters, One and All" is to find the boy within 19 seconds. With "Help a Brother Out", it is one of only two stranger missions of Red Dead Redemption 2 that award gold medals.
During "Mother Superior Blues", she and five unnamed nuns are at Las Hermanas but take no part in its defense; with them, the settlement counts fourteen survivors instead of eight, according to the Red Dead Wiki. According to the mission page, if Las Hermanas is under attack when John returns, undead can appear in the background of the closing scene in the church.
