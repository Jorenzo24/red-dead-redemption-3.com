Relationships
John Marston
Ricketts watches John kill three bandits on his arrival in Chuparosa in 1911, trains him and rides with him through the four missions of his strand (see Biography). John tells him that stories about him were told when he was a boy, and Ricketts later tells him that all those stories were true. Ricketts calls him partner, Mr. Marston and, once, his sarcastic little apprentice. In "The Mexican Wagon Train", John tells him how he left the gang and why he is hunting its former members.
Their last ride ends with a handshake at the border. According to the Red Dead Wiki, John also mentions Ricketts during a poker game at camp in Red Dead Redemption 2.
Luisa Fortuna
Ricketts describes Emilio's sister to John as a teacher and a fine young woman, and says the army holds her because she is a rebel close to Reyes. He frees her from her cell in "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" and carries her out of the caves. In "The Mexican Wagon Train" she finds him drinking with John and asks for help with Allende's prisoners (see Biography). Before leaving, he tells John to talk to her.
Agustin Allende
At their first meeting Ricketts describes the colonel to John as the head of a foul local government who runs the region like a feudal king. Ricketts fights his soldiers to free Luisa and the prisoners of the convoy (see Biography).
Abraham Reyes
Ricketts never meets the rebel leader on screen. At his first meeting with John he speaks of another local man promising the peasants their freedom, like the two or three before him, without giving a name. In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" he names Reyes as the rebel leader Luisa is close to.
Javier Escuella
Ricketts helps John look for him in Chuparosa. In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" he relays John's questions about him to Emilio Fortuna, then asks Luisa whether a Javier Escuella was among her jailers, and concludes that Javier is still in Mexico. In "The Mexican Wagon Train" he has no news of him.
Bill Williamson
John names Bill to Ricketts as one of the two men he is after. In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again", Ricketts says he has heard that John's recent encounters with Bill did not go his way.
Irish
In "We Shall Be Together in Paradise", before leaving John in Mexico, Irish says he once met an American in Chuparosa, saw him shoot a man and drank with him. The game does not name this American; the Red Dead Wiki's transcript links him to Ricketts.
Jack Marston
Jack never meets Ricketts on screen, but his 1914 voice lines name him, among them, after a kill in Dead Eye: "Landon Ricketts, eat your heart out."
Sadie Adler
In "Further Questions of Female Suffrage" (Red Dead Redemption 2), after Sadie starts the gunfight with the Lemoyne Raiders, Arthur tells her: "A new pair of pants and you think you're Landon Ricketts".
His wife
The games do not name her. In "Lucky in Love", winning at the poker table, Ricketts says: "Thank God my wife died. Unlucky in love, lucky in cards." The Red Dead Wiki dates her death to before 1911, cause unknown.
Emilio Fortuna
Luisa's brother is talking with Ricketts at the hotel when John arrives in "Landon Ricketts Rides Again". He cannot say whether the Escuella he heard of is Javier (see Javier Escuella).
Carlos
Ricketts has been told that this butcher of El Matadero can help them reach Luisa. Carlos distracts the guards at the caves, leaves horses for the escape and takes Luisa away at the end (see Biography).
Andreas Müller
Ricketts plays poker with this German silver prospector at the Chuparosa cantina and teases him at the table. When Müller accuses John of cheating and draws, Ricketts tries to calm him down; John then kills him in a duel, and Ricketts says Müller always played his cards too early (see Biography).
Ramon
This man of Chuparosa runs to Ricketts when bandits attack the bank wagon in "The Gunslinger's Tragedy" and asks for his help "again". Ricketts calls him by name and promises to take care of it.
Gunfighting and weapons
What he teaches John
Ricketts gives the four missions of his strand in Red Dead Redemption, all starting in Chuparosa (see Missions). In the first, "The Gunslinger's Tragedy", he hands John a Schofield Revolver with the words "See if this Schofield makes a difference. Now that's a real gun." He tells him to keep his back straight, because a bent back makes the gun jump. The mission unlocks the third level of Dead Eye: the player marks three bottles, then at least two of the vultures Ricketts scares off the rooftops. The Schofield is the mission's reward, with the new Dead Eye level.
"Lucky in Love" introduces poker and dueling to the player. On the way to John's duel with Andreas Müller, Ricketts explains: "A duel is all about timing. If you pull your gun too soon, you'll be less accurate. After you draw, pick your shots carefully, like I showed you." In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" the player chooses between taking the train with him to Casa Madrugada and riding there; in the caves he sets the dynamite charge on Luisa's cell door while John covers him. The mission page notes that he is nearly invincible in that mission. Every mission of his strand fails if the player attacks or kills him; three of them also fail if the player abandons him or kills his horse.
Weapons
The Red Dead Wiki lists the Cattleman Revolver, the Schofield Revolver, the Volcanic Pistol, the Semi-automatic Pistol and the High Power Pistol among his weapons. Despite his praise for the Schofield, he is seen with a Cattleman Revolver in most scenes, the wiki notes. According to the mission pages, he sometimes uses a Semi-automatic Pistol in "The Gunslinger's Tragedy", and in the dynamite cutscene of "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" his Cattleman is sometimes replaced by a High Power Pistol or a Volcanic Pistol. The Red Dead Wiki also points out playing cards on his right thigh when the camera widens as John sits down at the poker table in "Lucky in Love".
Missions
Ricketts has a strand of four story missions in the second act of Red Dead Redemption, all starting in Chuparosa. The first becomes available after "We Shall Be Together in Paradise", the last mission of the first act. "The Gunslinger's Tragedy" gives John the Schofield Revolver and the third level of Dead Eye. "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" opens the strand of Luisa Fortuna. "Lucky in Love" introduces poker and duels, and unlocks the stranger mission "Eva in Peril". "The Mexican Wagon Train", which introduces the hijacking of vehicles, closes the strand and leads to the Abraham Reyes mission "The Gates of El Presidio". In Undead Nightmare, he gives one side mission.

Development and performance
Casting
Landon Ricketts appears in Red Dead Redemption and its expansion Undead Nightmare, both released in 2010. He is played by Ross Hagen, credited in the role by the Red Dead Wiki and by Wikipedia's article on the game. Hagen, born Leland Lando Lilly on 21 May 1938 in Williams, Arizona, was an American actor, director, screenwriter and producer. According to Wikipedia, his credits include the series Daktari, in which he played the photographer Bart Jason from 1968 to 1969, and the films The Hellcats (1967) and The Sidehackers (1969).
After Ross Hagen's death
Ross Hagen died of cancer at his home in Brentwood, Los Angeles, on 7 May 2011, at the age of 72, according to Wikipedia; the Red Dead Wiki specifies prostate cancer. Ricketts became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode with the Myths and Mavericks pack, released later that year. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rockstar gave him lines taken from the single-player game, out of respect for Hagen, rather than cast another actor. Wikipedia reports that two places in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) were named in his honor: Mount Hagen and Hagen Orchards, a plantation in the Bayou Nwa region of Lemoyne.
Reception
Character rankings
In a list of the ten best characters of the Red Dead Redemption franchise, published by Sportskeeda on 13 August 2023, Aniket Mukherjee ranked Ricketts fourth. He comes after John Marston, Dutch van der Linde and Bonnie MacFarlane, and ahead of Bill Williamson (fifth). The author wrote that he "earns the fourth place for his pivotal role as a mentor" to John. On 1 June 2019, Stacey Henley of WhatCulture placed him tenth in a list of Western characters to recreate in Red Dead Redemption 2, noting that the outfit can be based on his cigarette card in that game.
Talk of a Red Dead Redemption 3
Rockstar has not announced a Red Dead Redemption 3. Ricketts has been named in press articles about what players expect from one. On 9 December 2021, Alisa Eiber, writing for the German edition of EarlyGame, considered him the most likely protagonist among three candidates, as a famous gunslinger whose story would take the series back to an earlier period of the Old West. On 26 July 2026, the Brazilian site Ei Nerd! (Vinicius Miranda) reported that fans favored him as a protagonist for a game set in his prime, while stressing that there was no official confirmation.
Other media and legacy
Red Dead Redemption 2
Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899, never shows Ricketts but refers to him several times, besides the two mission lines listed above. A cigarette card of the Famous Gunslingers set shows him as a younger man; it lies on a barrel behind the house facing the undertaker in Armadillo. The newspaper article "Cigarette Cards Give Fame to Murderers", printed in the Blackwater Ledger, the New Hanover Gazette and the Saint Denis Times, describes the Falcon Claw tobacco set and says the Butcher Brothers featured in it ran afoul of the legendary gunslinger Landon Ricketts. According to the Red Dead Wiki, John can also mention him during a poker game in camp.
Ricketts also appears in the dime novel Wild West Heroes, No. 132, titled "Mexican Ballad of Blood", found on a nightstand in the main bedroom of Downes Ranch. In its chapter VII, the gunslinger Jim "Boy" Calloway, wearing a deputy's badge, creeps through a Mexican camp to reach a besieged fort. Ricketts shoots the knife out of his hand, knocks him down and holds a Schofield Revolver on him. He tells Calloway that the general he nearly killed hates the outlaw holed up in the fort as much as he does. He also says the line about jumping from one side of the fence to the other that he tells John in "The Mexican Wagon Train". According to the Red Dead Wiki, the book is one Arthur can give to Jack.
Red Dead Online
Ricketts is neither seen nor named in Red Dead Online. One of its passive ability cards, Landon's Patience, unlocked at rank 18, refers to him: waiting up to 15 seconds between shots slightly increases the damage dealt.
Red Dead Redemption multiplayer
Two versions of Ricketts are playable in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption. The Undead Nightmare pack of 2010 added Zombie Ricketts, an undead version of the character, in the Zombies section of the Outfitter. The Myths and Mavericks Bonus Pack then added the living Ricketts in the Revolution section. According to the Red Dead Wiki, a Rockstar Newswire post of July 27, 2011, which set a September release window, already named him as a playable character. The pack was released free on September 13, 2011 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, with eight new character models, among them Javier Escuella, Uncle, Nastas and Vincente de Santa.
Like the other characters of the pack, Ricketts has only seven taunts. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Rockstar reused lines from the story mode for him rather than cast another actor, out of respect for his voice actor, who had died. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, contain the story and Undead Nightmare but not the multiplayer mode, so neither Ricketts model is available in them.
Marketing
Rockstar released official artwork of Ricketts for Red Dead Redemption, and a promotional screenshot showing him with John. For Undead Nightmare, an official artwork shows him as one of the undead. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the expansion's official trailer includes a shortened line from the start of "Biographies and Lies": "I have never seen anything quite like this." According to their captions on the wiki, promotional images of Myths and Mavericks show Ricketts in a shootout at Nekoti Rock in which he kills Javier Escuella, in a Gang Shootout at Gaptooth Breach with Uncle and Nastas, and in a Stronghold match at Beecher's Hope with Uncle, Eli and Jonah. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Ricketts was also shown on the Xbox LIVE Marketplace and PlayStation Store to promote the Legends and Killers pack of August 10, 2010, although he is not in it: that pack's eight characters come from Red Dead Revolver.
Trivia
Red Dead Redemption
According to the Red Dead Wiki, when the camera widens as John sits down at the poker table in "Lucky in Love", a few playing cards can be seen on Ricketts's right thigh, under the table.
In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again", if John sets off on horseback and then jumps onto the train on the way, Ricketts comments on it, for example: "So much for my relaxing train ride. Did you really miss me that much?"
The mission page of "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" describes Ricketts as nearly invincible during the mission.
In "The Mexican Wagon Train", Ricketts reproaches John for working for both sides of the war even if the player has done none of the missions of Vincente de Santa, Luisa Fortuna or Abraham Reyes.
None of the four prisoners named on the mission page of "The Mexican Wagon Train" is the writer or the local official Luisa describes at the start of the mission.
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
According to the Red Dead Wiki, if undead get into Casa Madrugada, for example during a Missing Persons mission, they do not attack Ricketts, and he does not treat them as a threat, however close they come.
After "Biographies and Lies", Ricketts sits on a chair in Casa Madrugada. Shooting a bottle near him, or his hat, makes him attack John. He can then be killed with no effect on the story, and Casa Madrugada stays a safe zone. According to the Red Dead Wiki, like Amos in the main game, he can survive a Cattleman Revolver shot to the face.
Some undead in the expansion use a zombified lookalike of Ricketts, without his hat, and can appear among the hordes anywhere in the world, even before he is met alive in "Biographies and Lies". According to the Red Dead Wiki, Bolters and Retchers use this model, the Retchers with differences in design.
