'''Priscilla Johnson''' is a [[Characters in Redemption#Unseen/Deceased|minor, deceased character]] featured in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

== History ==

=== Background ===
Priscilla was the wife of Marshal [[Leigh Johnson]] until she died on April 23, 1903, however the cause of her death is unknown. 

==Trivia==
*Although Marshal Johnson never mentions her in the game, her tombstone can be found in the [[Coot's Chapel]] Cemetery.
*Her tombstone reads "Forever Smiling In Our Hearts".
