## Problems

1. IMPRECISE. Origins: "One of his uncles cultivated land in the region". Transcript (The Demon Drink): "land cultivated by my uncle": one uncle, no "one of". Fix EN: "His uncle cultivated land in the province, and Javier Escuella's father, a drunk, worked on it as a laborer." FR to match ("Son oncle cultivait des terres dans la province...").

2. IMPRECISE. Facts grid Family: "An unnamed uncle, who owned farmland in the province". "Owned" is the wiki's reading; the transcript only says "land cultivated by my uncle". Fix: "An unnamed uncle, on whose land Javier Escuella's father worked".

3. COPIED. Origins: "the market sits on the lowest level and Allende's mansion on the highest, with houses and shops in between." Wiki Escalera page: "with the market being on the lowest and Colonel Allende's mansion on the highest, with a variety of shops and houses in between." Fix: "According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on the town, Allende's mansion stands on the top level and the market at the bottom."

4. COPIED + STYLE. Origins: "Allende takes land from the peasants and sells it to aristocrats, has those who complain beaten or killed, and makes the former owners work the same land for little or no pay." Wiki: "taking their land and selling it to aristocrats, while beating and killing those who complain. Allende then forces them to work on the land they previously owned for little, if any, compensation." Same sequence, same terms, three facts in one sentence. Fix: split and reword, e.g. "According to the Red Dead Wiki, he seizes peasant land and sells it to aristocrats. Peasants who protest are beaten or killed, the wiki says, and the former owners end up working the land for almost nothing."

5. IMPRECISE. Demon Drink: "wonders aloud whether to have him dragged around town by a horse or thrown to the dogs". Transcript: "or let the dogs fight you". Fix: "...or set the dogs on him" (Personality already says "set upon by dogs").

6. IMPRECISE. Demon Drink: "he says his family brought them civilization and God, that they despise his people for it". The transcript does not link the contempt to the gift ("We brought civilization, and these people, these fucking monkeys, despise us. We brought them God, and they turned their back on him."). Fix: "...brought them civilization and God, that the locals despise his people and turned their back on God, and that they need a ruler."

7. IMPRECISE (source hierarchy) + DUPLICATE. Mexican Caesar: "According to the mission page, Allende never pays the 20,000 pesos." A transcript supports it: in "Cowards Die Many Times" John throws at De Santa "Like the 20,000 pesos you promised, when you sent me on a suicide mission with Espinoza?". Fix: "The money never comes: in "Cowards Die Many Times", John reminds De Santa of the 20,000 pesos he was promised." Same fact also in Relationships > John Marston ("The promised 20,000 pesos are never paid") and the Missions table ("never pays"): keep Biography + the table cell, cut the Relationships sentence.

8. IMPRECISE. An Appointed Time: "The rebels then free prisoners from Allende's jail". Transcript: a rebel asks John to save the prisoners, "(Marston goes to the jail and kills the guards.)", Victor thanks "amigo". reyes_A: "Reyes sends John to free his men"; john_B: John "frees Reyes and other prisoners". Fix: "John then frees rebel prisoners from Allende's jail, and the rebels storm the hill." (Also fix half2 "Reyes's rebels" entry, which says the rebels free them.)

9. IMPRECISE. "John takes a machine gun while the rebels blow the gate open with gunpowder barrels". Mission page: John mans the Gatling and shoots the TNT placed at the gates; transcript: Reyes "We will blast through the door with gunpowder barrels... Wait for my signal! Now, John!". Fix: "John mans a Gatling gun and, on Reyes's signal, blows the gunpowder barrels the rebels have set against the gate."

10. DUPLICATE. "According to the mission page, it is guarded by two riders, a gunner on the Gatling gun mounted at the back and a guard riding shotgun beside the driver." and "the coach stops once John kills the driver or the horses" are repeated almost word for word in B "Weapons and stagecoach" (driver, guard riding shotgun, Gatling gunner, two riders; John stops it by killing the driver or the horses). 11. IMPRECISE. Ledger: "Reyes, quoted in the same article, calls it a great victory". The article reports him in indirect speech ("claimed this was a great victory"), no quotation. Fix: "According to the same article, Reyes claimed it was a great victory...".

## Story guide RDR1 Act II (EN + FR): Allende statements

G1. WRONG. EN: "Allende is sheltering Bill Williamson and <a>Javier Escuella</a>, which is what forces John to deal with him." John does not know this when he works for the army: he deals with Allende because Allende and De Santa promise to find the two men (Civilization, Demon Drink, Mexican Caesar); Reyes reveals only in "The Gates of El Presidio" that they "paid Allende to hide them".
- EN fix: `Allende promises to find Bill Williamson and <a href="/characters/javier-escuella/">Javier Escuella</a> for John in exchange for his help against the rebels. Reyes later tells John that the two men paid Allende to hide them.`
- FR (current "Allende protège Bill Williamson et Javier Escuella, ce qui oblige John à traiter avec lui.") fix: `Allende promet à John de lui retrouver Bill Williamson et <a href="/fr/personnages/javier-escuella/">Javier Escuella</a> s'il aide l'armée contre les rebelles. Reyes lui apprendra plus tard que les deux hommes ont payé Allende pour qu'il les cache.`

G2. IMPRECISE (HOUSE_FACTS wording). "Allende and Bill Williamson are killed as they flee by stagecoach" (narrative paragraph AND Key events). They die after the coach is stopped and Allende hands Bill over, shot by John or by Reyes.
- EN paragraph fix: `John frees Reyes, the rebels take Allende's villa, and Allende and Bill Williamson flee by stagecoach. Once the coach is stopped on the road, Allende offers Bill to John in exchange for his life; both men are shot by John or by Reyes (player's choice).`
- FR paragraph (current "et Allende comme Bill Williamson sont tués dans leur fuite en diligence.") fix: `John libère Reyes, les rebelles s'emparent de la villa d'Allende, et Allende s'enfuit en diligence avec Bill Williamson. Une fois la voiture arrêtée sur la route, Allende livre Bill à John contre sa propre vie ; les deux hommes sont abattus par John ou par Reyes, au choix du joueur.`
- EN Key events li fix: `In Escalera, Luisa Fortuna is killed trying to save Reyes; the rebels take Allende's villa, and Allende and Bill Williamson, fleeing by stagecoach, are shot on the road by John or by Reyes (player's choice).`
- FR li fix: `À Escalera, Luisa Fortuna est tuée en tentant de sauver Reyes ; les rebelles prennent la villa d'Allende, et Allende et Bill Williamson, en fuite dans une diligence, sont abattus sur la route par John ou par Reyes (au choix du joueur).`

G3. IMPRECISE (minor). "De Santa lures John to Chuparosa and has him seized for execution." It is Allende who announces the false capture and sends John there with De Santa (Cowards transcript).
- EN fix: `In "Cowards Die Many Times", Allende tells John that Bill and Javier have been captured in Chuparosa; De Santa takes him there and has him seized for execution.`
