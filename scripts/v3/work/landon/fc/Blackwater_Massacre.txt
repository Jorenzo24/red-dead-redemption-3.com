The '''Blackwater Massacre''' was an infamous and violent gunfight that occured in the city of [[Blackwater]] in April 1899. The incident involved the [[Van der Linde gang]], the [[Blackwater Police Department]], the [[Pinkerton National Detective Agency]], and supposedly the famed gunslinger [[Landon Ricketts]]. The gunfight occurred in the aftermath of a ferry boat robbery at the city's docks.

After April 1899, the massacre had received widespread publicity throughout states such as [[West Elizabeth]],<ref>Blackwater Massacre was mentioned in [[Blackwater Ledger No. 213]], [[Blackwater Ledger No. 62]], [[Blackwater Ledger No. 63]]...</ref> [[New Hanover]],<ref>Blackwater Massacre was mentioned in [[New Hanover Gazette No. 27]], [[New Hanover Gazette No. 28]]...</ref> and [[Lemoyne]],<ref>Blackwater Massacre was mentioned in [[Saint Denis Times No. 43]], [[Saint Denis Times No. 44]]...</ref> with coverage of the massacre appearing in at least one [[Newspapers|newspaper]] from each state. The incident quickly attracted widespread attention due to its occurrence in a rapidly growing city, the murder of a [[Heidi McCourt|female civilian]], and the fact that it was the largest heist the area had seen in years.

==Events==
According to reports of the incident, the ferry was transporting $150,000 in bank funds. Believing it to be a lucrative target, [[Dutch van der Linde]] organized a robbery, encouraged by [[Micah Bell]], one of the newest members of the [[Van der Linde gang]]. Despite the absence of senior gang members [[Arthur Morgan]] and [[Hosea Matthews]], who were pursuing their own separate lead at the time and were skeptical of the heist,{{Citation needed}} the rest of the gang proceeded with the robbery.

According to Javier Escuella, the robbery initially proceeded as planned, with the [[Van der Linde gang]] gaining access to the money inside the ferry. However, agents of the [[Pinkerton National Detective Agency]], accompanied by [[Blackwater Police Department|local law enforcement]] and bounty hunters, quickly descended upon the ferry in overwhelming numbers. The gang was subsequently forced into a fierce gun battle with the authorities.<ref>According to [[Javier Escuella]] in the mission "[[Enter, Pursued by a Memory]]".</ref> Although the precise sequence of events remains unclear, it is known that at some point during the robbery, Dutch fatally shot [[Heidi McCourt|a young woman]] in the head. The circumstances surrounding the shooting remain ambiguous; however, according to [[John Marston]],{{Citation needed}} who witnessed the killing alongside Javier, and found it brutal,{{Citation needed}} Dutch appeared to have been encouraged by Micah to carry out the killing.

The gunfight proved to be devastating for the Van der Linde gang. During the incident, John and [[Mac Callander]] sustained gunshot wounds, while [[Davey Callander]] and [[Jenny Kirk]] were mortally wounded. Meanwhile, [[Sean MacGuire]] was rendered unconscious under unknown circumstances and subsequently captured by [[Ike Skelding's Boys|bounty hunters]].

The surviving members of the gang were forced to abandon the robbery and flee the scene. Unable to escape with the stolen money, they concealed it in an undisclosed location somewhere in [[Blackwater]], a hiding place known only to Dutch and Hosea.{{Citation needed}} After regrouping, the surviving members of the gang hastily broke camp and fled north through a blizzard into the [[Grizzlies West|western region of Ambarino]], successfully escaping the pursuing lawmen and Pinkerton agents.

==Aftermath==
Following the shootout, [[Blackwater|the]] [[Great Plains|southern]] [[Tall Trees|half]] of West Elizabeth, including the neighboring state of [[New Austin]] were put on lockdown with Pinkerton agents constantly patrolling the borders of the regions, which would remain in place for a significant portion of the year. While the Pinkertons and local law enforcement couldn't confirm if the gang got away with the money, it didn't stop local residents tearing apart gardens and backyards trying to find it.<ref>[[Blackwater Locked Down]]</ref> It's thought that the city took a long time to get over the incident, with a bartender in 1907 commenting on how the townsfolk said that Blackwater would never recover.

The Van der Linde gang itself was hit hard, having to escape north into the mountains of Ambarino, eventually settling in an abandoned settlement called [[Colter]]. Morale among the gang members were extremely low following the massacre, with Arthur and Hosea in particular blaming Micah for persuading Dutch to go ahead with something so reckless. The massacre also resulted in a number of casualties:
*'''John Marston''' was shot in the arm during the massacre, although it appears to have been a fairly minor injury as he was sent out scouting shortly after the gang had reached Ambarino. However, the gunshot wound to the arm — coupled with the snowstorm — appeared to have weakened him significantly as he was unprepared when wolves attacked him and left him further wounded. The combined injuries left John bedridden while the gang were in Colter, and he wouldn't recover until they reached [[Horseshoe Overlook]]. 
*'''Charles Smith''' burned his hand due to what he described as a “stupid mistake,” and while unable to properly use a bow was still well enough to manage the gang's horses and otherwise help with camp work in Colter.
*'''Davey Callander''' sustained what proved to be a fatal gunshot wound to the gut during the massacre,<ref>According to Arthur's journal.</ref> but survived long enough to accompany the rest of the gang in heading north. He passed away shortly after the gang reached Colter, and was subsequently buried there.
*'''Mac Callander''' was also shot and was unable to leave with the escaping gang. He was found by [[Andrew Milton|Agent Milton]] shortly afterward, who described Mac as "pretty shot up", indicating he sustained multiple gunshot wounds and was on the verge of death. The detective questioned Mac — who acted defiant and refused to divulge information about the gang — and later killed him in what he mockingly described as a "mercy killing" sometime later.<ref>In the mission "[[A Fisher of Men]]".</ref>
*'''Jenny Kirk''' was mortally wounded and died shortly after the gang's escape, passing away sometime before Davey. She was buried just north of Colter.
*'''Sean MacGuire''' wasn't injured, but was knocked out and captured by bounty hunters led by [[Ike Skelding]] shortly after the events of the massacre, who intended to transfer him to a federal prison. Sean was also tortured at some point but refused to reveal anything. He was later [[The First Shall Be Last|rescued]] by his fellow gang members.

Later following the disastrous events of the [[Banking, The Old American Art|bank robbery in Saint Denis]], which also saw the deaths of some gang members like Hosea Matthews and Lenny Summers and led to John’s arrest, Arthur once again blamed Micah, claiming how the Pinkertons knew they were coming like in Blackwater, though the latter angrily replied it was nothing like that. [[Bill Williamson]] described this event as "worse than Blackwater".

==Trivia==
*The extraordinarily large number of Pinkertons and the speed at which they appeared has prompted claims that the ferry robbery may have been a set-up. In the mission "[[Outlaws from the West]]", Dutch may say in optional dialogue: "''That many men? Oh, they knew we were coming.''".
*It is implied that [[Karen Jones]] was at camp during the massacre, as she recounts the boys riding back into camp afterwards covered in blood.
*Dialogue by Javier Escuella in his conversation with Arthur Morgan in "[[Enter, Pursued by a Memory]]" explicitly states that the following gang members were present on the ferry or in its direct vicinity during the heist: Dutch van der Linde, Micah Bell, Davey Callander, Mac Callander, Javier Escuella, Sean MacGuire and John Marston. Javier furthermore states: "By the time you boys showed up on the other side of town, we were only just holding on", indicating that Arthur Morgan and other gang members bailed out the gang members who were cornered on or near the ferry by Pinkertons and other lawmen by attacking them from behind. Strangely, Javier does not mention Jenny Kirk, thus implying she may not have been part of the ferry robbery and was instead killed likely while helping bail out the gang members involved in the heist or while escaping law enforcement, during the shootout.
*Campfire dialogue between Lenny Summers and Sean MacGuire strongly suggests that Lenny was not present during the ferry robbery: Lenny imagines that it was Davey Callander's fault "things went so bad in Blackwater", to which Sean replies that he can't remember, 'cause he was knocked unconscious immediately after things "kicked off", prompting Lenny's question what kicked them off.
*Charles Smith tells Arthur Morgan during "[[The Aftermath of Genesis]]" that he "still" does not know "what happened on that boat," which makes it seem very likely that he also was not part of the crew that robbed the ferry.
*In camp, John Marston may give an account of what happened in the massacre. He states: "Sometimes, I start thinking about that girl. Well... Javier knew what we saw and it weren’t pretty", suggesting that he may be mentally scarred by the incident. He also says that Micah encouraged Dutch to kill [[Heidi McCourt]], and that Dutch later justified the event by saying that the gang needed to survive.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwSheuhL43E</ref>
*[[Landon Ricketts]] was rumoured to have been involved in the event somehow.<ref>[[Landon Ricketts Dead]]</ref>
*It is implied that the [[Strange Man]] may have been present during the massacre. This is apparently evidenced by the gruesome and detailed first-hand account he gives of Heidi McCourt's death, recalling details that even John Marston himself could not remember. Arthur also mentions in his [[Journal (RDR 2)|journal]] that in the streets of Blackwater he heard about a man who "sounded like [[Josiah Trelawny|Trelawny]]". If the player visits Trelawny's house, he will mention taking a visit to the town of Blackwater not too long ago, although it's never specified ''when'' he visited, leaving it ambiguous as to whether the man in question was him or the Strange Man.
**Also in ''Red Dead Redemption'', during the stranger side-mission "[[I Know You]]", the massacre is mentioned by the [[Strange Man]] during a conversation with John. Despite giving a vivid account of Heidi McCourt's death, John does not seem to remember her. This was retconned by what John says about her in ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', where he suggests that he can't get the image of it out of his head, although the time gap of twelve years might explain this. However, John never explicitly mentions knowing the girl's name nor having found out about it, which likely means he didn't forget her and simply didn't know Heidi McCourt by name.
*[[Landon Ricketts]]' involvement with the massacre is never made clear. While a newspaper in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]'' directly names him as a survivor, nothing in ''[[Red Dead Redemption 2]]'' mentions or even alludes to his involvement with the events that transpired.
*Later, when the Van der Linde gang [[Banking, The Old American Art|decided to rob the Lemoyne National Bank]] in [[Saint Denis]], which also included a sudden arrival of a large number of Pinkerton agents and an all-out shootout between both parties, [[Bill Williamson]] described it as "worse than Blackwater". However, unlike the Blackwater Massacre, the city and its region are not put under lockdown or patrolled by Pinkertons and will only be on alert after Arthur and [[Sadie Adler]] rescue John from the [[Sisika Penitentiary]] during Chapter 6. 
*The massacre is fairly similar to [[w:c:gta:Harwood Incident|The Harwood Massacre]] mentioned in another Rockstar title, ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'', as both massacres are essential in setting up the plot of their respective games and involve the playable protagonists (John Marston and Tommy Vercetti, respectively). Additionally, both events are also not depicted in the respective games they are important to.
*In "[[The New South]]", Hosea mentions the gang's original plan was to flee south and lay low in safe spots they picked out in [[New Austin]] after carrying out the heist. But the disaster of the heist forced the gang to flee north instead.
