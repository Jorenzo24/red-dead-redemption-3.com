'''Marshall Johnson of Armadillo Retires''' is one of the [[Newspapers|newspaper articles]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

==Text==
MARSHALL JOHNSON OF ARMADILLO RETIRES
----
'''Legendary [[Armadillo]] [[U.S. Marshals|Marshall]], [[Leigh Johnson]], has retired at the remarkable old age of 54.'''
----
Mr. Johnson, who served as town marshall for 17 years, is credited with taking down some of [[New Austin]]'s most notorious outlaws including [[Walton's Gang]], the [[Two Crows]] Boys, One-Lung Lester Riley, the [[Bollard Twins gang|Bollard Twins]] and Wildcat Willie. Armadillo residents held a riotous leaving party in his honor at the [[Armadillo Saloon|town's saloon]], at which he arrested three men and reportedly was the last man standing at the end of the night. When asked about his plans for the future, he said that he would be moving as far away from Armadillo as humanly possible.
