## Problems

1. IMPRECISE (Production, attribution). Draft: "According to IGN's retrospective ... published by Luke Reilly on 22 May 2020 and cited by Wikipedia, the first scene recorded for the game was John Marston's meeting with Dickens." IGN (read directly) quotes Rob Nelson, Rockstar North co-studio head: "I was lucky enough to work with director Rod Edge and the team on the very first cutscene shoot with Rob Weithoff as Marston in LA, when Marston finds West Dickens injured out in the prairies." The claim is Nelson's, made to IGN; IGN's datePublished is 21 May 2020 (18:31 UTC; Wikipedia gives 22 May, likely Australian time). Fix: "Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, told IGN (Luke Reilly, May 2020) that the very first cutscene shoot, in Los Angeles with Rob Wiethoff and director Rod Edge, was the scene where John finds the injured Dickens on the prairie." Drop "cited by Wikipedia". Consequence for john_D.py line 126 (and john_marston.py line 205): the substance is right, but "producer Rob Nelson" should be "Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North" and the source is IGN 2020; no Variety 24 Oct 2018 article with this statement could be found (searches failed), so the Variety attribution in john_marston.py's register is unverified and should be replaced by IGN.
