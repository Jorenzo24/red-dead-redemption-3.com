## Problems

1. **IMPRECISE** (doctor, spelling and basis). Draft: "According to the mission page, they take him to Doctor Nathaniel Johnston in Armadillo." Sources: the in-game sign reads "NATHANIAL JOHNSTON, M.D." (fc/sign_johnston.jpg; wiki page title "Nathanial Johnston"). The Exodus mission page names no doctor in its text (only a link). The Exodus transcript's last stage direction has the wagon "head to MacFarlane's Ranch"; nobody is shown driving. The New Friends mission page says Bonnie "and her father Drew" took him, which the Exodus transcript contradicts (Amos). The best evidence is two transcript lines: John in Old Friends, New Problems, "She found me half-dead on the side of the road and took me to the doctor", and Bonnie in This is Armadillo, USA, "The doc's a good fellow, he saved your life" (about the Armadillo doctor). No source says whether the bullets were removed in Armadillo or at the ranch. Fix: "Bonnie and Amos find him by the road and lift him into their wagon. John later says she took him to the doctor; Bonnie credits the Armadillo doctor, Nathanial Johnston, with saving his life." Do not say where the operation took place, and do not name Drew.

2. **WRONG** (Names). Draft: "according to the Red Dead Wiki, the only other time is in "Paternal Pride", in Undead Nightmare." The Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane transcript has John say "Bonnie, are you okay?" when he cuts her down. Fix: "He uses it again when he cuts her down in "Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane" ("Bonnie, are you okay?"). In Undead Nightmare, according to the Red Dead Wiki, he also uses it in "Paternal Pride"." Any "John calls her Bonnie only twice" trivia in part C must go.

3. **IMPRECISE** (order and speaker, Wild Horses). Draft: "On the way back, she tells John about her governesses and her suitors (see Origins) ... Amos then reports more horses outside Armadillo." Transcript: on the way back they talk about her father and ranch life; back at the ranch DREW relays Amos's report ("Hey Bonnie, Amos was saying some fine horses were spotted outside of Armadillo"); the suitors/governesses talk and John's "woman in a man's world" come on the ride out to those horses. Fix: "Back at the ranch, Drew passes on Amos's report of more horses outside Armadillo. On the ride there, she tells John about her governesses and her suitors (see Origins) ..."

4. **IMPRECISE** (Origins). Draft: "she finds the ranch foreman Amos a little countrified." Amos is the head ranch hand (wiki "Amos"); the fiche calls him "ranch hand Amos" everywhere else. Fix: "the ranch hand Amos".

5. **UNSUPPORTED** (1907 absence). Draft: "Bonnie and her father are away. The sources give two reasons..." No transcript or primary source gives a reason; the two wiki reasons conflict (character page: cholera outbreak; ranch page: death in the family), and the EN "outbreak" looks like a carry-over from the 1898 Online intro (outbreak in Armadillo). The absence itself is confirmed by Game Informer (Javy Gwaltney, 28 Nov 2018: "no Bonnie or her father in sight"). Fix: state the absence with the Game Informer attribution; then one sentence: "The game gives no reason; the Red Dead Wiki gives two that conflict, a cholera outbreak on her page and a death in the family on the ranch's page." Keep the FR wiki sentence only if needed (it adds no reason).

6. **IMPRECISE** (1907 buildings). Draft: "several of its buildings do not exist yet, among them the train station and the general store." Ranch page: "not built yet or are in early stages, such as the foreman's office, train station, general store, and the barn". Fix: "several buildings are missing or unfinished, among them the train station and the general store" (barn already in Trivia).

7. **SPECULATION** (barn motive). Draft: "According to the mission page, Williamson's men set the fire in retaliation for the family's care of John." Who: the mission page and the character page say Williamson's men (consistent with bill_A and john_D); the transcript names no one, but John says "That gang seems to really want you out of here." The retaliation motive is the page's inference. Fix: "According to the mission page, Williamson's men set the fire. Afterward John tells Bonnie the gang seems to want the family out."

## Consistency with the story guide and other fiches (fix outside this fiche)
