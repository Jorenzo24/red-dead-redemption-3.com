Orville Swanson

Van der Linde gang Alive in 1907 Reverend RDR2

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Reverend Orville Swanson is a former clergyman and a member of the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar's description notes that, had he not once saved Dutch's life, the gang would probably not have kept him around.

He gets sober, leaves the gang before its collapse, and leads a church in New York by 1907.

Biography

A fallen reverend

Swanson says he lost his vocation, his faith and his family. He is addicted to morphine and alcohol. He once saved Dutch's life, which is why the gang keeps him; the game never explains how.

He speaks the game's first line, in the snowstorm of the opening mission, about the dying Davey Callahan: "Abigail says he's dying, Dutch. We'll have to stop someplace."

Who is Not Without Sin

In chapter 2, in "Who is Not Without Sin", Arthur finds him drunk at Flatneck Station, playing poker. Arthur saves him from a beating, then pulls him off the Bard's Crossing railway bridge, where his foot is stuck in the tracks, just before a train passes, and brings him back to camp.

Sobriety and departure

In chapter 4, after the O'Driscoll attack on Shady Belle, Dutch has him bury Kieran Duffy with Charles and Pearson. After the gang's return from Guarma, Swanson is sober. He leaves the gang at Beaver Hollow.

At the end of "The Fine Art of Conversation", if Arthur's honor is low or he has not completed "Of Men and Angels", Swanson is the one Arthur meets at Emerald Station, boarding a train. Otherwise, Arthur meets Sister Calderón there.

1907

An in-game newspaper article, "Reverend Swanson Leads NY Church", reports that he has become minister of the First Congregational Church of New York, after preaching on street corners and serving as an assistant pastor in Ohio.

Development and performance

Swanson is played by Sean Haberle, an American actor who provided the voice and the motion capture.

Reception

Game Informer's December 2018 ranking of the gang placed Swanson 17th, describing a character who "bounces back and forth between a wise man and fool". Polygon's Colin Campbell called him "the drunken, self-loathing padre" in November 2018. In April 2019, GamesRadar cited Swanson and Uncle as addicts the camp protects and shelters.
