The '''Famous Gunslingers Card Set''' is a collectible set of [[Cigarette Cards]] players may put together in ''[[Red Dead Redemption 2]]''. Mailing the set to [[Phineas T. Ramsbottom]] awards Special Snake Oil and $50.
