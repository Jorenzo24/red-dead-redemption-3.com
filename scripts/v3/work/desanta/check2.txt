Missions
De Santa appears in six story missions of Red Dead Redemption, all in the second act, set in Nuevo Paraíso, and in one mission of Undead Nightmare. He gives the five missions of his own strand, all of which begin in Escalera. The sixth, in which he dies, belongs to the strand of Luisa Fortuna. Abraham Reyes mentions him in two other missions of the act.

Civilization, at Any PriceAct II: Nuevo ParaísoMission giver. Meets John Marston at the manor in Escalera and has him escort a decoy train out of Chuparosa. 26th mission. Rewards: 50 dollars and 250 fame; unlocks the Luisa Fortuna strand and the stranger mission "Poppycock".

Empty PromisesAct II: Nuevo ParaísoMission giver. Takes John on his wagon to the rebel-held fort of Torquemada, then puts off his questions. 28th mission. Rewards: 100 dollars, a Rolling Block Rifle (gift) and 300 fame; unlocks the stranger missions "Love is the Opiate" and "Funny Man" (part 4).
Mexican CaesarAct II: Nuevo ParaísoMission giver. Brings two women to Allende and offers John 20,000 pesos and information on Javier Escuella to escort an army train; stays behind while John rides with Espinoza. 29th mission. Rewards: 75 dollars and 200 fame; dynamite goes on sale and Butter Bridge is rebuilt.
Cowards Die Many TimesAct II: Nuevo ParaísoMission giver. Takes John to Chuparosa on the pretext that Bill and Javier are held in the church, holds a shooting contest on the way, then has him knocked out and set before a firing squad. 36th mission; also requires "Must a Savior Die?". Rewards: up to 75 dollars from the contest, 50 honor and 250 fame; unlocks the Reyes strand and "Father Abraham".
Captain De Santa's DownfallAct II: Nuevo ParaísoTarget. Executes a rebel at Sepulcro, is hogtied and beaten by John, names Casa Madrugada, and is shot by John or by the rebels (player's choice). 39th mission, given by Luisa Fortuna, from Campo Mirada to Casa Madrugada. Rewards: 125 honor and 300 fame.
Mother Superior BluesUndead NightmareRises from his grave as the undead boss of the Sepulcro cemetery. Mission given by the Mother Superior of Las Hermanas. Reward: Holy Water.
