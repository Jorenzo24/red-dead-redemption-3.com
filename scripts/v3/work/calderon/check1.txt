
Sister Calderón is a Catholic nun in Saint Denis in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. She teaches street children to read with Brother Dorkins and collects money and food for the poor. Arthur Morgan recovers her stolen crucifix in chapter 4 and gives to her collection in an optional mission. If he has completed that mission and his honor is high, he meets her again at Emerald Station in chapter 6, as she leaves for a mission in Mexico; there he tells her that he is dying. Otherwise, Orville Swanson appears in her place.
In Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911, the mother superior of the Las Hermanas convent asks John Marston for alms, and in the non-canon Undead Nightmare she helps him against the plague. The Red Dead Wiki treats the two as one character. According to cast listings, Irene DeBari voices her in Red Dead Redemption 2.
Key facts
She gives only the name Calderón; no game gives her a first name, a birth date or a nationality.
In "Brothers and Sisters, One and All" (chapter 4), Arthur gets back the crucifix her mother gave her.
In "Of Men and Angels", Arthur gives her food or 10 dollars for the poor; the mission must be done before "The Fine Art of Conversation".
At Emerald Station, after "Of Men and Angels" and with high honor, Arthur tells her he has tuberculosis and is afraid, before she boards a train for Mexico.
In Red Dead Redemption (1911), John can give 10 dollars to the mother superior of Las Hermanas, in Nuevo Paraíso, or rob her ("I Know You"); the Red Dead Wiki identifies her with the Saint Denis nun.

Biography
Origins
No game gives Sister Calderón a first name, a date of birth or a place of birth (see Names). The Red Dead Wiki treats the Saint Denis nun of 1899 and the Mother Superior of Las Hermanas in 1911 as a single character. In 1899 she works in Saint Denis with Brother Dorkins, a monk, and teaches street children to read. According to the Red Dead Wiki, both are attached to the Church of the Holy Blessed Virgin, led by Father O'Leary.
What is known of her past comes from her own words to Arthur Morgan. She says that she was married and that her husband died long ago. Her crucifix came from her mother: as a street boy runs off with it, she says her mother gave it to her when she was a girl; when Arthur returns it, she says her mother gave it to her when she was a novice, shortly before she died. She also tells Arthur that she was once like him. By her account, she did terrible things and could not stop, because she did not believe goodness existed, until the day she saw that love did exist; from then on, doing wrong seemed absurd to her. She gives no details of those acts.
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 4: Saint Denis
Arthur first hears of her through Brother Dorkins. In the stranger mission "Help a Brother Out", he frees two captives for Dorkins, who tells him he often works at the old church on Gaspar Street. The follow-up, "Brothers and Sisters, One and All", opens there: the Sister and Dorkins are helping street children through a reading lesson. She coaxes a boy who is struggling with the letters, and gently tells Dorkins that his grammar corrections are confusing the child. Dorkins introduces Arthur, and she says Dorkins has told her about the good turn he did; Arthur says Dorkins talks a lot of nonsense.
One of the boys then grabs her crucifix and runs. Arthur gives chase; she calls after him not to hurt the child, and Dorkins says they will wait at the cathedral. Arthur finds the boy held by a man who accuses him of stealing his watch, makes the man let him go, and picks up the crucifix the boy drops. He then runs into Edith Downes, now working as a prostitute, who calls the police on him; he has to lose the lawmen before going to the cathedral.
He finds the Sister there, talking with a priest about the daily challenges of her work. She can hardly believe he has the cross back and asks after the boy. She tells Arthur that Brother Dorkins was right about him and that he is the most wonderful man, then hugs him; Arthur answers that Dorkins is greatly deceived. She explains what the crucifix means to her (see Origins). As Arthur leaves, she tells the priest that Brother Dorkins found him and that Dorkins was fantastic.
Chapter 5 or 6: the collection
"Of Men and Angels" can be played in chapter 5 or 6. It becomes available only if "Brothers and Sisters, One and All" was completed, and only after "A Fork in the Road", the mission in which Arthur learns he is ill. It must be played before "The Fine Art of Conversation", after which it is no longer offered. Arthur finds the Sister outside the Saint Denis cathedral with a collection box, asking passers-by for money and food for the poor. She introduces herself by name, says she and Dorkins had hoped to see him again, and gives him news of Dorkins (see Relationships).
She asks how he is; he says never better, and she asks whether he is sure. She tells him about her own past (see Origins). Arthur says he only hopes for a dignified exit, and she says she will pray for him. He answers that he is not a religious man but an outlaw. She laughs, and tells him that religion is only a word, that to her God is people and people are God, and that actions lead and the heart follows. She suggests he find food for the starving, and adds that she will still think of him in very nasty terms if he wishes. Arthur gives either the food he is carrying or some money (10 dollars); both choices raise his honor. She calls him wonderful; when he protests, she corrects herself with a joke and lets him go. The second part of the mission completes automatically.
Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow
Her last scene with Arthur closes "The Fine Art of Conversation". After Arthur and Charles Smith get Captain Lyndon Monroe away from the failed talks between Colonel Favours and Rains Fall and put him on a train at Emerald Station, Charles rides off and Arthur is left on the platform, coughing. If "Of Men and Angels" was completed and his honor is high, the Sister comes up to him; otherwise he meets Reverend Orville Swanson. She says she is on her way to Mexico, where she is finally being sent on a mission, and that Dorkins is very jealous.
Arthur starts coughing again and she helps him onto a bench. He tells her he is dying of tuberculosis, which he says he caught beating a man to death for a few dollars, and that he has lived a bad life. She answers that everyone sins, and that the trouble is that Arthur does not know himself: each time they have met, he was helping someone. He tells her about the son he lost, the woman who loved him and whom he let go, the mother who died when he was a child and the father he watched die. She replies with the death of her own husband (see Origins). When he asks what he should do now, she tells him to be grateful that he finally sees his life clearly, and suggests he help someone.
Arthur says he still does not believe in anything; she laughs that she often does not either, until she meets someone like him. He admits that he is afraid. She tells him there is nothing to fear and urges him to act out of love; he promises to try. She boards the train, calling out her goodbyes. Arthur does not see her again. If he met her at the station and his honor is high, her advice about seeing his life clearly is one of the lines that come back to him on his last ride, in "Red Dead Redemption", the final mission played as Arthur. According to the mission page, this happens only if Arthur did not trigger a random encounter conversation with Mary-Beth Gaskill.
Red Dead Redemption (1911)
Act II: Nuevo Paraíso
In 1911 a Mother Superior runs the convent of Las Hermanas, in the Perdido region of Nuevo Paraíso. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she is Sister Calderón, and Las Hermanas is where she was sent twelve years earlier; the games do not say where in Mexico her 1899 mission took her. John Marston meets her in the second part of the stranger mission "I Know You", which opens once he has completed the first part and the Irish mission "We Shall Be Together in Paradise". At a campfire on the road west of El Matadero, the Strange Man tells John that an old nun is travelling from the monastery to take the money she has raised to the bank, on a road full of thieves. He suggests John help her, or rob her himself.
Outside Las Hermanas, the Mother Superior is asking for alms for the poor. When John asks whether looking after the flock is not the Lord's job, she answers that the Lord has sent him to her, and that a few dollars would show people at their wits' end that someone cares. John can drop 10 dollars in her basket; she thanks him in Spanish and blesses him. He can instead rob her.
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
Undead Nightmare, an expansion released in 2010, is not part of the series' canon. In "Mother Superior Blues", John reaches the Las Hermanas train station as an undead is strangling the Mother Superior. She breaks free and shoots it in the head, then fears she has committed a mortal sin; John tells her it was self-defense. She explains that her nuns are trapped in the church with the undead and are less willing than she is to use violence, and asks John to clear the convent. He can kill the undead himself or hand ammunition to the survivors.
Once the convent is safe, she tells him that the bishop fell ill the week before and ate half of the choirboys. She has no idea what is afflicting the people, but believes it is a sickness of the soul, and asks John to bring her a freshly turned undead. John hogties one and brings it back. When she pours holy water on it, a blue flame runs over the body and, for a few seconds, the undead looks alive again; then it starts growling once more. She wonders whether her faith is strong enough. She then sends John to cleanse the cemetery of Sepulcro and gives him Holy Water, which becomes available to the player. At Sepulcro, John burns the coffins and kills the undead, among them Vincente de Santa, who rises from his grave.
When John returns, she tells him in the church that a woman has told her the curse is the result of something done by Abraham Reyes, the next president, and sends him to Escalera. In the French version of the game, the mission description calls the informant a nun passing through; the Red Dead Wiki identifies her as Ayauhtéotl (see Relationships). According to the same wiki, the Mother Superior calls Reyes a vain, ambitious and repugnant parasite and a philanderer, and adds that he seems to have the most half-witted friends, which leads John to ask whether she knows Bill Williamson. The second part of the mission, which requires the side missions "Filth and Other Entertainment" and "Biographies and Lies", consists of riding to Escalera and finding Reyes in the presidential mansion. John does not see the Mother Superior again.
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of 6 September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang only and do not include her. When Arthur brings back her crucifix, her first question is about the boy who stole it (see Biography). The transcripts show her laughing in each of her three conversations with Arthur: at Dorkins's grammar lesson, at Arthur's claim to be an outlaw and at his admission that he believes in nothing. She tells Arthur that actions lead and the heart follows (see Quotes). In Red Dead Redemption (1911) she meets John Marston's objection to her appeal for alms with an answer of her own (see Biography). In Undead Nightmare, outside the series' canon, she is armed and uses her weapon (see Biography).

Names
Calderón is the only name the games give her. In "Of Men and Angels", Arthur greets her as "Sister" and she supplies the rest: "Calderón." No first name is ever given. Brother Dorkins introduces Arthur to her with "Sister, my friend, Mr. Morgan.", and Arthur only ever calls her Sister. In Red Dead Redemption, the Strange Man speaks of "an old nun" and John, after his donation, says "There you are, sister."; the dialogue reproduced by the Red Dead Wiki labels her Mother Superior. In Undead Nightmare, when John calls her sister, she corrects him: she is the Mother Superior, Mother Superior Calderón, according to the ireddead.com walkthrough and the expansion's Italian version. The Red Dead Wiki titles her page "Mother Superior Calderón" and also lists the name Sister Calderón. According to the same wiki, a drunk at Doyle's Tavern in Saint Denis refers to her as a Spanish nun.
Appearance
She is an elderly woman. Her habit is that of a Catholic nun, with a black veil over a white coif and a black tunic. In Undead Nightmare she keeps the habit and carries a rifle (see Charity work and gameplay).
