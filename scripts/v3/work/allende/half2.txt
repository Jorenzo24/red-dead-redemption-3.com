Relationships
John Marston
Allende promises to find Bill Williamson and Javier Escuella for John in exchange for his help against the rebels, then sends him to Chuparosa, where De Santa has him arrested and set before a firing squad (see Biography). The promised 20,000 pesos are never paid. John tells Luisa later that Allende betrayed him and that he saw him do things that disgust even him. John stops the colonel's stagecoach on the road out of Escalera (see Biography).
Abraham Reyes
The leader of the revolt against him. Allende tells John that men like Javier's father are natural allies for Reyes. Reyes holds President Sanchez responsible for everything Allende does, and says it is worse that Allende enjoys beating the people he swore to protect. Reyes looks forward to sleeping in his silk sheets. His rebels take Escalera, and Allende dies on the road, shot by John or by Reyes (see Biography).
Vincente de Santa
His captain and go-between with John. Allende insults him in Spanish in front of John, calls him a crybaby and tells him not to stand behind him, since he is not his shadow. De Santa brings him young women from the villages, which he presents as patriots. He defends his colonel to John: "Colonel Allende controls any situation he is in because he knows the situation can never be allowed to control him."
De Santa carries out the betrayal of John in Chuparosa, then dies at Sepulcro, shot by John or by the rebels; at the execution he oversees there, he condemns the prisoners in the name of Colonel Allende's government (see Biography).
Bill Williamson
Bill lives under the colonel's protection in Escalera; according to Reyes, he and Javier paid Allende to hide them. When the rebels break down the door of the mansion, Bill covers Allende's run to the stagecoach. Once the coach is stopped, Allende pushes him out and offers him to John in exchange for his own life. Both men die on the road (see Biography).
Javier Escuella
Allende tells John that Javier comes from the province and that his father, a drunk, worked land farmed by Allende's uncle (see Biography). Javier is under his protection at El Presidio; when John corners him there, Javier says that Bill is with Allende (see Biography).
Luisa Fortuna
Luisa blames Allende for the death of her father, killed by the army, and for the girls taken that day (see Biography). She tells John she cannot forgive him for having helped Allende, and says of the colonel: "Allende es pura maldad." She is killed in Escalera on the day the town falls (see Biography).
Landon Ricketts
The gunslinger never meets him. He describes him to John in "The Gunslinger's Tragedy": "Colonel Allende, he runs this place like a feudal king. He's an awful individual." When Luisa reports that Allende is sending a writer and a local official to their deaths, Ricketts answers: "For once, I agree with Allende. Some men need to be killed." He then fights the colonel's soldiers alongside John (see Biography).
Irish
In "We Shall Be Together in Paradise", Irish suggests to John that he try the provincial governor based in Escalera, whose name he cannot remember (see Names). He says he played Three Card Stud with him.
Espinoza
The army captain, De Santa's rival. He tells John: "I am Allende's brazo derecho. His right arm. I am one of the few men he respects." He tells John that the colonel himself told him De Santa had praised him and asked that he be given the munitions escort of "Mexican Caesar". John kills him in Chuparosa (see Biography).
Ignacio Sanchez
The president of Mexico, never seen in the game, in whose name Allende governs Nuevo Paraíso. Reyes calls Allende his puppet (see Names). The Blackwater Ledger reports the fall of Sanchez's government a few issues after Allende's death (see Biography).
Reyes's rebels
The rebels fight his army across the province and free the prisoners of his jail in Escalera before storming the mansion (see Biography). After the death of De Santa, Victor, one of them, tells John: "Allende is scared, and making mistakes." The executioner at El Presidio asks Reyes whether he really thought he could overthrow Allende.
Raul Zubieta
The army major who holds Reyes prisoner in Escalera on the day of the assault; John kills him in a duel (see Biography). The Red Dead Wiki lists Allende among his affiliations.
His uncle
Not named in the game. Allende mentions him once, as the man whose land Javier's father worked (see Biography). The Red Dead Wiki lists him as his only known relative.
Herbert Moon
The Armadillo shopkeeper lumps him with the other Mexican leaders in a free-roam line quoted by the Red Dead Wiki: "Sanchez, Allende, De Santa, Reyes; all the same! They just want Armadillo rail rides."
Weapons, stagecoach and villa
In the game
Allende appears in four story missions of the second act of Red Dead Redemption but gives none of them: Captain de Santa gives "The Demon Drink", "Mexican Caesar" and "Cowards Die Many Times", and Abraham Reyes gives "An Appointed Time" (the Missions table lists his role in each). The Red Dead Wiki notes that he is the only major character of the game who never gives John a mission directly. According to the mission page of "Mexican Caesar", the gates of his villa are locked after that mission and stay shut until "An Appointed Time".
In "An Appointed Time", the mission page describes several variants. John can kill both men, one of them or neither, and Reyes shoots whoever John spares. If John tries to lasso Bill while Allende pins him down, Reyes shoots both, which can block the mission. On rare occasions Allende runs into the brush and Reyes shoots him in the back; sometimes nobody shoots him and the mission stalls. After the mission, his body can stay on the road east of Escalera and cannot be looted. The gates of the villa still lie in pieces in 1914, and, according to the Red Dead Wiki, his portrait still hangs inside.
Weapons and stagecoach
The Red Dead Wiki lists a sabre and a Cattleman Revolver as his weapons; the French-language wiki calls the blade a golden sword. He owns an armored stagecoach with a Gatling gun mounted on the back, which appears only in "An Appointed Time". On his flight, the mission page counts a driver, a guard riding shotgun, a gunner on the Gatling and two riders behind the coach. John stops it by killing the driver or the horses.
Missions
Allende appears on screen in four story missions of Red Dead Redemption, all in the second act, set in Nuevo Paraíso. He gives none of them: Captain Vincente de Santa gives the first three and Abraham Reyes the last. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he is the only major character of the game who never gives John Marston a mission directly. His name comes up in many other missions of the act, on both sides of the war, and once at the end of the first act.

Development and performance
Casting
Colonel Allende appears in Red Dead Redemption, released in 2010. Wikipedia's article on the game credits Gary Carlos Cervantes with the role and describes the character as "the state's tyrannical governor"; the Red Dead Wiki credits him as the voice of Allende. Cervantes is an American actor born on January 24, 1953, in Maywood, California, according to the TMDb database. Wikipedia's cast lists name him in Commando (1985), as Diaz, and in Wild Wild West (1999), as Professor Guillermo Escobar, a scientist kidnapped by the villain. No interview in which he discusses Red Dead Redemption was found, and no source consulted names a performer for Allende's motion capture.
Before release
Rockstar released artwork of the colonel before launch. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he looks heavier in this pre-release artwork than in the game, his uniform has no epaulettes, and he has no tuft of beard under the lip. The wiki's page on the game's cut content lists nothing else about him.
Reception
Reviews
Game Informer's review of Red Dead Redemption, written by Matt Bertz and published on May 17, 2010, does not name Allende. It sums up the Mexican part of the story as John having to "get cozy with both the corrupt ruler of the border region and the upstart rebel hell-bent on taking power for himself."
Villain rankings
In "Red Dead Redemption: Every Villain Ranked From Worst To Best", published by WhatCulture on February 7, 2022, Matt Thompson ranks Allende 13th of 15 villains from both games. He writes that "Colonel Allende could have had more depth written into his character". He adds that the colonel's corruption, taste for murder and sexual assaults show in his words, "but it's all surface level", and that the game never explains how he rose to power and came to command such an army.
Academic study
In "The Mustang Diaries: A Study of Mexico in Red Dead Redemption 1", published in the Revista Panamericana de Comunicación (vol. 4, no. 2, 2022), Javier Aldape, of Texas A&M University, writes that the game "has two versions of caudillos in the personification of Colonel Agustin Allende on the Federales side and Abraham Reyes, the leader of the Mexican rebeldes." He compares the women and alcohol of Allende's house to period photographs of military men such as Victoriano Huerta with a cigar and a glass of cognac. He concludes that "Allende falls victim to his desire to rule Mexico through betrayals", in the manner of the caudillos of the time.
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
Allende became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Liars and Cheats Pack, detailed by Rockstar on August 27, 2010 and released on September 21, 2010. Players select him in the Damnation section of the Outfitter, with Abraham Reyes, Bill Williamson, Dutch van der Linde, Edgar Ross, Harold MacDougal and Irish. Most of his multiplayer lines are in Spanish (see Quotes). The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, contain the story and Undead Nightmare but not the multiplayer mode, so the Allende model is not available in them.
Marketing
Rockstar released official artwork of Allende for Red Dead Redemption, and an alternate artwork of him appears in the Red Dead Wiki's gallery. According to the wiki, he stands next to John Marston on the cover art of the Game of the Year Edition. Allende does not appear in Red Dead Redemption 2 or in Red Dead Online, which are set before 1911; the Red Dead Wiki lists him only in Red Dead Redemption.
Trivia
According to the mission page of "Mexican Caesar", Allende bends over and vomits at the end of the opening cutscene.
According to the same mission page, the gates of Allende's villa stay locked from "Mexican Caesar" until "An Appointed Time", and the player cannot enter. The wiki's page on Escalera dates the closing to after "The Gates of El Presidio" and says soldiers then patrol the town in greater numbers.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, rebel propaganda posters in Nuevo Paraíso attack Allende: one calls him the lapdog of General Ignacio Sanchez, another the puppet of the dictator Sanchez. The wiki's page on Sanchez places these posters in Nosalida.
According to the mission page of "An Appointed Time", if the player lassoes Bill while Allende is pinning him down, Reyes shoots both men, which can sometimes make the mission impossible to complete.
According to the same page, in rare cases, when John shoots Bill, Reyes does not shoot Allende at once: Allende pleads, runs off into the brush beside the road and is shot in the back by Reyes, outside of any cutscene. In other cases, Allende runs off and Reyes does not shoot him either, which leaves the player stuck until the last checkpoint is reloaded.
According to the mission page, Allende's body sometimes stays on the road east of Escalera after the mission, wherever the stagecoach was stopped. It cannot be searched, but if it is set on fire with fire bottles, screams come from the body.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Allende's portrait still hangs inside his villa after his death.
According to the mission page of "An Appointed Time", the gates of Allende's mansion are still lying on the ground in pieces when the player returns to Escalera in 1914.
According to the same page, shortly after the mission, a random encounter can show army soldiers executing rebels for speaking out against Allende, although he is already dead.
Allende does not appear in Undead Nightmare, the non-canon expansion. According to the wiki's Undead Nightmare page, Reyes has moved into Allende's former mansion in Escalera by then.
