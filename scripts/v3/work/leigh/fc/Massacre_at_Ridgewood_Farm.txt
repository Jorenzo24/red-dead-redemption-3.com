{{Infobox Newspaper article|Title=Massacre at Ridgewood Farm|Image=|Newspaper=[[Blackwater Ledger No. 55]]|Last=|Next=}}'''Massacre at Ridgewood Farm''' is one of the [[Newspapers|newspaper articles]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

== Text ==
MASSACRE AT RIDGEWOOD FARM
----A [[Williamson gang|group of bandits]] carried out a [[Spare the Rod, Spoil the Bandit|brutal massacre]] at [[Ridgewood Farm]] in [[Cholla Springs]]. Men and women were murdered in cold blood, seemingly with little motive. [[Armadillo]] lawmen stormed the farmhouse, defeated the criminals and freed the survivors. It has not yet been confirmed if this was the work of the Williamson gang; if not, beleaguered Armadillo Marshal [[Leigh Johnson]] has another serious problem on his hands.
