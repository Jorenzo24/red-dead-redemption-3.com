Simon Pearson

Van der Linde gang Alive in 1907 Cook RDR2

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Simon Pearson is the cook and butcher of the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2. A former US Navy sailor, he keeps the camp fed and supplied.

He leaves the gang before its collapse and, by 1907, runs the general store in Rhodes.

Biography

Origins

According to the official guide, Pearson's father and grandfather hunted sperm whales. He meant to follow them, but the trade was dying out by the time he left school. He served briefly in the US Navy, moved west, ran into money trouble, and was invited into the gang by Dutch.

The camp's cook

Pearson sets up each new camp with Susan Grimshaw and regularly sends Arthur out for meat. Through the camp ledger, he also crafts satchels and camp decorations. In chapter 1, with food running out in the mountains, he sends Arthur and Charles to hunt deer, then helps Arthur butcher the carcasses.

In chapter 3, an argument with Sadie Adler ends with Arthur taking her to Rhodes for supplies and to post Pearson's letter to his aunt. In chapter 4, Dutch orders him, Charles and Reverend Swanson to bury Kieran Duffy.

Leaving the gang

At Beaver Hollow, Susan Grimshaw orders Pearson and Bill to burn the body of Molly O'Shea. Shortly afterwards, Pearson leaves with Uncle and Mary-Beth. At the start of "Our Best Selves", Dutch announces their departure and calls them cowards.

1907

By 1907, Pearson is married and runs the general store in Rhodes, where John Marston can talk to him.

Development and performance

Pearson is played by Jim Santangeli, an American actor, writer and director, who provided both the voice and the motion capture. Santangeli had previously voiced a pedestrian in Grand Theft Auto IV. He took part in a panel with the game's cast at SacAnime in Sacramento in June 2019.

In 2026, Santangeli said during a TikTok livestream that he has a small part in Grand Theft Auto VI, as reported by GTA BOOM in June 2026.
