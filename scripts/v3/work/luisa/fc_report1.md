## Story guide RDR1 Act 2 (EN + FR): statements about Luisa

All Luisa statements are correct (El Presidio rescue at her request; shot dead in Escalera in "An Appointed Time" trying to save Reyes; rebel side), with one wrong framing and one imprecision:

G1. WRONG (role). EN: "Against them stands a rebel movement led by Abraham Reyes, a well-spoken young revolutionary, and Luisa Fortuna, a farm girl who believes in him completely." She does not co-lead the movement (Reyes leads it; she is a rebel close to him, per Ricketts), and she is presented as a teacher (Ricketts, transcript; Rockstar Newswire 2010), not a "farm girl".
- EN fix: "Against them stands a rebel movement led by Abraham Reyes, a well-spoken young revolutionary. Luisa Fortuna, a young teacher who fights with the rebels, believes in him completely."
- FR current: "En face, une rébellion est menée par Abraham Reyes, jeune révolutionnaire beau parleur, et par Luisa Fortuna, une paysanne qui croit en lui sans réserve."
- FR fix: "En face, une rébellion est menée par Abraham Reyes, jeune révolutionnaire beau parleur. Luisa Fortuna, jeune institutrice engagée chez les rebelles, croit en lui sans réserve."

G2. IMPRECISE (FR only). FR: "Luisa Fortuna est abattue en tentant de le sauver du peloton." In "An Appointed Time" Reyes is being beaten by Zubieta and his men before his execution; there is no firing squad (that was El Presidio). EN says "from execution", correctly.
- FR fix: "Luisa Fortuna est abattue en tentant de le sauver de l'exécution."

(Optional, not Luisa-specific: "Reyes promises the same thing, and sends him to raid army convoys and free prisoners." The prisoner rescue, "The Mexican Wagon Train", is given by Ricketts on Luisa's report, and the convoy ambush is passed on by Luisa for Reyes. Not wrong enough to require a change.)
