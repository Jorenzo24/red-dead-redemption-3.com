## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **Blackwater Ledger No. 55 (L195)**: EN wiki says the article "suggests her kidnapping was a crime of passion". The article text
   (`_m/Kidnapped_Woman_Found_Alive.txt`) says initial reports indicated a crime of passion but that, as "a spinster of 29 years", this
   "appears implausible". **Fiche right.**
2. **Suitors (L23)**: EN wiki "she had many suitors" all too countrified. Transcript (wiki's own ref, Wild Horses): "Some, I suppose...
   Amos, he's a little... countrified". **Fiche right.**
3. **John says "Bonnie" (L59)**: wiki trivia "only twice", and the Paternal Pride page says Women and Cattle + Paternal Pride. Transcripts
   show at least two in the main game: Women and Cattle ("I've said too much, Bonnie", l.31) AND Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane ("Bonnie, are
   you okay?", l.167). **Both wrong as written; FIX L59**: name both RDR1 occasions; Paternal Pride stays "according to its mission page".
4. **Undead Nightmare tone (L51)**: Bonnie page says "unfazed / nonchalant"; Paternal Pride page says she greets him "with open arms ...
   relieved". Wikis disagree and both are interpretation. **FIX L51**: drop "seems unmoved by the epidemic"; her greeting line ("First hell
   walks the earth...", L161) already shows the scene.
5. **FR wiki: married by 1914** vs EN wiki "fate unspecified". Nothing in the game supports a marriage. **Fiche right** (L49).
6. **FR wiki: John goes with Abigail to buy the cattle**. The Outlaw's Return transcript: it is Jack. **Fiche right** (L45).
7. **FR wiki: barn "under construction" in 1899, age 15**. EN wiki: barn not yet built in 1907. Fiche L196 says no barn in 1898/1899/1907
   (neutral). **Fiche fine.**
8. **Rescue at Fort Mercer**: EN wiki "Bonnie rescues John"; FR wiki and Exodus in America: with Amos. **Fiche right** (with Amos).
9. **Age 27 (L21)**: the This is Armadillo dialogue cache lacks the "27 years old" line, so the hedge is correct; but the GOTY guide (primary,
   quoted L54) gives 27. **Optional FIX L21**: "the official guide gives her age as 27 in 1911", rather than only the wiki.
   Same for governess/French-Russian (L23) and "own bed / patrolling / everyone armed" (L24): not in the cached transcripts, hedge stays.
