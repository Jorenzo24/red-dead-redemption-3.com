
Archer Fordham is an agent of the Bureau of Investigation who works under Edgar Ross. He first appears in Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911, and returns in the end credits of Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1907. There, he and Ross find the body of Micah Bell on Mount Hagen, question witnesses in Valentine and near Rhodes, and end up watching John Marston and his son Jack at Beecher's Hope.
In 1911, while the Bureau holds John's wife and son, Fordham and Ross put John on the train for Armadillo, collect Javier Escuella from him at the border, then hunt Dutch van der Linde with him in West Elizabeth, up to Dutch's death at Cochinay. He is voiced by David Wilson Barnes.
Key facts
In the 1907 end credits of Red Dead Redemption 2, he and Ross trace Micah's death to John Marston at Beecher's Hope.
In "The Gates of El Presidio", he takes Javier from John near the border bridge, alive or dead depending on the player, and sends John after Bill Williamson.
In "Bear One Another's Burdens", he drives John and Ross to the wreck of the Serendipity and finds the informant Nastas there with John.
He takes part in the ambush at the Blackwater bank and in the assault on Cochinay, and thanks John once Dutch is dead.
According to the English-language Red Dead Wiki, he is visible among the men facing John through the barn doors at Beecher's Hope later in 1911; the French-language wiki says he is not there.

Biography
Origins
Archer Fordham is an agent of the Bureau of Investigation who works under Edgar Ross. The games give no birth date and no birthplace for him, and they never show how he came to the Bureau. The English-language Red Dead Wiki says he joined it in 1907, the year it was founded, and calls him Ross's protégé; the French-language wiki only says he joined at some point before 1907. In Red Dead Redemption 2, newspapers of the epilogue period report the creation of the Bureau and Ross's appointment at its head (see the Edgar Ross fiche).
According to the Red Dead Wiki, his mother, Maybelle Fordham, died on July 24, 1900 and is buried in the Blackwater cemetery. The games say nothing else about his family. Fordham does not appear in the 1899 chapters of Red Dead Redemption 2, where Ross works for the Pinkerton Detective Agency alongside Andrew Milton. His first appearance in the story's chronology is in the end credits of 1907.
Red Dead Redemption 2: the epilogue (1907)
Epilogue II: Beecher's Hope
In "American Venom", the last mission of the epilogue, John Marston, Sadie Adler and Charles Smith reach Micah Bell's hideout on Mount Hagen, where Dutch van der Linde shoots Micah and John finishes him. Fordham appears in the end credits that follow, always beside Ross. The Red Dead Wiki dates these scenes to June 1907, from a letter of the mayor of Saint Denis received during the epilogue. No transcript of the credits exists on the wiki. In the first of their scenes, the two agents climb to the hideout and find Micah's body lying face down beside the cabin. According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on the credits, Ross tells Fordham to search the cabin while he examines the body. Fordham goes inside, and Ross crouches by the body, lifts it and sets it back down. Fordham comes back out with a shrug, and they leave.
Later scenes of the credits follow the two agents through the region. In Smithfield's Saloon in Valentine, the bartender points them to two customers, a woman and the auctioneer Morris Peyton, whom they question before leaving. Near Rhodes, they stop a stagecoach and talk to its driver; according to a caption on the Red Dead Wiki, Ross hands one of the passengers his card. The last of their scenes is set at Beecher's Hope, John's ranch: John is grooming a horse with his son Jack in the corral. From a distance, Ross and Fordham watch them through binoculars, nod to each other and walk away.
Red Dead Redemption (1911)
In 1911, Fordham is stationed in Blackwater, West Elizabeth, with Ross, who has his office on the upper floor of the police station. Before the game opens, the Bureau has taken John's wife, Abigail, and his son, Jack, from Beecher's Hope. It holds them to force John to track down the former members of the Van der Linde gang. The games do not show the abduction, nor Fordham's part in it.
Act I: New Austin
The game opens on "Exodus in America". John steps off the ferry Morningstar in Blackwater between two men who walk him through the town to the station and see him onto a train for Armadillo, in New Austin. The transcript calls them two lawmen; the mission page identifies them as Ross and Fordham. In Armadillo, a guide named Jake tells John that the town's marshal, Leigh Johnson, hired him after receiving a telegram from some important people in Blackwater who wanted a guide. John's first target is Bill Williamson, whose gang holds Fort Mercer. Fordham does not appear again in the first act.
Act II: Nuevo Paraíso
Bill escapes to Mexico, where Javier Escuella also lives. At the end of "The Gates of El Presidio", John either captures Javier or kills him, depending on the player's choice. He then meets Ross and Fordham near the border bridge, where they are waiting with an automobile. If Javier is alive, Fordham congratulates John, tells Javier he will see how far the hand of justice reaches and orders him into the car. When John asks whether one of his commitments is now cleared, Fordham answers that nothing is cleared until his obligations are met.
If John brings back Javier's body, Fordham gives him a disappointed look, remarks that the man does not look very healthy and reminds him that they told him to keep them alive. John replies that they should be glad to have him at all. Fordham opens the car door, tells John to remember his obligations to the government and closes it once John has thrown the body onto the back seat. In both versions, Fordham tells John to find Williamson and then return to Blackwater as quickly as he can, because the Bureau believes Dutch van der Linde is in the area. Ross adds that John's wife sends her regards, and the agents drive off with Javier. Abraham Reyes is not present at this meeting.
Act III: West Elizabeth
After Bill's death near Escalera, John reports to the police station in Blackwater in "Bear One Another's Burdens". Fordham greets him upstairs and asks about his journey. John demands to see his wife and son; Fordham says they are being well looked after and that Ross wants to speak to him about important developments. When John threatens to shoot him in the head on the spot, Fordham asks him to calm down. Ross takes over and explains that the deal covers every man of the old gang, Dutch included. Fordham adds that Dutch is believed to be holed up with a group of renegades near the wreck of the Serendipity, a riverboat. The three men leave in the agents' automobile, with Fordham at the wheel.
On the road, Fordham repeats to Ross a maxim he attributes to him about high stakes and playing the game smartly, and Ross answers that he cannot imagine having said anything so trite. Fordham says John is not broad-minded enough to appreciate the opportunity he is being offered, and tells him to watch his mouth when John insults him. When John asks where it will all end, Fordham answers that it ends when the agents decide, and that John is in no position to make demands. He tells John that cattle now mostly travel by rail and that he is not from anywhere, then says he does not understand people like him, who refuse the benefits of progress out of loyalty to a forgotten era. John retorts that the alternative is to pretend to be something one is not, working in an office and taking orders from suits who have never been east of Chicago.
Ross stays with the car on the cliff above the wreck while Fordham and John go down on foot. Fordham tells John to do as he says, sets the plan (take Dutch down quickly and get out) and warns him of the consequences if he steps out of line. The wreck is quiet. Fordham suggests that Dutch may have caught wind of the operation and says the informant had better not have lied to them. He notes that the place is usually full of lowlifes, hears a noise and sends John up to look while he waits below. John finds a man tied up on an upper deck, with a bag over his head according to the mission page. Fordham recognizes the Bureau's informant, Nastas, who warns them that it is a trap. Dutch's men surround them. Fordham tells John to carry Nastas, saying he does not think the man can walk.
Fordham leads the way back to the car once the attackers are down. In the car, he explains to Ross that it was a trap, that the man is the informant and that he speaks English; Ross tells him not to be snarky. John mocks the plan of sending two men against a whole gang, one of them an office clerk, and Fordham tells him to shut his mouth. The engine then fails. Ross calls Fordham a fool and orders him to fix it while he and John hold off Dutch's riders. Fordham gets the car running again. On the way back, Ross blames the breakdown on his driving, and Fordham answers that he was trying to escape an ambush.
Ross decides to take Nastas to Harold MacDougal. In Blackwater, Fordham presents the professor's office to John as that of an anthropologist thrown out of Yale, who helps the agents deal with the Native Americans of the area. He asks John for a hand with Nastas, promises MacDougal a doctor and tells him to find out what Nastas knows about Dutch, adding that he had better do so.
In "Great Men are Not Always Wise", an informant tells Ross that Dutch is about to call on the manager of the Blackwater bank. Fordham suggests taking position on a roof facing it, from where John shoots the gang members as they come out. Ross then sends John inside with two lawmen, Dell Hopkins and Benton Manning. Dutch shoots a hostage, the bank employee Muriel Scranton, and escapes. As the posse mounts up, Fordham reports that Dutch has fled in an automobile with the bank manager, and asks John whether Dutch has changed. When John asks whether the agents are lawmen or army, Fordham answers that they are neither, and Ross adds that he has authority over both.
The posse finds Dutch's car burning by the road, the manager's body beside it according to the mission page, and fights the gang members waiting further on, at Bearclaw Camp per the same page. Dutch escapes. Issue No. 60 of the Blackwater Ledger reports the events under the headline "Bank Robbery Foiled by Government Agents". It places the second fight "at an old logging camp at Broken Tree" (the mission page calls this an error) and names no agent. It reports that "a spokesperson for federal agents assigned to Blackwater hailed this as an important victory over 'the primitive and unprincipled forces that seek to undermine the freedoms and safety of the great American people.'" Its Miscellany column announces that the bank manager, Harmon Weinstein, "has been found dead near Broken Tree".
In "And You Will Know The Truth", John finds the agents preparing their weapons in the office. When Ross tells him he has failed, Fordham calls John a public menace and says they should have had him killed. During Ross's speech on civilization, Fordham calls Dutch a killer and asks what the alternative to the rules would be. The three men then leave Blackwater in a vehicle armed with a machine gun, which John mans. On the way, Fordham praises the progress of the armed forces, calls the vehicle a simple prototype and mentions what is being developed in Virginia. Ross explains that the army has made camp outside town and spread word that a large store of ammunition and food is kept there. Fordham adds that Dutch's gang needs constant supplies, so the bait should draw it in. Dutch's men attack the camp and are driven back.
The vehicle then gives chase off the road. Ross asks Fordham whether he can hold it steady, and Fordham answers that he is doing his best. He warns that a wagon is heading straight for the cavalry; John destroys it. An explosion set off by the gang then overturns the vehicle. According to the mission page, John, Ross and Fordham come out of it slightly injured.
"And The Truth Will Set You Free" follows directly. Picking himself up, Fordham calls Dutch insane. He then rides with Ross, John and a cavalry detachment led by a captain to Dutch's fort at Cochinay. According to the mission page, the conversations heard on the ride depend on John's position in the column. Fordham may tell John that this is what comes of mixing with "savages", and ask how he could ever follow such a man; John asks him the same question about Ross. Fordham calls Dutch a psychopath, a murderer and a rapist, asks John what made Dutch this way, tells John that somebody is always watching and that the Bureau has been watching him, blames him for wandering around Mexico instead of simply killing Bill at Fort Mercer, and suggests that his wife could always go out to work.
At Cochinay, Fordham is with the attackers when the soldiers blow the outer gate, and urges them on. John then goes on alone to face Dutch, who throws down his gun on a cliff edge and lets himself fall. At the foot of the cliff, Ross fires John's gun into the body for his report and tells John that his family has been sent back to Beecher's Hope. Fordham thanks John for everything, says he knows it was not easy and that John has done his country proud. Ross then calls Archer away, and the agents leave John to ride home.
Some time after John's return home, in "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed", soldiers and lawmen led by Ross attack Beecher's Hope. Uncle is killed, Abigail and Jack escape on one horse, and John is shot down by a firing squad at the barn door. The transcript does not name Fordham. According to the English-language Red Dead Wiki, Fordham is visible among the men facing John through the barn doors at Beecher's Hope in 1911 (he can be shot in the final Dead Eye and may disappear from the cutscene that follows); the French-language wiki says he does not take part in the mission (see Trivia).
Red Dead Redemption: 1914
Fordham does not appear in the part of the game set in 1914, when Jack tracks down the retired Ross, and, according to the Red Dead Wiki, no character mentions him. The Blackwater Ledger & Sentinel No. 213, which announces Ross's retirement, does not name him either. It credits Ross alone with "eradicating Native unrest in the region and eliminating some of the West's most wanted and dangerous criminals, including Bill Williamson, Dutch van Der Linde, Javier Esquella and John Marston". The games say nothing of Fordham's fate after 1911.
Personality
The Red Dead Wiki quotes the description of Fordham given in the Game of the Year Edition guide of Red Dead Redemption: "Archer is a young, sturdy, and keen-eyed government agent. He is Ross's right-hand man and he is very ambitious and aggressive." In the game, he often calls Ross "sir" and agrees with his proposals. He mostly addresses John as Mr. Marston, uses plain Marston in orders and insults, and calls him John only once, at the border bridge in the branch where Javier is captured.
He threatens John several times. He defends the Bureau's methods and the progress of the age: motorcars, rail transport of cattle, the army's new weapons. He says Dutch van der Linde is insane, calls him a psychopath, and speaks of "savages" when he asks John how he could follow such a man (see Biography).
In his own words
On the drive to the wreck of the Serendipity, in "Bear One Another's Burdens": "I don't understand you people. The trappings of progress are here for the taking and you're too stubborn to take them." Leaving Blackwater in the armed vehicle, in "And You Will Know The Truth": "Our armed forces have no equal. We have made incredible progress the past few years." Then, about the vehicle: "And this isn't much more than a simple prototype. You should see what they're working on in Virginia. Soon there will be no war we can't win." On the ride to Cochinay, in "And The Truth Will Set You Free": "There's always somebody watching, Mr. Marston. I thought you'd have gleaned that much by now."
Names
The cast list of the Red Dead Redemption manual names him Archer Fordham, and the Red Dead Wiki calls him Agent Archer Fordham. Ross addresses him as "Mr. Fordham" or simply Fordham, calls him "Agent Fordham" when he tells John to follow him on foot to the wreck, and uses his first name, "Archer", twice: when he says that he and Archer are the bad guys, and when he leads him away from the foot of the cliff at Cochinay. Harold MacDougal greets him as "Mr. Fordham". In the automobile, John describes one of the two men sent onto the wreck as "an office clerk or social secretary or some such".
Appearance
Fordham is a dark-haired, clean-shaven man. In 1907 he wears a charcoal three-piece suit, a white shirt, a black tie and a bowler hat. In Red Dead Redemption (1911), his three-piece suit is brown and his tie dark red. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he is much taller than Ross in Red Dead Redemption.
Relationships
Edgar Ross
Fordham is Ross's subordinate at the Bureau of Investigation, beside him in almost all his scenes, from 1907 to 1911 (see Biography). He drives for him and backs him against John. Ross often belittles him, from the maxim he denies having coined to the blame for the car's breakdown, yet counts him on his own side when he tells John that the two of them are the ones who enforce the rules.
John Marston
Fordham is one of the two agents who force John to hunt his former gang in 1911, and gives him most of his orders in the field (see Biography). Their exchanges are hostile on both sides until Dutch's death, when Fordham thanks him. He had already watched John at Beecher's Hope with Ross in 1907. According to the English-language Red Dead Wiki, Fordham is visible among the men facing John through the barn doors at Beecher's Hope in 1911 (he can be shot in the final Dead Eye and may disappear from the cutscene that follows); the French-language wiki says he does not take part in the mission.
Abigail Marston
The Bureau holds Abigail while John works for it; Fordham never sees her on screen. He fends off John's questions about her and, on the ride to Cochinay, makes a jibe about sending her out to work (see Biography).
Jack Marston
In the 1907 end credits, Fordham and Ross watch Jack and his father at Beecher's Hope (see Biography). In 1911 the Bureau holds Jack with his mother until Dutch's death.
Micah Bell
Fordham never meets him alive: he searches Micah's cabin on Mount Hagen in 1907 while Ross examines the body (see Biography).
Javier Escuella
Fordham takes charge of Javier near the border bridge after El Presidio, alive or dead depending on the player, and greets the two outcomes differently (see Biography).
Bill Williamson
Fordham never meets Bill, the first target the Bureau gives John; he sends John after him from the border, and on the ride to Cochinay reproaches John for running all over Mexico instead of killing Bill at Fort Mercer (see Biography).
Dutch van der Linde
Dutch is the target of Fordham's whole part of the hunt in 1911, from the wreck of the Serendipity to Cochinay, but the two men never come face to face (see Biography). Fordham first mentions him in neutral terms: he tells John at the border that "Dutch van der Linde is in the area", calls him the priority on the way down to the wreck and asks John whether Dutch has changed. Later, on the ride to Cochinay, he calls him a psychopath, a murderer and a rapist. John answers that Dutch was a good man once, a far better man than Fordham (see Quotes).
Nastas
For Fordham, Nastas is the Bureau's informant on Dutch's gang; he recognizes him on the wreck of the Serendipity and vouches for him to Ross (see Biography).
Harold MacDougal
The professor works with the agents on the Native Americans of the region; Fordham introduces him to John and puts the wounded Nastas in his care (see Biography).
Weapons and the automobile
Weapons
The Red Dead Wiki lists three weapons for him: a Cattleman Revolver, a High Power Pistol and a Winchester Repeater. According to the same wiki, he carries the Cattleman Revolver in his earlier missions and the High Power Pistol later, like Ross. The page of "Great Men are Not Always Wise" notes that Ross and Fordham use the Winchester Repeater, while the sheriffs and marshals of the mission carry the older Repeater Carbine.
Behind the wheel
Fordham is the agents' driver: he is at the wheel of the automobile in "Bear One Another's Burdens" and of the armed vehicle in "And You Will Know The Truth" (see Biography). The page of "Bear One Another's Burdens" says he gets the stalled car going again with the crank. The same page notes that he can also start it with the ignition without leaving his seat, a feature few cars of the period had.
In the game
Three of his missions fail if John attacks or kills him (conditions in the Missions table). According to the English-language Red Dead Wiki, Fordham is visible among the men facing John through the barn doors at Beecher's Hope in 1911 (he can be shot in the final Dead Eye and may disappear from the cutscene that follows); the French-language wiki says he does not take part in the mission. The page of "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed" adds that Ross cannot be killed there.
Missions
Fordham appears in six story missions of Red Dead Redemption, numbered 1, 40, 42, 43, 47 and 48 in the story order, and gives none of them. The last four belong to Edgar Ross's strand, which opens the West Elizabeth part of the game and ends with the death of Dutch van der Linde. The English-language Red Dead Wiki, but not the French-language one, also places him in the last story mission, number 57. In Red Dead Redemption 2 he appears only in the end credits that follow "American Venom".

Development and performance
Casting
The cast list printed in the manual of Red Dead Redemption (2010) credits David Wilson Barnes as Archer Fordham, next to Jim Bentley as Edgar Ross. Wikipedia's article on the game gives the same credit, and the Red Dead Wiki lists Barnes as his voice. No source consulted credits Barnes with the motion capture of the character. No performer credit for Fordham in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) was found; Wikipedia's list of the game's characters does not mention him.
According to Wikipedia, David Wilson Barnes was an American actor born on October 30, 1972, in San Diego, California. His film roles include Capote (2005), The Company Men (2010), The Bourne Legacy (2012) and Bridge of Spies (2015), and he had recurring roles in the television series Hell on Wheels and The Son. Red Dead Redemption is the only video game in his Wikipedia credits. He died of cancer in New York City on July 30, 2025, aged 52, a death reported by The Express on September 1, 2025.
Cut content
The files of Red Dead Redemption contain a cutscene, labeled fbi03, that was not used. In it, Ross scoffs at the idea of women wanting the vote, and Fordham answers that his Bessie does; Ross tells him to make sure she stays his Bessie and that he does not become hers. According to the Red Dead Wiki, subtitles left in the files of "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed" suggest an earlier version of the mission in which Fordham was to kill Rufus, the family dog.
The files of Red Dead Redemption 2 contain an unused 1899 version of Fordham, with a red vest, gloves, trousers and a different haircut. The Red Dead Wiki notes that this model carries a Pinkerton badge and reads it as a sign that he was first meant to appear as a Pinkerton agent before the epilogue. According to the same wiki, a cutscene of the end credits was meant to show him and Ross questioning townspeople and Sheriff Hanley in Strawberry; the wiki adds that Hanley may only be a placeholder in the cutscene files.
The French-language Red Dead Wiki reports an unused line for a passer-by found in the game files: "I hear that Bureau fella's never been the same since his mother got shot in the massacre". The line names neither Fordham nor the massacre in question. The same wiki links it to Fordham and to the Blackwater massacre of 1899, a reading it presents itself as a possibility.
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
Fordham is not a playable character in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption. He is absent from the fifteen story characters added to the Outfitter by the Liars and Cheats Pack, released on September 21, 2010, which includes Edgar Ross, and from the eight added by the free Myths and Mavericks Bonus Pack, released on September 13, 2011. The versions of the game released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, have no multiplayer mode.
Red Dead Redemption 2
Red Dead Redemption 2, released on October 26, 2018, is a prequel set in 1899 and 1907. Fordham appears only in its end credits, eight years after his first appearance in Red Dead Redemption (May 18, 2010 in North America). The Red Dead Wiki lists no appearance of Fordham in Red Dead Online or in Undead Nightmare.
Trivia
Five of the missions in which Fordham appears take their titles from the Bible, as their mission pages note: "Bear One Another's Burdens" (Galatians 6:2), "Great Men are Not Always Wise" (Job 32:9), "And You Will Know The Truth" and "And The Truth Will Set You Free" (John 8:32) and "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed" (1 Corinthians 15:26).
According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on the end credits of Red Dead Redemption 2, Ross and Fordham both wear Pinkerton badges in their 1907 scenes, although a newspaper of the epilogue reports that Ross now heads the Bureau of Investigation.
According to the mission page of "Americans at Rest" (Red Dead Redemption 2, chapter 2), the woman Ross and Fordham talk to in the Valentine saloon during the credits is one of the two women Javier Escuella is talking to at the bar in that mission.
The two Red Dead Wikis disagree about Fordham's presence in "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed". The English-language wiki places him among the men at the barn. It also names a U.S. marshal, Isaac McKinnon, who looks and dresses like Fordham and stands beside Ross in the last cutscene, and notes that the two are often confused. The French-language wiki holds that Fordham is not in the mission at all and that the man beside Ross is McKinnon.
