Karen Jones

Van der Linde gang Con artist Fate unknown RDR2

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Karen Jones is a thief and gunwoman of the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2. The game's official guide presents her as a skilled scam artist who enjoys the outlaw life and looks out for the other women of the gang.

Her fate after the gang breaks up is never shown.

Biography

Scams and robberies

Karen often plays a part in the gang's scams. During the trip to Valentine in chapter 2, a man she tries to rob at the Saints Hotel hits her, and Arthur deals with him. After Sean MacGuire is rescued and brought back to camp, the two begin a brief affair.

In chapter 3, she robs the Valentine bank with Arthur, Bill Williamson and Lenny Summers, in the mission "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah". She planned the job with Bill. The player chooses whether she distracts the staff by playing a drunk or a lost young woman.

After Sean

Sean is shot dead in Rhodes in "A Short Walk in a Pretty Town". From then on, Karen drinks heavily and is often found drunk in camp. She leaves the gang as it falls apart at Beaver Hollow.

Fate

The game never shows what becomes of Karen. In a letter to John Marston in 1907, Tilly Jackson writes that she has had no news of her and believes drink killed her. This is Tilly's guess, not a fact established by the game.

Relationships

Sean MacGuire

Her brief lover. His death in Rhodes sends her into heavy drinking.

Bill Williamson

Plans the Valentine bank job with her.

Arthur Morgan

Rides with her on the Valentine bank job.

Tilly Jackson

Her close friend, who later writes that she fears Karen drank herself to death.

Susan Grimshaw

Runs the camp's women; Karen turns on her after Molly's death.

Development and performance

Karen is played by Jo Armeniox, who provided both the voice and the motion capture.

In a joint interview with the gang's actresses published by GamesRadar in March 2019, Armeniox recalled her scenes with Roger Clark, who plays Arthur: "it was really rare also to have my own time with Roger and that was really special." In the same piece, Penny O'Brien, who plays Molly O'Shea, says Armeniox had to slap her twice in one scene.

In a video interview with Popternative in November 2019, she said she drew on an angry, rebellious side of herself for the role.
