Development and performance
Casting
Abraham Reyes appears in Red Dead Redemption and its expansion Undead Nightmare, both released in 2010. Wikipedia credits Josh Segarra with the role, in its article on the game and in his own article. Segarra is an American actor of Puerto Rican descent, born on June 3, 1986 in Longwood, Florida, and a graduate of New York University's Tisch School of the Arts. At the time of Red Dead Redemption he played Hector Ruiz in the PBS children's series The Electric Company (2009 to 2011). He later created the role of Emilio Estefan in the Broadway musical On Your Feet!, which opened on November 5, 2015, played Adrian Chase in the fifth season of Arrow (2016 to 2017), and appeared in She-Hulk: Attorney at Law (2022) and Scream VI (2023). No interview in which he discusses the role was found, and no source consulted names a performer for Reyes's motion capture.
Before release
In the screenshots Rockstar released before launch, his hair and goatee are shorter than in the released game, according to the Red Dead Wiki. The wiki's page on the game's cut content adds that an unused bounty poster of Reyes was left in the game files, with a goatee model that is not used in the final game.
Reception
Reviews
Game Informer's review of Red Dead Redemption, written by Matt Bertz and published on May 17, 2010, gave the game 9.75 out of 10. Bertz does not name Reyes, but describes the Mexican part of the story as John having to "get cozy with both the corrupt ruler of the border region and the upstart rebel hell-bent on taking power for himself." He judged that this "tangential plot drags on far too long without engaging the task at hand, and the game's momentum suffers for it."
Character lists
In a list of the ten best characters of the Red Dead Redemption franchise, published by Sportskeeda and last updated on August 13, 2023, Aniket Mukherjee ranked Reyes tenth, just behind Irish (ninth). He wrote that Reyes "is ranked 10th because of his significant contribution to the Mexican revolution, which adds depth to the Red Dead Redemption's narrative by introducing political intrigue."
Academic study
In "The Mustang Diaries: A Study of Mexico in Red Dead Redemption 1", published in the Revista Panamericana de Comunicación (vol. 4, no. 2, published on October 21, 2022), Javier Aldape, of Texas A&M University, reads Allende and Reyes as the two sides of the caudillo figure. He calls Reyes "The revolutionary Messiah who will restore peace to Mexico" and writes: "He womanizes and makes the same promise to all of them, which he never keeps." He also notes that Reyes is "more concerned about corridos of him than the heroic deeds of the soldaderas", the women who fought in the revolution.
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
Reyes became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Liars and Cheats Pack, detailed by Rockstar on August 27, 2010 and released on September 21, 2010. Players select him in the Damnation section of the Outfitter, with Agustin Allende, Bill Williamson, Dutch van der Linde, Edgar Ross, Harold MacDougal and Irish. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he sometimes speaks of himself in the third person in multiplayer, and many of his lines are in Spanish (see Quotes). The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, contain the story and Undead Nightmare but not the multiplayer mode, so the Reyes model is not available in them.
Marketing
Rockstar released official artwork of Reyes for Red Dead Redemption, and pre-release screenshots that show him, one of them with John. Reyes does not appear in Red Dead Redemption 2 or in Red Dead Online, which are set before 1911; the Red Dead Wiki lists him only in Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare.
Trivia
Red Dead Redemption
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the opening of "The Gates of El Presidio" is the only sex scene shown on screen in Red Dead Redemption. According to the mission page, the Game of the Year Edition covered the woman's breasts with clothing, a change reverted in later versions of the game.
When "The Gates of El Presidio" is replayed, Reyes always speaks as if the rebels, not John, had killed De Santa, according to the mission page.
According to the mission page of "Must a Savior Die?", hitting Reyes with the horse while picking him up fails the mission for being too aggressive.
According to the mission page of "The Great Mexican Train Robbery", if John fires his gun inside the armored car, Reyes asks him whether he is shooting at imaginary soldiers.
According to the mission page of "An Appointed Time", in rare cases, when John shoots Bill, Allende begs, runs into the brush and is shot in the back by Reyes outside of any cutscene.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Drew MacFarlane sometimes wears the same outfit as Reyes in "Must a Savior Die?" and "An Appointed Time".
Undead Nightmare (non-canon)
According to the Red Dead Wiki, the undead Reyes is the thinnest of all the Bruisers in the game. He also appears to be the only undead with two sets of sounds: he groans like the ordinary undead while idle, and roars like the Bruisers when he charges.
According to the mission page of "A Civilized Man", the undead Reyes's body cannot be searched: after the cutscene that follows his death it stays inside the mansion, whose doors are locked until the mission is over. In the catacombs, John picks up a Semi-auto Shotgun and remarks that Reyes brought protection.
