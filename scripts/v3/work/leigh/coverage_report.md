## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **When Bill left Fort Mercer.** Wiki: "a few days prior to the attack". Fiche L44: "the morning before". Fiche is right: Jonah, "Bill's already run off to Mexico yesterday morning" (The_Assault_on_Fort_Mercer_dialogues.txt; also HOUSE_FACTS.md).
2. **Javier's role.** Wiki: Bill went to Mexico "with the help of Javier Escuella". Fiche L44: John concludes Bill has gone to join Javier. Fiche is right: Marston, "He's gone to see Javier Escuella" (same transcript); no mention of help.
3. **Farewell.** Wiki: "Johnson and Marston thank each other". Fiche L44/L166: Johnson apologizes ("I'm sorry about this, John"), John says goodbye. Fiche is right (same transcript: no thanks exchanged at Fort Mercer).
4. **Railroad salary.** Wiki Background: the railroad threatens "to cut the marshal's salary if he doesn't turn a blind eye". Transcript (Political_Realities_in_Armadillo_dialogues.txt l.73): "the people who pay my salary trying to get me to turn a blind eye". No threat. Fiche L31 is right.
5. **Baxter Deaton and the women-beating rumor.** Wiki says Deaton "talks about Johnson beating the local women". The quote names "a certain Marshal around these parts" (Baxter_Deaton.txt l.30; the wiki itself captions it "probably gossiping about Leigh Johnson"). Fiche L194 ("an unnamed marshal") is the accurate version.
6. **Two-holster gun belt.** Wiki Leigh page: shared only with Norman Deek. Fiche L197 adds Enepay and Benton Manning: right per Norman_Deek.txt l.48 (wiki-only, consistent with the wiki's own Deek page).
7. **Undead Nightmare ending.** Wiki body: "is not seen again"; wiki trivia: stays in his office making calls, can be killed. Fiche follows the trivia and the mission page (L50 "plays no further part in the story", L199). Consistent; nothing to fix.
8. **Age.** Wiki: born c. 1860 (1914 article, 54); GOTY guide 50; FR wiki 1861 and "50 in 1911". Fiche L12/L25 reports all three, attributed. OK.
9. **Mount.** Wiki infobox: Standardbred Pinto; trivia: Tersk, then Kentucky Saddler. Fiche L97 gives all, attributed. OK.
10. **Internal fiche inconsistency (not wiki):** A L43 and B L76 paraphrase the Fort Mercer line as "cold-blooded killer"; the transcript (and C L164) says "stone-cold killer". Align L43 and L76 on "stone-cold".
