Javier Escuella

Died 1911 Van der Linde gang Born in Nuevo Paraíso Voiced by Antonio Jaramillo and Gabriel Sloyer

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 18, 2026

Javier Escuella is a Mexican former bounty hunter and revolutionary, a gunman of the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), and one of the former gang members John Marston is forced to hunt down in Red Dead Redemption (2010). He flees Mexico after a killing, joins Dutch van der Linde around 1895 and stays loyal to him until the gang breaks apart in 1899. In 1911 he is back in Nuevo Paraíso under the protection of Colonel Allende, and John finds him at El Presidio in "The Gates of El Presidio". The player then chooses whether John kills him or captures him and hands him to the Bureau of Investigation.

He is voiced by Antonio Jaramillo in the first game, and by Gabriel Sloyer, who also provided his performance capture, in the prequel.

Key facts

  • Born in a village in Nuevo Paraíso, he kills a high-ranking army officer over a woman, is wanted for murder and treason in Punta Orgullo and flees to the United States around 1894.
  • Starving and unable to speak English, he meets Dutch van der Linde around 1895 as both men try to steal the same chickens; Dutch takes him into the gang.
  • In 1899 he rescues John Marston from the wolves with Arthur Morgan, takes part in the Saint Denis bank robbery and is shipwrecked on Guarma, where he is shot in the leg and captured before Dutch and Arthur free him.
  • When the gang breaks apart, he sides with Dutch against Arthur and John. A newspaper of 1907 reports that he is believed to live in the mountains of Mexico.
  • In 1911 he lives under the protection of Colonel Allende, and Bill Williamson flees to him in Mexico. John finds him during the rebel attack on El Presidio and, at the player's choice, kills him or captures him alive. Dead or alive, he is handed to Edgar Ross and Archer Fordham of the Bureau; John later tells Abigail he is dead, whichever choice was made.

Biography

Origins

The games give no birth date for Javier. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he was born in a small village in Nuevo Paraíso, the Mexican province of Red Dead Redemption. In "The Demon Drink", Colonel Agustin Allende tells John Marston that Escuella comes from his province and that his father was a drunk who worked as a laborer on land cultivated by Allende's uncle. The wiki, citing camp dialogue in Red Dead Redemption 2, describes his mother as a strict Catholic who did her best to keep her son in line. In "The Gates of El Presidio", Abraham Reyes also says that Javier is from the province.

The Red Dead Wiki, again citing camp dialogue, reports an event from his childhood: his maternal uncle and four other men were castrated and fed to pigs for suggesting that the villagers ask the local landowner for a fair wage. According to the same source, this strained his relationship with his mother, who went on accepting peasant life as it was even after her brother's death. Javier brings up his mother at a campfire at Clemens Point, in chapter 3. He later became a bounty hunter and a revolutionary.

Two documents explain why he left Mexico. His biography in the Collector's Edition of the Complete Official Guide says that he killed a high-ranking army officer over a woman, and that he cannot return until there has been a change in power. A Mexican wanted poster pinned on a wall at Shady Belle in chapter 4 seeks him dead or alive for murder and treason in Punta Orgullo, Nuevo Paraíso, with a reward of $1,000. In a camp conversation in the summer of 1899, Javier says that it has been nearly five years since he left Mexico, which places his departure around 1894. The wiki adds that he spoke no English when he crossed the border and soon grew weak and hungry.

According to the official guide, he joined Dutch van der Linde four years before 1899, which places their meeting around 1895. According to the Red Dead Wiki, which cites a camp story, Javier was trying to steal chickens when he came across Dutch, who was stealing from the same coop. The two men laughed about it. Dutch fed him, gave him clothes and offered him a place in the Van der Linde gang. He became one of the gang's gunmen. According to the wiki, Arthur Morgan mentions in "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah" a bank robbery in Tucson, Arizona, in the mid-1890s, carried out with Javier and Karen Jones: Karen went into the bank dressed as a nun, with two pistols hidden in her clothes, and the job succeeded.

Another job, recalled by Arthur and Javier during their fishing trip in chapter 3, did not go ahead. Before 1899, the two men set out to rob a stagecoach on a tip from a crooked lawman in Reno, Nevada. When they found it full of women and children, they let it pass. The O'Driscoll Boys robbed the same coach the next day and killed everyone aboard. At some point during these years, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Javier learned that his mother had died and that his sister had married. He says he misses them, and that he would probably be killed if he went home.

In the spring of 1899, the gang is camped outside the town of Blackwater, in West Elizabeth. Dutch hears of a ferry carrying a large sum of money and, encouraged by Micah Bell, who has recently joined, plans to rob it. Javier takes part in the job with John, Micah, Sean MacGuire and the brothers Mac and Davey Callander. Pinkerton agents arrive during the robbery. On the ferry, Javier, like John, sees Dutch kill a young woman, Heidi McCourt. The robbery ends in a heavy gunfight in the streets, and the gang flees north into the snowbound mountains of Ambarino, leaving most of the money behind.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)

Chapter 1: Colter

The gang takes shelter from a blizzard in Colter, an abandoned mining town in the Grizzlies. John has gone scouting and has not come back for two days. In "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", Abigail asks Arthur to look for him, and Arthur is reluctant. Hosea Matthews tells him at least to go and look, then asks Javier to ride out with him, calling them the two fittest men the gang has. Javier agrees, saying that John would look for him if their places were reversed. He hands Arthur a sawed-off shotgun, and Abigail thanks them both. On the trail, Arthur suggests that John may simply have kept riding north, as he has done before. Javier rejects the idea: John would not leave like that.

Javier leads the search. He spots smoke from an abandoned campsite, follows the horse tracks across a river and up the mountain. On the way, Arthur, who was not on the ferry, asks him what happened in Blackwater. Javier explains that the gang had the money until the Pinkertons appeared everywhere. He admits that Dutch killed a girl in a bad way, but says it was a bad situation; Arthur answers that it does not sound like Dutch. Javier lists the wounded: Davey, Mac and John were shot, and nobody knows what became of Sean. He adds that his group was barely holding on when Arthur's group arrived on the other side of town. If Arthur runs short of stamina on the climb, Javier offers him a drink.

Higher up, Javier recognizes John's horse lying dead and mauled in the snow; John was riding it when they left Blackwater. He fires his revolver into the air, and John's cries answer from further up. The path becomes too narrow for the horses, so the two men continue on foot and find John on a ledge, wounded by wolves. On the way back, Arthur carries John, then hands him over to Javier, who carries him to his horse while Arthur draws the wolves away and kills them. More wolves attack on the ride down. Javier tells John he will be fine and suggests riding in the river for a while to hide their scent and avoid leading anything back to camp.

Back at Colter, Javier calls for someone to help John off his horse. Bill Williamson and Lenny Summers pull him down roughly, and Javier calls them idiots and tells them to mind his leg. Hosea thanks Arthur and Javier for going after John. In "Old Friends", Javier is in the men's cabin when Micah punches Bill; Arthur, Javier and Lenny hold the two men apart before Dutch bursts in. Dutch then leads Arthur, Micah, Bill, Javier and Lenny against the camp of Colm O'Driscoll's gang. From a cliff above the camp, he posts Javier and Lenny higher up to pick off the O'Driscolls while he and Arthur creep down. Once the fight is over, the two men bring the horses down, and Javier asks whether everyone is all right.

In "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?", the gang targets a train owned by the industrialist Leviticus Cornwall. Javier rides out with Dutch, Arthur, Micah, Lenny and Charles Smith. Dutch's plan gives Javier and Lenny the two front cars and their guards. On the way, Javier asks Lenny whether he is sure he is ready. The dynamite fails to go off, so Arthur, Javier and Lenny run to a tunnel and jump onto the train from above. Arthur lands on the roof and Lenny hangs on to the side, but Javier misses the jump and is left behind; according to the mission page, he is hurt in the fall. Lenny asks where he is; Arthur answers that he fell and that the others will pick him up. Javier later rides in with Dutch, Bill, Charles and Micah to help clear the remaining guards.

In "Eastward Bound", the gang leaves the mountains for the Heartlands. Javier rides alongside the wagon carrying Bill and Charles, ahead of Arthur and Hosea's. When a wheel comes off Arthur and Hosea's wagon after a river crossing, Javier stops and offers to help. Hosea declines, saying they can handle it, and Charles stays behind with them while Javier rides on. Later, Arthur and Hosea find Javier waiting by a rock at the entrance to the new campsite, Horseshoe Overlook. He tells Arthur to follow the track, then asks him to slow down so he can jump onto the back of the wagon. When Hosea asks whether he had any trouble getting in, Javier answers that it went well and that it is a good spot.

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

Hosea tells Arthur that Bill, Charles and Javier are already in Valentine. In "Americans at Rest", Arthur finds Javier and Charles drinking with two women at the bar of Smithfield's Saloon. Javier calls Arthur over to meet them, and Arthur's rudeness drives the women away; Javier remarks on his fine way with women. The three men wait at the bar for Bill, who arrives drunk and starts a brawl. Javier smashes a bottle over a man's head. Tommy, a large local, then comes down the stairs and goes for Javier, who lands a punch but is overpowered and thrown around the room. Bill tells Arthur to give Javier a hand, and the fight between Arthur and Tommy moves out into the street.

Javier comes out and watches the fight from the saloon steps, urging Arthur to stop playing with Tommy because drinks are waiting. After the fight, Dutch arrives with Josiah Trelawny, who greets Javier and Charles. Trelawny reports that bounty hunters are holding Sean, who went missing in Blackwater. Dutch sends Charles to find out more and tells Trelawny to take Javier with him, while Arthur goes to clean up. In "The First Shall Be Last", Arthur joins Charles and Javier on a clifftop overlooking Blackwater. Javier reports that the town is crawling with Pinkertons and bounty hunters and that posters of Dutch and Hosea are everywhere. The money from the ferry, he adds, will stay where it is for now. He also tells Arthur that Trelawny has not stopped talking since Valentine.

Trelawny learns that Sean is being moved by boat on the Upper Montana River by Ike Skelding's bounty hunters, who will stop at one of their camps before handing him over. Arthur sends Charles to the opposite cliff and takes Javier and Trelawny along the river. Two guards stand on the far bank. Javier suggests doing it quietly and has Arthur ready his knife. Trelawny rides over and fakes a seizure in front of the guards, and Arthur and Javier come up behind them and kill them silently. Trelawny then leaves. Arthur and Javier fight their way up the canyon while Charles covers them from above, join him on the cliff and storm the camp, where Sean hangs from a tree by his legs. They kill the last hunters and cut him free.

Arthur stays behind to search the camp. He sends Javier to escort Sean back to Horseshoe Overlook and tells Charles to ride separately, as patrols are everywhere. When Sean promises him stories, Javier answers that he can't wait. That night the gang holds a party for Sean's return. From this point, according to the mission page, Javier also offers Arthur a companion activity: the robbery of Chez Porter, a homestead in the backcountry. On the ride, Javier explains that the family has lived away from society for so long that it speaks its own version of English. The two men watch the house from the trees, and Javier sets fire to the stables to draw the family out. Arthur and Javier shoot them, among them a woman, Edie Porter, then find the stash under a crate in the barn and a second box on its upper floor. The take is 175 dollars.

The Red Dead Wiki notes that during this chapter Bill can be captured by bounty hunters. In that case Javier asks Arthur to help him free Bill; the two men attack the hunters' camp, kill them and cut Bill loose. At the end of the chapter, after a shootout in Valentine, the gang packs up and leaves Horseshoe Overlook for Clemens Point, on the shore of Flat Iron Lake in Lemoyne.

Chapter 3: Clemens Point

After "The New South", Javier invites Arthur to go fishing. The two men ride to Ringneck Creek in search of smallmouth bass. When Arthur gets no bites, Javier gives him crickets to use as bait, and the fish start to take. During the outing they recall the Reno stagecoach they once let go. After a while Javier decides to head back to camp, and the player can keep fishing or ride back with him. The Red Dead Wiki adds that Arthur remarks how much better Javier and Hosea are at fishing than he is.

Dutch is playing the two feuding plantation families of Lemoyne, the Grays and the Braithwaites, against each other. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he sends John and Javier to look into a business proposal from the Grays. In "Horse Flesh for Dinner", Arthur meets them at the stables of Caliga Hall, the Gray estate, where Tavish Gray asks the three men to steal the Braithwaites' prized horses and promises that they will sell for 5,000 dollars. At Braithwaite Manor, John tells a guard that they have come to invest in the stables, and the guard lets them in. Arthur persuades the stablehand to show them the three horses. While the man describes their names and history, Arthur can strangle him, hogtie him, or let him finish, in which case Javier knocks him out.

The three men put on their bandanas. Javier ties the three horses together and mounts the one at the front. John leads the way out and Arthur covers the rear while they fight their way off the grounds; when one stallion breaks loose, Arthur lassoes it back. They cross Scarlett Meadows to Clemens Cove, where a horse fence named Clay Davies buys the animals for only 700 dollars. Soon after, Jack Marston disappears from camp. In "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern", the gang concludes that the Braithwaites have taken him in revenge for the stolen horses. Javier rides to Braithwaite Manor with Dutch, Arthur, Hosea, John, Charles, Bill and Lenny, while Micah and Kieran guard the camp.

The confrontation at the manor turns into a gunfight. The gang kills most of the Braithwaites' men, Arthur breaks into the room where Catherine Braithwaite and her sons have barricaded themselves, and the gang searches the house without finding Jack. The outlaws set fire to the manor. Outside, Catherine reveals that her sons handed Jack to the Saint Denis crime boss Angelo Bronte. The gang spares her and rides back to camp, and Catherine runs back into her burning house, where she dies. Soon after, Pinkerton agents Andrew Milton and Edgar Ross find the camp at Clemens Point, and the gang moves again, to Shady Belle, an abandoned plantation house in the bayou near Saint Denis.

Chapter 4: Saint Denis

In "A Fine Night of Debauchery", Trelawny prepares a scheme against Desmond Blythe, who keeps extra collateral in the safe of the Grand Korrigan, a riverboat casino moored at Saint Denis. Arthur, in a new suit, goes aboard with Trelawny, Leopold Strauss and Javier, and the four split up. Arthur plays poker with Strauss signaling his moves, eliminates the other players and beats Blythe twice, the second time for Blythe's Reutlinger pocket watch. The pit boss then takes Arthur upstairs to the vault to collect, with Javier leading the way, disguised as one of the boat's guards in a uniform taken from a real guard.

When the safe is open, Javier knocks out another guard and holds the pit boss at gunpoint, and he and Arthur empty the vault. The pit boss draws a hidden gun, and Arthur shoots him. Back at the bar, Blythe is accusing Arthur of cheating when the guard whose uniform Javier took appears and shouts for him to be shot. A gunfight breaks out. Arthur, Javier, Strauss and Trelawny fight their way to the deck, jump overboard and swim ashore. On the bank, the four share the take, which is large. Strauss confirms that the watch is valuable, and Arthur keeps it. At one point, Javier asks how they are supposed to get off the boat, and Arthur blames Trelawny's planning.

After the gang gets Jack back from Bronte, Hosea plans one last large robbery, "Banking, The Old American Art", against the Lemoyne National Bank in Saint Denis, to pay for the gang's escape overseas. Javier is part of the crew and wears the formal suit in which he boarded the Grand Korrigan. According to the Red Dead Wiki, his job is to guard the side entrance of the bank. The Pinkertons surround the building. Milton, who has captured Hosea, kills him in the street, John is arrested, and Lenny is shot dead during the escape over the rooftops. If Arthur is not carrying a long gun when the gang heads for the roof, Javier throws him a Lancaster Repeater.

Javier escapes with Dutch, Arthur, Micah, Bill and Charles. The six men hide in an abandoned apartment until nightfall, then make for the docks. Charles draws the Pinkerton guards away so the other five can board a ship, the Antenor. The rest of the gang, Abigail and Jack among them, stays behind in Lemoyne.

Chapter 5: Guarma

In "Lost and Not Quite Found", a storm wrecks the Antenor at night. Dutch wakes Arthur, who follows Dutch, Micah, Javier and Bill through the ship until falling crates cut him off. When Arthur reaches the deck, the four men are already at sea in a lifeboat, and he jumps into the water. In "Welcome to the New World", Arthur wakes alone on a beach and follows a trail of smoke to the campfire where Dutch, Micah, Bill and Javier sit, relieved to see him. Dutch explains that they are on Guarma, a sugar island east of Cuba. Soldiers led by Levi Simon, who runs the plantation for Colonel Alberto Fussar, then surround them and put them in chains alongside captured local rebels.

Rebels led by Hercule Fontaine ambush the column. Arthur grabs a gun and kills the guards, and the prisoners free themselves. As more soldiers arrive, the others escape with Hercule, but Javier is shot in the leg while fleeing and captured. The group cannot reach him. That night, at the rebels' camp of La Capilla, Dutch plans to scout a route to the sugar factory, Aguasdulces, to get Javier back. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Fussar's men interrogate Javier about the identity of his companions and beat him, but he tells them nothing, and he is then locked in a cage.

In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", a local woman named Gloria guides Dutch and Arthur through a cave, and Dutch strangles her when she demands more money. Outside Aguasdulces, the two men see Javier being dragged behind a donkey while Fussar and his soldiers mock him. They kill two guards, slip into the refinery, tear open sugar sacks and use the furnace to set the building on fire. In the confusion that follows the explosion, they free Javier and fight their way out through the cane fields. Dutch carries the unconscious Javier while Arthur holds off the pursuers. At a stream, Dutch takes Javier on toward the rebel fortress of Cinco Torres and leaves Arthur to cover the bank.

If the player completes this mission before "Savagery Unleashed", Javier appears at the rebels' camp, where he thanks Arthur (see Relationships). Javier takes no part in the fighting that follows. In "Hell Hath No Fury", a warship sent from Cuba attacks Cinco Torres while he recovers. After the battle, Dutch and Bill go to fetch him. In "Paradise Mercifully Departed", Bill stays behind to guard the recovering Javier while Arthur, Micah, Dutch and Hercule destroy the coastal gun batteries and Arthur kills Fussar. The five gang members then sail back to America.

Back in Lemoyne, the rest of the gang has settled at Lakay, an abandoned village in the bayou, under Sadie Adler's lead. In "Fleeting Joy", Arthur arrives first, after finding Sadie's letter at Shady Belle. Micah and Javier arrive next, followed by Dutch, to the surprise of those who had stayed behind; Bill comes in last. The reunion is cut short by a Pinkerton attack led by Milton, which Arthur and Sadie break by flanking the attackers and turning a machine gun on them. In "That's Murfree Country", the gang moves north again, to Beaver Hollow, a cave in Roanoke Ridge cleared of the Murfree Brood by Arthur and Charles.

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

At Beaver Hollow, Javier takes a smaller part in the gang's jobs but stays loyal to Dutch while Arthur, John and others start to doubt him. In camp, he and Bill debate whether John, now held in Sisika Penitentiary, has betrayed the gang. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Javier also clashes in camp with Arthur, John, Charles, Abigail and Uncle over their lack of faith in Dutch; these exchanges are covered under Relationships. In "A Rage Unleashed", Javier and Karen escort Eagle Flies, son of the Wapiti chief Rains Fall, into camp. Eagle Flies reports that the US Army has confiscated his people's horses and calls it an act of war, and Dutch decides to help him.

In "My Last Boy", Eagle Flies leads Wapiti warriors against the Army at the Cornwall Kerosene and Tar oil refinery, and Rains Fall begs Arthur for help. Arthur agrees, then Charles, then Dutch and the rest of the gang, except Micah, Cleet and Joe. During the ride, the gang argues about loyalty and Dutch's plans. At the refinery, Arthur, Charles and Sadie ride with a group of Wapiti to reach Eagle Flies, while Javier goes with Dutch's group to attack the soldiers commanded by Colonel Henry Favours from another side. Only Dutch and Arthur then enter the warehouse to look for state bonds. Arthur's journal records the take from this job under the initials "A, D, C, B & S", without John or Javier.

In "Our Best Selves", the gang tries to rob an Army train carrying supplies and payroll outside Saint Denis. The train does not stop, and the gang has to chase it and fight its way aboard. John, back with the gang after his escape from Sisika, is shot while uncoupling a burning carriage and falls from the train. Dutch says he will go back for him and leaves with Javier, Micah and Joe, while Arthur, Bill, Sadie and Cleet hold off the soldiers following the train. Arthur and the others jump off with the money before the train plunges from the destroyed Bacchus Bridge. When the four men catch up, Dutch and Micah tell Arthur that John did not make it.

In "Red Dead Redemption", Arthur returns to Beaver Hollow and accuses Micah of informing for Milton. The two men draw on each other. John then walks into camp alive and accuses Dutch of leaving him to die. Susan Grimshaw turns her shotgun on Micah and orders him to put his gun down. At that moment Javier arrives, shouting that the Pinkertons are coming fast. Susan turns toward him, and Micah shoots her dead. Dutch demands to know who is with him and who is betraying him. Arthur calls on Bill and Javier to think for themselves. Javier lines up with Dutch, Micah, Bill, Cleet and Joe facing Arthur and John until the Pinkertons open fire on the camp.

The gang scatters under the attack. Dutch, Micah, Cleet and Joe chase Arthur and John after the two escape through the caves; Bill and Javier are not among the pursuers. This is Javier's last appearance in Red Dead Redemption 2. He does not appear in the 1907 epilogue.

From 1899 to 1911

The games do not show what Javier did after Beaver Hollow. According to the Red Dead Wiki, he went back to Mexico at some point after the gang broke up. In the 1907 epilogue of Red Dead Redemption 2, the newspaper article "Notorious Bad Man Alive" reports a sighting of Dutch after seven years on the run. It names Bill Williamson, Micah Bell, John Marston and Charles Smith as gang members still believed to be at large, and adds that a telegraph from Mexico reports that Javier Escuella is believed to live in the mountain region there. By 1911, according to Abraham Reyes in Red Dead Redemption, Javier and Bill have paid Colonel Allende to hide them. The wiki adds that Javier works for Allende as a hired gun, is protected by the Mexican Army as a result, and helped Bill reach Nuevo Paraíso.

Red Dead Redemption (1911)

In 1911, the Bureau of Investigation agents Edgar Ross and Archer Fordham hold John's wife and son to force him to hunt down his former companions: Bill Williamson, Javier Escuella and Dutch van der Linde. Javier is the first of the three that John catches. He appears in a single mission, "The Gates of El Presidio", but the hunt for him runs through the whole of the game's Mexican act.

Act I: New Austin

John's first target is Bill, who leads a gang from Fort Mercer, in New Austin. In "The Assault on Fort Mercer", John and a posse take the fort, but Bill is not there. A captured gang member says that Bill ran off to Mexico the morning before. John concludes that Bill has gone to see Javier Escuella and remarks that this should make things interesting. Irish then offers to take him across the border into Nuevo Paraíso.

Act II: Nuevo Paraíso

In Nuevo Paraíso, a civil war pits Colonel Allende's government forces against the rebels of Abraham Reyes. Allende, who is sheltering Bill and Javier, and his officers keep promising John the two men, and each time send him on another job for the army. In "Cowards Die Many Times", Captain Vincente de Santa leads John to Chuparosa on the pretext that Bill and Javier have been captured, and the army tries to kill him there. John then sides with the rebels. In "Captain De Santa's Downfall", he hogties De Santa and beats him until the captain names Casa Madrugada as Javier's hiding place; the house turns out to be a trap held by soldiers.

In "The Gates of El Presidio", Reyes tells John that Javier is at El Presidio, the army fortress where Reyes and John first met, but that he does not know about Williamson. John answers that Escuella will do for now. On the ride, John tells Reyes that Javier left him to die when he could have saved him. At the fort, John drives a wagon loaded with TNT into the gate and the rebels storm in. Reyes sends him to look for Escuella. John finds Javier in a storeroom upstairs in the barracks.

Javier greets him as a brother, says he heard John was coming and asks whether John would really shoot his own family. John answers that family stopped meaning much once Javier and Dutch went crazy. Javier mocks him for doing the government's work; John replies that Javier simply works for a different government. Javier suggests they go their separate ways. John says that the way Javier and Dutch left him was wrong and that, since it is one of them or the other, it might as well be Javier. Javier swears they thought he was dead, offers to give him Bill and claims that Dutch is in Colombia and that he can take John straight to him. John answers that Javier left him to die to save his own skin. Javier answers that John has it all wrong and that he has always loved him, then pushes a crate onto him, jumps out of a window and flees on horseback. During the chase he shoots at John and taunts him, telling him to go back to his farm and that he lacks the heart to kill him.

The player chooses how the chase ends. If John captures him, typically with the lasso, he hogties Javier and tells him he is taking him in to be judged by others. Asked where Bill is, Javier says that he does not know, then that Bill is with Allende. John warns him that he will be locked up for a very long time, unless the authorities decide to hang him. Javier reminds John that they were family, insists that things did not happen as John thinks and says it was Bill, not him. He then tells John that Dutch never liked him and begs to be let go, since it is Bill that John wants. On the way into the fort's jail, Javier accuses John of selling him out and asks whether the life they shared meant nothing. John throws him onto the stone floor. Javier swears that one day John will regret this; John answers that one day is about all Javier has left. Javier then tells him he hopes John, his wife and his children rot in hell. John answers that the life they lived is over and that it never meant anything: it was just an excuse, and they all knew it.

If the player kills him instead, John shoots Javier with a rifle as he rides away. The bullet strikes him in the heart, his sombrero flies off and he slumps from the saddle. John carries the corpse back to a cell in the fort, where he has to keep it as proof for the federal agents. As he turns to go, John stops to look at the body. He sheds a silent tear, wipes it away and spits on the corpse; the mission page notes that this is the only mission in which John cries. In both cases, Reyes calls John back to repel army reinforcements, and John drives them off with a cannon while the rebels take El Presidio.

John then fetches Javier from the cell, alive or dead, and Reyes remarks on the beauty of two old friends reunited. John takes him to Ross and Fordham, who are waiting with a car near the border bridge. If Javier is alive, Fordham says that he will now see how far the hand of justice can reach and orders him into the automobile; the Red Dead Wiki adds that Javier spits on John's arm. If he is dead, Fordham comments that he does not look very healthy and reminds John that they wanted the men alive, and John throws the body into the back of the car. If Javier is alive, Fordham tells John that nothing is cleared until his obligations are met; if he is dead, Fordham simply reminds him of his obligations to the government. In both cases John must find Williamson, then go to Blackwater, where Dutch is believed to be. The agents drive off with Javier.

Javier does not appear again. In "An Appointed Time", as he chases the stagecoach carrying Allende and Bill out of Escalera, John shouts at Bill to give up, telling him that he already has Escuella. Bill dies on the road outside Escalera in 1911, in a scene where the player again decides whom John shoots.

Act III: West Elizabeth

Back in Blackwater, in "Bear One Another's Burdens", John protests to Ross that he has delivered Williamson and Escuella and that the deal should be over. Ross answers that the agreement covers every member of his old gang and that they still do not have Dutch. The game never shows what happens to Javier after the handover, whichever choice the player made. After Dutch's death, in "Old Friends, New Problems", John tells Abigail on the wagon ride to the MacFarlane ranch that Bill, Javier and Dutch are all dead. The line plays whichever choice the player made at El Presidio. Later in the same mission he wonders whether the three men were simply being honest with themselves. According to the Red Dead Wiki, a newspaper of 1914 lists Javier among the members of the Van der Linde gang whose elimination is credited to Edgar Ross.

Personality

Rockstar's character description, posted on September 6, 2018, reads: "A notorious bounty hunter and Mexican revolutionary. Javier immediately had a strong connection to Dutch's ideals. Very committed, passionate and loyal." Red Dead Redemption 2: The Complete Official Guide, as quoted by the Red Dead Wiki, says that he feels a strong affinity with Dutch's ideals and will "stick by his leader through thick and thin". His character page in the guide's Collector's Edition goes further: Javier is "unquestioning in his loyalty to Dutch, perhaps sometimes to a fault", and counts among Dutch's "most dependable recruits". The same page calls him "somewhat vain", a man who "takes a lot of care over his appearance and is always immaculately presented". In 1899 he speaks less than most of the gang. According to the wiki, Arthur remarks that Javier is probably the most loyal of them all.

He does not let insults pass: a slur from Bill ends with a knife at Bill's throat, a jibe from Micah with a punch, and a remark from Sean about vanity with a threat (each scene is told under Relationships). He is a practicing Catholic; at Horseshoe Overlook the Reverend Swanson finds him at prayer. From chapter 6 he turns against the gang members who doubt Dutch, among them Arthur, John and Charles.

In 1911 John sums him up for Abraham Reyes on the ride to El Presidio: "Escuella was always a torn man. A cynic who desperately wanted to be a romantic." He adds: "Javier would die fightin' for what he believed in. He had a lot of passion, but no love." John also says that Dutch's collapse hit Javier "harder than any of us", and that it was as if the one thing he had ever believed in had turned out to be a fraud. When Reyes says John speaks of him fondly, John answers that they were close once, but that Javier never cared for him or for any of his so-called brothers.

In his own words

Rockstar's official character poster for him carries one of his lines: "If we have to fight, we fight. If we have to run, we'll run. If we must die, we'll die, but... we'll stay free." In "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" he explains why he agrees to search for John: "I know if the situation were reversed and...he'd look for me." On the same ride he complains: "I'm not designed for this snow..." In "The First Shall Be Last", asked by Arthur why he is so quiet, he points at Trelawny: "He hasn't stopped talking since we left you in Valentine. It's the longest ride of my life." Seeing a rainbow with Arthur, he says: "A rainbow without any rain? Our luck has changed. We're blessed."

Names and reputation

He calls Arthur "brother" in "Eastward Bound" and John "compadre" in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory"; at El Presidio in 1911 he still calls John "brother". Bill and Micah both call him a "greaser", a slur for Mexicans. In 1911 Abraham Reyes tells John what he has heard of him: "They tell me he was once a notorious bounty hunter, and also one of the early revolutionaries." According to the Red Dead Wiki, a prostitute at Casa Madrugada says she would not spit on him if he were on fire.

Appearance

In 1899 Javier is a slim man with black hair, brown eyes and a mustache shaved in the middle; his hair usually covers a scar on one eyebrow, and a second scar marks the front of his neck. Twelve years later his face is thinner and more lined, and his hair has grown long. In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again", John has Ricketts ask a witness whether the man was "about five foot eight, mustache".

Relationships

Dutch van der Linde

Dutch took Javier into the gang in 1895, after the two men met while stealing from the same chicken coop. While the gang looks for Sean, Dutch sends him with Charles and Trelawny to gather information. At Clemens Point, according to the wiki's camp events page, Javier joins Dutch at the lakeside, where he is fishing, and admits that the constant killing and running worries him; Dutch tells him to stay strong.

On Guarma Dutch and Arthur free him from Fussar's men, and Dutch carries him through the cane fields. In "Our Best Selves" Javier rides back with Dutch, Micah and Joe for John, and the group returns without him. In 1911, cornered at El Presidio, he tells John that Dutch is in Colombia and offers to take him there.

John Marston

Javier finds John with Arthur in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory"; back at Colter he snaps at the men lifting John down to mind his leg. At the dominoes table at Horseshoe Overlook John thanks him for the rescue, and Javier answers that he would do the same. At Clemens Point a meal shared by the fire turns to women and to Javier's reasons for leaving Mexico; John says he would like to see the world one day, Mexico included. Drinking with him at the poker table on another night, John complains about Abigail and says he wishes he could forget what he has seen. In chapter 3 they steal the Braithwaite horses with Arthur.

In chapter 6, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Javier calls John arrogant when John says the gang is not his family. In 1911 John hunts him down in Mexico for the Bureau. At El Presidio the player decides whether John kills him or captures him alive. If he is captured, Javier tells John that Dutch never liked him.

Arthur Morgan

Arthur and Javier ride together through the first chapters: the search for John, the rescue of Sean, the Braithwaite horses and the riverboat job. In the Valentine brawl of "Americans at Rest", Arthur pulls the strongman Tommy off Javier. At Shady Belle Javier mocks the new camp to Arthur: the place is so pleasant, he jokes, that he misses freezing in the mountains. Arthur and Dutch free him on Guarma; back at the gang's camp on the island, Javier thanks Arthur, and Arthur tells Micah that if Javier dies, it is on him.

After Guarma Javier refuses to discuss Dutch with Arthur, questions his loyalty and says he is only in it for the money; Arthur answers that he would be long gone if that were true. According to the wiki, Javier also accuses him of going behind Dutch's back and breaking Dutch's heart. At Beaver Hollow Arthur urges Bill and Javier to think for themselves. Javier sides with Dutch, but hesitates to aim his gun at Arthur and John, and he is not among the men who chase them.

Bill Williamson

At Horseshoe Overlook a drunk Bill finds Javier sharpening his knife at the poker table and calls him a "greaser". Javier pins him down with the knife at his throat, insults him in return, asks Arthur whether he should kill him, then lets him go. On Guarma Bill watches over the wounded Javier while the others destroy the gun batteries. At Beaver Hollow the two discuss whether John is a traitor and whether he should hang in Sisika.

In 1911, according to the wiki, Javier helps Bill reach Mexico before John's assault on Fort Mercer. Reyes tells John he has heard that the two men paid Allende to hide them. Cornered at El Presidio, Javier offers to give Bill up; if John captures him, he says that Bill is with Allende and that John should be after Bill rather than him. During the chase out of Escalera, John shouts at Bill that he already has Escuella.

Micah Bell

In "Old Friends" Javier, Arthur and Lenny pull Micah and Bill apart at Colter. At Horseshoe Overlook Micah, unsettled by Javier's stare at the campfire, tells him to go back to Mexico; Javier punches him and answers "Why don't you fuck off back to hell?" When Arthur praises the punch, Javier says: "Next time, it will be a bullet." In chapter 3, drunk, the two trade insults, Micah saying Javier is not bad for a "greaser" and Javier that Micah is not bad for an asshole. They are among the castaways of Guarma and reach Lakay together ahead of Dutch.

Charles Smith

In "Eastward Bound" Javier rides beside the wagon that Bill and Charles are driving, ahead of Arthur and Hosea's; when the wheel comes off, Charles stays back to help and Javier rides on. Javier and Charles are drinking with Arthur in the Valentine saloon when the brawl of "Americans at Rest" starts. In "The First Shall Be Last" Arthur finds them watching Blackwater from a cliff, and the three men free Sean. At Beaver Hollow, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Javier mocks Charles's lack of faith in Dutch and tells him to use his brain; Charles ignores him. According to the wiki's page on Arthur's relationships, after one of these exchanges Arthur tells Charles he should have left Javier to die on Guarma.

Hosea Matthews

At Colter Hosea asks Javier to ride out with Arthur to look for John, calling them "the two best fit men we've got", and thanks them both when they return. In "Eastward Bound" Javier offers to help with the broken wagon wheel, and Hosea declines. Javier escapes the Saint Denis bank job in which Hosea is killed.

Sean MacGuire

Javier helps free Sean in "The First Shall Be Last" and escorts him back to camp. When Sean promises "Have I got stories for you...", Javier answers: "Yeah... I can't wait." At Horseshoe Overlook, while Javier scrubs his boots, Sean tells him that vanity is a sin; Javier replies that his father told him to cut up anyone who disrespects him with a knife, and tells Sean to leave. In a rare scene at Shady Belle Javier asks Arthur what happened to Sean in Rhodes, and says he will miss him.

Lenny Summers

In "Old Friends" Dutch posts Javier and Lenny together above the O'Driscoll camp. On the ride to Cornwall's train Javier asks him: "You sure you're ready for this, kid?" Lenny answers that of course he is. Javier misses his jump onto the train, while Lenny makes it. Both take part in the Saint Denis bank job, in which Lenny is killed.

Abigail Marston

At Colter Abigail's plea leads Hosea to send Javier and Arthur after John, and she thanks them both when they bring him back. In chapter 6, according to the wiki's page on Arthur's relationships, Javier questions her loyalty to Dutch. In 1911, if John captures him, Javier tells him that Abigail would not want this, and John replies: "Abigail would've killed you already. She always thought you was a creep." On the ride home from the MacFarlane ranch, John tells Abigail that sometimes he wonders whether Dutch, Bill and Javier were simply being honest with themselves.

Jack Marston

Javier joins the assault on Braithwaite Manor in "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern" to get Jack back. At Jack's return party at Shady Belle he sings "Cielito Lindo" for the gang, and everyone welcomes the boy when the song ends. In 1911, if John captures him, Javier tells him from the cell at El Presidio that he hopes John, his wife and his children rot in hell.

Sadie Adler

Javier is stranded on Guarma while Sadie keeps the rest of the gang together and moves it to Lakay.

Susan Grimshaw

Javier accompanies Susan's songs at camp. At Beaver Hollow, while Susan holds Micah at gunpoint, Javier arrives shouting that Pinkertons are coming. Susan turns toward him, and Micah shoots her.

Kieran Duffy

At Horseshoe Overlook Javier tells Kieran, who is tending a horse, that he will never stop being an O'Driscoll. When Kieran protests that he has proven himself, Javier says he has only shown how quickly he betrays his friends, and warns that he is watching him even if Dutch trusts him. Arthur can back Javier up. Kieran reminds Javier that he saved Arthur's life.

Uncle

At Horseshoe Overlook, after Javier puts money in the camp box, Uncle joins him for a drink. Javier has just robbed a soldier on the road; Uncle claims he is saving himself for the big jobs. Javier picks up his guitar, and on other nights he accompanies Uncle's songs. In chapter 6, according to the wiki's page on Arthur's relationships, Javier questions Uncle's loyalty to Dutch.

Karen Jones

She is one of his partners in the Tucson bank job of the mid-1890s (see Origins). Javier accompanies her songs on the guitar at several camps. In chapter 6 he and Karen escort Eagle Flies into Beaver Hollow when he comes to report that the Army has seized the Wapiti's horses.

Simon Pearson

At the Horseshoe Overlook stew pot, Pearson and Javier agree that mud beats snow. Pearson passes on a tip: a woman and some outlaws at Watson's Cabin are said to keep money hidden there. Javier promises to report back if he finds it.

Tilly Jackson

According to the wiki's camp events page, during Sean's return party Javier serenades Tilly with "El Coyotito" by the women's tents.

Orville Swanson

At Horseshoe Overlook Swanson finds Javier praying behind a barrel and tells him "it's not too late to repent, my Mexican friend". Javier retorts that plenty of priests would hear Swanson's own confession. Swanson declares him doomed; Arthur answers that they are all doomed, and Javier gives him a thumbs-up. In "Who is Not Without Sin", if Arthur greets Javier while carrying the drunk reverend back to his tent, Javier says: "Not again."

Josiah Trelawny

Trelawny rides with Javier and Arthur to free Sean in "The First Shall Be Last" and distracts two bounty hunters so that the two men can take them quietly. On the riverboat of "A Fine Night of Debauchery", Trelawny, Strauss, Arthur and Javier board together, and after the vault is emptied all four jump overboard and swim ashore.

Leopold Strauss

On the riverboat of "A Fine Night of Debauchery", Strauss signals to Arthur how to play at the poker table while Javier poses as a guard. The four men share the large take from the vault once ashore.

Tavish Gray

In "Horse Flesh for Dinner" Tavish Gray meets Arthur, John and Javier at the Caliga Hall stables and promises them 5,000 dollars for the Braithwaites' prized horses. The buyer pays far less once the horses are delivered.

Catherine Braithwaite

Javier, Arthur and John steal Catherine Braithwaite's horses from her stables in "Horse Flesh for Dinner".

Leviticus Cornwall

Javier takes part in the robbery of Cornwall's train in "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?". In chapter 6 he fights alongside the gang and the Wapiti against the Army at the Cornwall refinery.

Alberto Fussar

On Guarma Fussar's soldiers drag the captured Javier behind a donkey outside Aguasdulces. According to the Red Dead Wiki, they beat and question him about the other gang members, and he tells them nothing. He is held in a cage until Dutch and Arthur free him.

Hercule Fontaine

After Levi Simon's militia put the castaways in chains, Hercule Fontaine ambushes the column; Arthur kills the guards and the prisoners free themselves. Javier is shot in the leg during the escape and captured.

Agustin Allende

In 1911 Javier is in Mexico under Colonel Allende's protection; the wiki describes him as Allende's hired gun. In "The Demon Drink" Allende tells John what he knows of Escuella's family.

Vincente de Santa

In "Mexican Caesar" de Santa offers John 20,000 pesos and information on Javier's whereabouts in return for his help. In "Cowards Die Many Times" he tells John that Bill and Javier are prisoners in a church at Chuparosa; neither man is there, and the soldiers knock John out.

Abraham Reyes

Reyes promises John to find Javier and Bill for him, and learns that Javier is at El Presidio. On the ride there he asks whether Javier ever taught John any Spanish, and John says he remembers "cabrón" being used a lot. During the assault Reyes sends John to look for Escuella. If John captures Javier, he shouts an insult after John from his cell as John leaves with Reyes.

Landon Ricketts

In "Landon Ricketts Rides Again" Ricketts tells John that the men with him have been talking about a Mr. Escuella. Through Ricketts, John asks Emilio Fortuna whether the man matches Javier's description and had a big American with him.

Edgar Ross

Ross holds Abigail and Jack to force John to bring in his former companions, Javier among them. Javier, alive or dead, is handed over to Ross and Fordham at the border after El Presidio. When the Bureau then sends him after Dutch, John protests: "I got you Williamson and Escuella. It's over!"

Archer Fordham

If John brings Javier in alive, Fordham tells him that Javier "gets to see how far the hand of justice can reach" and has him put in the car. If John brings the body, Fordham remarks: "He's not looking very... healthy. We told you to keep them alive."

Luisa Fortuna

According to the Red Dead Wiki, when Landon Ricketts mentions Javier's name to Luisa, she says she remembers it from prison, where bad people spoke of him.

Skills, weapons and horses

Music

Javier plays the guitar at camp and accompanies the gang's songs. According to the wiki's camp events page, he plays "Poor Lonesome Cowboy" for Karen and Pearson, "Otis Miller" for Uncle, Karen and Pearson, "One Eyed Riley" for Uncle, Karen and Susan, and "I Wouldn't Marry" for Susan and Karen. At Sean's return party he accompanies "The Louisville Maid", "Ring Dang Doo" and "Rye Whiskey", and sings "El Coyotito". At Jack's return party at Shady Belle he sings "Cielito Lindo" and, later that night, accompanies Karen, Uncle and Susan on "The Heartland Stage Company".

Knives, tracking and jobs

Javier is one of the two opponents, with Micah, whom Arthur can face at Five Finger Fillet in chapter 3. In chapter 2 he asks Arthur for oleander. According to the wiki, he is seen poisoning throwing knives during one of his requests, and he then regularly leaves throwing knives at Arthur's tent. In "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" he reads the tracks in the snow, tells Arthur to take the shotgun from his horse and has them ride in the river to lose the wolves' scent. In "The First Shall Be Last" he and Arthur take two bounty hunters quietly with their knives. On the riverboat of "A Fine Night of Debauchery" he poses as a security guard. In "Horse Flesh for Dinner" he ropes the three stolen Braithwaite horses together.

Companion activities and camp life

Two companion activities are played with him, a homestead robbery and a fishing trip; both are described under Missions. He pays into the camp funds. When Arthur stays away too long, Dutch may send Javier, as he may send Bill or Charles, to bring him back. An Arthur who turns too hostile toward the gang can be punched and thrown out of camp by Javier.

Weapons

In 1899 his sidearm is a unique Double-Action Revolver. Its barrel is a little longer than on the shop model and it has a firing sound of its own; the finish is silver with black engraving, and the black grip is inlaid with silver. The game's "New Game" menu icon shows this revolver in his hand. In the cutscenes of "My Last Boy" and at the start of "Rob a Homestead", however, he holds a standard Cattleman Revolver. He also uses a Carbine Repeater or a Lancaster Repeater, and carries a unique knife and a throwing knife that he never uses. In 1911 the wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver; if John kills him, his body shows an empty knife holster.

Horses

In 1899 Javier rides Boaz, a gray overo American Paint according to the wiki. In "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" the wounded John is put on Boaz for the ride back to Colter, and after "The First Shall Be Last" Sean rides back to camp on him with Javier. In "Horse Flesh for Dinner" the three stolen horses are tethered to Boaz. Boaz is at Shady Belle and at Lakay without being ridden in those chapters. Javier rides him again to the Cornwall refinery and in "Our Best Selves", and flees the Pinkertons on him at Beaver Hollow; the horse does not appear after that. In 1911 he rides a Cleveland Bay.

Missions

The English Red Dead Wiki lists 23 story missions of Red Dead Redemption 2 for Javier, from the search for John in the Grizzlies to the standoff at Beaver Hollow, plus two companion activities: a homestead robbery and a fishing trip with Arthur. The French edition also counts 23 story missions, but its list is not the same: it adds "Dear Uncle Tacitus", the crossing back from Guarma, leaves out "Lost and Not Quite Found" and gives no companion activity. Javier appears in no mission of the epilogue. He gives Arthur two story missions, "Americans at Rest" and "The First Shall Be Last". In Red Dead Redemption, the wiki lists one mission, "The Gates of El Presidio". Javier is never playable in either story mode.

Development and performance

Casting

In Red Dead Redemption (2010), Javier Escuella is voiced by Antonio Jaramillo. In Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), the role passed to Gabriel Sloyer, who provided both the voice and the performance capture. Sloyer is an American actor born on Long Island, New York, who began in theater, on and off Broadway. His television credits include Narcos, in which he plays the journalist Manuel de Dios Unanue, Orange Is the New Black, Power, Limitless, The Blacklist and Jessica Jones. Before Red Dead Redemption 2 he had worked for Rockstar on Grand Theft Auto V (2013), as the voice and motion capture actor of the arms dealer Oscar Guzman. Eurogamer, in an interview by Tom Phillips published on 12 December 2018, noted that this earlier role was why the studio already knew him.

Sloyer told Eurogamer that he signed on without knowing which game he was working on, or even the name of his character. The first scenes he shot were either the rescue of John from the wolves or the saloon brawl. The boots and the language told the cast they were making a Western, and he guessed which Western Rockstar would make; it took him the first couple of months to work out who he was playing. Shooting lasted five years, on a schedule of three weeks on and three weeks off; in that time he also appeared in seven television series, several films and several plays. He said lines often arrived the day before a session, on the morning itself or on the way into the soundstage, and that an actor with no lines in a scene was not given that scene at all.

In an interview by Liz Lanier published by Variety on 24 December 2018, Sloyer said sessions ran from about 8 or 9 a.m. to 5 or 6 p.m., in a vast, empty warehouse at an undisclosed location about an hour from Manhattan, where he dealt with 20 to 35 people each time he was on set. He called it one of the best sets he had worked on, while stressing that he could only speak for the actors, not for the developers. He praised the director Rod Edge. In the Eurogamer interview, he recalled cast members slipping into their roles during lunch breaks: Benjamin Byron Davis (Dutch) attempting the New York Times crossword, Noshir Dalal (Charles) doing one-handed push-ups, Rob Wiethoff (John) talking about mixing concrete and Roger Clark (Arthur) practicing a Bane impression.

Building the character

Asked by Matt Morgans of VGR, in an interview published on 18 December 2018, how he developed Javier, Sloyer said that fans of the first game already knew how his story would end. He wanted to win them over by stressing how loyal, nostalgic and likable Javier was, so that his later break with John would hurt more. Asked whether he had imitated Antonio Jaramillo's performance, he said his model was the bandit Joaquin Murrieta: he pictured him brooding from camp to camp, singing nostalgic ballads and trying to escape his past. He added that the role went well beyond voice work, since his walk, songs and facial movements were all captured live, and that the scenes in which Javier is dragged through the mud on a rope were really performed. He joked that he would like to think Javier's outfits owed something to him: "After all, he is the best dressed man in camp."

Sloyer drew on his own family. He told Eurogamer that his father, whose name is also Javier, emigrated to the United States and changed his surname to sound more American, and that he struggled to fit in. He described the character as "looking for home, somewhere to belong", and said the songs about Mexico that Javier sings at the campfire show how torn he is between the two countries. He told Variety that his father used to say he wanted an American family, and that immigrants can end up caught between two cultures, no longer at home in the old country and not fully accepted in the new one. He told Variety that many fans had started the game expecting to dislike Javier and ended up liking him, which made his end in the first game sadder; he told VGR that some regretted how they had treated him in the first game.

Music was a large part of the role. Sloyer told Eurogamer that he played and sang more than eight songs on the guitar. An animator had already designed a seated campfire pose for Javier, so he had to play in a twisted position, wearing a motion capture suit whose markers dug into his thighs, a heavy head camera and bandages with markers on his fingers. He also recorded variants of his lines for Arthur standing near or far, in a good or bad mood, and in many other situations. He told Variety he was proud to play a major Latino character, and singled out the party scene in which Javier sings "Cielito Lindo" and the gang joins in; he said people online wrote that they cried and stood up to applaud. At the SacAnime convention in Sacramento on 8 June 2019, he called Javier "the Brad Pitt of the gang" and said the character has a broken heart for Mexico.

At the same SacAnime panel, Sloyer explained one of his choices in the standoff at Beaver Hollow, in the mission "Red Dead Redemption": Javier holds his gun pointed upward instead of aiming at Arthur and John. He said he had to fight for it, because the crew wanted him to aim, and that he answered that John was his brother.

The surname

Rob Wiethoff, who plays John Marston, told the story of the surname in a video posted on his YouTube channel on 2 November 2025. He said he got the name wrong during a read-through of the script, on one of his first days on Red Dead Redemption, and that the others at the table laughed. The director then decided to keep his version, reasoning that John would probably mispronounce the name. Wiethoff said John has pronounced it that way ever since.

Cut content

Sloyer told Eurogamer that the rescue of John in the mountains was first shot differently, with a scene in which Javier fights off wolves in a cave with a torch. He also said the cast shot a lot of material that was later cut, and that he sometimes wondered whether some endings were filmed only to confuse the actors. The files of Red Dead Redemption 2 hold a tuxedo for Javier labeled with the gala outfit name used for the party of "The Gilded Cage", a mission he does not attend in the released game. They also contain an unused duster coat outfit, which the Red Dead Wiki links to his homestead robbery, two vests, a lower vest, a ponytail, a tie, a gunbelt, a coat and three shirts.

Javier has several unused beards of different lengths, some of which appear in trailers. His beard stays the same length on Guarma, unlike the other gang members'. A trailer shows him in trousers with a skeleton pattern, and promotional art dresses him very differently from the final game. His horse Boaz has a black overo coat in the first trailer. A cut item request appears to have been for a sombrero for Javier, and a throwing knife and beta designs of his gunbelt were left unused. Hunting, target practice and cattle rustling (the latter with John) were planned as activities with Javier. Files suggest that dialogue and a guitar song for Javier were made for a cut party at Dewberry Creek, then dropped or reused. A piece of gang concept art appears to show him with Bill and Karen Jones, and another with Charles Smith and Molly O'Shea.

Reception

Before release

In a Game Informer feature on the seven characters returning from the first game, published on 28 September 2018, a month before release, Jill Grodt described the Javier of Red Dead Redemption as "Craftier than Bill Williamson and more prudent than John Marston", and wrote that his actions in that game "paint a picture of a resourceful hypocrite". She also noted his skill at talking his way out of trouble, his readiness to let others do the fighting, and the bandolier he wears to advertise how dangerous he is.

Rankings

  • Game Informer, 27 December 2018: 15th of the 24 members of the Van der Linde gang in the staff ranking. The staff noted that, after his boasting in the first game, Javier gets little time at the center of the prequel and is mostly seen silent by the campfire or playing songs, but called him "easily the best dresser in the whole outfit (outside of Arthur, of course)" and said the scene in which he beats up Micah alone lifts him several places.
  • TheGamer, 10 August 2019: 7th of the ten most prominent members of the gang, in a ranking by Jerrad Wyche, who writes that Javier starts as a likable and trustworthy member, looks up to Dutch like his closest friend in the gang, Bill Williamson, and becomes "ultra-defensive" of Dutch once the leader begins to lose his mind.
Other media and legacy

Multiplayer

Javier became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Myths and Mavericks Bonus Pack. Engadget reported on July 27, 2011 that the pack would come out in September at no cost. It was released free on September 13, 2011 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360, with new character models, among them Landon Ricketts, Vincente de Santa, Nastas and Uncle, and new map locations for Shootout, Gang Shootout, Grab the Bag, Gold Rush, Hold Your Own and Stronghold. Players select Javier in the Revolution section of the Outfitter, where his model inspects a knife. The multiplayer model has a far narrower sombrero than his story outfit.

The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, contain the story and Undead Nightmare but not the multiplayer mode, so the Javier Escuella model is not available in them. In Red Dead Online, the Zapatero outfit is based on one of Javier's outfits, and one of the saddles sold at the stables carries promotional artwork of him.

Marketing

Javier appears in the official teaser artwork of Red Dead Redemption 2 and in promotional artwork of his own. The third trailer, released on May 2, 2018, also shows him. On September 6, 2018, Rockstar posted the portraits and character descriptions of the gang's members on Twitter, Javier among them; The Hollywood Reporter reproduced the series the next day.

Trivia

Across the series

  • His surname is close to "escuela", the Spanish word for school. A Rockstar character from Grand Theft Auto IV (2008), Manny Escuela, has a near identical name.

Red Dead Redemption 2

  • Javier rides with the caravan in the opening cutscene of "Outlaws from the West", but neither he nor his horse can be found at Colter during the mission. The gang's eight draft horses are not at Colter either.
  • In "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", a unique death scene plays if the player lets the wolves reach Javier and John.
  • One of the two women Javier drinks with before the brawl in "Americans at Rest" appears again in a scene of the end credits, with Edgar Ross and Archer Fordham.
  • In "Rob a Homestead", Javier fights the Porters with whatever long gun he has on him, if any, rather than his custom revolver. Arthur's cough can be heard at the end of the activity, when the two men move the crate; the activity can be played as early as chapter 2.
  • The Lancaster Repeater Javier throws to Arthur in "Banking, The Old American Art" is Arthur's own, with any upgrades already applied.
  • In "A Kind and Benevolent Despot", opening fire on Fussar or his soldiers once Javier is seen being dragged by the donkey fails the mission, and a cutscene shows the guards killing Javier.
  • In "Red Dead Redemption", after Arthur and John come out of the caves, Bill and Javier are not among the riders who chase them, and neither appears on the minimap.

Red Dead Redemption

  • Of the old gang members John hunts in 1911, Javier is the only one the player can deliver alive.
  • The cuffs on a hogtied Javier are either copper or rusted iron.
  • His 1911 outfit resembles those of Ramiro de la Torre and Benito Penagarza, in other colors and without their two bandoliers. He wears Mexican Cavalry boots, and his sombrero has a slightly broader brim than the Bandito Outfit's.
  • Javier does not appear in Undead Nightmare.
