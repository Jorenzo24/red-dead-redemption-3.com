Chapter 1: Colter

Red Dead Redemption 2 Chapter 1 of 6 Colter, Ambarino 1899

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 24, 2026

Chapter 1: Colter is the first of the six chapters of Red Dead Redemption 2, followed by a two-part epilogue. It is set in May 1899 in the Grizzlies West, in the state of Ambarino. It follows a failed ferry robbery in Blackwater, which the Red Dead Wiki dates to April 1899. The chapter has six story missions, and two of them, "The Aftermath of Genesis" and "Old Friends", can be played in either order. The Red Dead Wiki describes it as the shortest chapter of the game.

Full spoilers for the opening of Red Dead Redemption 2 follow.

In-game chapter overview

The game's chapter overview reads:

"After a failed robbery, the gang have fled from Blackwater into the Grizzly Mountains, but a late spring storm has left them half frozen and starving. They hole up in an abandoned mining town to lick their wounds and await a break in the weather."

The game itself opens on white text over a black screen: "By 1899, the age of outlaws and gunslingers was at an end. America was becoming a land of laws... Even the west had mostly been tamed. A few gangs still roamed but they were being hunted down and destroyed."

Background: the Blackwater ferry robbery

The robbery that sends the gang into the mountains is never shown. What the player learns about it comes from the characters during the chapter.

  • In his speech at Colter, Dutch van der Linde names the people the gang has lost or cannot account for: Davey Callander, Jenny Kirk, Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander. He says of Sean and Mac: "they may be okay, we don't know."
  • Javier Escuella, who was there, tells Arthur in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": "We had the money, it seemed fine, then suddenly they were everywhere." The attackers were Pinkerton agents.
  • Javier also says that Dutch "killed a girl in a... bad way, but it was a bad situation." Arthur answers that it is not like him. The Red Dead Wiki names the young woman Heidi McCourt.
  • According to Javier, Davey, Mac and John were all shot, and nobody knows what happened to Sean. He adds that the men on the boat "were only just holding on" by the time Arthur and the others showed up.
  • On the ride to the Adler Ranch, the player picks one of two questions. Depending on the choice, Dutch either says the money was "stashed with the rest of the money in town" and that the gang cannot go back for it any time soon, or says of the Pinkertons: "That many men? Oh, they knew we were coming."
  • Simon Pearson, the camp cook, says he could not bring supplies when they ran. At the next camp, Susan Grimshaw tells Arthur that most of his belongings from Blackwater were saved. Arthur answers that his money was not.
  • In "Eastward Bound", Hosea Matthews tells Arthur that the two of them kept telling Dutch the ferry job "didn't feel right", and that they had "a real lead in Blackwater".

Newspapers in the game later put the sum aboard the ferry at 150,000 dollars. The fates of Sean and Mac are revealed in Chapter 2.

The missions of Chapter 1

1. Outlaws from the West

The first mission is given by Dutch. It starts and ends at Colter and takes Arthur to the Adler Ranch.

At night, the gang's wagons push through a snowstorm in the Grizzlies. Reverend Orville Swanson tells Dutch that Abigail says Davey is dying and that they must stop. Hosea adds: "If we don't stop soon, we'll all be dying. This weather, it's May..." Arthur Morgan, sent ahead by Dutch, rides back and reports an abandoned mining town nearby. He leads the wagons there. The title card "Chapter I: Colter" follows.

Hosea opens the first cabin, revolver drawn. Abigail walks in first, followed by Bill Williamson and Arthur, who carry Davey Callander on a stretcher. Susan Grimshaw sends Mary-Beth to light the fire, Karen to fetch blankets and Pearson to check the food. Abigail says: "Davey's dead." Swanson tells her there was nothing more she could have done, and Hosea asks how they will get supplies. Dutch announces that he sent John and Micah ahead to scout and that he and Arthur will ride out to find one of them.

Dutch then addresses the gang. He says: "There ain't nobody following us through a storm like this one." He adds that they may be at Colter for a few days. He asks Pearson and Susan to turn the place into a camp. He ends with: "Stay strong. Stay with me. We ain't done yet!"

Outside, Arthur asks what really happened on the boat. Dutch answers: "We missed you, that's what happened." Charles Smith brings them two horses; Dutch tells him to get indoors and rest his hand. Arthur rides Charles's mare, Taima. On the trail, Arthur says he cannot believe they lost Davey too. Dutch answers: "He's the last one, Arthur. No more." He tells Arthur he is the only one he can rely on to stay strong. The player can then ask about the money or about whether Blackwater was a trap.

They meet Micah Bell, who has found a homestead "blazing with light and noise", where it sounds like a party. Dutch tells him that Davey and Jenny did not make it. Micah has not seen John since the storm came in. In one of two optional exchanges with Arthur, Micah says the gang has "too many mouths to feed."

At the homestead, Dutch sends Micah behind a wagon and Arthur behind a shed. He goes to the door alone: "One lonely man is a lot less intimidating than three nasty-looking degenerates." He poses as a lost traveler and asks for a few cans of food. Micah finds a body under a sheet in the wagon. One of the men recognizes Dutch and shouts: "It's goddamn Dutch van der Linde, you morons!" The men are Colm O'Driscoll's gang. Arthur or Micah fires first, and the three outlaws kill the O'Driscolls at the house. The mission page names two of the dead, Billy and Declan.

Dutch and Arthur search the cabin for food, medicine and ammunition. Arthur points out a large pool of blood on the floor. According to the mission page, he also finds a wedding photograph of the couple, and he remarks that "the poor bastard was married too." Dutch tells him to put down anything they cannot eat or drink. Arthur notes that there is a large price on Colm's head; Dutch answers that wanting Colm dead "is about the only thing me and Uncle Sam agree on." Arthur suggests moving the women and Jack to the house, but Dutch does not want the gang to split up.

In the barn, an O'Driscoll who hid during the shootout jumps Arthur from the loft, shouting: "You bastards shot my cousin!" Arthur beats him while Dutch watches. Under questioning, the man says the others are at an old mining camp southwest of the ranch, near a lake, and that they plan to rob a train and blow the tracks. Dutch leaves his fate to Arthur, who can kill him, knock him out or let him go. According to the mission page, a spared O'Driscoll reappears in "Old Friends", shouting "You should've killed me when you had the chance!", and is killed there. Arthur then calms a Tennessee Walker in the barn, and Dutch tells him to keep it.

A woman screams inside the house. Micah has found her hiding in the cellar and insists she is one of the O'Driscolls. She throws a bottle at him, then holds him off with a knife from the table. Dutch tells Micah: "No, she ain't, Micah. Look at her." Micah flips the table, a lantern falls and the house catches fire. Dutch and Arthur push Micah away, and Dutch calms the woman and leads everyone outside. She says the men came three days earlier and killed her husband. Arthur helps her onto Dutch's horse and tells her: "We're bad men, but... we ain't them." On the ride back she gives her name: Sadie Adler. The house burns with her husband Jake's body still in the wagon.

At Colter, Lenny Summers is on watch and calls the others out. Dutch tells Hosea that O'Driscolls are in the area, scouting a train; Hosea answers: "That's the last thing we need right now, Dutch." Dutch asks Tilly and Karen to look after Sadie. He tells Abigail: "They turned her into a widow... animals." He says he has not slept in three days. Susan hands out the rooms. Micah protests that Arthur gets a room to himself while he shares a bunk room with Bill and others, using a racial slur, and Hosea sends him to bed.

Rewards, according to the mission page: the Mahogany Bay Tennessee Walker from the barn. The mission continues directly into the next one.

2. Enter, Pursued by a Memory

The mission is given by Abigail, listed under her 1899 name, Abigail Roberts, on the mission page. It starts and ends at Colter and takes place in the Grizzlies West.

In the main cabin, Abigail tells Arthur that John Marston has not been seen for two days. Arthur is reluctant and mocks John, calling him "little John". Hosea tells him to at least go and look, and asks Javier to ride out with him, calling them "the two best fit men we've got." Javier agrees, saying that John would look for him if things were reversed. He hands Arthur a sawed-off shotgun.

John was last seen heading up the river. Arthur suggests he may have "kept riding north and never looked back", and adds that it would not be the first time; Javier says John would not leave like that. Javier spots smoke, and they find an abandoned campsite with the fire still smoking. Arthur judges it was left "a few hours ago, at most" and hopes it is not more of O'Driscoll's boys. They follow horse tracks across a stream and up the mountain. On the way, Javier gives his account of Blackwater.

Higher up, they find John's bay mare, dead and mauled; Javier says John was riding it when they left Blackwater. Javier fires into the air, and John's shouts answer from higher up. The two men leave their horses and climb on foot. If the player does not use provisions on the climb, Javier shares a bottle of Kentucky bourbon with Arthur, according to the mission page. They find John lying on a ledge, his face torn by wolves. John says: "Never thought I'd say this, but... it's good to see you, Arthur Morgan."

Arthur carries John on his shoulder. Arthur tells John: "See, I told Dutch you weren't the right man for this." Three wolves appear on the slope. Arthur hands John to Javier, draws the wolves onto himself and kills them. On the ride down, more wolves attack and are shot. John thanks them for coming for him. Javier answers: "That bullet in Blackwater, now this?" They ride in the water for a while to lose the scent. Arthur tells John they will need a better story for the scar.

Back at Colter, Bill and Lenny pull John off Javier's horse so roughly that Javier calls them idiots and tells them to mind his leg. Abigail thanks the two men and tells John that this is "a new low, even by your standards." Hosea thanks them as well. Arthur asks how the gang will get out of this; Hosea, who has been talking with Leopold Strauss, says they will keep heading east when the weather breaks. Arthur is surprised at the idea of moving toward civilization. Hosea answers: "the west is where our problems are worse."

The mission closes on Arthur writing in his journal: "We have been running for weeks. We found shelter and been... resting here in some old abandoned mining town... while we wait the thaw. Hardly the spring I had been hoping for." After this mission, the player can walk around the camp, according to the mission page. Rewards, according to the mission page: the sawed-off shotgun and the "Just a Scratch" trophy.

3. The Aftermath of Genesis

The mission is given by Simon Pearson at Colter, where it starts and ends. It can be played before or after "Old Friends".

In the camp's kitchen, Pearson tells Arthur they have a few cans of food and a rabbit for ten or twelve people. He starts a story about being stranded at sea for fifty days in the Navy, which Arthur refuses to hear. Pearson says he sent Lenny and Bill hunting and they found nothing. Charles cuts the argument short: "Enough of this. We'll go find something."

Arthur points out Charles's injured hand. Charles says he burned it, a "stupid mistake", and expects it to heal in a day or two. Pearson throws Arthur a can of "Assorted Salted Offal" for the road. Since Charles cannot draw a bow, he gives his own to Arthur and explains that a gun would scare every animal for miles.

On the ride, Charles says he still does not really know what happened on the boat; Arthur says Javier told him a bit, "but... it sure weren't good." Charles finds deer tracks near a stream, Beartooth Beck according to the mission page, and teaches Arthur to follow them on foot, stay low and aim for the neck or head. Arthur kills two deer, and the two men carry one each to their horses.

On the way back, Charles worries that even off the mountain they will "still have a big price on our heads." Arthur answers that it is a big country and that "Dutch and Hosea will have a plan." He complains that Pearson has had a bottle in his hand since Blackwater and did not bring "a crumb of food." The next exchange depends on the order of play. If "Old Friends" is done, Charles notes that the gang has picked up two more mouths to feed, and Arthur says the O'Driscoll prisoner will get nothing but the can of salted offal. In both versions Charles says Sadie has "a wild look in her eye", and Arthur says she has lost her husband, her home and everything she had, and may have family somewhere. In the "Old Friends" version, Charles adds that she may deal with the O'Driscoll for them. Arthur explains that the gang has been fighting the O'Driscolls over scores for years and that Colm and Dutch "go way back, and not in a good way."

A grizzly bear blocks the trail. Arthur notes that it has a lot of meat on it. Charles answers that they have enough and that there is no need to push their luck. He adds: "Spring storms like this are the worst for animals that sleep all winter." They go around it. According to the mission page, the bear cannot be killed in this mission.

Arthur notes that Charles has been with them for five or six months. Asked whether he expected the Blackwater mess, Charles answers: "Sooner or later a job's going to go wrong. Nature of life." He says he rode alone for a long time and is done with it, and that Dutch treats him fair: "And for a feller with a black father and an Indian mother, that ain't normally the case." Asked in return, Arthur says he has been with Dutch for "Twenty years, something like that. Since I was a boy." Dutch taught him and John to read, and Arthur adds: "Dutch saved me, saved most of us."

Charles then asks about Arthur's new horse. Arthur thanks him for lending him Taima. Charles says he does not know what Dutch would do if something happened to his horse, the Count; Arthur says the same of Bill and his horse Brown Jack. Arthur adds that the Count once bucked him off and "won't take nobody but" Dutch.

At Colter, Uncle is loitering at Pearson's table and leaves after trading jabs with Arthur. Pearson hands round a bottle of Navy rum. Charles leaves to rest his hand, and Arthur helps Pearson skin a deer. Pearson tells him pelts sell in almost any town, and asks him to bring anything else he catches.

Rewards, according to the mission page: the bow, 22 arrows and the Eagle Eye ability. If the mission is played before "Old Friends", Hosea can be found at the butcher's table for a conversation, according to the same page. If it is played after "Old Friends", it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".

4. Old Friends

The mission is given by Bill Williamson in the men's cabin at Colter. It takes place at Ewing Basin, in the Grizzlies West, and ends at Colter. It can be played before or after "The Aftermath of Genesis".

In the cabin, Micah mocks Bill and Lenny for their long faces. Bill says people miss the ones who fell. Lenny tells Micah there will be a party when he falls, and Bill laughs. Micah punches Bill in the face; Arthur, Javier and Lenny hold the two men apart. Dutch bursts in: "You fools punching each other... when Colm O'Driscoll's needing punching, hard."

Arthur asks whether a raid is wise when the gang is barely back on its feet, and reminds Dutch that he always said revenge is a luxury they cannot afford. Dutch answers that the O'Driscolls "were talking about trains and detonators", and that Colm always had good information. He hands Arthur a Carbine Repeater and a lasso. Dutch leaves Hosea, Charles and Pearson to watch the camp, and rides out with Arthur, Micah, Bill, Javier and Lenny.

They follow horse tracks southwest along the main trail. Dutch says: "Some things I can forgive, others I can forget. What he did to Annabelle, I can't do neither." Arthur reminds him that he killed Colm's brother. Dutch answers that he hopes the two will be reunited soon.

From a ridge above the camp near the lake, Dutch posts Bill and Micah in a hidden position outside the camp and keeps Lenny and Javier on the high ground. Through binoculars, Dutch and Arthur watch Colm strike one of his men, who rides away; Colm leaves soon after. Dutch says: "No, Colm can wait. Best to get some of them outta there." He adds that it is "much less fun to rob him and his score if he never finds out about it." As they creep down, he tells Arthur that the gang needs "supplies, equipment, and a way out of here." Arthur offers to take the lead, since the O'Driscolls are "going to be gunning for" Dutch.

In cover, Dutch lets Arthur decide who opens fire. The gang kills the O'Driscolls in the camp. While the bodies are searched, Dutch says Colm "doesn't give a damn about his men" and only cares about numbers. A second wave then comes out of the trees, and Arthur chooses to advance or hold ground. Bill searches a wagon, Micah a building and Arthur another, where he finds a box of dynamite. Micah hands Dutch a roll of paper "found on one of them": the plans for a train belonging to a "Mr. Leviticus Cornwall."

On the ride back, Dutch tells the men he is proud that not a man is down: "Outlaws for life, fellers." He promises that John, Mac, Charles and Sean will ride with them again. Arthur says the raid "will hurt him a lot more than any bullet in the head"; Dutch answers: "Especially when we rob this train, too." When Arthur warns that Colm will come after them, Dutch says the gang allowed itself to get a step behind in Blackwater and that it will not happen again.

Near the lake, Dutch spots a lone rider and asks whether he was at the camp with Colm. The man is the one Colm struck. Dutch tells Arthur to bring him back alive. The man flees on his horse, Branwen; Arthur chases him, ropes him off the horse and hogties him. On the ride to Colter, the prisoner first says he does not know his name, then gives it: Kieran Duffy. He says he has only been riding with the O'Driscolls for a couple of months.

At camp, Arthur offers to make him talk. Dutch refuses: "Oh no, now all we'll get is lies." He tells Bill and Uncle to tie him up somewhere safe and get him hungry first. He tells Kieran: "we shoot fellers as need shooting... save fellers as need saving... and feed 'em as need feeding." Kieran protests that he is not an O'Driscoll and hates Colm; Dutch answers with sarcasm. Arthur says he is sorry they missed Colm. Dutch answers that there is "time enough for that" and that he must now "figure out if we can hit that train."

Rewards, according to the mission page: the Carbine Repeater, the lasso and Dead Eye targeting. If "Old Friends" is played second, it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".

5. Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?

The mission starts in John's cabin at Colter, where Dutch announces the job. It takes place in the Grizzlies West and, according to the mission page, ends at Granite Pass.

John lies in bed with Swanson beside him. Arthur needles the reverend, who tells him to show some respect and leaves. John tells Arthur: "I owe you." Arthur answers: "And you'll pay me." Dutch comes in and says it is time for the train. John asks to come and tries to get up: "I was always ugly, Dutch... it's just a scratch." Dutch tells him to lie still. Abigail brings Jack to see his father, and the couple trade insults in front of the boy.

Outside, Dutch sends Bill ahead to set the charge "at the water tower, just before the tunnel." Hosea objects: the weather is breaking, the gang could leave, and the plan was to lie low and head back west. He says: "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch." Dutch answers that everything the gang owns is in Blackwater. Hosea warns that "Leviticus Cornwall's no joke"; when Arthur asks who that is, Hosea describes Cornwall as "a big railway magnate, sugar dealer, oil man." Dutch answers that Cornwall "has more than enough to share" and tells the gang: "Gentlemen, it is time to make something of ourselves. Get your horses ready, we have a train to rob."

Dutch rides out with Arthur, Micah, Charles, Javier and Lenny. He explains that, according to the O'Driscolls' plans, the train will come north from Big Valley and will be hit after it crosses into the Grizzlies. Charles is to watch for outriders. Dutch takes the driver and engineer, Lenny and Javier the two front cars, and Arthur and Micah the private car at the back. As they leave the snow, Arthur says: "Out of the snow, finally." Micah keeps asking about going back to Blackwater for the money; Charles says it would be crazy with the town full of Pinkertons, and Dutch ends the discussion. Dutch says that if the O'Driscolls are right, there will be a stack of railroad bonds on the train.

At the ridge above the water tower, Dutch sends Arthur down to check on Bill. Bill has Arthur unspool the wire and set up the detonator by the rocks. Dutch tells the men to cover their faces, and they put on their bandanas. When the train passes, Bill pushes the plunger and nothing happens. Arthur asks Dutch: "Where did you find that moron?" Dutch answers: "You said it was fine." Arthur, Javier and Lenny run to the far end of the tunnel and jump onto the train from above. Javier misses and is left behind; the mission page says he is hurt in the fall. Lenny ends up hanging from the side of a car, and Arthur can pull him up; otherwise Lenny climbs up alone and thanks him sarcastically.

Inside the first car, Lenny kills a guard with his knife. Arthur chooses whether he or Lenny takes the next one, which starts the gunfight along the train. Lenny asks what Bill was doing; Arthur says he hooked up the wire himself, "but we won't mention that." Near the engine, the engineer comes out of the coal bin and hits Arthur with a shovel. According to the mission page, if Lenny is ahead, the engineer attacks him instead and Arthur has to save him. Arthur overpowers the engineer, throws him off the train as it crosses a bridge and stops it. Guards pour out of the cars, and the rest of the gang rides in to help clear them.

Employees have locked themselves in the last car. One of them calls out: "I work for Leviticus Cornwall." Dutch counts down from five, has the gang fire on the car, then sends Arthur and Charles to blow the door with Bill's dynamite. The employees come out with their hands up; Dutch tells them: "We don't want to kill you... we just wanna rob your boss." He adds that next time, they should "open the goddamn door" when he says so. Cornwall himself is not on the train.

Arthur, Lenny and Micah search the car, which Lenny compares to a palace. Micah opens the safe and finds only papers. He hands them to Lenny, saying that at least Lenny can read, and skims railroad contracts and invoices himself. Lenny finds sugar imports from the Spanish West Indies and "Some fancy new boat he's ordered from Europe." Micah laughs that he will not rob another boat as long as he lives. Arthur finds the bonds in the car's cabinets. Dutch calls them bearer bonds and thinks they can be sold easily.

Dutch tells Arthur to get rid of the train and leaves the employees to him: "It's up to you." He lists the options: "Kill em', leave em' here... take 'em with you on the train... Just make sure they don't send no folk after us." Arthur can kill them, or put them back on the train, start the engine and jump off. According to the mission page, if he does neither, they run away, and the choice only affects the player's honor. Dutch tells Arthur that the gang will be moving on when he gets back.

Rewards, according to the mission page: dynamite. Two documents can be missed in the car: the Oil Company Letter to Cornwall and the Mining Company Letter to Cornwall. The mission continues directly into "Eastward Bound".

6. Eastward Bound

The last mission of the chapter is given by Dutch. It starts at Colter and ends at Horseshoe Overlook, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.

Arthur rides back from the train to find the camp loading the wagons. Dutch says the weather seems stable; Hosea reminds him they just robbed a Cornwall train. Dutch declares: "the worst is behind us." Hosea proposes Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine: "We'll be able to hide out there no problem... as long as we keep our noses clean." Charles and Abigail help John into a wagon. Dutch puts Arthur and Hosea in the same wagon, saying the two like to talk about "what's gone wrong with old Dutch."

According to the stage directions of the wiki's transcript, the convoy passes the frozen Lake Isabella and crosses a stream out of the snow. Dutch then sends Micah and Lenny ahead to check the road. Micah complains about being paired with "the boy", then tells Lenny he can buy him a whisky. The convoy later goes under a railroad bridge into Big Valley, in West Elizabeth, and rides into the Dakota River next to Cumberland Falls, on the border with New Hanover, according to the same stage directions. Once the wagons are over the river, a wheel comes off Arthur and Hosea's wagon. The wagon ahead, carrying Bill and Charles, with Javier riding alongside, stops. Hosea turns down Javier's help; Charles stays behind, and he and Hosea hold the wagon up while Arthur puts the wheel back on.

Three riders watch them from a cliff. Charles says: "If they wanted trouble... we wouldn't have seen them." Hosea says: "Poor bastards... we really screwed them over down here." The Red Dead Wiki identifies the riders as Rains Fall, Eagle Flies and Paytah, of the Wapiti tribe. Later in the game, in Saint Denis, Rains Fall tells Arthur: "We saw you on the wagon train... crossing the river at Cumberland Falls."

Charles rides on in their wagon. Hosea explains that the native people of the Heartlands lost the land and were killed or moved to reservations, and that he heard the Army there was particularly harsh. Charles asks: "How do you rob and kill people pleasantly? We don't, in spite of Dutch's talk." Hosea says he was simplifying "for the benefit of our blockheaded driver"; Arthur answers that Hosea is "a conman, Charles, born and bred."

Arthur asks Charles about his tribe. Charles says he does not know if he has one. His father was a Black man who lived with his mother's people for a while; when they were forced off their lands, the three fled. Soldiers later took his mother, and they never saw her again. His father drank, and at around thirteen Charles struck out on his own. Hosea says it was about the age they found Arthur: "A wilder delinquent you never did see. But he learned fast." Arthur answers: "Not as fast as Marston, apparently." When Charles asks what the problem is between the two, Arthur says: "It's a long story."

Hosea describes Valentine as a livestock town of "Cowboys, outlaws, working girls." He says there are probably O'Driscolls there too, and hopes there are no Pinkertons. He asks Arthur: "how low do you think Dutch is really going to lie?" He adds that it "isn't like Dutch to lose his head like that." Arthur answers that things go wrong sometimes and that the three of them must have got it right far more often than wrong. Hosea gives Arthur yarrow and ginseng he has been preparing. At the entrance to the site, Javier waits by a rock, shows them the track and jumps onto the back of the wagon.

At the new camp, Dutch says: "Gentlemen, we have survived." He adds: "Now it is time to prosper." Hosea answers: "Arthur and I were about to prosper in Blackwater. We were on to something big... then Micah got you all excited about that ferry and here we are." Dutch answers that they have all made mistakes, but that he kept the gang together and "kept the nooses off our neck." Hosea says he does not have long and wants people safe before he goes. He says they are now stuck "east of the Grizzlies and out of money". Dutch's plan is to make some money, go around the law, "be west of Uncle Sam" and buy land within a few months. Strauss leaves for the nearby town to "strike up a little business." Dutch says: "I prefer robbing banks to usury... seems more dignified somehow."

Dutch gathers the gang. He tells them they are safe but "far too poor", and that everyone must get to work. Hosea tells them to stay out of trouble: "Remember, we are itinerant workers." Dutch completes the cover story: "Laid off when they shut down our factory to the north." He adds: "Uncle, Reverend Swanson... no more passengers." Hosea points them to Valentine: "All mud and morons if I remember right." Pearson asks for real food every day, and Dutch sets out a box: "the camp gets its slice." Susan shows Arthur to his tent.

The title card "Chapter II: Horseshoe Overlook" appears, followed by "A couple of weeks later..." Arthur writes in his journal: "We got off the mountain and rode east into some pretty enough country called the Heartlands. Ain't been this far east in many a year. Dutch seems a little better."

Hosea then brings Arthur a cup of a hot drink. He tells him that Bill, Charles and Javier are already in Valentine, that Swanson "found something" at the train station by the lake, and that Strauss came back with "that creepy little smile on his face." Asked about his own plans, Hosea says: "I'm gonna read a book." The mission ends there.

Rewards, according to the mission page: free roam, a camera, five ginseng and five yarrow, the Volcanic Pistol, Varmint Rifle and Springfield Rifle at gunsmiths, and the "Back in the Mud" trophy for completing Chapter 1. Horseshoe Overlook becomes the gang's camp.

Camp life at Colter

The Camp Events page of the Red Dead Wiki lists a small number of optional scenes at Colter. They can be seen once "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" is complete, until the second of "Old Friends" and "The Aftermath of Genesis" is finished.

  • By the fireplace, Dutch tells Hosea he feels Hosea doubts him. Hosea says he is only worried. Dutch then asks Arthur the same question, and Arthur says he does not doubt him.
  • In the main cabin, Sadie sobs over her husband while Mary-Beth Gaskill comforts her. Mary-Beth tells Arthur that Sadie will be alright, and he agrees. Arthur can offer Sadie food; she says she is not hungry.
  • Susan Grimshaw gives the cabin a speech: keep warm, the mountain will be behind them in a few days, and the gang must stick together. Tilly Jackson says she hopes Susan is right. Susan asks Arthur for his opinion, and he agrees with her.
  • Swanson reads Isaiah 40:20-31 aloud. Karen Jones asks what it means; he says he has no idea, but it gives him comfort.
  • Abigail scolds the bedridden John for getting himself mauled by wolves. John says he has made his mistakes; Abigail takes offense, calls him annoying and tells him to rest.
  • When Arthur approaches, John says "Oh God no." Susan asks Arthur what he wants. He says he came to pay his respects to "the fallen hero", and Susan tells him to leave John alone.
  • Dutch asks Hosea to send someone "sensible" to bury Sadie's husband. Arthur can tell Hosea the body is in a wagon in front of the house, and Hosea says he will send someone. Arthur tells Dutch it was a good call; Dutch says it is the least they could do for Sadie. Arthur jokes about sending Micah, then changes his mind.
  • Dutch and Hosea argue about what comes next. Dutch tells Hosea that he was "the one with the death wish". Hosea brings up the Pinkertons and bounty hunters; Dutch says nobody would follow them in this storm. He says the plan is to lie low, make money and get out of the country, but admits he does not know where to yet. He ends by saying they are not done yet.
  • If "Old Friends" comes first, Kieran can be found tied up in the stable. He tries to scare Arthur with the O'Driscolls who will come looking for him, and learns that his group was not the first the gang has killed. If Arthur leaves and comes back, Kieran asks when he will be released and gets more taunts.
  • According to the mission page of "Outlaws from the West", Arthur can offer to give the horse from the barn back to Sadie; she says it belonged to an O'Driscoll.

The same page notes that Bill, Javier, Charles and Pearson have no camp events in this chapter. According to the Colter page of the wiki, the stable stands on the right when coming from the south, with Pearson's butcher stand beside it, and the ruined Colter Chapel, with a small graveyard, sits at the north edge of town. The same page says Arthur's health drains quickly if he strays too far from the camp during the chapter.

Characters in this chapter

Arthur Morgan, the playable character, takes part in every mission. Dutch van der Linde leads the gang and Hosea Matthews argues against the train job. John Marston is found wounded by wolves and spends the rest of the chapter in bed, visited by Abigail and their son Jack.

The riders of the chapter are Micah Bell, Bill Williamson, Javier Escuella, Lenny Summers and Charles Smith, whose burned hand keeps him off the bow. The camp is run by Susan Grimshaw and fed by Simon Pearson. Also present are Orville Swanson, Leopold Strauss, Uncle, Karen Jones, Tilly Jackson, Mary-Beth Gaskill and Molly O'Shea, whom Susan asks to show Dutch to his room.

Two newcomers arrive at camp in this chapter: Sadie Adler, taken in after the raid on her ranch, and Kieran Duffy, held as a prisoner. Colm O'Driscoll is seen only through binoculars at Ewing Basin. Leviticus Cornwall is named but does not appear. Rains Fall is identified with one of the riders on the cliff (see "Eastward Bound").

Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander are missing, and Josiah Trelawny does not appear until a later chapter.

Trivia

  • Davey Callander's grave is in a small graveyard at Colter and counts toward the "Paying Respects" trophy, according to the Red Dead Wiki. Jenny Kirk's grave, in the Spider Gorge area north of Colter, can also be visited, according to the same wiki.
  • Hosea's line "If we don't stop soon, we'll all be dying" is the one Arthur remembers in "A Fork in the Road" with high honor. His plea "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch" returns during Arthur's last ride with high honor, according to the wiki's mission pages.
  • The opening scene of the caravan in the storm takes place outside the playable map, northwest of Spider Gorge, according to the mission page. The same page says the Old Lantern exists only in "Outlaws from the West", and that the compendium can be opened during that mission, then stays locked until "The Aftermath of Genesis".
  • According to the mission page of "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", a short piece of the main theme of Red Dead Redemption plays after Arthur picks up John. On the climb, Arthur and Javier briefly leave the normal map boundaries. After the mission, an avalanche closes the mountain north of the ravine where John was found, and the area can no longer be reached.
  • The title "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" plays on the stage direction "Exit, pursued by a bear" from Shakespeare's The Winter's Tale, as does the later mission "Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego", according to the wiki.
  • Charles's mare Taima is the only gang member's horse the player rides in a mission, according to the mission page of "Outlaws from the West".
  • In "The Aftermath of Genesis", the first deer can be tracked by its prints in the snow without Eagle Eye, according to the mission page.
  • In "Old Friends", killing Kieran's horse Branwen during the chase costs honor and has no effect on the story, according to the mission page.
  • According to the mission page of "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?", the cutscene always shows the train stopping at Granite Pass, southwest of Window Rock, just before the bridge. The same page notes that Micah fires a Carbine Repeater at the employees' car, one of the rare times he uses a longarm.
  • The train robbery is reported under the headline "Train Robbed" in the Blackwater Ledger (No. 62), the New Hanover Gazette (No. 27) and the Saint Denis Times (No. 43). The clerk at Valentine station may mention it. Arthur's journal lists the job twice under "Gang Savings", the second time after Hosea cashes in the bonds, according to the wiki.
  • Colter is not revisited in the story after Chapter 1, but the player can return once free roam opens in Chapter 2. Playing as John, the player can hear the ghostly sounds of the gang talking there, according to the Colter page. In Red Dead Online, set in 1898, Colter is an O'Driscoll hideout, according to the wiki.

Key events of Chapter 1

  • May 1899: after the failed Blackwater ferry robbery, the gang flees into the Grizzlies West of Ambarino and shelters in the abandoned mining town of Colter. Davey Callander arrives dead; Jenny Kirk has already died; Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander are missing.
  • "Outlaws from the West": Dutch, Arthur and Micah kill a band of O'Driscolls at the Adler Ranch and bring back the widow Sadie Adler. Arthur keeps a Tennessee Walker from the barn.
  • "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": Arthur and Javier find John Marston on a mountain ledge, his face torn by wolves, and bring him back to camp.
  • "The Aftermath of Genesis": Charles teaches Arthur to hunt with a bow, and they bring two deer back to Pearson.
  • "Old Friends": the gang raids the O'Driscoll camp at Ewing Basin, takes dynamite and the plans for a Cornwall train, and Arthur captures Kieran Duffy.
  • "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?": the gang robs Leviticus Cornwall's train in the Grizzlies and takes railroad bonds, despite Hosea's objections.
  • "Eastward Bound": the gang leaves the snow for a new camp at Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.
