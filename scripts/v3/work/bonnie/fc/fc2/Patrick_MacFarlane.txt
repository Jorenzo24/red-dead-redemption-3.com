'''Patrick MacFarlane''' is a [[Characters in Redemption#Unseen/Deceased|minor, unseen character]] featured in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

==Background==
Patrick was born as one of the [[MacFarlane brothers]], the sons of [[Drew MacFarlane]]. By 1911, he is the only surviving male child of Drew, and his only child besides [[Bonnie MacFarlane|Bonnie]].

In the early 1900s Patrick left [[MacFarlane's Ranch]] and moved to [[wikipedia:New York City|New York]], where he became, according to one of his latest letters to his sister, a successful banker.

==Interactions==
As he is located in another state by the time of ''Redemption'', Patrick is never encountered by [[Marston]]. He is, however, mentioned by his sister in the [[Missions in Redemption|mission]] [[The Burning|"The Burning"]]. He is also mentioned and referenced by residents of [[Warthington Ranch]] and [[Armadillo]].
