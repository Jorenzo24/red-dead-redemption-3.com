The '''Legends and Killers''' Pack is a piece of downloadable content for ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''. It was released on August 10, 2010.

==Content==

The pack includes :
*9 New Multiplayer map locations
**[[Tall Trees]]
**[[Thieves' Landing]]
**[[Fort Mercer]]
**[[Manzanita Post]]
**[[Blackwater]]
**[[Punta Orgullo]]
**[[Escalera]]
**[[Hennigan's Stead]]
**[[Rio Bravo]]

*8 New Multiplayer playable characters from ''[[Red Dead Revolver]]''
**[[Red Harlow]]
**[[Jack Swift]]
**[[Mr. Kelley]]
**[[Annie Stoakes]]
**[[Buffalo Soldier]]
**[[Shadow Wolf]]
**[[Ugly Chris]]
**[[Pig Josh]]

* 10 New Achievements/Trophies
**[[Axe Master]]
**[[Call It a Comeback!]]
**[[Double Bagger]]
**[[Hail Mary]]
**[[Headhunter]]
**[[Legendary]]
**[[Original Gunslinger]]
**[[Reeeeal Good]]
**[[Stick and Move]]
**[[Who Needs Deadeye?]]

*A new projectile weapon: the [[Tomahawk]], with corresponding Challenges for single player and multiplayer.

==Reception==
'''Legends and Killers''' was met with mixed to fair reception. The PS3 holds 64/100 based on 4 reviews <ref>http://www.metacritic.com/game/playstation-3/red-dead-redemption-legends-killers/critic-reviews</ref> while the Xbox 360 version holds 61/100.<ref>http://www.metacritic.com/game/xbox-360/red-dead-redemption-legends-killers/critic-reviews</ref>

Eurogamer gave the DLC a 6/10, stating "An expensive indulgence, Legends and Killers undoubtedly improves the multiplayer in terms of variety, but that's just not enough to make it essential." The Itailan version of the website awarded the DLC with the same score, stating "This DLC is a little too small, compared to other additional contents from Rockstar. Forget the huge DLC for GTA: this time we have few maps, few characters from the previous Red Dead and one single new weapon. It's not enough for a 10 Euro purchase, unless you are addicted to the multiplayer of RDR."
