## Problems

1. **WRONG** (Names): "When he sings to himself in Benedict Point, he calls himself Uncle Irish."
   Source: Man_is_Born_Unto_Trouble_dialogues. The "Let Uncle Irish untie them strings" line is said while he is passed out, talking to himself (stage direction: "Irish is talking to himself"). The singing ("I love me faithless Flora") comes later, on horseback.
   Fix: "Talking in his sleep in Benedict Point, he calls himself Uncle Irish." FR to match.

2. **IMPRECISE** (Intro + Key facts): "Irish first sends John to an empty cabin" / "Irish then sends him to a cabin at Lake Don Julio where the gun is not."
   Source: Frenchman transcript and mission page. Irish rides with John and leads him to the ridge ("Accompany Irish to Lake Don Julio"). The cabin is guarded by armed men; only the gun is missing.
   Fix: intro: "Irish first leads John to a guarded cabin where there is no gun"; key facts: "Irish then leads him to a guarded cabin at Lake Don Julio, where there is no gun."

3. **IMPRECISE + DUPLICATE** (Origins): "He tells John that he has been to Mexico before, that he was very drunk the whole time, and that he has many friends there."
   Source: Paradise transcript: "I was really drunk last time I was here". It is "last time", not "the whole time". The same facts are told again in the Fort Mercer paragraph ("many friends in the south"), the Paradise paragraph ("more friends than he can count", "very drunk on his last visit") and Personality ("memories of Mexico are blurred by drink").
   Fix: delete the sentence from Origins. The Paradise paragraph owns the scene.

4. **IMPRECISE** (Fort Mercer): "Jonah reports that a captured gang member says Bill left for Mexico the morning before, somewhere near Chuparosa."
   Source: Fort Mercer transcript. Jonah reports only "yesterday morning". The bandit himself says "Someplace near Chuparosa" when Eli presses him. This is how leigh_A and bill_A tell it.
   Fix: "Jonah reports that a captured gang member says Bill left for Mexico the morning before; pressed by Eli, the prisoner places him somewhere near Chuparosa."

5. **IMPRECISE** (Paradise landing): "He remembers an American he saw shoot a man and with whom he drank in Chuparosa, then wonders whether that was in Canada."
   Source: Paradise transcript: "Or was that Canada...? No. That was Canada. Guy here, not funny, but he's real nice." Irish settles on Canada and then describes a different man in Mexico. The draft stops at the doubt.
   Fix: "...then decides that this was in Canada, and that the man in Mexico was not funny but very nice." Alternatively keep "wonders" and add "without settling it". landon_B/C only say "drank in Chuparosa with an American", which is still fine. FR to match.

6. **UNSUPPORTED (age)** (Origins): "left Ireland for the United States at the age of fourteen, shortly after he was caught in bed with his brother's wife; the wiki places that remark in 'Man is Born Unto Trouble'."
   Source: the age and the brother's wife are not in the Man is Born Unto Trouble transcript and not on the Irish/Quotes page; they are wiki Background/Trivia only. Twinkle-Toe Dave and "grew up poor and hungry" are not in either source either. The age also sits badly with the transcript's "met on the boat over a few years back" for a man shown well into adulthood. House rule: no age that is not taken from a source.
   Fix: drop "at the age of fourteen". Keep the brother's wife and Twinkle-Toe Dave only as attributed wiki claims ("the wiki says he mentions..."), which is already the frame.

7. **COPIED** (Origins), same sentence. Wiki: "Irish emigrated to the United States at fourteen years old shortly after being caught in bed with his brother's wife." Draft: "left Ireland for the United States at the age of fourteen, shortly after he was caught in bed with his brother's wife".
   Fix: reword, e.g. "The wiki also says he gives an affair with his brother's wife as one of his reasons for leaving Ireland."

8. **SPECULATION** (Origins): "He swears on his poor mother's life, which the Red Dead Wiki reads as a sign that she is still alive in 1911."
   Source: Man is Born transcript ("I swear it on me poor Mother's life"). The "still alive" claim is the wiki's own inference (Trivia), and the transcript does not say it.
   Fix: "He swears on his poor mother's life." Drop the wiki reading. The facts grid "and a mother" can stay.

9. **UNSUPPORTED** (Origins, first sentence): "an Irish immigrant who deals in stolen goods and weapons in New Austin."
   Source: the wiki infobox says "Contraband Dealer"; Dickens/John say "arms dealer"; no source calls them stolen goods. French accuses him of stealing their gun, and Irish denies it.
   Fix: "who deals in weapons and contraband in New Austin."

10. **IMPRECISE** (Names): "According to the French-language Red Dead Wiki, the French version of the game calls him l'Irlandais."
   Source: fr.txt. The FR wiki only titles and names him "L'Irlandais" and says nothing about the French version of the game.
   Fix: "The French-language Red Dead Wiki calls him L'Irlandais."

11. **DUPLICATE** (In his own words vs Quotes): "I came into this world fightin'. And I'll go out of it fightin'!" is quoted in "In his own words" and again in the Quotes block (half2: "Irish: I came into this world fightin'... / Marston: I expect you'll go out of it pissin' your pants.").
   Rule: OWNERSHIP says a line quoted in B is not repeated in C. Fix: keep it in one place. Keep the exchange in Quotes (C) and give "In his own words" another verified line, e.g. "I'm a good Catholic boy." Or drop the C entry.

12. **DUPLICATE** (Personality, paragraph 2 + first paragraph): "he blames his poor memory on whiskey"; "offers to cover John from a ridge, says the mines play havoc with his sinuses, and volunteers to fetch the wagon"; "on the river, he shoots back and calls out where the attackers are"; "admits his memories of Mexico are blurred by drink".
   Problem: each of these events is already told in the Biography. The whiskey line also appears as an "In his own words" quote, so it is told three times, and the sinuses line also appears in Quotes. OWNERSHIP says Biography owns events and Personality is not a second biography.
   Fix: reduce paragraph 2 to the traits with no mission retelling ("He avoids danger and lets John do the fighting; the river crossing is the only mission in which he fights, according to the mission page."). Drop the whiskey clause from paragraph 1.

13. **DUPLICATE**: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, he admits during the crossing that he cannot swim" is in the Biography (Paradise) and again in Skills (half2: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, he admits during the crossing...").
   Fix: keep it in the Biography and cut it from Skills, or the other way round. Tell it once.

14. **DUPLICATE**: "The game never gives his real name" is in Origins, in the facts grid ("no other name is given in the game") and in Names ("the game never gives him another name").
   Fix: drop it from Origins. Names and the facts grid own it.

15. **DUPLICATE** (Names): "The 1914 newspaper notice that the Red Dead Wiki attributes to him describes an Irish man of unknown name (see Biography)."
   Problem: this is already in the intro, the facts grid and the 1914 h3.
   Fix: delete it from Names.

16. **STYLE** (Intro, sentence 2): "He then takes John across the river to Mexico on his raft, under fire from gunmen on the Mexican bank, where he admits there is a bounty on his head, and leaves him on the other bank."
   Problem: four facts are packed into one sentence, and "where" reads as if he made the admission on the bank.
   Fix: "He then takes John across the river to Mexico on his raft. Gunmen fire on them from the Mexican side, and Irish admits there is a bounty on his head. He leaves John on the other bank."

17. **COPIED (minor)** (Undead Nightmare): "a half-eaten corpse lying next to the Armadillo train station that resembles him, with a Repeater Carbine beside the body."
   Wiki: "next to the train station in Armadillo, there is a half-eaten corpse, that if looked at carefully, has a resemblance to Irish, with a Repeater Carbine next to the body".
   Fix: reword, e.g. "The wiki also points to a partly eaten body near Armadillo's train station that looks like him, a Repeater Carbine lying beside it."

## Story guide, RDR1 Act 1 (story/rdr1-act-1-new-austin + FR)

A. **UNSUPPORTED / judgment**: EN "Irish is a drunk gunrunner who claims connections he mostly does not have." FR "Irish est un trafiquant d'armes alcoolique qui se vante de relations qu'il n'a pas."
   Problem: his connections do deliver. He leads John to the Gatling gun, and his contact Shaky provides the ammunition. Only his Mexican "friends" turn hostile. The FR version is stronger than the EN ("qu'il n'a pas" against "mostly"). The guide also never says that Irish misses the assault (HOUSE_FACTS).
   Exact fixes:
   - EN: "Irish is a drunken arms dealer who finds the Gatling gun and its ammunition for John, but does not turn up for the assault itself."
   - FR: "Irish est un trafiquant d'armes porté sur la bouteille ; il procure à John la mitrailleuse Gatling et ses munitions, mais ne se montre pas le jour de l'assaut."
B. Optional, not an error: the guide ends with "the hunt moves south" and never says how John crosses. If desired, add after it. EN: "Irish, who arrives once the fighting is over, takes John across the river into Mexico on his raft (\"We Shall Be Together in Paradise\")." FR: "Irish, arrivé une fois les combats terminés, lui fait passer le fleuve jusqu'au Mexique sur son radeau (\"We Shall Be Together in Paradise\")."
No other statement about Irish in either guide is wrong. The other mentions only name him among the recruits.
