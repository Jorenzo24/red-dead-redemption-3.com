## Problems

1. IMPRECISE. Key facts: "he is already inside the fort and lets Dickens's wagon through the gate."
   Source: The_Assault_on_Fort_Mercer_dialogues.txt: "(West pulls up to Fort Mercer's gate. Outlaws open the gate.)" then
   Seth: "Come on in... Welcome to my humble abode!"; en.txt: "The Williamson Gang then let in Nigel West Dickens' stage coach."
   Fix: "In "The Assault on Fort Mercer", he is already inside the fort and welcomes Dickens's wagon at the gate."
   (Biography already says this correctly.)

2. IMPRECISE. Intro: "he walked into Blackwater with a large haul of gold and precious stones found in the mountains of Tall
   Trees." Key facts: "reports his return from Tall Trees with gold and precious stones."
   Source: fc/Treasure_Found_1914.txt: he "walked out of the mountains in Tall Trees and into Blackwater with a massive haul";
   the article does not say where the haul was found, nor that he was "returning".
   Fix intro: "...reports that he walked out of the mountains of Tall Trees and into Blackwater with a large haul of gold and
   precious stones." Key facts: "...reports that he came out of the mountains of Tall Trees into Blackwater with gold and
   precious stones." (FR to match: "est sorti des montagnes de Tall Trees".)

3. UNSUPPORTED. Facts grid: "Former partners: Moses Forth, Aiden O'Leary".
   Source: Gentle Drive transcript only says O'Leary "said he had the body" and Seth "never liked him"; en.txt lists him
   under affiliations "(formerly)", never as a partner. Seth's only stated partner is Moses ("My partner. My boy. My man.").
   Fix: "Former partner: Moses Forth" (drop O'Leary, or move him to an "Associates" line attributed to the wiki).

4. IMPRECISE. Act I: "He says he talks to the dead long after everyone else has forgotten them and comforts them when they rot."
   Source: Exhuming transcript: "I tell 'em it's alright to be scared and alone. I embrace 'em when they're stinkin' and rotten."
   Fix: "...tells them it is all right to be scared and alone, and embraces them when they rot."

5. IMPRECISE. Act I: "John offers to help him get the map back, on condition that Seth helps him in return".

6. IMPRECISE. Act I (No. 53): "he was believed to be behind a string of such crimes across the West".

   Fix: "...and that he was believed to be solely responsible for a string of such crimes across the West..."
