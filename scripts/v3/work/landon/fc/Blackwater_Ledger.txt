The '''Blackwater Ledger''' is a [[Newspapers|newspaper]] publisher in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]'' and ''[[Red Dead Redemption 2]]''.

==Description==
The Blackwater Ledger is the primary newspaper source in ''Red Dead Redemption'', and one of three newspapers in ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', alongside the [[New Hanover Gazette]] and [[Saint Denis Times|Saint Denis Times Tribune]], that reports of various events actively taking place throughout the games' stories. The player can often find articles about events they were involved in, such as a robbery or disaster. Posting Mondays and Fridays, vendors can be seen selling these newspapers in local towns, such as [[Blackwater]] and [[Armadillo]] in ''Red Dead Redemption'', or Blackwater and [[Strawberry]] in ''Red Dead Redemption 2''.

The Blackwater Ledger is headquartered in the Blackwater Ledger Local News Printing & Publishing building in Blackwater. The company is owned by newspaper tycoon A. H. McInlay. 

In ''Red Dead Redemption'', the newspaper is published weekly.<ref>The article "Federal Government Increases Taxes, Extends Reach Further" in Blackwater Ledger No. 60 refers to the article "Tax Increase Necessary to Fund Government Expansion" from Blackwater Ledger No. 59 as having occurred "last week".</ref>

==Trivia==
* In ''Red Dead Redemption'', the last issue is considerably different from the others. It is called ''The Blackwater Ledger and Sentinel'' instead of simply ''The Ledger'', and generally has a different format.
* In ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', the player can purchase a maximum of 40 newspapers. Of the three 14th newspaper issues, only one can be purchased. For example, if the player purchases [[Saint Denis Times No. 56]], the other two 14th issues, Blackwater Ledger No. 75 and [[New Hanover Gazette No. 40]], will no longer be available for purchase.<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exO9duy2EOQ</ref>
* In ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', once a newspaper has been purchased and read, the other versions of that issue will no longer be available for purchase from newspaper vendors. For example, if the player purchases and reads [[Saint Denis Times No. 43]], the other two versions of the same issue, Blackwater Ledger No. 62 and [[New Hanover Gazette No. 27]], will no longer be available for purchase from newspaper vendors. This was presumably intended to limit the player to owning only 14 newspaper issues.
* In ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', if the player purchases and reads every available newspaper up to the latest issue at that point in the story, newspaper vendors will temporarily disappear from the game. They will reappear once a new issue becomes available. As a result, if the player purchases and reads all 14 newspaper issues, newspaper vendors will no longer appear for the remainder of the game. Although newspaper vendor icons may still appear in various towns after all 14 issues have been purchased and read, these icons will disappear as the player approaches the respective town.
