## Problems

1. **WRONG** (cross-reference). Relationships, MacDougal: "calls him a stupid man for a wise one (see Quotes)" (same "(see Quotes)" in Personality, half1). The Quotes block does NOT contain "For a wise man, you are a very stupid man, mister." (At Home with Dutch transcript). Fix: either add that line to Quotes (it is not used in "In his own words"), or drop "(see Quotes)" in both places.

2. **WRONG** (gallery). Image 3, alt "Nastas and John Marston outdoors", caption "Nastas and John." The image is the instant Enepay's bullet hits Nastas's forehead inside the Bear Claw cabin, John behind him (wiki file "Nastas's fate.png", "Nastas' death at the hands of Enepay"). It is a graphic gore frame. Fix: caption "Nastas is shot by Enepay in the Bear Claw cabin (For Purely Scientific Purposes)" / FR equivalent, or drop the image (gore; Joseph to decide).

3. **IMPRECISE** (gallery). Image 2, "Nastas, John Marston and others with hands raised" / "Nastas with John Marston." It is the Bear Claw cabin scene of "For Purely Scientific Purposes": Nastas, John and MacDougal held at gunpoint by Dutch's men just before the shooting (wiki file "Rdr scientific purposes.jpg", "Nastas being held at gunpoint"). Fix: "Nastas, John Marston and Harold MacDougal held at gunpoint at the Bear Claw cabin."

4. **IMPRECISE** (gallery). Image 4, alt "Nastas and Harold MacDougal on a dark staircase." It is canon, not Undead Nightmare: MacDougal (with smelling salts pose) and Nastas looking down at John as he wakes in MacDougal's room in Blackwater, end of "At Home with Dutch" (wiki file "Rdr 0587.jpg", listed in the Red Dead Redemption gallery: "Nastas and MacDougal look down at Marston"). Fix alt and caption accordingly. Image 5 (undead Nastas in a Blackwater street) and image 1 (portrait in MacDougal's room) are correct.

5. **IMPRECISE**. Guide and informant: "He went to school on the reservation". Transcript: "I grew up on a reservation and attended school." (At Home with Dutch); the wiki says a boarding school. Where he studied is not stated in game. Fix: "He grew up on a reservation and went to school". (The intro in half1, "went to school there", has the same issue.)

6. **IMPRECISE**. Missions: "'Bear One Another's Burdens' ... it opens MacDougal's strand, which continues with 'At Home with Dutch'". BOAB belongs to Ross's strand; its mission page lists as "next" both Ross's "Great Men are Not Always Wise" and MacDougal's "At Home with Dutch". MacDougal's strand begins with "At Home with Dutch". Fix: "it unlocks MacDougal's strand, which begins with 'At Home with Dutch' and continues with 'For Purely Scientific Purposes'."

7. **IMPRECISE**. Relationships, MacDougal: "MacDougal treats him as a specimen of his theories: ... wants skin samples from the gang's feet." The skin samples concern Dutch's men, not Nastas (FPSP transcript: "a skin sample from the soles of their feet"), so it does not illustrate how he treats Nastas; and "contradicts his ideas ... on the bear": MacDougal states no idea about the bear beforehand, Nastas comments after the kill. Fix: drop "treats him as a specimen" framing and the skin-samples clause (told in Biography); "contradicts him on the buffalo and remarks that the bear they shot was not aggressive".

8. **MISQUOTE** (minor, labeled). Quotes: "I think they are interested to find out what conclusions a white man has reached on hundreds of years of our culture and society ...". FPSP transcript: "what conclusions a white man was reached on hundreds of years of culture and society". The fiche takes the wiki quote list's wording and says so. Fix: keep the label, or quote the transcript wording with "[has]" (transcript "was" is likely a typo, but "our" is absent from it).

9. **SPECULATION**. Guide and informant: "Because friends of his ride with Dutch, he can also arrange a meeting with some of the gang's men." The game never links the two. Fix: two separate facts: "Friends of his ride with Dutch. He arranges a meeting with some of the gang's men (see Biography)."

10. **SPECULATION / STYLE**. Guide and informant: "He knows West Elizabeth well" and "He reads animals". Inferences. Fix: keep only the facts (he says he knows an unguarded way to Cochinay; he says the grizzly showed no sign of aggression).

11. **SPECULATION** (minor). Reception: "He singled out Nastas as a possible exception". Cork contrasts Nastas with "the loudmouth deputy", who "isn't necessarily a different take on what we've seen before" (Game Informer, Feb 26, 2010). Fix: "He contrasted Nastas with the more familiar loudmouth deputy: ..."
