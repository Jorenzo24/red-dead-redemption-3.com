## Problems

1. IMPRECISE. Charles Smith: "he has promised Rains Fall to stay out of the tribe's conflict with the Army." Source: A Rage Unleashed transcript (gsw l. ~12229): "You know I told your father I will not fight over some horses." The promise is about the horses, not the whole conflict (the bio states it correctly). Fix: "he has told Rains Fall that he will not fight over the tribe's horses."

2. IMPRECISE. Dutch van der Linde: "He tells Arthur that Dutch "talks a lot" and that his son follows him." Source: Archeology transcript: "he talks a lot... I don't know him, but my son is easily led." "follows him" is a reading. Fix: "...that Dutch "talks a lot" and that his son is easily led" (or drop, see item 13).

3. IMPRECISE. Dutch van der Linde: "At Beaver Hollow, Dutch praises Eagle Flies's courage while the chief begs his son to stay." Source: My Last Boy transcript: Dutch's "I love your courage, son" comes before Rains Fall enters the camp. Fix: "At Beaver Hollow, Dutch praises Eagle Flies's courage just before the chief arrives to beg his son to stay."

4. IMPRECISE. Paytah: "one of the three riders on the hill in "Eastward Bound""; Hosea Matthews: "three riders watch the gang's wagons from a hill". Source: Eastward_Bound.txt l. 35: "watching them from a nearby cliff"; the Missions table and the Biography both say "cliff". Fix: "cliff" in both entries.

5. IMPRECISE. Trivia: "He uses it at the peace talks to tell his men that Arthur and Charles are friends." Source: Fine Art transcript (l. ~12549): the Lakota line ("These men are our friends... come on... let's go") is said to his braves on arrival, before they ride into Favours's camp; Development correctly says "on the way to the peace talks". Fix: the whole item is cut anyway (item 18).
