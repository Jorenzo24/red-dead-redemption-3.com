## Problems

1. **WRONG** (Relationships, Dutch): "Fordham speaks of him only in insults, and John answers that Dutch was once a better man than Fordham (see Quotes)." Transcripts: Fordham also mentions Dutch neutrally ("Dutch van der Linde is in the area", El Presidio; "Van Der Linde is the priority", BOAB; "Has he changed?", Great Men). John's line ("Dutch was a good man once, a far better man than you", And The Truth Will Set You Free) is NOT in the Quotes block, nor in the Biography. Fix: "He mostly speaks of him in insults ('Van der Linde is a psychopath. A murderer and a rapist.'); on the ride to Cochinay John answers that Dutch was a good man once, a far better man than Fordham." Drop "(see Quotes)" or add the line to Quotes.
2. **IMPRECISE** (Relationships, Ross): "beside him in every one of his scenes from 1907 to 1911". Fordham goes down to the wreck without Ross (BOAB transcript), and Ross has 1911 scenes where Fordham's presence is disputed (The Last Enemy, EN vs FR wiki). Fix: "beside him in almost all his scenes, from 1907 to 1911".
3. **IMPRECISE** (Relationships, Bill): "he sends John after him from the border and later blames him for the detour through Mexico" — "him" reads as Bill. Transcript (And The Truth Will Set You Free): Fordham reproaches JOHN for "running all over Mexico" instead of killing Bill at Fort Mercer. Fix: "...and later reproaches John for chasing him all over Mexico instead of killing him at Fort Mercer."
4. **IMPRECISE** (Appearances table, BOAB): "searches it with John, finds Nastas". BOAB page and transcript: John finds the tied-up man ("Over here! I've found him."); Fordham identifies him as the informant. Fix: "searches it with John, identifies Nastas once John finds him".
5. **IMPRECISE** (Trivia, Americans at Rest): "one of the two women Javier Escuella drinks with". Americans at Rest page: Javier is "speaking to a pair of women" and Arthur scares them away; the drink is then with Arthur and Charles. Fix: "one of the two women Javier Escuella is talking to at the bar in that mission".
6. **UNSUPPORTED** (Cut content): "Ross mocks a newspaper story about women wanting the vote". Cut-content page (fbi03): "Can you believe this? Who would believe this poppycock! ... Women wanting to vote". No newspaper is named. Fix: "Ross scoffs at the idea of women wanting the vote".
7. **DUPLICATE**: Quotes block repeats two lines already quoted in "In his own words" (half1): "Our armed forces have no equal..." and "And this isn't much more than a simple prototype...". Part B owns them (fordham_B OWNERSHIP). Fix: delete both from Quotes.
8. **DUPLICATE**: Quotes, "What others say": John's "Nice of you to help us out... an office clerk or social secretary or some such." is already quoted in Names (half1), which part B owns. Fix: delete from Quotes (or cut the quotation from Names; keep one).
9. **DUPLICATE**: the ignition fact appears twice: "Behind the wheel" ("he can start it from the driver's seat with the ignition, rare for a vehicle of the time") and Trivia ("Fordham starts the automobile from the driver's seat with the ignition..."). Keep it in Behind the wheel (part B owns the automobile), delete the Trivia item.
10. **DUPLICATE**: failure conditions are given twice in half2: "In the game" (all three missions) and again in the Appearances table rows (BOAB, Great Men, And You Will Know), plus "Several missions fail if the player assaults or kills him" in the Missions intro. Keep the "In the game" paragraph; drop the "Fails if..." clauses from the table and the intro sentence.
11. **DUPLICATE**: Missions intro "Fordham appears in six story missions... The English-language Red Dead Wiki also places him in the last story mission" repeats the Biography (half1, Red Dead Redemption 1911: "Fordham appears in six story missions of Red Dead Redemption: one in each of the first two acts and four in the third. The English-language Red Dead Wiki adds a seventh"). Keep in Missions; cut from Biography.
12. **COPIED** (Trivia): "Fordham starts the automobile from the driver's seat with the ignition, which the page notes was rare for cars of the time." vs BOAB page "Fordham is able to start the automobile from the driver's seat via the ignition, which was rare for vehicles at the time." Resolved by deleting it (item 9); also reword the Behind the wheel clause, e.g. "a feature few cars of the period had, according to the same page".
13. **COPIED** (Trivia, McKinnon): "stands near Ross after John's death, wears a similar dark three-piece suit and bowler hat, and has similar features" follows the Isaac McKinnon page item for item ("standing near Edgar Ross after John Marston's death, wearing similar clothes (black three-piece suit with a bowler hat), and having facial features that closely resemble Fordham"). Fix: "The two men look alike and dress alike, and McKinnon is the agent-like figure seen beside Ross in the last cutscene."
14. **IMPRECISE** (Gallery 3 caption): "Ross and Fordham watching a ranch." The wiki file is BeechersHopeBureauInvestigation.jpg ("Fordham and Ross observing John and Jack Marston at Beecher's Hope"); 1907 end credits. Fix: "Ross and Fordham watch Beecher's Hope in the end credits of Red Dead Redemption 2 (1907)." FR accordingly.
15. **IMPRECISE** (Gallery 5 caption): "Ross, John and Fordham in Blackwater." File ArcherFordham.jpg, wiki caption "Fordham and Ross escorting John Marston through Blackwater"; Exodus in America page has the same escort shot (Exodus.jpg); John wears his opening Rancher outfit. Also Fordham is on the LEFT, Ross on the right. Fix: "Fordham and Ross escort John through Blackwater in 'Exodus in America' (1911)." Alt: "Fordham, John Marston and Ross walking in a town street".
16. **IMPRECISE** (Gallery 6 caption): "John with Ross and Fordham at the Bureau." File "Rdr another's burdens.jpg" = Bear One Another's Burdens; the office is Ross's, on the upper floor of the Blackwater police station (not a named "Bureau" building). Fix: "Fordham and Ross with John in their Blackwater office, in 'Bear One Another's Burdens' (1911)." (Mission attribution rests on the wiki file name.)
17. **IMPRECISE** (Gallery 7 caption): "The agents questioning people in a saloon." Fix: "Fordham and Ross question a woman and Morris Peyton in Smithfield's Saloon, Valentine (1907)."
18. **IMPRECISE** (Gallery 2 alt): "Two men walking towards a body in the snow". Image: Ross crouches over the body, Fordham stands behind by the cabin. Fix alt: "Ross crouching over a body in the snow, Fordham standing behind him" (FR: "Ross accroupi près d'un corps dans la neige, Fordham debout derrière lui"). Caption itself is fine.
