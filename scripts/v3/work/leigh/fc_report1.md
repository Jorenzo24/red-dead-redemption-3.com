Fix: "His full name is spoken by Jake, the guide who escorts John at the start of the game ("it was the Marshal who hired me. Leigh Johnson"), and by Bonnie in "Women and Cattle" ("Yes, from Leigh Johnson.")." FR to match.

2. WRONG. Origins: "By 1911, he commands two deputies, Jonah and Eli, who ride with him on every mission." Source: Political_Realities_in_Armadillo_dialogues.txt: Johnson sends Jonah out ("Jonah, get out of here for a minute") and only Johnson and Marston follow Walton; no deputy in the mission. Fix: "By 1911, he commands two deputies, Jonah and Eli, who ride with him in every mission from "Justice in Pike's Basin" on." (The same error is in the Jonah relationship entry, leigh_full_en.txt l.66 "who rides with him in all his missions": half2.)

3. IMPRECISE. Ridgewood newspaper: "Armadillo lawmen stormed the farmhouse, killed the criminals and freed the survivors." Source: fc/Massacre_at_Ridgewood_Farm.txt: "stormed the farmhouse, defeated the criminals and freed the survivors." Fix: "defeated the criminals" (and see item 12 on wording).

4. IMPRECISE. Political Realities: "Depending on the player, John kills Walton or wounds him in the leg. Johnson compliments John's shooting, invites him to call on him next time he is in town, and rides off with his prisoner." Johnson compliments John's shooting and invites him to call on him next time he is in town." Then keep the wiki-attributed sentence on both outcomes (it already says he takes a captured Walton to the Armadillo jail).

5. IMPRECISE. 1914 newspaper: "which becomes available after the final mission, "The Last Enemy That Shall Be Destroyed", with Jack Marston as the playable character."
