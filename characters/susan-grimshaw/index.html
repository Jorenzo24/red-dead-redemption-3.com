Susan Grimshaw

Died 1899 Van der Linde gang Voiced by Kaili Vernoff

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 19, 2026

Susan Grimshaw is a long-standing member of the Van der Linde gang and the woman who runs its camp, a supporting character of Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. A former partner of Dutch van der Linde, she sets up each new camp with Simon Pearson and keeps the others at their chores. In the fourth chapter she rescues Tilly Jackson from the Foreman brothers with Arthur Morgan. At the end of the fifth chapter she shoots Molly O'Shea, newly arrived at Beaver Hollow, after Molly claims to have informed on the gang. In the sixth she sides with Arthur and John Marston against Micah Bell, who shoots her dead.

She is played by Kaili Vernoff, an actress who worked on the game for about four and a half years.

Key facts

  • Dutch's companion in the early years, according to the official guide; the two stayed partners after their relationship ended.
  • Sets up each new camp, with Simon Pearson, and makes the younger women, Karen, Mary-Beth and Tilly, do their share.
  • Rides with Arthur to Radley's House in "No, No and Thrice, No" to free Tilly from the Foreman brothers.
  • Shoots Molly O'Shea at Beaver Hollow, at the end of chapter 5, after Molly's false claim that she told the Pinkertons about the Saint Denis bank job.
  • Holds Micah at gunpoint when Arthur names him as the informant, and is shot by Micah when Javier's warning that Pinkertons are coming makes her turn.

Biography

Origins

The games give no birth date and no birthplace for Susan Grimshaw, and her life before the gang is not told in the story. The Red Dead Wiki counts her among the first members of the Van der Linde gang, with Dutch van der Linde, Hosea Matthews and Arthur Morgan; the official guide's account of her early years is quoted under Personality. For a time she was Dutch's partner (their relationship is covered under Relationships). By 1899 she runs the daily life of the camp.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)

Chapter 1: Colter

After the failed ferry robbery in Blackwater, the gang flees into the snow of the mountains and takes shelter in Colter, an abandoned mining town. In "Outlaws from the West", as soon as Davey Callander's body is carried into the cabin, Susan hands out jobs: Mary-Beth Gaskill is to light the fire, Karen Jones to bring every blanket the gang has, and the cook Simon Pearson to see what food is left. Dutch then tells the group that they may stay a few days and asks Pearson and Miss Grimshaw to turn the place into a camp. At the end of the mission, Susan gives out the sleeping quarters. She sends Dutch to a cabin where Molly O'Shea will show him the way and gives Arthur a room of his own. She puts Micah Bell in the building shared by the other men. Micah protests that Arthur gets a room while he gets a bunk next to Bill Williamson, and Hosea sends him to bed.

The Red Dead Wiki lists Susan in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", but the transcript of that mission gives her no line. In "Eastward Bound", the gang loads its wagons in Colter, and Susan scolds Mary-Beth for reading instead of helping. When the gang reaches its new camp at Horseshoe Overlook, above Valentine, she walks Arthur to the tent the girls have set up for him. She tells him that most of his belongings from Blackwater were saved. Arthur points out that his money was not. When he adds that they can always make more, she answers that they are going to have to. On her way back she berates Tilly Jackson in coarse terms for doing a job badly.

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

In "Exit, Pursued by a Bruised Ego", Arthur tells Hosea that Susan sent Lenny Summers and Micah out scouting and that he expected them back by now. In "Polite Society, Valentine Style", Mary-Beth tells Arthur that the girls have been stuck in camp for two weeks and that Karen is close to murdering Grimshaw. Arthur asks whether Miss Grimshaw can spare them, Karen mocks him for worrying about chores, and he takes the women and Uncle to Valentine. In "We Loved Once and True I", Susan tells Arthur that a letter has come for him from Mary Linton, in Valentine. According to the mission page, one of the girls collected it after Mary recognized her as a member of the gang.

At the end of "Who is Not Without Sin", Arthur brings Reverend Swanson back from Flatneck Station, drunk, and drops him onto his bed. Susan comes over and asks what happened. Arthur answers that it was the usual. She pities Swanson, thanks Arthur and says she will keep an eye on him. The Red Dead Wiki also lists her in "A Strange Kindness", the mission that leads to the gang's move south to Clemens Point, but the mission page does not name her. The camp scenes of this chapter, among them Sean MacGuire's homecoming party, are covered under Relationships.

Chapter 3: Clemens Point

At Clemens Point, on the shore of Flat Iron Lake, the camp scenes with Dutch and Molly are covered under Relationships. At the end of "Blessed are the Peacemakers", Arthur escapes from the O'Driscolls who held him captive and rides back to camp badly wounded. Dutch calls for Miss Grimshaw and Swanson. Susan says to get him to bed, and Dutch asks her to sit with him for a while. She agrees and tells Arthur that he will be fine now that he is home. The Red Dead Wiki lists her in "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern", the attack on Braithwaite Manor, but the mission page does not name her.

"The Battle of Shady Belle" opens at Clemens Point, where the Pinkerton agents Andrew Milton and Edgar Ross walk into camp. Milton offers the others three days to disappear if Dutch surrenders to him. Dutch raises his hands in mock acceptance while the gang loads its guns, and Susan tells Dutch that it is time for his new friend to leave. Milton warns them that he will return with fifty men, and the gang moves to Shady Belle, an abandoned plantation house near Saint Denis. When the caravan arrives, after Arthur and John Marston have cleared the house of Lemoyne Raiders and dumped the bodies in the swamp, Dutch asks Miss Grimshaw and Pearson to work their magic on the new camp.

Chapter 4: Saint Denis

In "No, No and Thrice, No", Arthur is smoking and talking with Mary-Beth when Susan interrupts them. Tilly has been taken by the Foreman Brothers, the gang she once rode with, and Susan knows where: Radley's House, in Scarlett Meadows. She takes a revolver and rides there with Arthur in a wagon. What she says on the way depends on the order of play: before Jack Marston is brought back, she remarks that first Jack and now Tilly have been kidnapped; after it, she notes that Kieran Duffy has not been seen for some time. At the house, Susan engages the only guard outside in conversation, then stabs him. Arthur goes in, surprises two more Foremans and finds Tilly bound to a chair upstairs, and sets her free.

Tilly tells them that Anthony Foreman and his men took her and that some of them have gone out hunting. Anthony and two riders are seen fleeing from the back of the house, and Tilly asks for Anthony to be brought back alive. Arthur and Susan give chase on horseback and kill the two riders; according to the mission page's tips, if Arthur is too slow, Susan shoots one of them herself. Arthur ropes Anthony and brings him back hogtied; Susan leads his horse back. Tilly and Susan confront him and order him to leave Tilly alone, which he promises once Arthur threatens him. According to the Red Dead Wiki's character page, Susan urges Arthur to slit his throat. The mission page says she tells Arthur the decision is his before taking Tilly back to camp. The player chooses whether Arthur kills or spares him.

In "Horsemen, Apocalypses", the O'Driscolls attack Shady Belle in force. According to the character page, she fires on the attackers with a double-barreled shotgun until Dutch sends her into the house. The mission page says the camp's non-fighting members rush into the house, and an image on that page shows her taking cover there with Mary-Beth, Leopold Strauss, Abigail Roberts and Jack. Afterwards, the same page says, Dutch puts her and Pearson in charge of clearing the O'Driscoll corpses from the grounds.

Chapter 5: Guarma

Susan stays behind while Dutch, Arthur and the others are stranded on Guarma after the Saint Denis bank robbery. In "Fleeting Joy", Arthur rejoins the gang at Lakay, in the bayou, and the Pinkertons attack the camp; the mission page does not name her. Once the fight is over, she tells the gang to pack up before the Pinkertons come back, according to the wiki's list of camp events. "That's Murfree Country", the last mission of the chapter, takes the gang to Beaver Hollow, a cave in Roanoke Ridge that Arthur and Charles Smith clear of the Murfree Brood.

While the new camp is being set up, Uncle arrives with Molly O'Shea, who is drunk. In front of the gang, Molly claims that she told the Pinkertons about the Saint Denis bank job, and she taunts Dutch. Dutch goes to kill her, and Arthur asks him to let her go. Susan fires a shotgun into Molly's stomach, killing her. She tells Arthur that Molly knew the rules, and tells Bill and Pearson to burn the corpse. The killing ends the chapter. The camp's reactions, among them Karen calling Susan a murderer, are covered under Relationships.

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

Susan's exchanges with Dutch, Tilly and Jack in the Beaver Hollow camp are covered under Relationships. In "Red Dead Redemption", the last mission of the chapter, Arthur learns from Milton, before Abigail shoots the agent, that the Pinkertons questioned Molly a couple of times and that she never said a word, whereas they picked up Micah when the gang came back from Guarma and he has worked for them ever since. When Arthur rides back into camp, Micah is hurrying Susan and the others to pack, and she answers that they are doing their best. Arthur says that Micah is the rat. Micah calls it a lie, and the two men draw on each other. John, back from prison, accuses Dutch of leaving him to die, and Arthur tells everyone to pick a side.

When Micah tells Arthur to be quiet, Susan levels her shotgun at Micah and orders him to be quiet himself and put his gun down. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she is the only one besides John to side openly with Arthur. Javier Escuella calls out that Pinkertons are coming fast. Susan turns at the warning, and Micah shoots her dead. In the transcript, Dutch says nothing about her: he demands to know who is with him and who is betraying him, and the Pinkertons open fire. If Arthur goes back for the money with low honor, he begs Dutch, as Micah stabs him, to kill Micah: "Not Molly... not Miss Grimshaw... not even Colm." This line belongs to that ending only. After the gang falls apart, Charles Smith returns to Beaver Hollow, collects Susan's body and Arthur's, and buries them.

Red Dead Redemption 2, epilogue (1907)

Epilogue 1: Pronghorn Ranch

In 1907, once "Simple Pleasures" is done, John can visit Susan's grave. It stands on a hilltop above the railroad line, southwest of Elysian Pool, in Roanoke Ridge (New Hanover). According to the character page, her necklace hangs on the wooden cross. Arthur dies soon after Susan, so John is the only character who can visit it. It is one of the graves needed for the Paying Respects trophy.

Personality

Rockstar's character description, published on September 6, 2018, reads: "The undisputed boss and arbiter of justice in the camp, everything would have fallen apart years ago without Susan in charge. Tenacious and iron-willed, she stands for no nonsense." A character page from an official guide, reproduced on the Red Dead Wiki, expands on it: "She used to be in a relationship with Dutch back in the old days and, even after that ended, they've remained strong partners in crime. Years as an outlaw have left her a little jaded but she wouldn't have it any other way." The same page says she has been with the gang as long as Arthur has.

In camp she gives the orders and expects work in return. She hands out rooms and tents, sends the women back to their chores and scolds anyone she finds idle, with the younger women, Karen, Mary-Beth and Tilly, as her usual targets (see Relationships). By the fire she complains that the camp's girls are lazy, rude and full of themselves, regrets that Dutch will not let her take a horsewhip to them, and says she was disciplined that way herself and that it did her good. She also tells Arthur that she is too old for long journeys like the crossing of the Grizzlies. According to the French Red Dead Wiki, she admits she knows the camp women make fun of her, and says she did the same when she was pretty, and that they are right to. According to the English wiki, she says in camp dialogue that she is sad Davey Callander was killed.

In her own words

The Red Dead Wiki opens its page on her with a line it illustrates with an audio clip from the game: "I swear half of you would just rot in your own filth if nobody kept you in check." The wiki's quote list also gives a short line she says to Arthur: "My camp, my rules." According to the Red Dead Wiki's character page, when Tilly is kidnapped and Arthur remarks that Susan cares about the girls after all, she replies with a line from the wiki's quote list: "Of course I care. About all of you fools. Some just require a firmer hand than others, you especially."

Names

Most of the gang address her by her surname. Arthur, Dutch and Micah call her Miss Grimshaw in the mission transcripts. The French Red Dead Wiki gives Miss Grimshaw as her nickname. The English wiki notes that, like Pearson, she is rarely called by her first name. In "Polite Society, Valentine Style", Mary-Beth refers to her simply as Grimshaw. She uses the same formality with others: Mr. Morgan, Mr. Bell, Mr. Pearson, Miss Gaskill, Miss Jones, Miss Jackson and Miss O'Shea.

Appearance

According to the Red Dead Wiki, Susan is a middle-aged woman with grey hair, formerly blond, and blue eyes, who wears little makeup and keeps her hair in a tight chignon. The wiki gives two usual outfits: a black dress with a long-sleeved black top, or a pink shirt over the dress, with black boots. With the shirt she wears an oval pendant. The wiki adds that, like the rest of the gang, she changes outfits and hairstyles over the course of the game.

Relationships

Dutch van der Linde

Susan and Dutch were once a couple and stayed partners after it ended, according to the official guide (quoted under Personality). He leaves the camp's organization to her and turns to her when Arthur is brought back injured from Colm's camp (see Biography). At a Horseshoe Overlook campfire she says she is sure Dutch will pull them through and that she has never doubted it; Bill agrees, and she adds that Dutch is an ideal American. At Clemens Point she tells Dutch that Molly is unhappy and that her temper could cause trouble, and he brushes the worry aside. On another evening there he puts on music in front of his tent and asks her to dance, which she accepts. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Arthur says that Dutch has a soft spot for her.

At Beaver Hollow she asks Dutch what they are doing and tells him that, for now, they need to keep moving. Dutch flies into a rage, calls her a fool and shouts that they need money or they are all dead. She has sided with Arthur and John when Micah shoots her (see Biography).

Arthur Morgan

Susan calls Arthur Mr. Morgan. According to the Red Dead Wiki, when he stays away from camp for a long time she can take him to task; he answers that he is a wanderer, and she replies that she knows and calls him son. Besides his room at Colter and his tent at Horseshoe Overlook (see Biography), she finds him a place at Shady Belle: she has moved his things into an upstairs bedroom, thinking he would like it. Arthur's thanks come with a dry remark about the swamp's alligators and mosquitoes. After Sean's rescue, according to the wiki, she tells Arthur he did well to bring him back and that loyalty matters. At Shady Belle, after Jack's kidnapping, Arthur asks her to keep an eye on Abigail, and she agrees.

She sits with Arthur after Colm's captivity, rides with him to free Tilly in "No, No and Thrice, No" and stands with him against Micah at Beaver Hollow (see Biography).

Tilly Jackson

Susan is hard on Tilly in the early chapters. She berates Tilly's work on arrival at Horseshoe Overlook (see Biography). In another scene she grabs Tilly by the ear; when Tilly says she does not feel well, Susan answers "I don't care what you feel, girl. Get ready!" and sends her off. With Arthur listening, she likens the girls to fairy-tale princesses; Arthur's reply is that they must have found their fairy godmother. Over dominoes at Clemens Point, Tilly jokes with Arthur about killing Miss Grimshaw, and Arthur answers that Dutch prefers to avoid murder in camp.

At Shady Belle, Tilly warns Susan that the new camp is close to a Foreman brothers' safe house. When Tilly is taken, Susan organizes the rescue with Arthur (see Biography); according to the Red Dead Wiki, she calls Tilly her girl during the mission, and their relationship improves afterwards. In the Beaver Hollow chapter, according to the wiki, she reassures Tilly about the gang's situation and tells her she has grown into a fine young woman.

Karen Jones

Kaili Vernoff told GameFragger in February 2019 that Karen is the one who really mouths off to Susan. At Horseshoe Overlook, Karen joins Susan at the camp table, asks how her meal is and tells her that in Colter she had considered eating her. She lists possible dishes, saying she and Pearson could not agree on the best one, and Susan ends up leaving the table. At Clemens Point, when Susan finds her drunk at the poker table, Karen replies that she is having a baby, tells her she will not be bullied and that Susan should keep quiet unless she is the one feeding her. While sewing with Tilly, Karen complains about Susan's nagging, then tells Susan she is learning alchemy. The two still sing together (see Skills). After Molly's death at Beaver Hollow, Karen calls Susan a murderer; Susan answers that Molly broke the rules.

Mary-Beth Gaskill

At Horseshoe Overlook Susan tells Mary-Beth that she has killed people for betraying the gang; Mary-Beth asks whether reading counts as betrayal, and Susan answers that the problem is idleness, not reading. In another scene Mary-Beth says she has been working, and Susan slaps her and orders her to do more. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Susan scolds Mary-Beth and Lenny when they mock Pearson's cooking, telling them to mind their manners because Pearson takes good care of them. At Shady Belle, after Jack's return, she tells Mary-Beth she does not know where Kieran is.

Molly O'Shea

At Clemens Point Susan voices her concern to Dutch about Molly's unhappiness (see Dutch van der Linde). At Lakay, according to the Red Dead Wiki, she can comment on Molly's disappearance since the bank robbery. At Beaver Hollow, Susan kills Molly over a claim that later proves false (see Biography).

Micah Bell

Over dominoes at Horseshoe Overlook, Micah tells Susan she was something, a long time ago. She takes it as a compliment until he brings up how flowers wither, and she leaves in anger. In another scene there, as she cleans a Carbine Repeater, he says the sight gets him hot and bothered; she points the gun at him and warns him not to try his charm on her. At Clemens Point he tells her he imagines she was beautiful once, and laughs as she walks away. When Micah bullies Swanson, Susan orders him to leave the reverend alone. At Beaver Hollow he shoots her during the standoff (see Biography).

Simon Pearson

Pearson is Susan's partner in setting up each camp (see Skills). At Shady Belle she checks on him at the gazebo; he lists what he achieved at sea and complains that he now lives in a swamp waiting to be shot. Susan tells him self-pity is not like him; he blames the mud and goes back to work. She defends his cooking against Mary-Beth and Lenny (see Mary-Beth Gaskill), although, according to the Red Dead Wiki, she asks Arthur to find oregano to season it.

Orville Swanson

Susan takes charge of Swanson when Arthur brings him back drunk in "Who is Not Without Sin" (see Biography); "Poor bastard", she says. She protects him from Micah's bullying (see Micah Bell).

Uncle

At Horseshoe Overlook Uncle asks Susan to spend the evening in town with him; she agrees, then changes her mind when she realizes all he wants is money. According to the Red Dead Wiki, in another camp conversation Uncle teases her that she is a fine woman hiding behind a sour face and a sharp tongue, which he compares to a rose's thorns. They often sing together (see Skills).

Abigail Marston

At Horseshoe Overlook Susan tells Abigail that she thinks too highly of herself and has to earn her keep; Abigail answers that she will gladly sing for her supper, but will not go back to selling herself. By the fire, Abigail complains that she is too old to live under canvas in the wild, particularly with Susan eating three people's rations. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she calls Susan a hag. After Jack's kidnapping, Arthur asks Susan to watch over Abigail (see Arthur Morgan).

John Marston

At Colter, while John recovers from the wolf attack in the main cabin, Arthur comes in to tease him; Susan asks what he wants now and tells him to leave John alone. At Beaver Hollow both side with Arthur against Micah (see Biography).

Sean MacGuire

At the party for Sean's return, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Sean says he likes Susan even though she is ruthless about work (their song that night is listed under Skills). After his death in Rhodes, she and Karen dedicate "A Prisoner for Life" to him at Shady Belle.

Hosea Matthews

At Horseshoe Overlook Susan tells Dutch and Hosea that their tents will be ready shortly. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she later passes on to Arthur that Hosea has found a lead at Emerald Ranch.

Lenny Summers

At Colter Susan puts Lenny in the shared quarters with Micah, Javier, Charles and Bill. She later sends him and Micah out scouting (see Biography).

Charles Smith

At Clemens Point Susan sings "Dance by the Light of the Moon" at the campfire while Charles plays the harmonica. Charles is with the Wapiti when she is killed; after the gang breaks up, he returns to Roanoke Ridge and buries her there (see Biography).

Javier Escuella

Javier plays the guitar for several of Susan's songs in camp (see Skills). At Beaver Hollow it is his warning that the Pinkertons are coming that makes her turn her head (see Biography).

Jack Marston

At Beaver Hollow, Jack goes around the camp calling for the missing dog Cain and asks Susan whether she has seen him. She has not, but tells him the dog is a wanderer and will turn up.

Leopold Strauss

Susan and Pearson sleep near Strauss at Beaver Hollow; if Arthur confronts him at night, the two wake up and watch, frightened by Arthur's anger. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Susan, like Swanson, approves of Arthur's decision to throw Strauss out, and tells him afterwards that she knows he did the right thing and will not protest.

Josiah Trelawny

At Clemens Point, Susan interrupts Trelawny and Karen's conversation about a book to ask whether Karen plans to do any work. Trelawny answers with a magic trick, pulling a crow out of his hand. Susan leaves in disgust, and Karen laughs.

Mary Linton

Susan tells Arthur that a letter from Mary has arrived (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, she brings up her dislike of Mary more than once, which annoys Arthur.

Andrew Milton

When Milton and Ross walk into Clemens Point, the gang takes up its weapons and Susan tells Dutch it is time for his new friend to go (see Biography; the line is under Quotes).

Sadie Adler

According to the Red Dead Wiki, Sadie is the woman in camp Susan has the least to do with. Both are among the members who wait at Lakay while Dutch's group is on Guarma.

Skills, weapons and horses

Skills and weapons

Running the camp

Susan sets up each new camp, with Pearson. Wikipedia's list of the game's characters gives this as their joint job, and Dutch hands it to the two of them in the story (see Biography). She allocates the sleeping places, puts the others to work and reminds Arthur to keep paying into the camp's money box. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she remarks on what Arthur has been up to when he is in camp, whether he has killed innocent people, held back his donations or lingered too long among the tents. At Beaver Hollow she can also tell him that the donation box is gone because someone broke it.

If Arthur goes too long without a bath, she takes matters into her own hands and scrubs him in a water barrel in camp. She is unavailable in camp while "No, No and Thrice, No" is waiting to be played, as are Tilly and Mary-Beth.

Songs

Susan sings at the poker table and the campfire, mostly with Karen and often with Uncle. The Red Dead Wiki's camp events page lists "Come, Bustle, Bustle" and "One Eyed Riley" (with Uncle and Karen), "I Wouldn't Marry" (with Karen) and "Wild Free Rover" (at Sean's return party, with Uncle, Sean and Karen) at Horseshoe Overlook; "Dance by the Light of the Moon" at Clemens Point; and "A Prisoner for Life", "The Sailor Boy" and "The Heartland Stage Company" (with Karen and Uncle) at Shady Belle.

Weapons

The Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver, a Pump-Action Shotgun, a Carbine Repeater and a Double-Barreled Shotgun, and observes that in cutscenes she is most often seen with a shotgun. According to the character page, she uses a double-barreled shotgun briefly when the O'Driscolls attack Shady Belle. A shotgun is also what she uses on Molly and against Micah at Beaver Hollow; she takes a revolver and uses a knife in the rescue of Tilly (see Biography). The carbine appears in a camp scene with Micah (see Relationships).

She gives one of them, "No, No and Thrice, No", jointly with Mary-Beth Gaskill according to its mission page. Four missions on the list do not show her in their mission page or transcript; the table says so case by case. A second table adds three missions in which she is only mentioned or plays a brief part in camp.

Development and performance

Casting

Susan is played by Kaili Vernoff, who provides her voice and her performance capture. Vernoff is a stage and screen actress trained at Boston University; according to Wikipedia, she has appeared in six Woody Allen projects, among them Sweet and Lowdown (1999), The Curse of the Jade Scorpion (2001) and the series Crisis in Six Scenes (2016). Around the game's release she had recurring roles in The Path and Grey's Anatomy and appeared in the series finale of House of Cards, as she told Forbes in February 2019.

Red Dead Redemption 2 was both the first video game she worked on and the first she ever played, she told Heather Newman of Forbes in an interview published on 12 February 2019. For her first booking she was sent a few scenes to learn and told to wear "comfortable undergarments", and she did not realize she would be wearing a motion capture suit until she arrived at the studio. She told GameFragger (Josh Wilding, 21 February 2019) that she worked a couple of days, then did not return for months and assumed her part was over, and only later understood that she was playing Susan. According to Forbes, it took her a few months to learn that the project was a new Red Dead game. She worked on it "for about four and a half years, off and on", sometimes a week straight, then nothing for months.

Vernoff also worked for Rockstar on Grand Theft Auto V (2013), as the voice of Miranda Cowan, an aging party girl. She told Forbes and GameFragger that this was a voice-only booth job recorded in the middle of the Red Dead shoot, that she did not know which game it was until she arrived, and that she had to make sure Miranda sounded nothing like Susan.

Playing Susan

Vernoff described Susan's rules to Forbes: "Everyone must earn their keep; she cannot abide hangers on or laziness; and she values loyalty above all." She said the character "isn't necessarily likable off the bat", which made her fun to play, and that Susan can be almost coquettish around Dutch and flustered around Micah. To GameFragger she said that the gang is Susan's family. Susan believes, she added, that the gang could not survive without her. As she came to understand the character over the years, she wanted Susan's vulnerability to come through, in the moments when the men tease her or make advances. She also cited a camp scene in which Susan tells Mary-Beth, caught looking at herself in a mirror, that vanity will get her nowhere. Samantha Strelitz, who plays Mary-Beth, told GamesRadar+ in March 2019 that a scene she shot with Vernoff was about vanity, aging and two women struggling to connect.

Some early scenes were later adjusted. Vernoff told GameFragger that she was called back to the voiceover booth to rework a few moments shot before she really knew who Susan was, so that they matched the character she had become. She told Forbes that the motion capture director Rod Edge shot as many takes as a scene needed. The scene in which Susan makes Arthur wash took her and Roger Clark "quite a few takes", she told the same outlet, and fans often mention it to her.

The rescue of Tilly was the first time she worked with a gun in the game, she told GameFragger. In the GamesRadar+ feature by Rachel Weber, published on 7 March 2019, she said she knew few "middle-aged badass video game characters who will take a guard down with a knife in the throat", and that the mission balanced the scenes in which Susan is vicious with the girls in camp. She recalled one of her first scenes, in which Peter Blomquist, as Micah, hauls Susan onto his lap and teases her about her age, and said she burst out laughing on most takes.

On Susan's death, she told GameFragger that she was surprised at how betrayed she felt when the character was shot, with the standoff carrying on and nobody running to her. Rod Edge, standing next to her, kept asking for "one more moan, one more cry" so that her pain would color the scene. Asked whether Susan deserved a happier ending, she answered: "I think Susan dies defending her family and she wouldn't have it any other way." The moment that moved her most on set, she said, was Susan's farewell to Tilly, played by Meeya Davis-Glover, with whom she had grown close.

Cut content

According to the Red Dead Wiki, the files of Red Dead Redemption 2 contain unused earrings and an unused corset for Susan. Audio from a cut mission strand about a character named Eliza has Susan asking Arthur at Clemens Point whether he has seen her. An unused camp encounter, set after Karen leaves the gang, has Susan nervously telling the camp's girls that Karen stormed off the night before after telling her to go to hell. A piece of concept art shows her with Reverend Swanson and Hosea.

Reception

Critics

In a Polygon column of 22 November 2018 titled "Red Dead Redemption 2 is a confection of cowboy clichés", Colin Campbell took Susan as his first example. He described her as a frontierswoman who mothers her charges and turns into an "angry she-bear" to protect them, a figure that exists "to offer support and wisdom to the male hero", and compared her to Ma Ingalls in Little House on the Prairie, Mrs. Jorgensen in The Searchers and Mrs. Claggett in Destry Rides Again. He concluded: "She's literally a walking cliché."

In Game Informer's staff ranking of the 24 gang members, published on 27 December 2018, Susan comes 11th. The staff called her "the hawk of the camp", cited her amusing scenes and described her killing of Molly O'Shea as "cold-blooded" and "vengeful"; in the same list, the entry on Molly calls her "vengeful mama bear Susan Grimshaw". Vernoff told Forbes in February 2019 that the reaction she heard most often from fans was that they hated Susan at the start of the game and had come to love and admire her by the end.

Other media and legacy

Marketing

On September 6, 2018, Rockstar posted Susan Grimshaw's portrait and character description on Twitter, in a series covering the members of the gang, and The Hollywood Reporter reproduced the series the next day. Rockstar also released official artwork of her for Red Dead Redemption 2. In the composite image of the 23 gang members, she stands in the front row, between Tilly Jackson and Sadie Adler.

Trivia
  • In "No, No and Thrice, No", Arthur chases Anthony Foreman on a leopard-coat Appaloosa commandeered at Radley's House, often the only horse nearby once the mission ends.
  • The same mission fails if the player assaults, kills or abandons Susan.
  • After Micah shoots her in "Red Dead Redemption", her body can be looted for shotgun shells and a Miracle Tonic, at the cost of some honor. The Pinkerton attack makes her body hard to reach.
  • At a cast panel at SacAnime Summer 2019, on June 8, 2019, Jim Santangeli, who plays Pearson, was asked for his favorite character besides his own. He said: "I don't know how you can't love Miss Grimshaw." He added that as Pearson he felt she was his teammate in the camp.
