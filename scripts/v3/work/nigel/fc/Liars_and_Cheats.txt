The '''Liars and Cheats''' Pack is the third downloadable content pack for ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''. It was released on Tuesday, September 21, 2010.

==Contents==
The pack includes:<ref name="new-rdr-dlc">[http://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/6781/announcing_new_downloadable_content_plans_for_red_dead_redemption.article Rockstar News Wire - Announcing New Downloadable Content Plans for Red Dead Redemption ]</ref><ref name="dlc-update">[http://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/8041/red_dead_redemption_dlc_update_liars_and_cheats_pack_coming_9_21_with_expanded_free_roam_content_&_new_details_on_undead_nightmare_pack.article Rockstar News Wire - Red Dead Redemption DLC Update: Liars and Cheats Pack Coming 9/21 with Expanded Free Roam Content & New Details on Undead Nightmare Pack]</ref>
*[[Multiplayer]] [[Poker]] and [[Liar's Dice]] Games.
*Multiplayer horse races ([[Grand Prix]]).
*The [[Explosive Rifle]]: A new weapon with its own new single-player and multiplayer challenges
*7 New [[Gang Hideouts]].
*4 New [[Hunting Grounds]]: New Hunting Grounds are now visible on the map for all to see.
*[[Stronghold]] Competitive Multiplayer Mode: Teams take turns in attack and defense in multi-tiered Competitive games.
*[[Posse]] Scoring and Leaderboards.
*15 additional [[Multiplayer Characters|multiplayer characters]] from the Red Dead Redemption storyline.
*New [[Achievements/Trophies]]
*Pricing: $9.99 (PlayStation Network), 800 microsoft points (Xbox LIVE)

==Gang Hideouts==
Listed below are 7 new gang hideouts available in the pack:[http://www.rockstargames.com/reddeadredemption/news/article/8531/first_screenshots_from_the_red_dead_redemption_liars_and_cheats_pack]

*[[Crooked Toes]]

==Free Roam Pack==
The Free Roam Pack was intended be a free DLC pack including posse scoring and additional [[Free Roam]] challenges. However, due to a restriction on how many pieces of downloadable content a game may give away for free, the Free Roam Pack's contents have been added into the Liars and Cheats Pack.<ref name="dlc-update"/>
