# Coverage report: RDR2 Chapter 1 (Colter) story guide, EN draft

Checked 24 Oct 2026. Our text: `guide_full_en.txt` (Lnn). Coverage base: the 6 mission pages (Story, objectives, rewards,
Gold Medal, Trivia, Aftermath/Consequences), `Colter.txt`, `Missions_in_Redemption_2.txt` (Chapter 1), `Camp_Events.txt`
(Colter Chapter). Facts confirmed against the wiki `*_dialogues.txt` transcripts, `fc/gsw.txt` and the fiches.

Length: our EN text is about **5,690 words** (5,818 tokens minus the 132 `Lnn:` tags). The Story sections of the 6
mission pages add up to **2,592 words** (Outlaws 597, Enter 574, Old Friends 461, Aftermath 606, Cornwall 264,
Eastward 90). **Ratio: about 219 %.**

Overall: every Story section event is covered. What is missing is mostly (a) mission rewards, unlocks and trophies,
(b) the non-speculative Trivia items, (c) a set of transcript lines that carry facts (cover story, Kieran's horse, Hosea's
last scene), and (d) the remaining details of the Camp Events.

Status key: CONFIRMED (source) = backed by a transcript or a fiche; WIKI-ONLY = only on the wiki page, may be added
with attribution; SPECULATIVE = interpretation, comparison or "may be a reference": do NOT add.
