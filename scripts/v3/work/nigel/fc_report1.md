## Problems

1. WRONG. "Dickens appears in seven missions of Red Dead Redemption: six in the first act, from "Old Swindler Blues" to "We Shall Be Together in Paradise", and one in the third act". Counting the Act I missions from Old Swindler Blues to We Shall Be Together in Paradise gives SEVEN (Old Swindler Blues, You Shall Not Give False Testimony, Liars Cheats, Can a Swindler, Sport of Kings, Assault on Fort Mercer, We Shall Be Together in Paradise), plus Bear One Another's Burdens = EIGHT (en.txt "Mission appearances" lists 8). Fix: "Dickens appears in eight missions of Red Dead Redemption: seven in the first act, from ... to ..., and one in the third act." FR likewise (huit / sept).

2. IMPRECISE. ""The Assault on Fort Mercer" brings together Dickens, John, Leigh Johnson, his deputies Eli and Jonah, Irish and Seth." Irish misses the fight: he turns up at the end ("Hold on! Hold on...I missed it.", Assault transcript) and John tells him on the raft "you conveniently missed all the action at Fort Mercer" (Paradise transcript). Fix: "...his deputies Eli and Jonah, and Seth; Irish only turns up once the fighting is over."

3. SPECULATION. "He says he knows of one son, who died shortly after birth, and that he left the boy's mother because of her demands." The wiki's quoted line is only "His mother was making all sorts of unreasonable demands"; he never says he left her (that is the wiki's own reading). Fix: "...who died shortly after birth, and that the boy's mother was making all sorts of unreasonable demands." (paraphrase without quotes).

4. IMPRECISE. "he says he has probably fathered many children". Wiki quote: "I'm sure I have many", attributed to the virility given by his elixir. Fix: "he says he is sure he has fathered many children, which he credits to his elixir's effect on virility".

5. IMPRECISE. "Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) never shows him but gives one episode of his career before 1911." RDR2 gives two: the Moncrief letter and the 1907 article (told just after). Fix: "Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) never shows him, but two in-game documents mention him. The first is the Wheeler, Rawson and Co. catalogue..."

6. UNSUPPORTED. "Issue No. 53 of the Blackwater Ledger, which can be bought after "Political Realities in Armadillo"". Neither the Ledger No. 53 page, the Newspapers page (only "issue numbers 53 to 60" for John) nor the Political Realities page gives this unlock. Fix: "Issue No. 53 of the Blackwater Ledger, the first issue John can buy, runs..." (the Newspapers page supports "first": issues 53 to 60).

7. COPIED. 1907 article paragraph: "his demonstrations show that a simple medicinal tonic cures rheumatism, neuralgia, rickets, dysentery and scarlet fever. The response has been enthusiastic, and in more than one town the local doctor has been taken to task." Article/wiki: "He has begun demonstrations showing that sufferers of rheumatism, neuralgia, rickets, dysentery and scarlet fever can be cured of their maladies by use of a simple medicinal tonic. The response has been enthusiastic, and in more than one town the local doctor taken to task". Also "a learned man of letters, botany, ornithology and the history of home medicine" vs "a studied man of letters, botany, ornithology and the history of home medicine". Fix: either quote verbatim inside quotation marks (it is a primary text) or rephrase, e.g. "According to the paper, he gives public demonstrations in which a plain tonic is said to cure five illnesses, among them rheumatism and scarlet fever. It adds that audiences have welcomed them and that several towns have turned on their own doctor."

8. COPIED. Undead Nightmare: "Dickens tells John he is trying to reach Mexico and will be at Solomon's Folly in a few days." UN page: "He also tells John that he is trying to get to Mexico, and should be in Solomon's Folly in a few days." Fix: "He says he means to get to Mexico and that John can find him at Solomon's Folly a few days later."

9. IMPRECISE. Personality: "waits outside the gate of Fort Mercer once his part is done (see Biography)". Transcript: he says "I think my work here is done ... I bid you farewell!", leaves, then comes back to the gate asking to be let in. Fix: "and drives off once his part at Fort Mercer is done" (and see 12 for the duplicate).

10. IMPRECISE. "He presents himself as an Englishman". He never says he is English in any transcript; what he does is speak with an English accent (GOTY guide) and give a London address. Fix: "He speaks with an English accent, and when he introduces himself..." (or drop the sentence, see 13). Same caution for the facts grid "Nationality: American (speaks with an English accent)": "American" rests only on the wiki-quoted Fort Wayne line that is not in the transcript; hedge: "American according to the Red Dead Wiki (Fort Wayne, Indiana, by his own account); speaks with an English accent".

11. IMPRECISE. Burdens: "Ross tells the officer to let him go and leaves with John for the wreck of the Serendipity. Dickens walks out of the station." Order is reversed: stage direction "[West Dickens leaves. Ross and friends pile into an automobile and start off.]". Fix: "Ross tells the officer to let him go. Dickens walks out of the station, and Ross leaves with John for the wreck of the Serendipity."

12. DUPLICATE. "He admits to John that he is broke." (Personality) repeats Biography ("and admits he is broke", False Testimony). Keep Biography; cut from Personality.
