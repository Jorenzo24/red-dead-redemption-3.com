'''MacFarlane's Ranch''' is a [[settlement]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]'', ''[[Red Dead Redemption 2]]'', and ''[[Red Dead Online]]'' in the [[Hennigan's Stead]] region of the State of  [[New Austin]].

==Background==
=== ''Red Dead Redemption 2'' ===
In ''Red Dead Redemption 2'', players can visit MacFarlane's Ranch. There are, however, some differences since the game takes place before the story of ''Red Dead Redemption'': some buildings are not built yet or are in early stages, such as the foreman's office, train station, general store, and the barn. The ranch is almost empty, as the MacFarlane family temporarily left due to a death in the family. The barn not being built is not in line with what Bonnie says in the first game, stating that it was built by her Pa when she was a little girl.  

===''Red Dead Redemption''===
MacFarlane's Ranch is a cattle ranch, home to [[Bonnie MacFarlane]] and [[Drew MacFarlane]], and the largest ranch in the game. The ranch plays a key supporting role in the narrative of ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]'', especially the first chapter. In addition, the player can find work here such as [[Cattle Herding|cattle herding]] and [[nightwatch]] jobs. On the ranch, there is a small [[Sheriff's Office|foreman's office]], a [[General Store|general store]], a [[Doctor's Office|doctor's office]], a [[Trains|train station]], a large pen for [[cattle]], a corral for [[horses]], a large barn, and a small [[chicken]] coop (with the doors being open, as the chickens are often roaming around the ranch).

It is one of the first places that the player will visit in the game. Here, Marston will awaken after an initial confrontation with [[Bill Williamson|Bill Williamson.]] After recovering, Marston will follow a few tutorial missions introducing the player to the gameplay mechanics.

All of the story missions in the [[Bonnie MacFarlane]] [[Missions in Redemption#Bonnie MacFarlane|mission strand]] begin at this location. The [[Irish]] [[Missions in Redemption#Irish|mission]] [[On Shaky's Ground|"On Shaky's Ground"]] ends here and a portion of both [[Abigail Marston]] [[Missions in Redemption#Abigail Marston|missions]] [[The Outlaw's Return|"The Outlaw's Return"]] and [[Old Friends, New Problems|"Old Friends, New Problems"]] take place in this location.

===''Undead Nightmare''===
Despite the best efforts of the ranch's residents and workers, the ranch has not been spared the destruction of the undead uprising; the undead prowl the street, attacking any living thing they can get their hands on and the town's survivors have been forced to either flee or get to high ground, grab the nearest weapon they can get their hands on, and focus on trying to eliminate the undead hordes clawing at their feet. Bonnie MacFarlane has locked herself inside her family home, and Drew MacFarlane has decided to take on the difficult task of herding as many of the undead as possible into the barn in a vain attempt to allow the other residents to carry on with their day-to-day lives.

In the ''[[Undead Nightmare]]'' [[DLC]] pack, this is one of 23 locations that must be cleared of the [[Undead]] and then protected from re-infestation thereafter. Also during the ''Undead Nightmare'', the [[Missions in Redemption#Side-mission|side-mission]] [[Paternal Pride|"Paternal Pride"]] begins and ends at this location. Once the mission is completed, the player will receive the [[Winchester Repeater]]. The survivors defending the ranch are [[Solomon Flake]], [[Granville Berry]], [[Eunis Randle]], [[Nancy Drummond]], and an unnamed elderly woman.

In [[Redemption Multiplayer|Multiplayer]], this is one of 7 locations that has a [[Land Grab]] game available. The post to start the game is located on the on top of the foreman's office.

==Safehouse==
===''Red Dead Redemption''===
{{Main|MacFarlane's Ranch Safehouse}}
Located within MacFarlane's Ranch is a [[Safehouses|safehouse]] (shed), accessible to the player early in the gameplay narrative. It is the very first safehouse the player is given. It's located near the pen where the horses are kept. In there, you can find a chest with ammo in it. You can [[save]] your game here as well.

===''Undead Nightmare''===
In the ''[[Undead Nightmare]]'' [[DLC]] the player has a different safehouse instead of the shed, which is the cell in the Foreman's Office where the player can save, change [[outfits]], and fast-travel to another destination.

==Known Glitches==
* The flag at the train station acts quite strange; at times stretching to extreme lengths.
* If you play [[Poker]] and are caught cheating, after the [[Dueling|duel]] (If you win) the playing cards may appear to be stuck in Marston's right arm.
* If you wait in the [[Cattle]] pen, the bull may charge into the fence, glitch, and eventually bounce out of the area. It can then be seen running around New Austin.
* After unlocking the blue train, while riding the red train the blue train may come through the red train, knocking the player over.
* In ''Undead Nightmare'', just like Blackwater, MacFarlane Ranch can become a lost town without warning. It will give the player no notification of an attack prior.
* Sometimes a horse might spawn as a bull in singleplayer and allows a player to have a bull as a mount in singleplayer but it makes horse sounds and has the ability to jump
*If the player goes to the police station with a [[Sawed-off Shotgun]], they can get a certain camera angle when shooting someone inside of the cell and then glitch into it. In order to escape, the player will need to use dynamite to kill themselves.

==Trivia==
===General===
* On the road sign east of the ranch, MacFarlane's Ranch is spelled as "McFarlane Ranch".
* Contrary to popular opinion, the American Flag seen at the train station is correct as it has 46 stars; however, the flag is inaccurate in 1914 as the flag should have 48 stars.
* The layout of MacFarlane's Ranch appears to be inspired by the Tyler Ranch in the 1992 film ''[[Wikipedia:Of Mice and Men (1992 film)|Of Mice and Men]]''.

===''Red Dead Redemption''===
* North of the ranch is a targeting range with bottles and cans that can be locked on with Dead Eye.
* The general store owner, [[Walter Fisk]], makes a reference to another fictional general store owner, from the 2000 comedy film [[Wikipedia:O Brother, Where Art Thou?|"O Brother, Where Art Thou?"]]. Both characters say to the protagonist the identical line: "''You want something, we can order it. Be here in a couple of weeks.''"
* Sometimes NPCs will say ''"I heard the'' ''[[Blackwater Ledger|Blackwater Ledgers]] don't print about a big fire in the East"''<ref>https://youtu.be/Pqaku0Pqb4M</ref>, which could be a nod to the [[Wikipedia:Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire|Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire]] that occurred on March 25th, 1911, the same year ''Red Dead Redemption'' takes place.
* A part of the Southwestern Railroad Company is located here and at [[Benedict Point]]. The main part of the company is located at [[Armadillo]].
* MacFarlane's Ranch is the only location in ''Red Dead Redemption'' where both the trains stop at the station.
* During the night, the people of the ranch can be seen gathering around the campfire near the safe house, talking, or playing an instrument. 
* If the player goes into the MacFarlane's Ranch foreman's office and shoots the prisoner in the cell, which is always [[Gilroy Massey]], he will become hostile and pull out a Winchester Repeater, although he can easily be killed and most of the time he cannot shoot even through the cell bars. However, killing him may give the player a wanted level.
* Two [[chests]] can be found in the ranch; one is inside the Poker building, and the other is underneath the bridge which lies next to the main house. The player should weary if [[Claude Banfield]] is at the poker table though, as they might get a wanted level and he will start shooting at them should the player open the chest.

===''Undead Nightmare''===
* In ''[[Undead Nightmare]]'', once the town has been saved, a couple of the town's defenders can be found around the campfire, just as the ranch's past inhabitants could be in ''Red Dead Redemption''.
* It's possible to get stuck behind the counter of the telegraph office if the player climbs on the awning outside and jumps in through the window. The door of the telegraph office is boarded shut, and the windows at ground level cannot be jumped through.
* The US flag in MacFarlane's Ranch is realistic in that it only flies when there is a wind in the air, otherwise, it will remain limp; it also appears to be burnt and torn.
* While the town is [[Town Saving|under attack]] by the undead hordes, it is possible for a single undead to spawn on the roof of the cattle pen. This would be an unusual spawning point for the undead, as the only way to gain access to the roof is by using the ladder on the north side, and undead cannot climb.
** In addition, one of the [[survivors]] may take up position on the roof of the cattle pen while the town is under attack, leaving them vulnerable to the undead should one spawn on the roof.
* While the town is under attack, undead [[prostitutes]] are among the undead that roam across the ranch. However, in ''Red Dead Redemption'', there are never any prostitutes in MacFarlane's Ranch.
* If the player loads a saved game in MacFarlane's Ranch (or fast travels to the location), their mount will still be hitched at the post outside of the shack where the safehouse is located in ''Red Dead Redemption'', despite the safehouse in ''Undead Nightmare'' being moved to the foreman office's jail cell.
* The barn doors are locked until the player completes the side-mission "[[Paternal Pride]]". Upon completing the mission, the doors will unlock, although the debris used to block the rear doors will still be in place.
* The ladder that leads up to the silo next to the barn is repaired and accessible, unlike in ''Red Dead Redemption''.
* For some odd reason, [[Granville Berry]] can be found defending the ranch despite the fact he is a criminal from [[West Elizabeth]].
* MacFarlane's Ranch is one of two settlements, the other being [[Armadillo]], that does not provide a weapon for saving it.

===''Red Dead Redemption 2''===
* The ranch reappears with minor differences. One notable difference is that the barn house is nowhere to be seen, even though Bonnie stated during the mission [[New Friends, Old Problems|"New Friends, Old Problems"]] that her father built the barn himself when she was a little girl. This might be a developer oversight.
* Bonnie's house also appears to be made of different materials in the second installment when compared to the ''Red Dead Redemption''. The front porch of the house is also much shorter in ''Red Dead Redemption 2''. The columns, front door, and shutter colors are also different.
* The cliffs north of the ranch, while climbable in ''Redemption''​​​​​​, were made unclimbable in this game, and the player will simply slide back down if they try. The cliffs now directly connect to the inaccessible parts of Tall Trees.

=== ''Red Dead Online'' ===
* Bonnie can be found there as players can do stranger missions for her which will increase the player's [[honor]].
* The ranch is one of the several locations which can be defended through the telegram [[Call to Arms]] missions.
