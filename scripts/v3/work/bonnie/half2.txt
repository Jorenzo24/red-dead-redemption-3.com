Relationships
John Marston
Bonnie and her ranch hand Amos find John by the road after Fort Mercer, and she has him treated and lodged at the ranch in exchange for work (see Biography). She teases him constantly, about his "trigger itch", his riddles and his smell, and he answers with dry jokes. In "This is Armadillo, USA" she calls their meeting "an unusual start to a friendship"; John says he did not realize they were friends, and she brushes the remark aside. She tells him she does not think he is a bad man, and later that he could make a fine rancher if he stopped killing people for a living.
In "Women and Cattle" John tells her about his family and his years in the gang (see Biography). In "Wild Horses, Tamed Passions" he says his wife is like her, "a woman in a man's world", a phrase he uses again when Abigail asks him what Bonnie is like. After the barn fire, she says her family owes him a debt, and he asks only that the family sell him cattle once he is home, which it does in 1911 (see Biography). Her last canon scene with him is the corn delivery of "Old Friends, New Problems". The official guide's view of their bond is quoted under Personality.
Drew MacFarlane
Her father owns MacFarlane's Ranch; he tells John that the family came from the East thirty years earlier and that he has buried more of his children than he has raised. Bonnie tells John that her father's debts are terrible, that losing the ranch would kill him and that he is "frailer than he looks". She says he once hired cheap governesses to keep the family from being savages. When he fails to come back from a ride in "The Burning", she says he is all she has (see Biography). The Red Dead Wiki credits Chuck Kelley as his voice. In Undead Nightmare, which is not canon, Bonnie loses him in the barn (see Biography).
Amos
The Red Dead Wiki describes Amos as the head ranch hand of MacFarlane's Ranch. He is with Bonnie when she finds John after Fort Mercer. In "A Tempest Looms" he wants to keep the men out of the storm; Bonnie overrules him to save the herd and calls him a stupid man. When John asks why she never married, she names Amos as an example of the local men, who are a little "countrified". Her last scene in the main story ends as she follows him into the general store (see Biography). The wiki credits Dan Campbell as his voice and notes that he plays poker with the ranch hands.
Patrick MacFarlane
Bonnie's only surviving brother left for the East about ten years before 1911 and, according to his last letter, is a banker in New York. She tells John she does not want his help, but that she fears a little for his soul, and that she has never met a man in a tie she could trust. She never says his name; according to the Red Dead Wiki, residents of Warthington Ranch and Armadillo speak of the success of Patrick MacFarlane in New York. After he left, she says, it fell to her to become the man of the ranch.
The MacFarlane brothers
Bonnie had six brothers, and five died, of sickness or foolish choices, she says. She mentions that one of them used to read penny dreadfuls. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the five are Hank, Owen, Gus, Ethan and Cole, buried at Coot's Chapel; their graves give deaths from tuberculosis (Hank, 1893), chronic diarrhea (Owen, 1896), a gunshot wound to the head (Cole, 1899), an attempt to milk a bull (Ethan, 1903) and a bar fight (Gus, 1905).
Abigail Marston
Bonnie's telegram asking for corn reaches Beecher's Hope, and Abigail, who cannot read, has John read it aloud before deciding to come along (see Biography). At the ranch the two women meet for the first time. Bonnie asks how she is after her ordeal; Abigail answers that she has been through worse. When John jokes about Abigail's cooking, the two women side against him, and he says he has never felt so outnumbered. On the ride home Abigail tells John she likes Bonnie (see Biography).
Jack Marston
Bonnie meets Jack in "The Outlaw's Return", when he comes with his father to buy cattle (see Biography). John tells him to say hello to Miss MacFarlane; when John complains about the arrogance of youth, Bonnie replies that the boy must take after his father. In 1914, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Jack can still use the cabin at the ranch but never sees Bonnie (see Biography).
Bill Williamson
When John wakes up at the ranch, Bonnie calls Bill the worst bandit in the county and asks John what he was like. She later refers to him as "that nice Mr. Williamson" when she suggests John see the marshal. Bill's gang burns the ranch barn and kidnaps her (see Biography). In 1911 she asks John what became of Bill, and he answers that they settled their differences.
Leigh Johnson
Bonnie advises John to visit the marshal in Armadillo after their race, and she later hears from Johnson about John's plans. On the way to Tumbleweed, Johnson asks John whether he is taking advantage of the MacFarlanes, who, he says, are vulnerable in different ways. He leads the posse that frees her in "Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane" (see Biography).
Norman Deek
Bill Williamson's right-hand man, held in the Armadillo jail, is the prisoner the gang demands in exchange for Bonnie. He is shot by his own side when the exchange at Tumbleweed turns into an ambush (see Biography).
Nathanial Johnston
The Armadillo doctor removes the bullets from John; Bonnie pays the 15-dollar bill (see Biography). In "This is Armadillo, USA" she sends John to his office for medicine, paying for the first one herself, and tells him to be polite to the man who saved his life.
Uncle
Bonnie never meets him. When John tells her in "This is Armadillo, USA" that Uncle is looking after his farm, she remarks that he sounds like the perfect person to leave in charge of a whole livelihood, and advises John to get back there.
Bonnie's suitor
In Red Dead Redemption 2, a man found near death on the shore near Flatneck Station, or south of Van Horn, hands the player a letter addressed to her (see Biography). In it, according to the letter's text, he apologizes, writes that her father does not accept him and says he is leaving to make his fortune before coming back for her.
Red Dead Online protagonist
In 1898 Bonnie first takes the online protagonist for a robber, then hires them to recover a missing wagon (see Biography), before offering the free roam missions described under Skills.
Skills, weapons and horses
On the ranch
Bonnie gives John the first mission strand of the game (see Missions), and each one shows a part of her work. She patrols the property at night with a rifle, and the mission page of "New Friends, Old Problems" notes that the player does not need to shoot the rabbits and coyotes because she kills them herself. She races John on horseback in "Obstacles in Our Path": according to the Red Dead Wiki, she is hard to beat with John's first horse, the conversation changes with the state of the race, and winning brings no bonus. She drives the ranch wagon, which she hands over to John on the way to Armadillo. She herds cattle, ropes wild horses and directs the ranch hands through a thunderstorm in "A Tempest Looms". According to the wiki's page on Drew MacFarlane, her father taught her to break horses and run the ranch.
She also equips John. "New Friends, Old Problems" gives him a horse she picked for him (a Cleveland Bay in the original version, a War Horse in the Game of the Year Edition and later versions) and the Repeater Carbine he uses on the patrol. The same mission opens the safehouse at MacFarlane's Ranch and the ranch's Nightwatch job. In "Wild Horses, Tamed Passions" she hands him a lasso, and she lets him keep the stallion that broke away from the herd, a Kentucky Saddler, as a thank-you from the family.
In Red Dead Online, 1898
In Red Dead Online she is a Stranger at MacFarlane's Ranch, in New Austin. After her introduction (see Biography), she offers four kinds of free roam missions: Recovery (getting back a stolen wagon), Delivery, Caravan Escort and On The Hunt, in which the player tracks a grizzly bear. According to the Red Dead Wiki, accepting her missions raises the player's honor. Their start and end points are listed in the Missions table.
Weapons and horses
The Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver, a Repeater Carbine and a Double-barreled Shotgun as her weapons. Artwork shows her holding a Winchester Repeater, a weapon she never carries in the game, according to the same wiki. Her horses, as the wiki lists them, are a Painted Quarter Horse, a Standardbred Pinto and a Painted Standardbred.
Missions
Bonnie gives John the first missions of Red Dead Redemption after "Exodus in America": her strand runs from "New Friends, Old Problems" to "Hanging Bonnie MacFarlane" and teaches the basics of the game (shooting, riding, driving a wagon, herding, the lasso, climbing). She returns in two missions of the Homestead chapter and in one side mission of Undead Nightmare. In Red Dead Online she gives free roam missions. The events are told in the Biography.

Development and performance
Casting and motion capture
Bonnie is played by Kimberly Irion, credited in the role by Wikipedia's article on Red Dead Redemption and by the Red Dead Wiki. According to the wiki, Irion plays her in the main game and in Undead Nightmare (2010), voices the multiplayer version of the character added by the Liars and Cheats pack, and returns to the role in Red Dead Online, where Bonnie is a minor mission giver. No interview in which Irion discusses the role was found. The Red Dead Wiki states that Bonnie's motion capture for the opening train scene was performed by Steve J. Palmer, the actor who plays Bill Williamson.
Name
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Bonnie is named after an aunt of Rob Hanson, a former designer at Rockstar San Diego. Hanson designed the ranch, renamed it MacFarlane's Ranch and gave the rancher's daughter the name Bonnie MacFarlane shortly before he left the studio. The wiki gives no source for the account.
Cut content
The Red Dead Wiki's page on the game's cut content states that the family's original surname was MacFarling, a claim the page itself marks as needing a citation. The same page says the ranch was called "Hennigan's Ranch" at one stage, then "MacFarling's Ranch". The game files also contain an unused Penny Dreadful item, the kind of novel Bonnie says her brother used to read; its intended purpose is unknown.
Reception
Game Informer ranked Bonnie 29th in its list of the 30 characters who defined the decade, published in its December 2010 issue; John Marston came second. The cover art commissioned for the feature, revealed by the magazine on November 5, 2010, shows Bonnie's hand resting on John's shoulder.
In Western American Literature in 2012, Sara Humphreys described Bonnie as "insightful and resourceful" rather than "a woman masquerading as a male figure", while noting that she defers to simple domestic tasks when her father is present. In his 2020 book on the game for Boss Fight Books, Matt Margini points out that on one occasion she becomes a damsel in distress. In a retrospective for the game's tenth anniversary, on April 4, 2020, the German magazine PC Games called her a modern woman, assertive and direct.
Her return in Red Dead Online drew coverage at the launch of the online beta. On November 28, 2018, Javy Gwaltney of Game Informer described finding MacFarlane's Ranch deserted in the story of Red Dead Redemption 2, then meeting Bonnie online, "years before meeting John Marston", with a mission to find a wagon.
Other media and legacy
Multiplayer
Bonnie became a playable character model in the multiplayer mode of Red Dead Redemption with the Liars and Cheats Pack, detailed by Rockstar on August 27, 2010 and released on September 21, 2010. Players select her in the Redemption section of the Outfitter, alongside John, Abigail, Jack, Leigh Johnson, Luisa Fortuna, Nigel West Dickens and Seth Briars. The Red Dead Wiki lists 28 taunts for her model, a few of which are quoted above. The versions of Red Dead Redemption released on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch on August 17, 2023, then on PC on October 29, 2024, do not include the multiplayer mode.
Marketing and merchandise
Rockstar released promotional images and artwork of Bonnie for Red Dead Redemption, and she appears on the back of the fold-out map packaged with the game. According to the Red Dead Wiki, her outfit could be bought for avatars in PlayStation Home, Sony's former online space on PlayStation 3. According to a Red Dead Wiki gallery caption, the Undead Nightmare trailer includes a shot of John and Bonnie embracing.
Red Dead Online and Red Dead Redemption 2
Bonnie is one of the strangers of Red Dead Online, set in 1898, where she gives free roam missions at MacFarlane's Ranch (see Biography and Missions). In Red Dead Redemption 2 she is only mentioned, through the letter of the shipwreck survivor.
Trivia
The Blackwater Ledger article "Kidnapped Woman Found Alive", published after her rescue, calls her "a spinster of 29 years" and judges a crime of passion implausible for that reason. The age contradicts the 27 years given elsewhere, including by the Game of the Year Edition guide.
During the tour of the ranch in "New Friends, Old Problems", Bonnie says her father built the barn himself when she was a little girl. In Red Dead Online (1898) and Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899 and 1907), the ranch has no barn.
By the Red Dead Wiki's dates, Bonnie would be about 14 in Red Dead Online, set in 1898; her model there looks like a woman in her early twenties.
When the shipwreck survivor of Red Dead Redemption 2 wakes up to hand over his letter, Arthur exclaims "Whoa, I thought you was already gone!" and John "Jesus! I thought you were dead...".
"New Friends, Old Problems" rewards John with a Repeater Carbine, a horse (a Cleveland Bay in the original version, a War Horse in the Game of the Year Edition) and the first level of Dead Eye. "Wild Horses, Tamed Passions" gives him the lasso and the Kentucky Saddler, and "Paternal Pride" a Winchester Repeater.
The $15 Bonnie paid the doctor is never deducted from John's money during the game.
Winning or losing the race in "Obstacles in Our Path" makes no difference: the reward is 20 Fame either way.
"The Burning" is the only mission of Bonnie's strand that does not unlock a Stranger side mission.
In the original release, when Amos passes Bonnie at the end of "Old Friends, New Problems", he says "Ma'am" while the subtitles read "Howdy, miss". The Game of the Year Edition fixed it.
In Undead Nightmare, two of her lines are written as graffiti on walls in Blackwater: "You do so love to talk in riddles" and "Civilization is truly a beautiful thing".
Artwork shows her holding a Winchester Repeater, a weapon she never uses in the game.
