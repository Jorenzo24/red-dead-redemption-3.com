### "Civilization, at Any Price": decoy train, not a train "to capture" Reyes
Transcript (Civilization_at_Any_Price_dialogues.txt) wins: De Santa says "My men are trying to lure him into a trap" and later "We will lure the rebels into a trap. There is a train leaving Chuparosa soon and we're going to escort it. They will think it is a supply train, but there are no supplies on it! ... We must draw the rats out of their holes." They ride on horseback alongside the train ("They arrive at the train station and get on their horses"); John is not "on" the train. The mission page's "as they attempt to capture Reyes" is a loose summary. reyes_C.py already has it right.

Exact fix in scripts/fiches/parts/john_B.py line 84-85 (then regenerate characters/john-marston/ + fr/personnages/john-marston/):
- EN old: `then takes him on a train to capture the rebel leader [[abraham-reyes|Abraham Reyes]].`

- FR old: `puis l'emmène dans un train pour capturer le chef rebelle [[abraham-reyes|Abraham Reyes]].`
- FR new: `puis l'emmène escorter, au départ de Chuparosa, un train leurre censé attirer les rebelles d'[[abraham-reyes|Abraham Reyes]] dans un piège.`

Also john_D.py line 138-139 (Abraham Reyes relationship): "When Colonel Allende turns on John" is wrong: it is Captain de Santa who betrays John in Chuparosa ("Cowards Die Many Times").
