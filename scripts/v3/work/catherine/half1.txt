
Catherine Braithwaite is the head of the Braithwaite family, owners of Braithwaite Manor in Scarlett Meadows, in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. Her family sells moonshine and has long feuded with the Grays of Caliga Hall. In chapter 3 the Van der Linde gang works for both families: Hosea Matthews and Arthur Morgan do jobs for her while the gang steals her horses for Tavish Gray. Her men then kidnap Jack Marston. The gang storms the manor and sets it on fire, and she reveals that her sons gave the boy to Angelo Bronte. Left alive, she runs back into the burning house and dies there.
She is played by Ellen Harvey.
Key facts
She runs Braithwaite Manor, a plantation in Scarlett Meadows whose family now lives off moonshine, and she has been doing business with Angelo Bronte for five years.
In "Advertising, The New American Art", she pays Hosea and Arthur to hand out her stolen moonshine for free in a Gray saloon in Rhodes.
In "The Fine Joys of Tobacco", after a game of cribbage, she hires Arthur and Sean MacGuire to burn the Grays' tobacco fields at Caliga Hall.
In "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern", the gang kills her sons, burns the manor and learns from her that Jack is with Bronte, in Saint Denis or on a boat to Italy.
She dies in 1899 after running back into her burning house; her body can be found in the ruins from chapter 4.

Biography
Origins
The games give no date or place of birth for Catherine Braithwaite. In 1899 she is the head of the Braithwaite family and its oldest living member. The family owns Braithwaite Manor, a plantation house by the water in Scarlett Meadows, in the state of Lemoyne, outside the town of Rhodes. The estate has tobacco, cotton and corn fields, boathouses, workers' cabins and stables for the family's prized horses. Catherine has three sons, Bartholomew, Gareth and Gerald, and a daughter. Her niece Penelope also lives on the estate and calls her Aunt Catherine. No husband is named or seen. The Red Dead Wiki describes the family as American, of English descent. During the raid on the manor, Catherine says the Braithwaites have lived in the house for 120 years.
The daughter is never shown in the story missions. Beau Gray tells Arthur that Catherine has a daughter nobody has seen for years, and that she was not right. According to the Red Dead Wiki, her name is Gertrude and it appears only in the credits; the wiki adds that the family keeps her locked in an outhouse on the estate so as not to damage its name. A photograph kept in the manor shows, according to the wiki, Catherine, Gertrude and the sons posing with the family's Black servants.
The Braithwaites are locked in a long feud with the Grays of Caliga Hall, the other old plantation family of the area. The Grays hold the law in Rhodes through Sheriff Leigh Gray and own the town's saloon. Josiah Trelawny tells Dutch van der Linde that the two families have been fighting over Confederate gold and marriages. The Rhodes deputy Archibald MacGregor tells Arthur that each family believes the other stole a fortune from it long ago, and that he cannot say whether the story is true. The Red Dead Wiki traces the feud to 1806: Lucille Braithwaite is said to have shown Douglas Gray where her family's gold was hidden, and he took it, along with gold from his own family, to fund abolitionist groups.
Archibald describes the Braithwaites as an old cotton family that lost its fortune when the labor laws changed and turned to moonshine. The law keeps finding their stills all over Lemoyne. He adds that Catherine has an expensive taste for thoroughbred horses. According to the Red Dead Wiki, she sells the liquor to the Lemoyne Raiders, and the family has taken over the moonshine trade in Lemoyne with Danny-Lee Caton, a former cook of the bootlegger Maggie Fike, running production.
Catherine also does business with Angelo Bronte, the crime boss of Saint Denis. A letter she wrote to him lies on a nightstand in the manor. It opens with "Dear Signor Bronte" and answers a message he sent the week before. Catherine writes that "our two proud and ancient families, have worked together successfully for five years now", that the Braithwaites "have always provided a quality product and paid our debts", and that he has been "the most valued of customer". She mentions "a small cash flow problem, and a minor disruption in supply" and asks him not to end the arrangement. She promises: "We will cover our debts shortly, I assure you of that. I agree that they have grown bigger, but we will pay the interest within the quarter." She adds: "My spendthrift middle son has reformed his ways." The supply problem, she writes, has been "resolved entirely". The letter stops after the word "Furthermore" and does not name the son.
Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)
Chapter 3: Clemens Point
After the move to Clemens Point, near Rhodes, Dutch decides to exploit the feud and gives the Braithwaites to Hosea Matthews to work on. In "American Distillation", Sheriff Gray makes Dutch, Arthur Morgan and Bill Williamson temporary deputies to shut down a Braithwaite moonshine still in the swamp; the buyers who turn up are Lemoyne Raiders. Archibald MacGregor hands most of the seized liquor to the gang.
In "Advertising, The New American Art", Hosea and Arthur drive the moonshine to Braithwaite Manor. A guard tells them that the man of the house is a lady, Mrs. Catherine Braithwaite. Catherine meets them in front of the house and says she is not about to buy what already belongs to her. She sells it at a dollar a bottle, and Hosea asks for fifty cents a bottle as a reward for finding it. Armed men come out of the house; according to the Red Dead Wiki, Bartholomew points his shotgun at the visitors. Catherine laughs and tells her men to pay. When Hosea hints that they acted on someone's orders, she says she knows that old Sheriff Gray sent them. She then decides she does not want the liquor back. She offers ten dollars more for the two men to give it away for free in the Rhodes saloon run by the Grays, which she calls a promotional expense, and invites them to come back for a game of cribbage.
Hosea and Arthur pour free drinks until a group of Lemoyne Raiders arrives, angry to find the liquor they were due to buy being given away. The two men shoot their way out and escape by wagon. Once clear of the town, Arthur asks whether Catherine set them up; Hosea does not think so, but is not sure. He rides back to the manor to bring her some of the moonshine as a peace offering. At camp, Dutch and Micah Bell explain the plan to Arthur: convince each family that the gang is on its side, rob them both and be gone before they understand. Dutch works the Grays and Hosea the Braithwaites, and Dutch has sent Sean MacGuire to the manor as well.
In "The Fine Joys of Tobacco", a doorman shows Arthur into the manor, where Catherine is playing cribbage with Hosea and Sean while Bartholomew looks on. Catherine asks Hosea whether Arthur is as big and as stupid as he looks. Hosea answers with a joke at Arthur's expense, then raises the question of payment for the job at Caliga Hall; Catherine laughs and says the family has money. When Bartholomew insults Hosea, Hosea replies with a jibe about Catherine's children (see Quotes). The job is to burn the Grays' tobacco fields with the rest of the moonshine. According to Sean, Catherine points out that there has been no rain for weeks and finds the use of the liquor fitting. On the road, Sean tells Arthur that keeping quiet in front of her and her son took everything he had. That night the two men set the fields and a barn on fire and ride off with a payroll from a supply wagon.
The gang works the other side at the same time. In "Horse Flesh for Dinner", Tavish Gray receives Arthur, John Marston and Javier Escuella at Caliga Hall. He blames Catherine and her sons for ruining the county and for killing his uncle, and asks the three men to steal the Braithwaites' prized horses, which he says will fetch 5,000 dollars. On the ride over, Arthur tells John that he should see the Braithwaite woman. At the south gate of the manor, John tells a guard that they have an appointment about a large investment in the stables. The three men take three horses and fight their way off the estate. The fence Clay Davies, at Clemens Cove, pays only 700 dollars for them.
The scheme falls apart in Rhodes. On the way to a job offered by the Grays in "A Short Walk in a Pretty Town", Bill says the Grays believe the Braithwaites burned their fields; the job is an ambush, and Sean is killed. When Arthur gets back to Clemens Point, Abigail Marston is looking for her son Jack, aged four. The kidnapping is never shown. Hosea says that they think the Braithwaite woman took him, because Kieran Duffy saw a couple of men who sounded like Braithwaite boys. Hosea adds that the family must have worked out what the gang was doing, and the gang links the kidnapping to the theft of the horses. At Shady Belle, Kieran later apologizes to Abigail: knowing that Arthur and Hosea had dealings with the Braithwaites, he did not think they had come for Jack.
In "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern", Dutch rides to Braithwaite Manor with Arthur, Hosea, John, Charles Smith, Bill, Javier and Lenny Summers, leaving Micah and Kieran to guard the camp. On the way, Hosea admits that he no longer believes in the family gold. Dutch calls the family out of the house, and one of her sons tells the gang to get off their land. According to the mission page, Bartholomew is that son and Dutch shoots him; the subtitles name him Gareth. A gunfight follows against the family's men, who include cousins and hired guns according to the Red Dead Wiki. Dutch, Arthur, John and Hosea go inside looking for Jack and for Catherine, and find an upstairs room barricaded from within. While the others hold off reinforcements, Arthur helps John break down the door. Catherine is inside; Gareth and Gerald are killed in the attack on the room.
Dutch and Hosea question Catherine about Jack. She insults them, says that her family never had any trouble except from Yankees, and accuses them of killing her sons; she does not say where the boy is. Dutch shoots one of her sons in front of her, and she screams. He drags her from the stairs while Hosea and John set the house on fire; the search has found no trace of Jack. Dutch carries her out over his shoulder, sets her down in front of the burning house and holds his gun to her head. When Hosea asks why she took a child, she answers that the gang stole her liquor and her horses, and that there are no rules in war. She then says that her sons gave Jack to Angelo Bronte, and that the boy is either in Saint Denis or on the boat to Italy. Dutch tells the others to leave her. As the gang walks away, Catherine runs back into the burning manor, where she dies.
The mission leads straight into "The Battle of Shady Belle". The next morning, at Clemens Point, Arthur tells Dutch that the gang killed all those people and stirred up all that trouble for nothing. The Pinkerton agents Andrew Milton and Edgar Ross then walk into camp, and the gang moves to Shady Belle, an abandoned plantation house near Saint Denis.
Chapter 4: Saint Denis
In "Angelo Bronte, A Man of Honor", Bronte agrees to return Jack once Arthur and John have cleared the Saint Denis cemetery of grave robbers. On the way, Arthur tells John that the Braithwaites gave the boy to Bronte for money, and that with the family dead and unable to pay, Bronte has no further use for him. Jack is handed back unharmed. The burned manor can be visited from this chapter on. According to the Red Dead Wiki, armed survivors of the family still guard the grounds, and Catherine's charred body lies near what used to be the front entrance; nothing but the name shown on the body identifies her.
Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow
Penelope outlives her aunt. In "The Course of True Love V", she writes to Arthur that her family is keeping her prisoner on the plantation and threatening to send her away to stop her suffrage work; Arthur finds her at the cabins behind the ruins of the main house. On the ride to Rhodes station, she mentions the business with her Aunt Catherine and her cousins, and says that everyone left alive is angrier than before. Beau Gray, Tavish's son, is waiting at the station, and the couple leaves by train. Braithwaite riders chase the train because Penelope has taken the family's jewels, and Arthur fights them off before the couple sets out by coach for Boston.
According to the Red Dead Wiki, Arthur can stop on the way at the outhouse where Gertrude is kept. Penelope then explains that her cousin is mentally ill and that the family chose to keep her on the property rather than send her to a sanatorium, and says she will do what she can for her from Boston. Arthur can also come across the outhouse on his own; his journal entry on the discovery is given under Quotes.
Red Dead Redemption 2: the epilogue (1907)
By 1907, according to the Red Dead Wiki, no Braithwaite is left at the manor. The ruins are overgrown, the guards are gone, and a small fishing community has settled by the old docks. Gertrude's remains are still inside the outhouse. In Rhodes, Sheriff Harmon Thomas, elected after Leigh Gray's death, has promised to rid the town of the influence of both families, the wiki adds. Simon Pearson, who now runs the general store in Rhodes, tells John that people in town still talk about how the Grays and the Braithwaites met their end (see Quotes).
Personality
Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang and do not include her. In "American Distillation", the deputy Archibald MacGregor calls the Braithwaites an "old cotton family" turned to moonshine and says that Catherine has "a rather... expensive interest in thoroughbred horses". Dutch sums up the family as "Moonshiners... hypocritical". Tavish Gray speaks of "that hag and her inbred sons". Sean MacGuire insults her at length after the cribbage meeting.
In the game she gives the orders: her sons draw their guns, and she tells them to stand down. She answers threats with a laugh and turns a sale she refuses into a paid errand. She knows who is behind the theft of her liquor before anyone tells her. She addresses outsiders with open contempt, and "Yankee" is her insult of choice. Even at gunpoint, she meets the gang's questions with insults.
In her own words
"I ain't in the market for what's already mine." (to Hosea, who offers to sell her back her moonshine)
"Oh, I know exactly who gave you your orders. Old Sheriff Gray."
"So this one... he as big and as dumb as he looks?" (on meeting Arthur at the cribbage table)
"We have lived in this house for a hundred and twenty years. We never had no problems 'cept for Yankees."
"Oh, I know your type. Common scum." (to Dutch)
"You stole my liquor. You stole my horses. Ain't no rules in war, Mr..." (to Hosea, who tells her that boys are off limits)
Names
The guard at the gate introduces her as "Mrs. Catherine Braithwaite", and Hosea calls her Mrs. Braithwaite. One of her sons calls her "Momma". After the saloon job, Hosea calls her "old Ma Braithwaite". Dutch calls her "old woman" and "hag", a word Sean and Tavish Gray also use. Her niece Penelope speaks of her as "Aunt Catherine". The gang usually refers to her as the Braithwaite woman.
Appearance
She is an elderly woman with gray hair gathered in a bun. At the manor she wears a purple dress with a wide gray collar, fastened at the throat by a brooch set with a blue stone.
