L65: Near the lake, Dutch spots a lone man he thinks was at the camp with Colm and tells Arthur to bring him back alive. Arthur chases him, ropes him off his horse and hogties him. On the ride to Colter, the prisoner first says he does not know his name, then gives it: Kieran Duffy. He says he has only been riding with the O'Driscolls for a couple of months. At camp, Dutch refuses to have him beaten, since that would only produce lies. He tells Bill and Uncle to tie him up somewhere safe and get him hungry first. Kieran protests that he is not an O'Driscoll and hates Colm; Dutch answers with sarcasm.
L66: The rewards are the Carbine Repeater, the lasso and Dead Eye targeting, according to the mission page. If "Old Friends" is played second, it continues directly into "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?".
L67: 5. Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?
L68: The mission starts in John's cabin at Colter, where Dutch announces the job. It takes place in the Grizzlies West and, according to the mission page, ends at Granite Pass.
L69: John lies in bed with Swanson beside him and tells Arthur: "I owe you." Dutch comes in and says it is time for the train. John asks to come and tries to get up: "I was always ugly, Dutch... it's just a scratch." Dutch tells him to lie still. Abigail brings Jack to see his father, and the couple trade insults in front of the boy.
L70: Outside, Dutch sends Bill ahead to set the charge "at the water tower, just before the tunnel." Hosea objects: the weather is breaking, the gang could leave, and the plan was to lie low and head back west. He says: "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch." Dutch answers that everything the gang owns is in Blackwater. Hosea warns that "Leviticus Cornwall's no joke"; when Arthur asks who that is, Hosea describes Cornwall as "a big railway magnate, sugar dealer, oil man."
L71: Dutch rides out with Arthur, Micah, Charles, Javier and Lenny. He explains that, according to the O'Driscolls' plans, the train will come north from Big Valley and will be hit after it crosses into the Grizzlies. Charles is to watch for outriders. Dutch takes the driver and engineer, Lenny and Javier the two front cars, and Arthur and Micah the private car at the back. Micah keeps asking about going back to Blackwater for the money; Charles says it would be crazy with the town full of Pinkertons, and Dutch ends the discussion. Dutch says that if the O'Driscolls are right, there will be a stack of railroad bonds on the train. As they leave the snow, Arthur says: "Out of the snow, finally."
L72: At the ridge above the water tower, Dutch sends Arthur down to check on Bill. Bill has Arthur unspool the wire and set up the detonator by the rocks. When the train passes, Bill pushes the plunger and nothing happens. Arthur, Javier and Lenny run to the far end of the tunnel and jump onto the train from above. Javier misses and is left behind; the mission page says he is hurt in the fall. Lenny ends up hanging from the side of a car, and Arthur can pull him up; otherwise Lenny climbs up alone and thanks him sarcastically.
L73: Inside the first car, Lenny kills a guard with his knife. Arthur chooses whether he or Lenny takes the next one, which starts the gunfight along the train. Lenny asks what Bill was doing; Arthur says he hooked up the wire himself, "but we won't mention that." Near the engine, the engineer comes out of the coal bin and hits Arthur with a shovel. Arthur overpowers him, throws him off the train and stops it. Guards pour out of the cars, and the rest of the gang rides in to help clear them.
L74: Employees have locked themselves in the last car. One of them calls out: "I work for Leviticus Cornwall." Dutch counts down from five, has the gang fire on the car, then sends Arthur and Charles to blow the door with Bill's dynamite. The employees come out with their hands up; Dutch tells them: "We don't want to kill you... we just wanna rob your boss." Cornwall himself is not on the train.
L75: Arthur, Lenny and Micah search the car, which Lenny compares to a palace. Micah opens the safe and finds only papers. Lenny reads them: railroad contracts, invoices, sugar imports from the Spanish West Indies and "Some fancy new boat he's ordered from Europe." Micah laughs that he will not rob another boat as long as he lives. Arthur finds the bonds in the car's cabinets. Dutch calls them bearer bonds and thinks they can be sold easily.
L76: Dutch tells Arthur to get rid of the train and leaves the employees to him: "It's up to you." Arthur can kill them or put them back on the train, start the engine and jump off. The mission page says the choice only affects the player's honor. Dutch tells Arthur that the gang will be moving on when he gets back.
L77: The reward is dynamite, according to the mission page. Two documents can be missed in the car: the Oil Company Letter to Cornwall and the Mining Company Letter to Cornwall. The mission continues directly into "Eastward Bound".
L78: 6. Eastward Bound
L79: The last mission of the chapter is given by Dutch. It starts at Colter and ends at Horseshoe Overlook, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.
L80: Arthur rides back from the train to find the camp loading the wagons. Dutch says the weather seems stable; Hosea reminds him they just robbed a Cornwall train. Dutch declares: "the worst is behind us." Hosea proposes Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine: "We'll be able to hide out there no problem... as long as we keep our noses clean." Charles and Abigail help John into a wagon. Dutch puts Arthur and Hosea in the same wagon, saying the two like to talk about "what's gone wrong with old Dutch."
L81: As the convoy leaves the snow, Dutch sends Micah and Lenny ahead to check the road. Micah complains about being paired with "the boy", then tells Lenny he can buy him a whisky. After a stream crossing, a wheel comes off Arthur and Hosea's wagon. The wagon ahead, carrying Bill and Charles, with Javier riding alongside, stops. Hosea turns down Javier's help; Charles stays behind, and he and Hosea hold the wagon up while Arthur puts the wheel back on.
L82: Three riders watch them from a cliff. Charles says: "If they wanted trouble... we wouldn't have seen them." The Red Dead Wiki identifies them as Rains Fall, Eagle Flies and Paytah, of the Wapiti tribe. Charles rides on in their wagon. Hosea explains that the native people of the Heartlands lost the land and were killed or moved to reservations, and that he heard the Army there was particularly harsh. Charles asks: "How do you rob and kill people pleasantly? We don't, in spite of Dutch's talk."
L83: Arthur asks Charles about his tribe. Charles says he does not know if he has one. His father was a Black man who lived with his mother's people for a while; when they were forced off their lands, the three fled. Soldiers later took his mother, and they never saw her again. His father drank, and at around thirteen Charles struck out on his own. Hosea says it was about the age they found Arthur: "A wilder delinquent you never did see. But he learned fast."
L84: Hosea describes Valentine as a livestock town of "Cowboys, outlaws, working girls." He tells Arthur that it "isn't like Dutch to lose his head like that." Arthur answers that things go wrong sometimes and that the three of them must have got it right far more often than wrong. Hosea gives Arthur yarrow and ginseng he has been preparing. At the entrance to the site, Javier waits by a rock, shows them the track and jumps onto the back of the wagon.
L85: At the new camp, Dutch says: "Gentlemen, we have survived." Hosea blames Micah for the ferry job. Dutch answers that they have all made mistakes, but that he kept the gang together and "kept the nooses off our neck." Hosea says he does not have long and wants people safe before he goes. Dutch's plan is to make some money, go around the law, "be west of Uncle Sam" and buy land within a few months. Strauss leaves for the nearby town to "strike up a little business."
L86: Dutch gathers the gang. He tells them they are safe but "far too poor", and that everyone must get to work. Hosea tells them to stay out of trouble: "Remember, we are itinerant workers." Dutch adds: "Uncle, Reverend Swanson... no more passengers." Pearson asks for real food every day, and Dutch sets out a box: "the camp gets its slice." Susan shows Arthur to his tent.
L87: The title card "Chapter II: Horseshoe Overlook" appears, followed by "A couple of weeks later..." Arthur writes in his journal: "We got off the mountain and rode east into some pretty enough country called the Heartlands." According to the mission page, completing "Eastward Bound" opens free roam and gives Arthur a camera, five ginseng and five yarrow, and Horseshoe Overlook becomes the gang's camp.
L88: Camp life at Colter
L89: The Camp Events page of the Red Dead Wiki lists a small number of optional scenes at Colter. They can be seen once "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" is complete, until the second of "Old Friends" and "The Aftermath of Genesis" is finished.
L90: By the fireplace, Dutch tells Hosea he feels Hosea doubts him; Hosea says he is only worried. Dutch asks Arthur if he doubts him too, and Arthur says he does not.
L91: In the main cabin, Sadie sobs over her husband while Mary-Beth Gaskill comforts her. Arthur can offer Sadie food; she says she is not hungry.
L92: Susan Grimshaw tells everyone to stay warm, that they will be off the mountain in a few days and that the gang must stick together. Tilly Jackson says she hopes she is right.
L93: Swanson reads Isaiah 40:20-31 aloud. Karen Jones asks what it means; he says he has no idea, but it gives him comfort.
L94: Abigail scolds the bedridden John for getting himself mauled by wolves. When Arthur approaches, John says "Oh God no", and Susan tells Arthur to leave him alone.
L95: Dutch asks Hosea to send someone "sensible" to bury Sadie's husband. Arthur can tell Hosea the body is in a wagon in front of the house.
L96: Dutch and Hosea argue about what comes next. Hosea brings up the Pinkertons and bounty hunters; Dutch says the plan is to lie low, make money and get out, but admits he does not know where to yet.
L97: If "Old Friends" comes first, Kieran can be found tied up in the stable. He tries to scare Arthur with the O'Driscolls who will come looking for him.
L98: The same page notes that Bill, Javier, Charles and Pearson have no camp events in this chapter. According to the Colter page of the wiki, Pearson's butcher stand sits beside the stable, and Arthur's health drains quickly if he strays too far from the camp during the chapter.

L108: Characters in this chapter
L109: Every member of the gang at Colter makes a first appearance in the game in this chapter. Arthur Morgan, the playable character, takes part in every mission. Dutch van der Linde leads the gang and Hosea Matthews argues against the train job. John Marston is found wounded by wolves and spends the rest of the chapter in bed, visited by Abigail and their son Jack.
L110: The riders of the chapter are Micah Bell, Bill Williamson, Javier Escuella, Lenny Summers and Charles Smith, whose burned hand keeps him off the bow. The camp is run by Susan Grimshaw and fed by Simon Pearson. Also present are Orville Swanson, Leopold Strauss, Uncle, Karen Jones, Tilly Jackson, Mary-Beth Gaskill and Molly O'Shea, whom Susan asks to show Dutch to his room.
L111: Two newcomers arrive at camp in this chapter: Sadie Adler, taken in after the raid on her ranch, and Kieran Duffy, held as a prisoner. Colm O'Driscoll is seen only through binoculars at Ewing Basin. Leviticus Cornwall is named but does not appear. Rains Fall is one of the three riders on the cliff in "Eastward Bound", according to the Red Dead Wiki.

L113: Trivia
L114: Davey Callander's grave is in a small graveyard at Colter, and Jenny Kirk's lies north of Colter, near Spider Gorge, according to the Red Dead Wiki. Both count toward the "Paying Respects" trophy.
L115: Hosea's line "If we don't stop soon, we'll all be dying" is the one Arthur remembers in "A Fork in the Road" with high honor. His plea "I just don't want any more folks to die, Dutch" returns during Arthur's last ride with high honor, according to the wiki's mission pages.
L116: "Old Friends" unlocks Dead Eye targeting, and "The Aftermath of Genesis" unlocks Eagle Eye, according to the mission pages.
L117: After "Enter, Pursued by a Memory", an avalanche blocks the mountainside north of the ravine where John was found, according to the mission page.
L118: The title "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" plays on the stage direction "Exit, pursued by a bear" from Shakespeare's The Winter's Tale, as does the later mission "Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego", according to the wiki.
L119: "Outlaws from the West" is the only mission in which a gang member's horse, Charles's mare Taima, is meant to be ridden by the player, according to the mission page. Arthur later thanks Charles for lending her.
L120: The train robbery is reported under the headline "Train Robbed" in the Blackwater Ledger, the New Hanover Gazette and the Saint Denis Times. Arthur's journal lists the job twice under "Gang Savings", the second time after Hosea cashes in the bonds, according to the wiki.
L121: Rains Fall later tells Arthur, in Saint Denis, that he saw him with the wagon train crossing the river at Cumberland Falls.
L122: Colter is not revisited in the story after Chapter 1. In Red Dead Online, set in 1898, it is an O'Driscoll hideout, according to the wiki.
L123: Key events of Chapter 1
L124: May 1899: after the failed Blackwater ferry robbery, the gang flees into the Grizzlies West of Ambarino and shelters in the abandoned mining town of Colter. Davey Callander arrives dead; Jenny Kirk has already died; Sean MacGuire and Mac Callander are missing.
L125: "Outlaws from the West": Dutch, Arthur and Micah kill a band of O'Driscolls at the Adler Ranch and bring back the widow Sadie Adler. Arthur keeps a Tennessee Walker from the barn.
L126: "Enter, Pursued by a Memory": Arthur and Javier find John Marston on a mountain ledge, his face torn by wolves, and bring him back to camp.
L127: "The Aftermath of Genesis": Charles teaches Arthur to hunt with a bow, and they bring two deer back to Pearson.
L128: "Old Friends": the gang raids the O'Driscoll camp at Ewing Basin, takes dynamite and the plans for a Cornwall train, and Arthur captures Kieran Duffy.
L129: "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?": the gang robs Leviticus Cornwall's train in the Grizzlies and takes railroad bonds, despite Hosea's objections.
L130: "Eastward Bound": the gang leaves the snow for a new camp at Horseshoe Overlook, near Valentine, in the Heartlands of New Hanover.
