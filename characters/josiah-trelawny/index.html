Josiah Trelawny

Van der Linde gang Con man Fate unknown RDR2 & Online

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 6, 2026

Josiah Trelawny is a conman and magician associated with the Van der Linde gang in Red Dead Redemption 2. According to the official guide, he is allowed to come and go from Dutch's gang as he pleases.

He leaves for good in chapter 6, and also appears in Red Dead Online.

Biography

The gang's fixer

In chapter 2, Trelawny brings Dutch word that bounty hunters are holding Sean MacGuire. In "The First Shall Be Last", he fakes a fit to distract the guards while Arthur and Javier move in, and Sean is freed.

In chapter 3, he twice ends up in the hands of others: in a prison wagon in "The New South", then held by bounty hunters until Arthur and Charles free him in "Magicians for Sport". In "Friends in Very Low Places", he distracts an opera singer while Arthur opens a strongbox, on a tip from a Rhodes postal clerk.

In chapter 4, in "A Fine Night of Debauchery", he plans the robbery of the riverboat Grand Korrigan. He has Arthur shaved and dressed for the occasion. The job, with Arthur, Javier and Strauss, ends in a shootout and a swim to shore.

Family and departure

Trelawny has a wife and two sons in Saint Denis, who know nothing of his criminal life. At the start of "The Fine Art of Conversation", in chapter 6, he leaves the gang for good, with Arthur's blessing. His fate afterwards is not shown.

Red Dead Online

In Red Dead Online, Trelawny lives in a caravan near Rhodes. He introduces himself by faking a suicide, the gun turning into a bird, then gives the player a series of jobs, starting with the theft of one of Catherine Braithwaite's horses.

Development and performance

Trelawny is played by Stephen Gevedon, an American actor who co-wrote and starred in the film Session 9 (2001) and appeared in the series Oz and The Deuce.

In an October 2019 podcast interview with ComiCulture, Gevedon said he did not know at casting that the project was a game, and expected about a week of recording. He described the character's accent as a "bad Katharine Hepburn", chosen in part to keep Trelawny mysterious. Rockstar later expanded the role, adding his wife and children in the final years of development.

Reception

In Polygon, in November 2018, Colin Campbell described Trelawny as "an affectatious dandy, a gentleman thief with swell togs and a crisp English accent".
