## Problems

1. WRONG (gallery). Image 5 alt "Seth holding a light inside a dark building" and caption "Seth searching a building in 'Let the Dead Bury Their Dead'". The source file is Rdr_let_dead_bury_dead16.jpg (wiki caption: "What the goddamn hell is this? A glass eye?"). The image shows Seth holding up the glass eye, not a light. Fix: alt "Seth holding up a glass eye in a dark room" / "Seth tenant un œil de verre dans une pièce sombre"; caption "Seth finds the glass eye in the chest, in 'Let the Dead Bury Their Dead'." / FR equivalent.

2. IMPRECISE (gallery). Image 4 caption "Seth on the wall of Fort Mercer during the assault." The source file is Rdr_assault_fort_mercer13.jpg, whose wiki caption is "Seth inviting West Dicken's coach inside the fort". It is the gate welcome, not the wall dance seen through the spyglass (fort05). Fix: "Seth welcomes Dickens's wagon into Fort Mercer." (alt: "Seth leaning over the top of the fort's gate").

3. DUPLICATE (quotes). Relationships > John: "You remind me why I hate people. For a man who kills so much, you sure seem to have a problem with the dead." The same line is repeated in Quotes (Gentle Drive, "after John calls him a sick man"). Keep it in Quotes (mission line). In Relationships, keep "a sick man" and paraphrase Seth's reply.

4. DUPLICATE (quotes). Relationships > John: "Hunting dead men's treasure ain't done me no favors. Sure. Sure, I'm ready for the living." The line is repeated in Quotes ("the last line of his strand"). Keep it in Quotes and paraphrase in Relationships.

5. DUPLICATE (quotes). Relationships > Dickens: "You and Seth have a lot in common. You both rob people, for one. Mind you, at least he waits until they're dead." The line is repeated in Quotes (What others say). Keep it in Quotes and paraphrase in Relationships.

6. DUPLICATE (quotes). Relationships > Dickens: "for a minute, he will delight and amuse them. That's when he'll get us inside." The line is repeated in Quotes (Fort Mercer, Dickens). Keep it in Quotes and paraphrase in Relationships (the Biography already paraphrases it).

7. DUPLICATE (quotes). Relationships > Moses: "Seth'd sell his own mother". The line is repeated in Quotes (Moses to John, hogtied) and paraphrased in Biography. Keep it in Quotes and drop the quotation marks in Relationships.

8. DUPLICATE (quotes). Quotes: "Thanks for all the hard work, Seth! Now kill 'em, boys!" The first half is already quoted in Names (B), and the Biography paraphrases it. Drop it from Quotes (Names owns it) or drop it from Names.

9. DUPLICATE. Relationships > John: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, John sometimes says 'Seth, I'm sorry about all the insults'..." The same three lines appear in Trivia > Undead Nightmare. Keep them in Trivia only (gameplay barks, not a relationship scene) and cut them from Relationships. This also removes Undead Nightmare content from a canon relationship paragraph.

10. DUPLICATE. Relationships > His family: "if the player fails the ride ... several times, Seth says that the last thing he ate was a crow ... piglet his father killed". The same fact is told in Biography > Origins, which is the backstory owner per OWNERSHIP. The two wordings also differ: Origins says "a pig ... when he was a child" while this entry says "the piglet ... when he was a boy". Cut it from His family.

11. DUPLICATE. Grave robbing: "He tells John that Bill Williamson's gang sometimes calls on him for special jobs (see Relationships)." This is the third occurrence: Origins paraphrases it and Relationships > Bill quotes it. Cut the sentence.

12. DUPLICATE. Grave robbing: "he searches the corpses in the back of the wagon ... a few bullets and a bottle of elixir before he finds the piece of map". This retells Biography (Gentle Drive paragraph: "finding a few bullets and a bottle of elixir before he finds the map"). Cut it or reduce it to a pointer. The same applies to "the grave he is digging is that of Josephine Byrd", which is already in Biography (Exhuming paragraph) and again in Trivia.

13. IMPRECISE. Grave robbing: "he takes the bodies of flu victims from Odd Fellow's Rest before they are even buried". Biography itself hedges this: the mission starts at Coot's Chapel, and only the character page says the bodies come from Odd Fellow's Rest. The transcript names no place. Fix: "he takes the bodies of flu victims before they are even buried (according to the Red Dead Wiki, from Odd Fellow's Rest)", or drop the place.

14. DUPLICATE. In the game: "His first introduces wanted levels, bounties and hogtying ... 'A Gentle Drive with Friends' introduces fire bottles." The same is said again in the Missions intro paragraph. Keep it in Missions (C) and give In the game a pointer.

15. DUPLICATE. In the game: "the player can keep the letter and pay the bounty instead". The Trivia also says "At the end of 'Exhuming and Other Fine Hobbies', the player can pay off the bounty with money instead and keep the pardon letter." Keep one: Trivia is allowed only facts found nowhere else, so cut it from Trivia.

16. DUPLICATE. Weapons and horse: "In Undead Nightmare ... he pulls a revolver and fires at John if John attacks him". The Trivia (Undead Nightmare) says "if Seth survives, he draws a revolver, fires at John and tries to flee". Keep one, preferably Weapons, and cut the clause from Trivia. The trigger is also IMPRECISE: the mission page describes it after a stick of dynamite or a fire bottle is thrown into the party at the end of the mission (the Moses page adds shooting his cards twice), not "if John attacks him" in general. Assaulting Seth during the mission simply fails it.

17. DUPLICATE. Relationships > Moses, 2nd paragraph (Undead Nightmare: cards at The Old Bacchus Place, dancing with Mary Lou and Francine) retells Biography > Undead Nightmare ("He dances a jig with an undead woman he calls Mary Lou, while Moses dances with another he calls Francine"). Reduce it to one clause. It is also IMPRECISE: Mary Lou is Seth's dance partner and Francine is Moses's (Moses page). The draft lists them as Moses's "other undead guests".

18. DUPLICATE. Relationships > Irish: "Seth passes on a message from Dickens: there is no ammunition in John's machine gun ... only plays if John has already collected the gun". The Biography already tells this, with the same condition ("If John already has the machine gun from Irish, Seth passes on a message from Dickens"). Keep John's quoted reply in Relationships and turn the rest into a pointer.

19. DUPLICATE. Other media > "Red Dead Redemption 2" subsection: "Seth does not appear in Red Dead Redemption 2". This repeats the first sentence of Development > Unused Red Dead Online model. Keep it once, in the RDR2 subsection, and start the Development paragraph with the PCGamesN report.

20. IMPRECISE. Relationships > Aiden O'Leary: "says he died in the flu epidemic before the dead were even buried". The transcript says: "Aiden died in that flu epidemic and the bodies weren't even buried yet." The bodies are the ones not yet buried, and the draft garbles this. Fix: "says he died in the flu epidemic, whose victims had not yet been buried".

21. UNSUPPORTED. Relationships > Aiden O'Leary: "He never appears on screen." Seth's line "Honest folk, off to a better place. Apart from that Aiden O'Leary fella" is addressed to the bodies in the wagon, and seth_A's register says the wagon bodies include O'Leary. Whether his corpse is on screen is unknown. Fix: drop the sentence.

22. IMPRECISE. Relationships > Dickens: "When John reports the meeting ... Dickens defends grave robbing ... John answers: 'You and Seth have a lot in common...'". In the transcript the line is said later, on the road, in reply to Dickens's "soft spot for life's flotsam and jetsam", not to the Egypt remark. Fix: "Later, on the road, John tells him: ...", or follow problem 5 and paraphrase.

23. IMPRECISE. Missions: "'Let the Dead Bury Their Dead' is one of the four strands that must be completed". It is the last mission of one of the four strands. Fix: "Seth's strand, which ends with 'Let the Dead Bury Their Dead', is one of the four that must be completed before...".

24. IMPRECISE. Trivia (Undead Nightmare): "Seth and the niece of Mordecai Robbard are the only characters the undead never attack" and "Seth and Nigel West Dickens ... never fight the undead". The same character page's trivia says that Undead Bait makes Moses attack Seth, who then shoots him. Fix: soften to "do not take part in fighting the undead during the story" and keep the attribution, or cut the second sentence.
