Karen Jones

Van der Linde gang Con artist Fate unknown Voiced by Jo Armeniox

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 19, 2026

Karen Jones is a supporting character of Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. She is a con artist and gunwoman of the Van der Linde gang. During the Horseshoe Overlook chapter she starts looking into the Valentine bank. She plans the job with Bill Williamson and robs the bank with him, Lenny Summers and Arthur Morgan in "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah", during the Clemens Point chapter. She has a brief affair with Sean MacGuire. From the Clemens Point chapter onward she drinks more and more, and she leaves the gang at Beaver Hollow as it falls apart. Her fate is never shown: in 1907 Tilly Jackson writes that she believes drink killed her.

She is played by Jo Armeniox, an American actress.

Key facts

  • A long-standing scam artist of the gang; according to the Red Dead Wiki, she robbed a bank in Tucson with Arthur and Javier in the mid-1890s, dressed as a nun.
  • In "Polite Society, Valentine Style" a man she tries to rob at the Saints Hotel beats her before Arthur steps in; he had boasted about the town's bank, which becomes her next target.
  • She robs the Valentine bank with Bill, Lenny and Arthur in "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah", going in first as a lost girl or a drunken harlot.
  • She drinks more and more from the Clemens Point chapter onward; she turns on Susan Grimshaw after Molly's death and is gone from Beaver Hollow by "Our Best Selves".

Biography

Origins

The games give no date of birth for Karen and do not say when she joined the Van der Linde gang, only that she was a member before 1899. One earlier job is known. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Arthur Morgan brings up in an optional exchange during "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah" a bank robbery in Tucson, Arizona, in the mid-1890s, carried out with Javier Escuella and Karen. Karen walked into the bank disguised as a nun, with two pistols hidden under her clothes. The wiki's page on Karen adds that she shot someone in the knee during the job. The robbery succeeded.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)

Chapter 1: Colter

After the failed ferry robbery in Blackwater, the gang takes refuge in the snow at Colter, an abandoned mining town. In "Outlaws from the West", as soon as the group enters the main cabin, Susan Grimshaw sends Karen to bring in every blanket the gang has. At the end of the mission, Dutch van der Linde introduces Sadie Adler, widowed by the O'Driscolls, and asks Tilly Jackson and Karen to warm her up and give her a drink. The Red Dead Wiki also lists Karen in "Enter, Pursued by a Memory" and "Eastward Bound", the mission in which the gang comes down from the mountains to Horseshoe Overlook. She has no line in the transcript of either mission.

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

"Polite Society, Valentine Style" begins as Arthur wakes Uncle and suggests a trip to Valentine. Karen asks Arthur to take her, Tilly and Mary-Beth Gaskill along. Mary-Beth explains that the three have been stuck in camp for two weeks. When Arthur asks whether Miss Grimshaw can spare them, Karen mocks him for worrying about chores, and he gives in. On the wagon, the women sing a bawdy song at Uncle's request. If Arthur refuses to help a driver whose horses have broken loose, Karen asks Uncle and then Arthur why they did not help. Near Valentine, Tilly wonders whether they should have invited Molly O'Shea, and Karen answers that Molly is now too grand for them and for any real work. In town, the women split up to look for leads while Arthur and Uncle go to the general store.

Mary-Beth later tells Arthur that, as far as she knows, Karen and Tilly went to the hotel to pick up drunk men and rob them. After dealing with a man who is manhandling Tilly in the street, Arthur goes to the Saints Hotel. Karen is upstairs in room 2B, a number the hotel owner gives Arthur if he asks for her. She is shouting at a client who is hitting her and calling her a thief. Arthur breaks down the door and fights the man, whom he can knock out or strangle. Karen admits that she tried to play him and failed. She tells Arthur that the client was boasting about the bank: small-town banks are rarely worth it, she says, but Valentine is a livestock town with plenty of cash at times. Arthur tells her to keep investigating. Outside, she thanks him, while saying she does not like to be rescued. She tells Tilly that the man only punched her.

Back at camp, Molly confronts Karen over what Karen said about her (see Relationships). When Sean MacGuire is brought back from the bounty hunters in "The First Shall Be Last", the gang throws a party, during which Karen goes off with Sean (see Relationships). In "Who is Not Without Sin", if Arthur walks past her while carrying a drunk Reverend Swanson to his tent, she can remark that the reverend should learn to hold his liquor. The bank plan does not go ahead in this chapter. After the shootout of "The Sheep and the Goats" in Valentine, the gang has to leave Horseshoe Overlook for Clemens Point, on Flat Iron Lake in Lemoyne.

Chapter 3: Clemens Point

Karen has not given up on the Valentine bank, and at Clemens Point she brings Bill Williamson into the plan (see Relationships). In "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah", Bill calls Arthur over and says they have been preparing the job since Horseshoe Overlook, until Arthur's trouble in Valentine got in the way. When Bill complains that his mistakes get him called an idiot while Arthur's are put down to bad luck, Lenny Summers and Karen agree with him. Karen then asks Arthur outright to rob the bank with them. She explains that four people can take it if they move fast, that the law will come only if they linger, and that the end of the stock sales means plenty of cash. Arthur agrees to take a look, and Karen goes to change out of her everyday clothes.

On the ride, Bill sets out the plan: Karen goes in first as a distraction, and the men follow. Karen says she can play either a lost young girl or a drunken harlot, and that she saw only a couple of guards when she scouted the bank. In optional conversations, she suggests stopping for whiskey, which Arthur puts off until after the job, and asks him about the feud between the Grays and the Braithwaites, which Arthur says is the business of Dutch and Hosea Matthews. In another optional exchange, Lenny tells Arthur that he told the others they needed him along. In Valentine, the player picks her routine. As the lost girl, she sobs to the teller that a man said he loved her and would make a lady of her, that she is with child and that she does not want to go back to the workhouse. As the harlot, she plays drunk and accuses the teller of pretending not to know her.

Once the staff is distracted, Karen draws her gun and orders everyone to raise their hands, and Arthur, Bill and Lenny rush in. Lenny gives her the key, and she unlocks the door behind the counter. While Arthur forces the teller to open the vault, then cracks the safes or blows them with dynamite, Karen keeps watch on the hostages and checks the front of the bank. If lawmen gather outside, as they do when the safes are blown, she walks out first with her hands up, apologizing for a terrible mistake, then draws and opens fire, and the four shoot their way to the horses. If the safes were cracked quietly, she leads the way out. The gang shakes off the pursuing lawmen thanks to a passing train and splits the money. The mission page gives Arthur's reward as $2,500.

In "Blessed are the Peacemakers", the O'Driscolls capture Arthur during a supposed parley. He escapes and rides back to Clemens Point, where he collapses. Mary-Beth, Karen and Dutch are the first to find him, and Dutch calls for Miss Grimshaw and Reverend Swanson; Arthur is then carried to his tent. Sean is later shot dead in the Rhodes ambush of "A Short Walk in a Pretty Town"; Karen is not there. The Red Dead Wiki also lists her in "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern" and "The Battle of Shady Belle", whose mission pages give her no particular role. In the second, the Pinkerton agents Andrew Milton and Edgar Ross come to Clemens Point. Milton offers the others three days to disappear if Dutch gives himself up. The gang refuses and moves to Shady Belle, a plantation house near Saint Denis that Arthur and John Marston have cleared of Lemoyne Raiders.

Chapter 4: Saint Denis

At Shady Belle, Karen is often drunk in camp, which worries Tilly (see Relationships). When Jack Marston is brought back from Angelo Bronte's mansion in "Angelo Bronte, A Man of Honor", she is among those gathered at the campfire to welcome him, and she drinks heavily at the party that follows (see Relationships). In "Horsemen, Apocalypses", the O'Driscolls send Kieran Duffy's mutilated body into camp and then attack Shady Belle. While most of the members who do not fight hide in a room of the house, Karen fires on the attackers from the balcony at the back of the house, according to the mission page.

Chapter 5: Guarma

Karen is not among the members stranded on Guarma after the failed Saint Denis bank robbery. The Red Dead Wiki next lists her in "That's Murfree Country", at the end of the chapter, when the reunited gang settles in a cave at Beaver Hollow, in Roanoke Ridge. While the camp is being set up, Uncle arrives with a drunk Molly, who claims she informed the Pinkertons about the Saint Denis job. Susan kills her with a shotgun. Karen turns on Susan right after the shooting (see Relationships).

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

According to the Red Dead Wiki's page on her, Karen is drunk most of the time at Beaver Hollow and turns angrily on anyone who worries about her. In "A Rage Unleashed", she and Javier escort Eagle Flies, son of the Wapiti chief Rains Fall, into camp while Dutch is talking with Arthur. Eagle Flies reports that the Army has seized his people's horses, and Dutch decides on the spot to help him. After "My Last Boy", Karen is no longer at the camp, nor are Uncle, Simon Pearson and Mary-Beth. At the start of "Our Best Selves", Dutch tells Arthur that Uncle, Pearson and Mary-Beth have run off and calls them cowards. He does not mention Karen. Her departure is not shown on screen, and she takes no part in the train robbery or in the collapse of the gang that follows.

Epilogue (1907)

Epilogue 2: Beecher's Hope

Karen does not appear in the epilogue, and the game never shows what became of her. Her name comes up once. After "A Quick Favor for an Old Friend", John can meet Tilly, now married, in Saint Denis. A letter from Tilly to Abigail and John then waits on the dining table at Beecher's Hope. In it, Tilly writes that she misses Karen, that she never heard what happened to her, and that she believes drink killed her. This is Tilly's belief: the game confirms neither Karen's death nor her survival.

Personality

Rockstar Games' official description of Karen, published in September 2018 (see Marketing), reads: "A consummate scam artist and trigger woman who can drink pretty much anybody under the table. Bold and full of fun, she loves the outlaw life and wouldn't have it any other way."

A character page from an official guide, reproduced on the Red Dead Wiki, follows that description closely, adding of her drinking that she "does so most nights", and that "If a bank needs robbing, just say the word and she'll be the first on her horse." It also notes that "the debauchery is starting to take a toll on her after all these years". It ends: "She speaks her mind and takes no nonsense but is big-hearted and looks out for everyone, especially the other girls."

Sitting with Tilly and Mary-Beth at Horseshoe Overlook, she tells them that poor women have no choice but to get by as best they can in a world run by men. On guard duty she jokes that the girl men once had to rescue now keeps watch over them, rifle in hand. According to the Red Dead Wiki's camp events page, she starts to be seen drunk in camp during the Clemens Point chapter.

In her own words

Leaving the Saints Hotel in "Polite Society, Valentine Style", she tells Arthur: "Thank you, Arthur. I don't much like being saved, but... when I have to be." On the ride to Valentine in "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah", she teases Arthur about his drinking, and admits to her own: "You've always been a bad drunk... and, before you say it, I know... takes one to know one." The Red Dead Wiki opens its page on her with a line she says to Molly O'Shea on the dock at Shady Belle: "Listen, it's a hard life, this world, for anyone born without means. Any as judges those who find it hard, is a fool. I'm a lot of things, I'm not quite a fool."

Names

She is only ever called Karen Jones. In "Outlaws from the West", Susan Grimshaw addresses her as "Miss Jones" when she hands out the jobs at Colter, and Dutch calls her "Miss Karen" when he asks her and Tilly to look after Sadie Adler. Neither the games nor the guide give her a nickname or another name.

Appearance

The guide's portrait shows a woman with curly blonde hair, light eyes and freckles, in the outfit she wears for the Valentine bank robbery: a dark red dress with puffed sleeves, and a black top hat with a buckled band and feathers. In "Polite Society, Valentine Style", Arthur describes her to the owner of the Saints Hotel as "Mid twenties, blonde". At Colter her face is covered except for her eyes and nose, according to the Red Dead Wiki, which adds that her hair becomes more unkempt as her drinking gets worse and often lacks its ringlets late in the story.

Relationships

Tilly Jackson

Tilly is her closest friend in camp. Doing chores at Horseshoe Overlook, Tilly says that one day men will help with the work, and Karen laughs and tells her not to count on it. At Clemens Point, Tilly says she robbed a man but let a woman go because the woman needed the money for her son, who had fought in the Civil War. Karen calls her soft, since the son fought for the wrong side; if Arthur is nearby, he will not agree that Tilly has gone soft, and Karen calls him soft too. Tilly also tells Karen and Mary-Beth how Malcolm Foreman took her from her mother.

At Shady Belle, sewing in the gazebo, Karen complains about the swamp and Tilly answers that it beats freezing to death. Inside the manor, Tilly sees that something is wrong; Karen says the good times may not have been as good as she remembers, and Tilly tells her it will be all right. Tilly also tells Arthur she is worried about Karen's drinking and that talking to her will not work. In her 1907 letter (see Biography), Tilly calls Karen a woman "who was a sister to me".

Mary-Beth Gaskill

Mary-Beth shares the young women's chores and outings with Karen and Tilly. At Horseshoe Overlook the two agree that Valentine is not a romantic town, and Karen passes on a rumor of a curse laid on it by Native Americans after a massacre. Mary-Beth tells Tilly she knows that she and Karen make fun of her romance novels. At Clemens Point, Mary-Beth hopes women will one day vote; Karen says men will not allow it and asks Arthur his opinion, and he answers that voting was never his thing.

At Shady Belle they argue about Saint Denis: Karen says the city is built on dirt, Mary-Beth on memories, and Karen walks off. Karen later tells her she heard of someone in Saint Denis who drank blood, which Mary-Beth finds romantic. In another scene, after Mary-Beth says she stole a necklace from a mansion, Karen admits that the city scares her; Mary-Beth finds it all romantic, and Karen calls her mad. At the party for Jack's return, when Karen vomits by Pearson's wagon, Mary-Beth tries to get her to bed and Karen tells her to leave her alone.

Sean MacGuire

At Horseshoe Overlook, behind John's tent, Sean asks Karen whether she missed him. She first claims she cried every night, then says angrily that she missed Davey, Mac and Jenny more. When he tries to hold her she slaps him and threatens to stab him if he touches her again; he answers that he loves it when she talks nasty to him.

At the party for his return, according to the Red Dead Wiki, Sean tells her he loves her; she answers that he only loves her when he is drunk, and says she hates him. They go into John's tent and have sex. During the act she cries and says she wants someone to see her, and Sean answers that he does; she leaves the tent as soon as it is over. After his death in Rhodes, Karen tells Arthur at Shady Belle that camp is not the same without Sean, who was full of himself but whom she liked; Arthur tells her Sean had a soft spot for her. She and Susan sing "A Prisoner for Life" and dedicate it to him.

Susan Grimshaw

Karen is the young woman who answers Susan back the most. At Horseshoe Overlook she sits down beside Susan at a meal and recalls that, back in Colter, she had thought about eating her, going as far as describing the dishes she and Pearson had in mind, until Susan leaves the table. At Clemens Point, when Susan finds her drunk at the poker table, Karen replies that she is having a baby, says she will not be bullied, and tells Susan to keep quiet unless she is the one feeding her. While sewing with Tilly, she complains of Susan's nagging, then tells Susan she is learning alchemy.

The two still sing together at the poker table and the campfire (see Skills). Right after Susan shoots Molly O'Shea at the end of "That's Murfree Country", Karen lashes out at her. Susan points to the camp's rules, which Molly had broken; Karen rejects that and calls her a murderer.

Molly O'Shea

Karen mocks Molly on the way to Valentine (see Biography). Back at Horseshoe Overlook, Molly confronts her, says Karen is not as clever as she thinks and that Dutch loves her very much; Karen keeps her answers vague, and Molly ends up swearing and leaving in a rage. At Clemens Point Molly demands to know what Karen's problem with her is. The Red Dead Wiki's pages describe the blows differently: on the camp events page Molly slaps Karen, who punches her in the face; on Karen's page Karen slaps Molly when Molly grabs her arm. At Shady Belle, on the dock, Karen tells Molly that nobody is laughing at her (see In her own words); after Molly walks off, Arthur says she is losing it, and Karen answers that she lost it months ago.

Arthur Morgan

Arthur deals with the man who hits her at the Saints Hotel in "Polite Society, Valentine Style", and rides with her on the Valentine bank job (see Biography). At Horseshoe Overlook Karen can invite him to sit down and talk. Depending on the player's choices, he owns up to crimes that weigh on him and says he thinks he is letting his anger out; she warns him that it can only end well if he keeps his head. At Clemens Point, when Dutch dances with Susan at night, Arthur can dance with Karen.

At Shady Belle, and again at the party for Jack's return, Karen, who has been drinking, comes over to praise how manly Arthur is; when he tells her she has had too much, she says she has not.

Bill Williamson

At Clemens Point Bill asks Karen about her plan to rob the Valentine bank. She is willing to bring him in, as long as the job stays hers and he does not cut her out. Lenny and Arthur join them later (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, Bill asks Arthur whether Karen is sweet on him, and Arthur doubts it.

Lenny Summers

Lenny is the fourth member of the Valentine bank job (see Biography). On the ride there, when Arthur says he is glad she and Lenny came along, Karen answers that someone has to keep the boys out of trouble, and that she is not sure Lenny can keep his head after the night he and Arthur spent in Valentine.

Hosea Matthews

When Hosea comes back to camp at Clemens Point with a case of drinks after a big win at cards, Karen asks how he did it; he answers that he cheated, like a proper gentleman, and asks her to sing (see Skills). According to the Red Dead Wiki, in another camp scene Karen tells Mary-Beth, who fears the law, that she trusts Dutch and Hosea to protect the gang.

Dutch van der Linde

At Horseshoe Overlook Karen tells Arthur that the Blackwater ferry job shook her. What stays with her is the sight of the men coming back to camp soaked in blood, Dutch most of all, wearing an expression she had never seen on him.

Kieran Duffy

At Horseshoe Overlook Karen tells the bound Kieran she thought O'Driscolls ate babies but that he looks like one; when he asks her for water, she refuses and throws her cigarette at him. Once he is free, he stops by the poker table to tell her he is still alive; Karen asks Arthur to reassure him, Arthur warns him to watch himself instead, and Karen tells Kieran that the men only act tough. At the party for Jack's return she urges him to drink; he says he might have a little, then insists he will just watch.

Micah Bell

At Horseshoe Overlook Micah whistles at Karen as she works and asks her for a kiss; she leaves without answering. At Shady Belle she calls him Mr. Bell and will not use his first name, so that nobody thinks she likes him. Micah replies that whiskey is all she cares for, that he is the only one in camp who does not worry about her, and that she will get what she deserves.

John Marston

At Horseshoe Overlook John, after a few drinks, makes a pass at Karen; she points out that he has Abigail and leaves. If Arthur is close by, John asks him whether he is making a fool of himself, and Arthur says he is.

Abigail Marston

At Shady Belle, while Jack is missing, Abigail cries on John's bed and Karen sits with her, telling her that nobody is going to hurt the boy.

Uncle

Uncle rides with Karen, Tilly and Mary-Beth to Valentine in "Polite Society, Valentine Style". When Tilly says she can smell the sheep on the way into town, Karen asks: "Or is that Uncle?" On the road, after Arthur declines to help a stranded driver catch his horse, she asks Uncle why he did not help, and Uncle pleads lumbago. Uncle, Karen and Susan sing together at the poker table and the campfire (see Skills).

Simon Pearson

Pearson sings with Karen at the poker table, "Poor Lonesome Cowboy" and, with Uncle, "Otis Miller" (see Skills). According to Karen herself, at Colter the two of them could not agree on which dish to make of Susan (see Susan Grimshaw).

Josiah Trelawny

At Clemens Point Trelawny asks Karen about the book she is reading by the wagons, which she calls a silly romance. Susan cuts in to ask whether Karen means to do any work. Trelawny answers with a trick, producing a crow from his hand; Susan walks off, disgusted, while Karen laughs. After he plays a similar trick on Mary-Beth, Karen calls him a devil; Mary-Beth calls him a kind devil.

Orville Swanson

At Colter Swanson reads a passage of Isaiah aloud from his Bible. When he finishes, Karen asks him what it means; he says he does not know, but that the words comfort him.

Sadie Adler

Dutch puts Sadie in the care of Karen and Tilly when he brings her to Colter (see Biography). Later the two are the only women of the gang who stand guard (see Skills).

Javier Escuella

Javier plays the guitar on several of Karen's songs in camp (see Skills), and in chapter 6 the two escort Eagle Flies into Beaver Hollow (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, Karen says Javier was a close friend of Jenny Kirk, and the two talk about her in camp.

Davey and Mac Callander

The brothers are lost after the Blackwater robbery: Davey reaches Colter already dead of his wounds, and Mac is missing. Karen tells Sean she missed them more than him (see Sean MacGuire). According to the Red Dead Wiki, she also says that she used to yell at the noisy brothers to shut up all the time, and that she misses doing it now that they are gone.

Jenny Kirk

Jenny, a young member of the gang, is wounded in the Blackwater robbery and dies during the flight to Colter, according to the Red Dead Wiki. The wiki adds that Karen says in a camp conversation that she misses her and that Jenny died too young.

Skills, weapons and horses

Guard duty and camp work

Karen and Sadie Adler are the only women of the gang who take turns on guard duty, according to the Red Dead Wiki. In camp she does chores with Tilly and Mary-Beth, sews with Tilly, and reads romance novels by the wagons at Clemens Point.

Songs

She is one of the camp's regular singers, and Javier plays the guitar on several of her songs. The Red Dead Wiki's camp events page lists two songs she sings alone: "Lorena", by the fire at Horseshoe Overlook, and "Dance by the Light of the Moon", at Hosea's request at Clemens Point. With Susan, Uncle, Pearson or Sean, she sings "Come, Bustle, Bustle", "One Eyed Riley", "I Wouldn't Marry", "Poor Lonesome Cowboy" and "Otis Miller" at Horseshoe Overlook, and "The Louisville Maid" and "Wild Free Rover" at the party for Sean's return. At Shady Belle she sings "A Prisoner for Life", "The Sailor Boy" and "The Heartland Stage Company".

Weapons and horse

The Red Dead Wiki gives her a Cattleman revolver and a Carbine Repeater. In "A Rage Unleashed" she holds a double-barreled shotgun, without firing it, when Eagle Flies first comes to Beaver Hollow. Her horse is Old Belle, a smoky black Nokota mare according to the wiki's page on the horse. Karen rides her only once in the story, for the Valentine bank robbery; the rest of the time the mare is kept at camp, for as long as Karen is with the gang.

Development and performance

Casting

Karen is played by Jo Armeniox, an American actress born in 1983; according to the Red Dead Wiki, her birth name is Megan Armeniox and she was born in Greensboro, North Carolina. The wiki says she was cast as both the voice and the motion capture performer for Karen. On television she has appeared in episodes of series including Mercy, Boardwalk Empire, Blue Bloods, Body of Proof, Unforgettable, Chicago Med, Shades of Blue, Bull and City on a Hill.

Rockstar recorded the cast's performances with motion capture, with cameras filming the actors' faces for animation, according to Wikipedia's list of the game's characters.

Interviews

In a GamesRadar+ feature by Rachel Weber, published on March 7, 2019, in which the actresses of the gang look back on the shoot, Armeniox called her time with them "a beautiful emotional journey". She recalled scenes in which Karen and Arthur "got wasted together, proclaimed our best friend love for each other, were on a boat and blew up a building together", and said that, after so much work with the other women, "it was really rare also to have my own time with Roger [Clark, who plays Arthur] and that was really special." In the same piece Penny O'Brien, who plays Molly O'Shea, says that catching up with Armeniox in the green room was one of her best memories, before a scene in which Armeniox had to slap her in the face twice.

Speaking to Peter Roumeliotis of Popternative in a video interview released on November 12, 2019, Armeniox said, as summarized by Wikipedia, that she drew on an "angry, rebellious" side of herself for Karen and enjoyed expressing strong emotions she would not usually show in real life.

Kaili Vernoff, who plays Susan Grimshaw, told Josh Wilding of GameFragger in an interview published on February 21, 2019, that Susan and Karen "have such a funny dynamic", since Karen "is the one who will really mouth off to her". She said she and Armeniox had fun working out how far Karen could push Susan before Susan struck back. In an interview with Heather Newman of Forbes, published on February 12, 2019, Vernoff named Armeniox, Meeya Davis (Tilly) and Sam Strelitz (Mary-Beth) as the actresses she worked with most closely and said they still spoke "almost daily".

Recording and cut content

On the wagon ride of "Polite Society, Valentine Style", Karen sings "in Berryville" where the women's song has "across the lane", then says she messed it up. According to the Red Dead Wiki, this was a genuine slip during the recording session that was kept in the game.

The Red Dead Wiki lists unused assets for Karen in the game files: two torsos, a loose blouse, a hairstyle and knickers. The files also name a cut item request for "Karen's book". An unused camp encounter, set after she leaves the gang, has Susan nervously telling the camp's young women that Karen stormed off the night before after telling her to go to hell; the wiki notes that the scene's conditions may be impossible to meet, since Mary-Beth, who takes part in it, leaves at the same time as Karen. Concept art published by the wiki shows Karen with Bill, Tilly, Uncle, Mary-Beth, Strauss and Pearson, and she appears to be present, with Bill and Javier, in another concept piece of the gang.

Reception

Game Informer ranking

In Game Informer's staff ranking of the 24 members of the Van der Linde gang, published on December 27, 2018, Karen comes 14th. The introduction of the piece lists "lewd Karen" among the game's memorable characters. Her entry calls her "The lewdest, drunkest, most unapologetic member of the group" and compares her to Pam Poovey, a character of the animated series Archer. The staff wrote that, whether she is acting out a part to distract the tellers during a bank robbery or arguing drunkenly with the rest of the gang, "Karen steals every scene she's in", and closed on her disappearance: "Now if only we could figure out where the hell she went."

Other media and legacy

Marketing

Rockstar released official artwork of Karen for Red Dead Redemption 2. On September 6, 2018, it posted her portrait and character description on Twitter, in a series covering the members of the gang; The Hollywood Reporter reproduced the series on September 7, 2018. The same descriptions appear on a page of Rockstar's website presenting the Van der Linde gang; the French version, cited by the French Red Dead Wiki, was archived on July 6, 2022. In the composite image of the 23 gang members that opens Wikipedia's list of the game's characters, Karen stands in the front row, between Mary-Beth and Abigail.

Trivia
  • In "Polite Society, Valentine Style", no weapon can be used inside the Saints Hotel. Strangling is the only way for Arthur to kill the man who beats Karen, and her remark to Tilly afterwards changes depending on whether Arthur killed him or knocked him out. One of the Gold Medal objectives is to find Karen in the hotel within 45 seconds.
  • Attacking, killing or abandoning Karen fails both "Polite Society, Valentine Style" and "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah"; in the bank mission, letting her be killed also fails it.
  • While "Sodom? Back to Gomorrah" is available but not yet played, Karen takes part in no camp scenes, according to the Red Dead Wiki's Camp Events page.
  • She is one of the few members of the gang who never ask Arthur to bring back an item for them, according to the Red Dead Wiki.
