## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **Surname.** Wiki trivia: "His last name is never mentioned at all in either games." Fiche L11, L55: printed in the 1914 paper. **Fiche right**: Blackwater Ledger & Sentinel No. 213, "Treasure Found" (`fc/Treasure_Found_1914.txt`, image `fc/Blackwater_Ledger_Sentinel_No_213.png`): "Seth Briars", "Mr. Briars".
2. **1914 fate.** FR wiki (and the wiki "Newspapers" page): he returned to his family and went back to work in Blackwater. Fiche L43: the article says nothing about his family. **Fiche right** (same article).
3. **Benedict Point door.** Wiki: Seth knocks and Moses slams the door in his face. Transcript (`Exhuming..._dialogues.txt` l.123-129): Seth calls "Moses? You got a visitor", Moses bursts through the door and runs. **Fiche L33 right.**
4. **Fort Mercer gate.** Wiki: "The Williamson Gang then let in Nigel West Dickens' stage coach"; FR wiki: Seth "gives a signal". Transcript (`The_Assault_on_Fort_Mercer_dialogues.txt` l.49): Seth opens the gate ("Welcome to my humble abode!"); Dickens raps on the wagon as the signal (HOUSE_FACTS). **Fiche L39-40 right.**
5. **Williamson gang.** Wiki infobox "Williamson gang (formerly)", FR wiki "gave up gang life". Transcript (Exhuming): "Sometimes they call me on when they got some special job needs doin'" (present tense). **Fiche L26, L70 right.**
6. **Mask theft.** Wiki "a few months later"; fiche L47 "three months later". **Fiche right** (On a Pale Horse page: "three months later").
7. **Origin of the wagon bodies.** Character page: Odd Fellow's Rest; mission page: the mission starts at Coot's Chapel. Fiche L35 gives both with attribution. No change.
8. **Campfire glass-eye story.** Character page "gunned down", mission page "eaten by a ghost". Fiche L173 gives both. No change.
9. **Childhood.** EN wiki: father killed a piglet; FR wiki: his father abandoned him. Neither is in a transcript; fiche keeps the EN version, attributed (L26). Keep.
10. **GOTY guide vs game.** The guide (quoted L49) says his partner "died on him, with the map"; in the game Moses Forth is alive in 1911 (Exhuming). Optional: B may add one factual clause after the quote ("In the game, Moses Forth is alive and held at Benedict Point"), no comment.
11. **L83, UN revolver (fiche inaccuracy).** Fiche: "he pulls a revolver and fires at John if John attacks him, according to the mission page". The mission page says: if Seth survives a stick of dynamite or a fire bottle thrown into the party, he draws a revolver, shoots and flees; assaulting Seth otherwise fails the mission. **Reword L83** to match.
12. **Quote wording.** Wiki "'S a glass eye!", "I've only ever cut into a bonified flesh", "twisted little freak" vs transcripts "A glass eye! It's a glass eye!", "I wanna cut into a bonafide man's flesh! Ain't never cut into a live one before", "twistly little freak". Fiche follows the transcripts. No change.
