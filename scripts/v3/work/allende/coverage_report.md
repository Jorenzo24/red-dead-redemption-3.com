## 2. Places where the fiche differs from the wiki

1. **Allende "came from poverty" (Reyes, "The Great Mexican Train Robbery")**: the wiki cites a Reyes/John exchange that is absent from its
   own transcript (The_Great_Mexican_Train_Robbery_dialogues.txt; no "poverty" or "peasant who wants" anywhere in the mission cache).
   Fiche (A L26) is right to present it as the wiki's claim and to say the line is missing from the transcript. Keep.
2. **Land seized and sold to aristocrats (Luisa, "Must a Savior Die?")**: line absent from Must_a_Savior_Die_dialogues.txt (no
   "aristócratas"/"aristocrat"). Fiche (A L27) right to attribute and flag. Keep.
3. **Who ordered John's execution**: wiki says Allende ordered De Santa to kill him. Cowards_Die_Many_Times_dialogues.txt shows only De
   Santa's betrayal and his line "I am just glad Colonel Allende was able to offer you this gift". Fiche (A L38) hedge is right.
4. **Stagecoach escort**: wiki says "three guards". Mission page An_Appointed_Time.txt l.49 lists two riders behind the cart, the Gatling
   gunner and a guard riding shotgun, plus the driver. Fiche (A L42, B L89) is right.
5. **How the coach stops**: wiki says John kills the guards and the driver. Mission page l.49: "Either shoot all the horses ... or ... kill
   the driver". Fiche (driver or horses) is right.
6. **Who kills Allende**: wiki says John if he shoots, Reyes if the player hesitates. Fiche (A L43, HOUSE_FACTS l.133) is more precise:
   John can shoot either or both, Reyes shoots whoever is spared, both if John fires at neither. Consistent with HOUSE_FACTS.
7. **Portrait left in the villa "hinting that the rebels did not occupy it"**: the hint is interpretation; fiche (L87/L192) keeps the fact
   only. Right. (Fiche L195 notes Reyes in the mansion in non-canon Undead Nightmare, kept separate. Right.)
8. **Vomit at the end of the Mexican Caesar cutscene**: wiki mission page adds bile/hangover/heavy drinker interpretation; fiche (L186)
   keeps only the observable fact. Right.
9. **De Santa's insult**: transcript says "esa bruja" (that witch); fiche L32 says "the woman he brought him". Acceptable paraphrase, no fix.

No case where the wiki is right and the fiche wrong.
