A local man, Seth Briars, walked out of the mountains in Tall Trees and into Blackwater with a massive haul of gold
and precious stones that he had apparently spent years of his life pursuing. Mr. Briars, who was once a known
associate of Moses Forth, denies any connection to famous New Austin grave robbings by which Mr. Forth terrorized
the dead in that region three years ago.
