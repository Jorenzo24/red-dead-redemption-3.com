Nastas, real-world and extra sources gathered by the B/D drafter (22 Oct 2026)

1. Rockstar character card (Red Dead Wiki file "Nastas22.jpg", 280x280, uploaded 23 Jun 2010, gallery caption
   "Nastas' character description"; image saved here as rockstar_card_Nastas22.png, read on image):
   "NASTAS / A NATIVE AMERICAN UNSURE ABOUT THE JOYS OF LIFE ON A RESERVATION."
   Visible: red headband, turquoise earring, shoulder-length dark hair, pale jacket.

2. Wikipedia "Benjamin Byron Davis" (raw wikitext read 22 Oct 2026): videography row
   "Red Dead Redemption | Dutch van der Linde / Nastas" (2010). Lead: "Davis voiced and provided performance capture
   for multiple characters in various Rockstar titles" (general, not specific to Nastas). Born June 21, 1972.
   IMDb title tt1479962 characters page title (search result): "Benjamin Byron Davis as Dutch van der Linde, Nastas"
   (page itself not read).

3. Game Rant, Sean Mackey, "Red Dead Redemption 2 Voice Actor Also Played Character in Red Dead 1", 14 Jun 2020
   (WebFetch): a Reddit user pointed out the detail; "Benjamin Byron Davis also played Nastas the Native American
   informant who tries to help John capture Dutch in 2010's Red Dead Redemption." Called one of "many fun Easter eggs".
   No statement from Davis.

4. Game Informer, Jeff Cork, "Red Dead Redemption Crammed With Colorful Characters", 26 Feb 2010 (WebFetch):
   "Nastas could prove to be an interesting character if he's truly conflicted about his place in the world."
   Cork says Rockstar San Diego included "nearly every stock Western character", "from the shifty federal agent to the
   snake-oil salesman".

5. Wikipedia "Red Dead Redemption" (raw wikitext, 22 Oct 2026), Themes: "Elston recognized the Native American
   character Nastas as an example of Gerald Vizenor's "manifest manners", a falsification of the indigenous experience
   being told as truth." sfn Elston 2013 p. 149. Reference: M. Melissa Elston, "Allegorical Confrontation Meets Gaming
   System: Rhetoric and Trauma within Red Dead Redemption / Undead Nightmare", in Cynthia J. Miller and A. Bowdoin
   Van Riper (eds.), Undead in the West II: They Just Keep Coming, Scarecrow Press, 2013, pp. 142-158. Chapter itself
   NOT read: attribute through Wikipedia.

6. NYMGamer, Phill Alexander, "Red Dead Redemption and the Myth of the Noble Savage", 17 Apr 2014 (WebFetch):
   "a Native named Nastas, a sort of guide-meets-confidant who aids with a large portion of the game";
   "Nastas shows some classic Native American traits (he's generally quiet, he tends to not be combative, he has a deep
   tie to nature and the environment), but he is not presented to the gamer as a cartoon."
   MacDougal "has no sense that Nastas is intelligent and aware of his surroundings/knows English."

7. Blackwater Ledger No. 59 (available after "The Prodigal Son Returns (To Yale)"), Miscellany, as transcribed in
   reyes/fc/ireddead_newspapers.txt: "Nastas (Surname Unknown), of Indian descent, has passed away at Bear Claw Cabin
   in Tall Trees. May you find God, my dear friend. Prof. Harold MacDougal Esq." (The wiki Nastas page quotes
   "May you find God, dear friend": the transcription has "my dear friend".)

8. Red Dead Wiki "Red Dead Redemption Cut Content" (_m/): "A beta version of Nastas can be found in the files under
   "NativeAmericanContact", sharing the same belt and shirt. However, the high resolution textures for this version of
   him were deleted."

Checked, nothing usable: Twinfinite Feb 2019 Davis interview (403); Giant Bomb (403); xboxygen (403); UND CRC blog
"Native Dancer" (Ariann Rousu, 18 Oct 2022) does not name Nastas.
