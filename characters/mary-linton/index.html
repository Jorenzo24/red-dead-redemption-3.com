Character · Red Dead Redemption 2

Mary Linton

Arthur's former fiancée Widow Alive Played by Julie Jesneck

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 20, 2026

Mary Linton, born Mary Gillis, is a supporting character in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. She was engaged to Arthur Morgan when they were young, and the engagement ended years before the game. She later married Barry Linton, who has since died. In chapter 2 she writes to Arthur from Valentine and asks him to bring her brother Jamie back from the Chelonians, a cult. In chapter 4 she asks for his help with her father in Saint Denis. In chapter 6 her last letter returns the engagement ring Arthur gave her. During the end credits she is seen at Arthur's grave.

She is played by Julie Jesneck.

Key facts

  • She and Arthur were engaged when young; her family, her father above all, disapproved, and the engagement ended before 1899.
  • In "We Loved Once and True", she asks Arthur to bring her brother Jamie back from the Chelonians; the player can refuse.
  • In "Fatherhood and Other Dreams", in Saint Denis, Arthur recovers her late mother's brooch, sold by her father to a loan shark.
  • Her last letter returns Arthur's engagement ring; in 1907 John Marston proposes to Abigail with it.

Biography

Origins

The game gives no date or place of birth for Mary, and no age. Her father is Mr. Gillis, and her younger brother is Jamie Gillis. Her mother died before 1899; she had given Mary a brooch, which plays a part in chapter 4. As a young woman, Mary was engaged to Arthur Morgan, already a member of the Van der Linde gang. Arthur got on well with Jamie, whom he taught to ride. The Gillis family disapproved of the engagement, her father most of all.

The engagement ended before 1899, because Arthur would not leave the outlaw life. Mary then married a man named Barry Linton. He died of pneumonia, and Mary is a widow by 1899. Her family has fallen apart in the meantime. Jamie has joined the Chelonians, a religious cult. According to the Red Dead Wiki, her father has become distant and speaks abusively to his children. He drinks, gambles and pays for prostitutes, and he has begun selling the family's possessions.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)

Mary appears in Red Dead Redemption 2 through two optional mission strands given to Arthur, "We Loved Once and True" in chapter 2 and "Fatherhood and Other Dreams" in chapter 4, and through three letters. In each strand the player can accept or refuse to help her, and her second and third letters change with those choices.

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

In "We Loved Once and True I", Susan Grimshaw gives Arthur a letter from Mary at the Horseshoe Overlook camp. Mary writes that she has started the letter "a hundred times or more". She recalls that, the last time they spoke, she was leaving to get married and said they would not speak again. She has been in Valentine for "a couple of months" after some bad luck she does not explain. She saw "a couple of the girls" who rode with Arthur in town and heard of a man who sounded like him. She asks to see him and gives her address: a rented room at Chadwick Farm, north of Valentine.

According to the Red Dead Wiki, Arthur is surprised that she still writes to him after so many years apart, and the letter leaves him lost in thought. If he has read it before "Money Lending and Other Sins III" starts, Leopold Strauss asks him: "Are you okay?" Arthur answers: "Yes. I was miles away I'm afraid." Strauss then sends him after Thomas Downes. In "We Loved Once and True II", Arthur rides to Chadwick Farm. An older woman, Miss Chadwick, opens the door with a gun in hand, then goes inside to fetch Mary. Mary tells Arthur that her husband is dead. She asks him to find Jamie, who has joined the Chelonians in Cumberland Forest; she calls the cult members mad and fears for her brother's life.

Mary has to plead with him before he agrees to listen, and she tells him she thinks of him often (her words are quoted under Personality). The player chooses. If Arthur refuses, he tells her that, given their past, she should find someone else; according to the Red Dead Wiki, Mary says she understands, that she misses him and that he should take care of himself. If the player lets the timer run out without answering, Mary storms back inside saying he has not changed, and the third part is lost.

If Arthur agrees, "We Loved Once and True III" follows. He finds Jamie among the Chelonians and either talks his way past their leader or chokes him unconscious. Jamie runs, falls from his horse and is cut off by a passing train; he then puts a revolver to his own head, and Arthur shoots the gun out of his hand. Arthur brings him to Valentine station, where Jamie tells his sister he is coming home. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Mary thanks Arthur for his help. Before boarding the train with her brother, she tells him that he will never change. According to the wiki, Arthur then admits that she leaves him feeling both happy and foolish and that he cannot say no to her. He draws their initials in his journal, with a heart between them.

Chapter 4: Saint Denis

A second letter from Mary reaches Arthur in chapter 4. Its opening depends on chapter 2. If he helped, she thanks him for saving Jamie's life and says Jamie is thinking of going back to college, although he and "Daddy" are still arguing. If he refused, she writes that he was perhaps right and that the situation with Jamie has worked itself out. If he accepted but failed the mission, she writes that Jamie may have escaped the Chelonians on his own. In the two versions where he accepted, she asks why he could not "put down all those fantasies that shroud your judgement". Every version says that she has made a mess of her life and that she and her family are in another mess, which she says is not her fault. She is staying at the Hotel Grand in Saint Denis and begs him to come, ashamed to ask.

In "Fatherhood and Other Dreams", Arthur finds her at the hotel. She asks for help with her father. Arthur refuses at first; according to the Red Dead Wiki, he and Mr. Gillis dislike each other. Mary tries to defend her father and, according to the wiki, says he was not always like this. Arthur answers by listing what has become of her respectable family: a son in a cult, a father who drinks and gambles, and a daughter begging an outlaw for help. The wiki also has him call Mr. Gillis a bully and a coward. The player then decides. A refusal ends the strand there. If Arthur gives in, he takes her to the stables where her father is. While Mary goes inside, a young man sweeping the pavement tells Arthur to move on, and Arthur threatens him. Mr. Gillis comes out berating his daughter for coming after him and walks off; according to the Red Dead Wiki, he is surprised to see her and angered to see Arthur.

Mary and Arthur follow him and see him sell the brooch her mother gave her to a loan shark named Ashton; Gillis turns down the loan Ashton offers him. Mary confronts her father while Arthur goes after Ashton. He chases the coach on horseback, leaps aboard, throws the driver off and drives it into the Bayou Nwa swamp. There he can pay Ashton $100 for the brooch, beat him until he hands it over, or kill him. When Arthur comes back, Mary is sitting alone in the alley. Her father has gone, and she turns down Arthur's offer to look for him, saying she no longer cares where he went.

Arthur returns the brooch, and the two walk toward the trolley station. On the way, Mary asks him to take her to the theater, as friends; the player can accept or refuse. If he accepts, they buy tickets at the Théâtre Râleur and watch several acts, then walk on to the trolley stop. There Mary speaks of how her life has gone wrong, asks whether it is too late for the two of them and asks him to run away with her. Arthur says he wants to, but that he is a wanted man who would put her in danger, that people in the gang depend on him and that they would need money. He promises that this life is coming to an end and that he will come once his business is settled. The two part, and they do not meet again.

Chapter 5: Guarma

After the gang returns from Guarma, in "A Fork in the Road", a doctor in Saint Denis tells Arthur he has tuberculosis. Leaving the surgery, Arthur hears lines from earlier in the story, chosen by his honor. With high honor, they include Mary telling him she thinks of him often; with low honor, her plea not to make her beg (see Personality).

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

At the end of "Goodbye, Dear Friend", the mission in which Colm O'Driscoll is hanged in Saint Denis, Tilly Jackson hands Arthur a last letter from Mary at Beaver Hollow. The letter comes with the ring Arthur gave her when they were young and a photograph of the two of them. Its opening depends on chapter 4. If Arthur helped her, she writes that he never showed up. If he ignored her letter from Saint Denis, she calls it foolish to have asked for his help again and promises it will not happen again. If he refused to help with her father, she apologizes for asking. In every version she has read about him in the newspapers, and she does not expect the letter to reach him.

Mary writes that she will always miss him, but that she cannot live his way and he cannot live any other. With him, she writes, the world makes sense; apart from him, she sees that no one can escape his world. She apologizes "for everything, for everything long ago", and for starting it all again. She writes that there is a good man within him, but that he is fighting a giant who keeps winning (both sentences are quoted under Quotes). In the versions where Arthur helped her or refused to help with her father, she adds that he has broken her heart again, that she fears she has broken his, and that he must let her go now. She returns the ring because she cares for it too much and it reminds her too much of him. She hopes he will find "some people in love who can use this", and that by returning it she "can finally be free". She signs off with "Goodbye".

Mary comes up twice more before Arthur's death. If the player has completed the optional mission "Of Men and Angels" and Arthur's honor is high, Arthur meets Sister Calderón at Emerald Station at the end of "The Fine Art of Conversation". According to the Red Dead Wiki, he tells her about a girl who loved him and says he regrets not running away with her. In the final mission, "Red Dead Redemption", lines from the story come back to Arthur as he rides toward the camp. With high honor, one of them is Mary's sentence about the good man fighting a giant; with low honor, it is her sentence about his world being one no one can escape. Later in the mission, in both endings, Arthur gives John Marston his hat and his satchel.

Epilogue (1907) and end credits

Epilogue 2: Beecher's Hope

Mary does not appear in the 1907 epilogue, but her ring does: by then John has the ring and the photograph. In "A Really Big Bastard", John tells Sadie Adler that he means to propose to Abigail with the ring Arthur once gave Mary. According to the mission page of "A New Future Imagined", John is holding the photograph of the young Arthur and Mary when he takes the ring at the start of the mission. He proposes to Abigail on the lake at Blackwater that day, and she accepts.

End credits

Mary is seen one last time in the end credits, which play after the last epilogue mission. Dressed in black, she stands by Arthur's grave and wipes away a tear without a word. The grave is covered in flowers if Arthur ended chapter 6 with high honor, and bare if his honor was low. The game does not show how she learned of his death, gives no date for the scene and says nothing of her later life.

Personality

Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the members of the Van der Linde gang, and Mary is not among them.

In her own words

At Chadwick Farm, when Arthur is slow to hear her out, she tells him: "Don't make me beg you, Arthur." When she asks him to find her brother, she says: "I understand if you don't wanna help me, but... but... I think of you often." Before boarding the train with Jamie at the end of "We Loved Once and True III", she tells him: "I've... You're... Oh, you'll never change. I know that."

While they follow her father through Saint Denis in "Fatherhood and Other Dreams", she comments on Arthur's looks, according to the mission page. If he is above his ideal weight, she says he looks well and recalls how thin he used to be. Otherwise she tells him he looks dirty, however clean he is.

Names

She was born Mary Gillis, the name of her father and of her brother Jamie. Linton is her married name, taken from her husband, Barry Linton. She signs her first letter "Mary Linton" and the later ones "Mary", which is also what Arthur calls her.

Appearance

Her hair is long and dark brown and her eyes are brown; a beauty mark sits on her right cheek. At Chadwick Farm her hair is braided low; afterwards she wears it gathered in a low bun. At Arthur's grave, in the end credits, she is dressed for mourning, in a black hat.

Relationships

Arthur Morgan

Arthur and Mary were engaged when they were young; the engagement ended before 1899. In 1899 she asks him for help twice, first with her brother and then with her father, and her last letter returns the ring he gave her (see Biography).

Mr. Gillis

Mr. Gillis is Mary's father; his decline and his sale of her late mother's brooch in Saint Denis are told in the Biography. The Red Dead Wiki credits Robert Newman as his voice.

Jamie Gillis

Her younger brother has joined the Chelonians, a cult, when Mary asks Arthur to bring him back (see Biography). Her second letter gives news of him (see Biography). The Red Dead Wiki credits Adrian Enscoe as his voice.

Barry Linton

Mary married him after her engagement to Arthur ended. He died of pneumonia before 1899 (see Biography).

Mrs. Gillis

Her mother is dead by 1899 and is never named in the game. She gave Mary the brooch that Mr. Gillis sells in Saint Denis (see Biography).

Susan Grimshaw

Susan tells Arthur that a letter from Mary has arrived (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, she says she never liked Mary, which annoys Arthur.

Tilly Jackson

Tilly hands Arthur Mary's last letter at Beaver Hollow, at the end of "Goodbye, Dear Friend" (see Biography). According to the Red Dead Wiki, she tells him Mary is not worth it.

Mary-Beth Gaskill

At the start of "No, No and Thrice, No", Arthur can bring Mary up, and Mary-Beth tells him that Mary was a fool then (see Quotes). According to the Red Dead Wiki, Mary-Beth also mentions Mary in private conversations with Arthur at Horseshoe Overlook and Beaver Hollow.

Karen Jones

According to the Red Dead Wiki, Karen asks Arthur whether he will get back together with Mary, and he answers that it is too late.

Abigail Marston

According to the Red Dead Wiki, Abigail, unlike Susan, Tilly and Mary-Beth, says she always liked Mary. A note on the wiki's Arthur Morgan page also has her remember Arthur and Mary playing dominoes together. In 1907 John proposes to her with the ring Arthur once gave Mary (see Biography).

John Marston

According to the Red Dead Wiki, John brings Mary up once while he and Arthur argue about their lives in "The Sheep and the Goats".

Leopold Strauss

Strauss notices that Arthur is lost in thought after reading Mary's first letter, at the start of "Money Lending and Other Sins III" (see Biography).

Miss Chadwick

In 1899 Mary rents a room at Chadwick Farm, near Valentine, where Miss Chadwick receives Arthur at the door (see Biography).

Ashton

This Saint Denis loan shark buys the brooch of Mary's mother from Mr. Gillis, and Arthur takes it back from him (see Biography).

Sister Calderón

Arthur speaks of Mary to her at Emerald Station, in "The Fine Art of Conversation" (see Biography).

Missions

She gives Arthur two optional mission strands, both missable: "We Loved Once and True" in chapter 2 and "Fatherhood and Other Dreams" in chapter 4. Completing them counts toward the honor story missions needed for the Lending a Hand trophy. Outside those missions she is heard through her letters and the lines Arthur remembers, and seen once more in the end credits.

Development and performance

Casting

Mary is played by Julie Jesneck, who is credited in the role by the Red Dead Wiki and by Wikipedia's list of the game's characters.

Mary's last scene, her visit to Arthur's grave during the end credits, has no dialogue. Roger Clark, who plays Arthur, told IGN that Jesneck was not available for that scene and that he performed Mary's movements in motion capture himself; a scene without dialogue, he explained, can be played by another actor (reported by Juan Sanmartín in Vida Extra on November 26, 2024).

Cut content

The game files keep traces of an unused meeting between Arthur and Mary at the saloon in Rhodes, where she has rented a room, according to the Red Dead Wiki's page on cut content. Its text and animations are filed under the labels "MRY2" and "FLME". An unused letter left in the files has Mary ask Arthur to help her in Rhodes, and the leftover text has her say that the people she is hiding from may kill her if he does not. According to the wiki, a character model named "rflme_debt_man" suggests that those men were after a debt. The files also hold an unused Chelonian intended for "We Loved Once and True" and two unused hats for Mary.

Two cut characters wear clothes identical or close to hers. The niece of Leon Fuentes, who was to be rescued on Guarma by Arthur and Bill, wears the same dress model and textures as Mary and a funeral hat almost identical to hers, with a veil added. Mary's funeral outfit in turn uses the niece's hairstyle. A character labeled "Gunslinger's Widow" has hair, boots and a funeral hat similar to Mary's. In the files, her father is named "Mr. Linton" instead of Mr. Gillis, and her brother Jamie is also referred to as a Linton.

Other media and legacy

Mary Linton exists only in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). She does not appear in Red Dead Redemption (2010), set in 1911, and the Red Dead Wiki lists her in no other game, Red Dead Online included.

Trivia
  • During the meeting at Chadwick Farm, Arthur takes off his hat and holds it if he is wearing one, and puts it back on when the conversation ends.
  • The gold medal objectives of "We Loved Once and True III" are to persuade the Chelonians to let Jamie go without violence and to bring him back to Mary within two minutes.
  • Paying Ashton the $100 he asks for Mary's brooch raises Arthur's honor; beating him until he hands it over brings none, and killing him lowers it.
  • Taking Mary to the theater is one of the gold medal objectives of "Fatherhood and Other Dreams", along with catching Ashton's wagon and driving it to the secluded spot within 1 minute 45 seconds.
  • The three letters from Mary and the "Young Mary & Arthur Photograph" are listed among the documents of Red Dead Redemption 2: the first can be taken from Arthur's tent in chapter 2, the second from his room in chapter 4.
