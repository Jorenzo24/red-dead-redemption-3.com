'''Wild West Heroes''' is a series of western dime novels. Issue No. 132, '''Mexican Ballad of Blood''', centers around [[Jim "Boy" Calloway]] and stars [[Landon Ricketts]].

== Full Text ==
CHAPTER VII

''Jim Calloway had been a rancher and a scout in the Army, but still he knew the risks of traveling through this ravine at night. If captured, banditos knew him to be the Deputy Sheriff, and torture would be his fate. He reloaded his rifle and revolvers, felt for his knife, and with caution snuck along the ridge in the moonlight. There would be, without question, Mexican sentinels posted and on the lookout, but Jim was not scared.''

''Jim was a hero. Women loved him and he shot men in the face, not the back. Death did not frighten him, only dishonor did. And not a bad guy alive would stop him. The banditos had sealed off all roads leading to Fort Ballard, and provisions had stolen en route. A message had managed to make it out well on 35 days ago, and the news was grim. The fort had been overrun by an outlaw gang who had taken the residents hostage.''

''Many inside the fort were sick and starving. But that didn't explain the presence of a ragtag bunch of ex-Mexican Army soldiers surrounding the place. Jim aimed to find out.''

''In the moonlight he could see the circle of banditos surrounding the fort, and along the base of the hills leading up to it were cook fires where more Mexicans were camping. Two hundred ponies were seen in the valley feeding. He waited with stolid patience for night to deepen, and then was on the move.''

''With the caution of a panther, he made a steep descent into the valley. Two sentries were on either side of him, silent as ghosts. He slowly snuck through, then saw what looked to be their leader, for he was dressed as a Mexican General and standing not twenty paces distant.''

''The General stopped and listened. Jim Calloway did not want to spill blood, but he had no choice. He had to get to that fort.''

''He slowly moved on the General, preparing his knife, ready to grab his throat and drive the knife deep into his heart. Suddenly white hot pain exploded through his hand as a gunshot rang out, his knife having been shot out of his grasp. His feet were kicked out from under him, and a Schofield Revolver was pointed in his face.''

''"Jim Boy Calloway. What are you wearing that Deputy badge?" the man holding the revolver scoffed.''

''You keep jumping from one side of the fence to the other, you might just get impaled on it.''

''"I could say the same thing about you, Landon Ricketts," Calloway scoffed.''

''"I heard you and your brother opened up a brothel. What makes a buffalo hunter, saloon keeper, gold miner and now brothel owner put on a badge?" Ricketts said.''

''"Clanton Fitch is holed up in that fort. He killed my brother in [[Blackwater]], and I aim to kill him.''

''Damn you, Ricketts, don't get in my way!" Calloway replied.''

''"That man you nearly cut open is General Emiliano Salgando. He hates Fitch as much as you do," Ricketts said. "They killed his wife, and have his daughter prisoner in the fort. That walking beer keg Colonel Baldwin damn near let the outlaws walk in the front door, he and his men were so drunk. Rumor is though they're getting desperate inside since supplies have been cut off."''

''A cry rang out like a trumpet from one of the banditos posted as a lookout. The front doors of the fort had opened up and a raiding party of dozens of men were headed directly for them.''

''"You still the fastest left handed draw or have gone soft?" Ricketts said.''

''"You didn't hear 'bout them fourteen men I killed at Lewsey Hollow?" replied Calloway.''

''"I heard you shot some of them in the back," Ricketts responded.''

''"They deserved it," Calloway said.''

''Ricketts grinned.''

''The renegades were riding hard in their direction and the Mexicans had steadied their rifles in anticipation. Calloway smiled and drew his rifle. He took aim down the sight and casually pulled the trigger. A man far off in the distance fell off his horse.''

''The air filled with blood and lead, and soon every man was fighting for his life. Landon Ricketts suddenly let out a yell, saying''
