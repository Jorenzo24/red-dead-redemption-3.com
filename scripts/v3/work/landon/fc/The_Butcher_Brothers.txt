}}'''The Butcher Brothers''' are minor characters mentioned in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]'' and ''[[Red Dead Redemption 2]]''.

==History==
===Background===
The Butcher Brothers renowned gunslingers and outlaws of the Old West. In 1896, both of them were shot and killed by [[Landon Ricketts]]<ref>[[Landon Ricketts Rumored Alive]]</ref>. The circumstances of their deaths aside from this are unknown.

===Events of ''Red Dead Redemption 2''===
The player can find a cigarette card depicting the Butcher Brothers, as part of the [[Famous Gunslingers Card Set]]. They are mentioned in the [[Newspapers/Red Dead Redemption 2|newspaper]] articles "Cigarette Cards Give Fame To Murderers" and "Mongrel Gang Terrorizing Area".<ref>[[Cigarette Cards Give Fame to Murderers]]</ref><ref>[[Mongrel Gang Terrorizing Area]]</ref>
