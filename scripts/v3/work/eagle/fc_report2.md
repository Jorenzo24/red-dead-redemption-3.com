## Problems

1. **WRONG**: Arthur Morgan: "On the way to rescue Abigail in "Our Best Selves", Arthur names him to Sadie...". Source: gsw l. 13457 places the ride with Sadie under "Red Dead Redemption (Get the Money)", line at l. 13489. _m/Red_Dead_Redemption_mission_dialogues.txt (Sadie ride) and the Red Dead Redemption mission page (ride to Van Horn) agree. The fiche's own mentions table and quote caption also say "Red Dead Redemption". Fix: "On the way to rescue Abigail in "Red Dead Redemption", ...".

2. **IMPRECISE**: Paytah: "He waits with the canoes for the horse raid and boards the boat with him". Missions table, A Rage Unleashed: "takes part in the night raid on the Army boat with Dutch, Arthur, Charles and Paytah". Source: gsw l. 12250-12257 has Paytah say "Good luck." as the canoes reach the steamboat. On board, only Dutch, Arthur, Charles and "boys" act. The mission page does not put Paytah aboard, and the EN wiki says "the four men ... climb aboard" (Dutch, Arthur, Charles, Eagle Flies). Fix (Paytah): "He waits with the canoes near Van Horn for the horse raid." Fix (table): "...the night raid on the Army boat with Dutch, Arthur and Charles; Paytah brings the canoes."

3. **UNSUPPORTED**: Karen Jones: "she holds a double-barreled shotgun, without firing it, when he arrives." I looked in the A Rage Unleashed mission page (it says only "escorted into camp by Karen and Javier"), the transcript l. 12195-12230 (no stage note), en.txt/fr.txt and the parts. Fix: cut the clause and keep "Karen and Javier escort Eagle Flies into Beaver Hollow in "A Rage Unleashed" (see Biography)." If it comes from a gallery or screenshot, Joseph should confirm it.

4. **IMPRECISE**: Rains Fall: "In the canoe after Fort Wallace, Eagle Flies says that there will be more trouble and that even his father may have to fight." Source: gsw l. 13084-13090 has the line after "Let's get over to the shore... I think we lost 'em", so it is on the bank, not in the canoe. Fix: "On the riverbank after Fort Wallace, ...".

5. **IMPRECISE**: Rains Fall: "and he fears he is a great disappointment to him." Source: gsw l. 12319 has a flat statement, "I'm… I'm a great disappointment to my son." Fix: "and that he is a great disappointment to his son." Also see Problem 12 (duplicate of the Biography).
