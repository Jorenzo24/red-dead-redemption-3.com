''[[Arthur Morgan|Arthur]] enters [[Valentine Sheriff's Office]] in [[Valentine]], [[New Hanover]]. The [[Curtis Malloy|Sheriff]] and a lawman are sitting down inside.'' 

'''Sheriff:''' Well... looky here. Maybe this is our man.

'''Lawman:''' You a bounty hunter, mister?

'''Arthur:''' Maybe... it depends.

'''Sheriff:''' Well, I'm guessing you ain't here to pass the day... turn yourself in... or discuss the finer points of country law. Then I don't need to know much more than that... till I got a reason to.

'''Arthur:''' Alright...

'''Sheriff:''' You smell that? This here's a livestock town, mister... attracts delinquents and reprobates like flies. Now I'm not a feller to pass a quick judgment... but I've been around long enough to know... you don't hire a saint to catch a sinner. You bring me what I need... and I'll pay you well... and I won't ask no questions.

'''Arthur:''' So... what is it you need?

'''Sheriff:''' Why don't you have a look at that poster... on the wall over there.

'''Lawman:''' [[Benedict Allbright|He]]'s a low down huckster. He's been poisoning folks with his miracle cure... from here to [[Annesburg]]. Killed more than [[Landon Ricketts]] without even pulling a trigger. Get some kind of sick satisfaction out of it.

'''Sheriff:''' The feller over at the saloon says... he thought he saw him by the gorge... straight north of here. You think you can bring him in? The money's good. I need him alive though. I wanna make sure the women he widowed... get compensated before he swings.

'''Arthur:''' I'll see what I can do.

'''Sheriff:''' Well good luck to you.

''Arthur leaves the Sheriff's office.''

'''Sheriff:''' And we need him alive... that's an important point.

'''Arthur:''' Alive, I got it.

''Arthur rides to the last known location of the bounty near the [[Dakota River]]. He finds him sitting by a campfire.''

'''Arthur:''' What we got here? Are you Benedict Allbright?

'''Benedict:''' No, sir...

'''Arthur:''' You kinda look like him... and I was told he'd be up here.

'''Benedict:''' No... not me sir.

'''Arthur:''' It's because... I wanna buy some medicine... and... I heard... I heard good things. I'll pay... in gold... i-if you can help me find him. It's just... my mother's real sick.

'''Benedict''': Oh, well if that's the case... if it's for a sick woman... I'd be happy to help. I'm a healer you know... a medical man. Finest medicine in the state.

''Benedicts gives a bottle of medicine to Arthur, who throws it away and points his gun at him.''

'''Arthur:''' Game's over, mister. Put your hands up... I'm taking you in.

'''Benedict:''' Taking me in? What for?

'''Arthur:''' Apparently that stuff you're pushing is killing folk... and there's a price on your head. I don't know, it ain't my business.

'''Benedict:''' Come on, partner, that's crap. I'm a healer. I've got an aura... I speak to spirits... I'm a scientist. Folks get real angry for no good reason... this... this is a mistake.

'''Arthur:''' Keep your hands up buddy... they only want you for questioning. 

'''Benedict:''' I have to insist that this is a mistake.

'''Arthur:''' Don't be a fool. 

''Benedict steps back and falls from the cliff but Arthur holds him back by his collar.''

'''Arthur:''' Hold on.

'''Benedict:''' I'm slipping. Shit.

''Arthur pulls him up.'' 

'''Benedict:''' You saved me...

'''Arthur:''' Now, come on, buddy, let's make this easy.

'''Benedict:''' You're taking me in.

'''Arthur:''' It's just for questioning.

'''Benedict:''' You saved me life.

'''Arthur:''' Ain't we had enough drama?

'''Benedict:''' I-I'd be better off jumping.

''Benedict rushes to jump but Arthur stops him.''

'''Arthur:''' They expressly told me not to kill you.

'''Benedict:''' This is about you?

'''Arthur:''' Don't jump...`

'''Benedict:''' O-or what? You can't shoot me.

'''Arthur:''' Please.

'''Benedict:''' I'll take my chances.

'''Arthur:''' Come here.

'''Benedict:''' Bye, friend.

''Benedict jumps off the cliff and ends up in the river, carried away by the currant.''

'''Arthur:''' I really do not like you.

''Arthur jumps on his horse and go after Benedict.''

'''Benedict:''' I shouldn't have done that. Help me!

'''Arthur:''' Shit. I-I gotta cross. Swim to the edge. Or... grab onto something. Don't you dare drown! Look out, the rapids. Hold your breath.

'''Benedict:''' Oh no!

'''Arthur:''' Get over to the bank.

'''Benedict:''' Help! Help... help!

''Arthur drags Benedict out of the water using his lasso.''

'''Arthur:''' Come here... come here.

'''Benedict:''' Who made you God, friend? Who made you judge?

'''Arthur:''' I'm only in for the money.

'''Benedict:''' That's even worse.

'''Arthur:''' I'm sure it does seem that way.

''Arthur hogties Benedict and knocks him out.''

'''Arthur:''' Alright, let's take you in.

''Arthur picks up Benedict and stows him onto his horse, he then mounts and starts riding back to the Sheriff's office.''

'''Benedict:''' Sir... I think I must be suffering from exposure.

''Arthur hit him to silences him.''

'''Arthur:''' Suffer a little longer.

'''Benedict:''' I seem to keep blacking out...

Arthur hit him to silences him.

'''Arthur''': You do to seem blacking out.

''Arthur reaches the Sheriff's office in Valentine. He dismounts his horse, picks up Benedict, and starts bringing him over to the office. Arthur enters the office, where the sheriff is speaking with a woman.''

'''Sheriff:''' Oh, my lord... I wasn't expecting you back so soon. Uh, Mrs. Calthorpe was, uh... was just leaving.

''Arthur drops Benedict down on the floor in front of the cells. He unties his bounds.''

'''Benedict:''' There's been some terrible mistake. I never did it... any of it.

'''Arthur:''' I thought you to shut up, partner.

''Arthur helps Benedict to get up.''

'''Benedict:''' But I told...

'''Arthur:''' But nothing. Shut up... and err... good luck.

''Arthur opens the door of the cell and shoves Benedict into it before closing the door.''

'''Arthur:''' It's been real fun.

'''Benedict:''' You ain't much of man. If you ask folks for forgiveness you remember this, partner.

'''Arthur:''' I've already forgotten it. Now, Sheriff... how much was you offering?

'''Sheriff:''' Fifty dollars... here. 

''The Sheriff hands Arthur some bills.''

'''Arthur:''' Thank you. Madam.

''Arthur shoves the dollar in his satchel while leaving the office.''
