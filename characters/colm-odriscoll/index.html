Character · O'Driscoll Boys

Colm O'Driscoll

Died 1899 O'Driscoll Boys Dutch's rival Played by Andrew Berg

By Joseph Lambert · Updated October 20, 2026

Colm O'Driscoll is the founder and leader of the O'Driscoll Boys, the outlaw gang that has been at war with the Van der Linde gang for years. He is an antagonist of Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018), set in 1899. His feud with Dutch van der Linde goes back to two killings: Dutch killed Colm's brother, and Colm killed Annabelle, the woman Dutch loved. In the Colter chapter the Van der Linde gang raids his camp and steals the train robbery he was planning. In chapter 3 he lures Dutch to a parley and has Arthur Morgan captured, as bait for a trap meant to deliver the whole gang to the law. Arrested later, he is hanged in Saint Denis in chapter 6, under the eyes of Dutch, Arthur and Sadie Adler.

He is played by Andrew Berg.

Key facts

  • He founded the O'Driscoll Boys, around 1889 by the Red Dead Wiki's reckoning, and leads them across several Western states. According to the same wiki, he twice escaped the noose before 1899.
  • In "Old Friends", Dutch and Arthur watch him strike Kieran Duffy and leave his camp at Ewing Basin, just before the Van der Linde gang wipes it out and takes his plan to rob a train.
  • In "Blessed are the Peacemakers", he meets Dutch for a parley while his men capture Arthur; at Lone Mule Stead he tells Arthur he plans to hand the whole gang to the law, but Arthur escapes.
  • Arrested and sentenced to death, he is hanged in Saint Denis in "Goodbye, Dear Friend" after Dutch, Arthur and Sadie foil his men's rescue attempt.

Biography

Origins

The game gives no birth date or birthplace for Colm O'Driscoll. The Red Dead Wiki describes him as an American of Irish descent. According to the same wiki, he and Dutch van der Linde were at first loosely partnered, before each man led his own gang: the O'Driscoll Boys for Colm, the Van der Linde gang for Dutch. In 1899, the newspaper article "O'Driscoll Captured" says that his gang has been active across numerous Western states for nearly ten years. From that figure, the Red Dead Wiki dates the founding of the gang to around 1889. The wiki also states that he was convicted of murder twice and got away both times before he could be hanged.

The feud between the two gangs goes back to two killings: Colm's brother, killed by Dutch, and Annabelle, a woman Dutch loved, killed by Colm. Dutch and the Red Dead Wiki give the two deaths in opposite orders (see Relationships, Dutch van der Linde). By 1899 the two gangs had been fighting over scores for years, as Arthur Morgan explains to Charles Smith in "The Aftermath of Genesis". He describes the O'Driscolls as a big gang and says that Colm and Dutch have known each other a long time, on bad terms. He adds that the Van der Linde gang had barely run into them over the previous six months.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (1899)

Chapter 1: Colter

After the failed ferry robbery in Blackwater, the Van der Linde gang hides in the snow of the Grizzlies, where Colm's men are also active. In "Outlaws from the West", Dutch, Arthur and Micah Bell find O'Driscolls occupying the Adler ranch, where they have killed the husband of Sadie Adler. One of them recognizes Dutch and shouts to the others: "Colm is going to shit his pants!" A shootout follows. Searching the house, Arthur remarks that the bounty on Colm is almost as high as the one on Dutch. Dutch replies that he and Uncle Sam agree on hardly anything except wanting Colm dead. An O'Driscoll caught in the barn tells Arthur that Colm is with the others at an old mining camp to the southwest, near a lake. He adds that they are preparing to rob a train by blowing up the tracks.

In "Old Friends", Dutch breaks up a fight between Micah and Bill Williamson, telling them that Colm is the one who needs hitting. He then leads Arthur, Micah, Bill, Javier Escuella and Lenny Summers to the O'Driscoll camp at Ewing Basin, talking with Arthur about the feud on the way (see Relationships, Dutch van der Linde). From a ridge, Dutch and Arthur watch Colm through binoculars. He calls a young man over, mounts his horse and strikes him. The young man rides away, and Colm's party leaves soon after. Dutch decides that Colm can wait, saying it would be much less fun to rob him and his score if he never found out about it.

The gang kills the men left in the camp, then the reinforcements that come out of the woods. Micah finds the plans for the train robbery, and Arthur finds dynamite. On the way back, Arthur captures the young man Colm had struck, Kieran Duffy, who is brought to Colter as a prisoner. In "Who the Hell is Leviticus Cornwall?", the gang puts to use what Dutch calls the information kindly provided by the O'Driscolls. It stops the private train of Leviticus Cornwall in the Grizzlies and takes the railroad bonds on board. Once the guards are dead, Lenny remarks that he now sees why the O'Driscolls had brought so many men for this job.

Chapter 2: Horseshoe Overlook

In "Paying a Social Call", Kieran, tied up at the Horseshoe Overlook camp for close to a month, is threatened with gelding. He names Six Point Cabin, in Cumberland Forest, as the place where Colm is holed up. Arthur rides there with Bill and John Marston, with Kieran as their guide. According to the Red Dead Wiki's mission page, if the player talks to neither man on the way, John brings up the murder of Annabelle and calls Colm a woman-killer in front of Kieran. The three men kill the O'Driscolls at the hideout, but Colm is not there. Kieran then saves Arthur's life and is allowed to stay with the gang.

Colm's men cross the gang's path twice more in this chapter. In "Blessed are the Meek?", Micah, jailed in Strawberry, shoots his O'Driscoll cellmate as he breaks out. In "An American Pastoral Scene", a large band of O'Driscolls attacks Arthur and Micah near the Dakota River to take the banking coach the two men have just stolen.

Chapter 3: Clemens Point

At Clemens Point, in "Blessed are the Peacemakers", Simon Pearson reports that a couple of O'Driscolls he met on the road into town have suggested a parley to end the feud. Hosea Matthews is against it. Micah grants that it is probably a trap but asks what they have to lose. Dutch agrees to go with Micah, while Arthur covers them with a rifle from higher ground. At the meeting point, Colm mocks Dutch's leadership and asks about the score the gang stole from him. He then says that he has been offered a price to bring Dutch in, and that he might still take it. Their exchange is quoted under Personality.

While the two men talk, O'Driscolls creep up on Arthur, knock him out and carry him off. He wakes up wounded at Lone Mule Stead, in Big Valley, where the O'Driscolls hold him prisoner and torture him. One guard thinks that Colm has gone into town with a man named Patrick to speak to the law. Another says that Colm is right and that Arthur will draw the whole Van der Linde gang to them; he also blames the gang for the death of a man called Seamus. Colm then comes down to see Arthur. He offers him a place in his gang and accuses him of killing many of his men at Six Point Cabin, which Arthur denies.

Arthur urges Colm to let him go and end the feud, since both gangs now have bigger problems. Colm answers that once they have Dutch, they will forget about him. He then reveals his plan: Dutch will come to rescue Arthur, the O'Driscolls will capture them all and hand them over to the law waiting for them, and then vanish. He admits that the parley was only a way to seize Arthur. The character page of the Red Dead Wiki says that Colm had made this deal with the Pinkertons; in the mission itself, Colm and his men speak only of the law. Once Colm has left, Arthur frees himself, kills a guard and escapes on horseback. He reaches Clemens Point and collapses. Mary-Beth Gaskill, Karen Jones and Dutch find him, and he tells Dutch that Colm was going to set the law on them. His recovery takes a few weeks.

Chapter 4: Saint Denis

Colm does not appear in person in this chapter, but his gang strikes the Van der Linde camp at Shady Belle. Kieran disappears after the party held for the return of Jack Marston. In "Horsemen, Apocalypses", his horse carries him back into camp dead: the O'Driscolls have beheaded him, gouged out his eyes and set his head in his own hands. A large band of O'Driscolls attacks Shady Belle at once. The gang falls back into the house and holds it, and the surviving attackers flee.

Chapter 6: Beaver Hollow

Colm is arrested while the gang is camped at Beaver Hollow. The newspaper article "O'Driscoll Captured" reports that a deputy caught him during a routine coach inspection. Judge Taylor examined him the next day, and he was sentenced to hang for murder in Saint Denis. In "Icarus and Friends", Arthur rescues Sadie from O'Driscolls chasing her near Annesburg, and she tells him the news. Arthur points out that Colm has escaped before. Sadie answers that she means to make sure he does not get away this time.

In "Goodbye, Dear Friend", Dutch and Sadie decide to attend the hanging, because Colm has already escaped the noose twice, and they bring Arthur. Dutch and Arthur go disguised as Saint Denis policemen, and Sadie dresses as a society lady. Before the execution, Dutch reminds Sadie of what each of them has lost because of Colm: her husband, his Annabelle, and Kieran. Near the gallows, the three spot two O'Driscolls in the crowd. Dutch and Arthur follow one of them and overhear the rescue plan. Arthur deals with Paul, a sniper posted on a rooftop facing the gallows, and takes his place with the rifle. Dutch and Sadie each hold an O'Driscoll at gunpoint in the crowd.

On the gallows, Colm at first looks confident that his men will save him. He then sees them held at gunpoint and looks in turn at Dutch, at Sadie and at Arthur, on the roof in place of his sniper. The Red Dead Wiki gives his last words as: "As well you may. I've been a bad man. These charges..." Colm is hanged. Sadie then kills both hostages, which starts a shootout with the remaining O'Driscolls and with the city's lawmen. Arthur covers Dutch and Sadie from the roof, and the three get back to camp.

The gang Colm leaves behind does not last long. In "Mrs. Sadie Adler, Widow", Sadie finds the last O'Driscolls at Hanging Dog Ranch, in Big Valley. Arthur first says he no longer cares about them since Colm's hanging. If he agrees to go, the two wipe out the gang. Sadie kills the last of them, Tom, whom she names as the man who killed her husband. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Tom had led the gang since Colm's death.

Tom carries a letter Colm wrote him from his cell in Saint Denis. Colm says that he is held apart from the main jail, on the other side of town, in a small room with a big door and many guards working in shifts. He would pass on the guards' names and addresses if he knew them. He expects people in the crowd at his execution to call for his blood, and hopes to see a few friendly faces as well. He writes that prison has given him time to think about his past, some of which he regrets and some of which he does not. He was caught, he explains, in the bed of a disloyal, loud-mouthed woman. He asks Tom to visit her, to tell her that all is forgiven and perhaps to leave her something lasting to remember him by.

By 1907, according to the Red Dead Wiki, some of the gang's former hideouts are held by the Laramie Gang. In the ending in which Arthur goes back for the money with low honor, as Micah stabs him, Arthur begs Dutch to see that Micah, not Molly, Miss Grimshaw or even Colm, is the informant.

Personality

Rockstar's character descriptions of September 2018 cover the Van der Linde gang and do not include Colm, and no character page from an official guide is known for him. The most direct portrait in the game comes from the press. The newspaper article "O'Driscoll Captured" accuses his gang of robbing coaches, beating men and women, murder and torture. It adds: "His wild, hot-headed and impulsive ways are the stuff of outlaw legend." The same article says that bounties for his death or capture have long been posted far and wide.

In "Paying a Social Call", John tells Kieran, according to the quote box of the Red Dead Wiki's mission page: "Colm's a sneak thief and a killer... Dutch is... Dutch is more like a teacher." After Kieran's death in "Horsemen, Apocalypses", Arthur says of Colm: "That man can really hate." Kieran, who rode with him, describes him in camp, according to the Red Dead Wiki. When Colm speaks kindly to you, Kieran says, it feels as if the sun is shining; when he is angry, it feels as if the devil were upon you.

In his own words

At the parley in "Blessed are the Peacemakers", Colm greets Dutch with "Hello, Dutch... it's been a while." He goes straight to Dutch's leadership: "So, uh... how's your gang doing? They still believing in ya? Better world... pure world, hmm? How's that coming along?" He then asks about the train the Van der Linde gang took from his men: "How's that score you stole off us?" Dutch: "Which one?" Colm: "Oh, I like that. It's like I said, this a charismatic leader." He reveals the offer he has received: "They offered me a price, Dutch... to bring you in." Dutch asks why he did not take it. Colm: "Well... still might." When Dutch says he is sorry about his brother, Colm answers: "Yeah, well I never liked him much." Dutch replies that he liked Annabelle, and Colm says: "You always loved the ladies, Dutch van der Linde. I like that about you."

With Arthur tied up at Lone Mule Stead, he opens with: "Arthur Morgan... it's good to see ya." He asks: "How's the wound?" Arthur says he hardly feels it, and Colm answers: "You will... septic, it ain't nice." His offer follows: "Now, tell me... fine gun like you... why you still running around with old Dutch? Could come ride with me and make real money." Arthur: "It ain't about the money, Colm." Colm: "Oh, no... it's Dutch's famous charisma." When Arthur denies the killings at Six Point Cabin, Colm says: "Oh, you lie, my friend... and I thought Dutch preached truth." Once he has explained his trap, he leaves Arthur with: "Oh, Arthur... Arthur, I missed you."

Names

In dialogue he is only ever called Colm O'Driscoll, Colm or O'Driscoll. A letter that can be taken from Dr. Ben Calloway in Valentine, signed by an O'Driscoll named Donal, calls him "Mr O'D". In it, Donal tells the doctor that their mutual friend is happy they are all friends again. He reminds him that friends do not tell lies about friends, and mentions the yellow dress Mrs. Calloway wore at dinner. The Red Dead Wiki lists this as Colm's only alias. His brother's name is never given.

Most characters say his first name as it is spelled. In one of the campfire scenes at Clemens Point, Sean MacGuire loses his temper because everyone gets the name wrong, and keeps at Kieran until he says it the Irish way. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Kieran points out that Colm himself says it as it is spelled. Wikipedia's list of the game's characters, citing Game Rant and PC Gamer, notes that Sean pronounces it correctly. The Red Dead Wiki adds that some O'Driscolls who speak with an Irish accent also get it right. The same wiki gives Colm as a Gaelic form of the Latin columba, meaning dove.

Appearance

Colm is a thin man in middle age, with long gray hair and a few days' growth of beard. His clothes are cut much like Dutch's, with a dark vest and a black hat. In the snow of the Grizzlies he adds a coat lined with fur.

Relationships

Dutch van der Linde

Two killings stand between Colm and Dutch: Dutch killed Colm's brother, and Colm killed Annabelle, a woman Dutch loved. Dutch gives the order himself in "Blessed are the Peacemakers", on the ride to the parley: "I killed Colm's brother... long time ago... then he killed... a woman I loved dear..." The Red Dead Wiki's page on "Old Friends" tells it the other way round, with Dutch killing the brother in retaliation for Annabelle. The game never says why or how the brother died, and Colm never gives his own account of either death.

On the way to the raid of "Old Friends", Dutch tells Arthur: "I learned a long time ago that you hit Colm O'Driscoll, wait for him and people you love will die." When Arthur says the feud must be put to rest, Dutch answers: "Some things I can forgive, others I can forget. What he did to Annabelle, I can't do neither." Arthur: "You killed his brother, Dutch." Dutch: "Yes, I did. And I hope the bastards'll be reunited soon enough." Before the parley he admits: "You know, I've been fighting Colm for so long now... I can barely remember a time when it was different." Their meeting, the trap that follows and the hanging are told in the Biography.

Arthur Morgan

Arthur watches Colm through binoculars at Ewing Basin, then regrets the outcome of the raid: "I'm just sorry we missed out on Colm." He also tells Dutch that the raid will hurt Colm a lot more than a bullet in the head. In "Blessed are the Peacemakers" he is captured by Colm's men and held at Lone Mule Stead, where Colm offers him a place in his gang (see In his own words). At the hanging in "Goodbye, Dear Friend", he takes the place of Colm's sniper (see Biography).

According to the Red Dead Wiki's camp events page, Dutch apologizes to Arthur in camp after his escape for the ambush he was caught in. At another time, Micah apologizes to him as well. Each gives the same account: he and Dutch expected to be attacked themselves, the O'Driscolls simply left, and Arthur never turned up on the way back.

Annabelle

Annabelle was Dutch's lover, and Colm killed her (on the order of events, see Dutch van der Linde). She is never seen; according to the Red Dead Wiki, Dutch, Hosea and Arthur all mention her. According to the same wiki, after the hanging Dutch can tell Arthur that he can finally say goodbye to her.

Kieran Duffy

Kieran is the young O'Driscoll Colm strikes at the Ewing Basin camp before riding off, when Dutch and Arthur first watch the camp in "Old Friends". As a prisoner of the Van der Linde gang he protests that he is not an O'Driscoll and that he hates Colm. His capture, the trip to Six Point Cabin and his death at the hands of Colm's men are told in the Biography; his description of Colm's moods is under Personality.

Sadie Adler

Colm's gang killed Sadie's husband, Jake Adler, and occupied their homestead in the Grizzlies, where Dutch, Arthur and Micah find her. She brings Arthur the news of Colm's arrest in "Icarus and Friends" and attends the hanging with Dutch and Arthur. If Arthur agrees to help, she finishes off the last of the gang with him at Hanging Dog Ranch (see Biography).

Micah Bell

In the Grizzlies, Micah is the one who finds the homestead the O'Driscolls have taken, and Dutch reports back to camp: "Colm O'Driscoll and his scum, they beat us to it." In chapter 3 he argues for accepting Colm's offer of a parley, asking: "Can we really afford to be fighting on all these fronts, and O'Driscoll?"

Simon Pearson

Colm's offer of a parley reaches the gang through Pearson. He says that a couple of O'Driscolls he met on the road into town suggested a meeting to end the feud "like gentlemen". Dutch's first reaction: "Gentlemen? Colm O'Driscoll? Have you lost your minds?" When Arthur comes back wounded from his captivity, Pearson apologizes to him, and Dutch answers that it is a bit late for apologies.

Hosea Matthews

When Pearson brings Colm's offer back to camp in "Blessed are the Peacemakers", Hosea's answer is immediate: "They want a parley? It's a trap."

John Marston

John rides to Six Point Cabin in "Paying a Social Call" in the hope of finding Colm there (see Biography). On the way he contrasts Colm with Dutch for Kieran (see Personality).

Bill Williamson

Bill is among the men Dutch takes to Colm's camp in "Old Friends". When Arthur says the feud has to be put to rest one way or another, Bill answers: "It will be." He is also one of the two men who ride with Arthur to Six Point Cabin in "Paying a Social Call" (see Biography).

Charles Smith

Charles never meets Colm. In "The Aftermath of Genesis", he tells Arthur that he has heard a lot of talk about the O'Driscolls, and Arthur explains who they are (see Origins).

Leviticus Cornwall

The train Colm was planning to rob in the Grizzlies belonged to Cornwall, and the Van der Linde gang robs it with Colm's plans (see Biography). At the parley, Colm brings it up with Dutch as the score that was stolen from his men (see In his own words).

Tom

Colm's letter from his cell is addressed to Tom and signed "Your brother in arms, Colm". Arthur can take it from Tom's body after Sadie kills him at Hanging Dog Ranch (see Biography).

Paul

Paul is the O'Driscoll posted with a rifle on a rooftop facing the gallows to free Colm in "Goodbye, Dear Friend". Arthur takes him out in a fight on the roof, killing him or knocking him out according to the Red Dead Wiki's mission page, and takes his place.

Skills, weapons and horses

Leader of the O'Driscoll Boys

The gang he built

Colm founded and leads the O'Driscoll Boys (on the gang's age, see Origins). Before the game's release, on October 23, 2018, Rockstar Games presented the gang on Twitter (now X): "the O'Driscolls, long-standing rivals of Dutch's gang, are a big crew that cover a lot of ground, attracting a seemingly endless supply of young guns ready to risk it all." Several of the men around him have Irish first names, such as Seamus and Patrick, both mentioned by his guards in "Blessed are the Peacemakers" (see Biography).

Dutch despises the way he recruits. Looking at the dead O'Driscolls in "Old Friends", he says: "Colm doesn't give a damn about his men. All he cares about is numbers. If you can shoot a gun, and ride a horse, and kill without thought, you're in." He adds that Colm probably does not even know half their names. Of the train Colm was planning to rob, Dutch also tells Arthur: "Colm always had good information."

In 1899 his men hold ground across several territories. In the snow of the Grizzlies they occupy the Adler homestead and a camp near the lake at Ewing Basin. Later come Six Point Cabin in Cumberland Forest, Lone Mule Stead in Big Valley, where Arthur is held, and Hanging Dog Ranch, also in Big Valley, where the last of them end up. A later newspaper article, On Outlaw Gangs: "We Must Stop Them", printed in the Blackwater Ledger (No. 71), the New Hanover Gazette (No. 36) and the Saint Denis Times (No. 52), reports that gangs such as the O'Driscolls "have been hunted to the brink of extinction".

Weapons and horse

The Red Dead Wiki lists a Cattleman Revolver and a Double-Barreled Shotgun as his weapons; one of its gallery images shows him aiming the revolver. He rides a black American Standardbred, according to the same wiki. He never fights on screen: he is away from the camp when it is attacked in "Old Friends", absent from Six Point Cabin in "Paying a Social Call", and gone from Lone Mule Stead when Arthur escapes.

Missions

The Red Dead Wiki lists three missions of Red Dead Redemption 2 in which Colm appears on screen, one in each of chapters 1, 3 and 6. The French edition gives the same three. In several other missions he is only talked about, or his gang acts in his name; they are listed in the second table.

Development and performance

Casting

Colm O'Driscoll appears only in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). He is played by Andrew Berg. According to the Red Dead Wiki, Berg is an American actor and provides both the character's voice and his motion capture. Wikipedia's list of the game's characters also credits him in the role. The wiki lists no other part for him in the series, and no interview in which Berg discusses the role has been found.

The pronunciation of his name

Roger Clark, who plays Arthur Morgan, is an Irish-American actor and knows that the Irish name Colm sounds closer to "column" than to "comb", PC Gamer reported on 30 August 2024. Answering fan questions in an interview with Gamology, he named the way Arthur says the name as the only thing he would change about his performance: "The only thing I regret is saying Colm O'Driscoll incorrectly. Rockstar made me say it wrong. I told them it was wrong. They didn't care." PC Gamer's Joshua Wolens noted that Arthur, as a character, would have had little reason to know how Irish names are pronounced. How the characters say the name in the game is covered under Names.

Cut content

The game files show that Colm was meant to have a horse of his own, a unique model like those prepared for several outlaw gangs. Rockstar gave him a standard horse instead. According to the Red Dead Wiki, it appears in just three brief cutscenes across "Old Friends" and "Blessed are the Peacemakers".

Reception

A real Colm O'Driscoll

In the days after the game's release, an Irishman named Colm O'Driscoll began receiving hostile messages from players on Instagram. On 3 November 2018 he reported it on Twitter: "So it turns out I share the same name as a #RedDeadRedemption2 character and now I'm receiving hate mail online." LADbible reported the story on 7 November 2018, in an article by Matthew McGladdery. He told Eurogamer that he found the whole thing "kinda funny", but that the messages were "starting to get annoying". The Irish Post covered it on 13 November 2018. The paper noted that he owned neither a PlayStation 4 nor an Xbox One, and that one message warned him he would be shot on sight.

Assessments

  • The Irish Post, Jack Beresford, 13 November 2018: the paper presented the character as "a nefarious gang leader and the main antagonist" of the game, whose men keep turning up in the open world to start firefights.
  • Game Rant, Rafly Gilang Pratama, 8 August 2020: a list of ten little-known facts about him calls him "an old-time, degenerate friend of Dutch" and "the game's most-hated antagonist".
Other media and legacy

Red Dead Online

Colm's character page on the Red Dead Wiki lists no appearance in Red Dead Online, but his gang survives him there as one of the hostile gangs of the online mode. Colter and Millesani Claim are among their hideouts that players can clear. O'Driscolls also attack players in random encounters and stranger missions, and when they deliver Trader goods. In a recovery mission, the Strawberry station clerk Hector Barlow asks the player to take back a stagecoach from the O'Driscolls at Hanging Dog Ranch without harming them, calling them good friends of his.

Trivia
  • Several of Colm's cutscenes take place away from the player character: Arthur sees his camp in "Old Friends" through binoculars and follows the parley through his rifle sights.
  • The subtitles of "Blessed are the Peacemakers" spell Annabelle's name "Annabel".
  • In "Horsemen, Apocalypses", one of the attacking O'Driscolls can be heard taunting Arthur by asking about his shoulder.
