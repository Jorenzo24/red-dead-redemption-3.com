{{Infobox Newspaper article|Title=Landon Ricketts Rumored Alive|Image=|Newspaper=[[Blackwater Ledger No. 55]]|Last=|Next=}}'''Landon Ricketts Rumored Alive''' is one of the [[Newspapers|newspaper articles]] in ''[[Red Dead Redemption]]''.

== Text ==
LANDON RICKETTS RUMORED ALIVE
----Reports from Mexico suggest that possibly the last of the great gun men, [[Landon Ricketts]] may be alive and well just south of the border. Mr. Ricketts, who survived the [[Blackwater Massacre]] of 1899 and who shot and killed both of the [[The Butcher Brothers|Butcher Brothers]] in 1896, amongst many other rumored and verified killings, was long believed dead after a late night gun fight in eastern California in 1902. Now it seems the last of the men who lived by the speed of their reflexes may still be among us. It is unclear whether he will now join a Wild West show.
