## Problems

1. **WRONG (internal contradiction about the sons' deaths).** Draft: "According to the mission page, Bartholomew is that son and Dutch shoots him ... Gareth and Gerald are killed in the attack on the room ... Dutch shoots one of her sons in front of her". That makes four sons die, and she has three. Transcript (gsw l. 24514-24534): the son at the door says "Get the hell off our land", Dutch says "If you ain't gonna be civilized about this…" and then armed men come out ("A firefight breaks loose"). Nobody is shown shooting the son at the door. In the room she says "You killed my sons"; after "And what about them?" comes "[Dutch kills the old bitch's son.]", and Arthur then says "reckon they're all dead". Mission page story: "After killing two of Catherine's sons ... Seeing that one of her sons is barely alive, Dutch shoots him". Fix: "...one of her sons tells the gang to get off their land; according to the mission page, this is Bartholomew, although the subtitles name him Gareth. A gunfight follows..." (drop "and Dutch shoots him"). Then: "Catherine is inside with her sons, who are shot when the room is stormed (Gareth and Gerald, according to the mission page)." And: "Dutch shoots a son who is still alive in front of her, and she screams." NOTE FOR THE LEAD: dutch_D (Bartholomew "shot in the head, which starts the battle") and dutch_A ("shoots Bartholomew Braithwaite when the fighting starts") have the same problem. No transcript shows that shot.
2. **IMPRECISE (wrong motive).** Draft (ch. 4): "Arthur tells John that the Braithwaites gave the boy to Bronte for money". Transcript l. 25852: "I figure… the Braithwaites were going to hold Jack ransom, for all the money we cost 'em. They must have sent him here so we couldn't get to him. But… Bronte knows by now there's no Braithwaites left to pay him." Fix: "On the way, Arthur guesses that the Braithwaites meant to hold Jack for ransom and sent him to Bronte to keep him out of the gang's reach. With the family gone and nobody left to pay, he reasons, Bronte has no further use for the boy."
3. **IMPRECISE (needless wiki attribution for a fact the game states).** Draft: "According to the Red Dead Wiki, she sells the liquor to the Lemoyne Raiders". Transcript l. 17276: Archibald says "I know the Braithwaites are in business with them", and in the saloon (l. 18560s) a Raider says "You're the bastards who stole the liquor we was going to buy." Fix: "Archibald adds that the family is in business with the Lemoyne Raiders. According to the Red Dead Wiki, the family also..." (keep the Danny-Lee sentence attributed).
4. **IMPRECISE.** Draft: "While the others hold off reinforcements, Arthur helps John break down the door." Transcript l. 24575-24606: Dutch sends Arthur out ("Arthur, get out there! We've got this door covered"), Arthur fights the reinforcements until "Looks like they're running!", and then helps John break the door. Fix: "Arthur goes back out to fight off reinforcements, then helps John break down the door."
5. **IMPRECISE (omits the key fact).** Draft: "Kieran later apologizes to Abigail: knowing that Arthur and Hosea had dealings with the Braithwaites, he did not think they had come for Jack." Camp_Events.txt (Shady Belle chapter) and kieran_B: he SAW the Braithwaites come to camp, but because Arthur and Hosea had dealings with them he did not think they would take Jack. Fix: "Kieran later apologizes to Abigail: he saw the Braithwaites come to camp but, knowing that Arthur and Hosea had dealings with them, did not think they would take Jack."
6. **IMPRECISE.** Draft: "Jack, aged four". jack_A gives "about four in 1899" (birth around 1895 is the wiki's estimate; HOUSE: "Jack born around 1895"). Fix: "aged about four".
7. **IMPRECISE.** Draft: "Arthur finds her at the cabins behind the ruins of the main house". Penelope's letter (l. 39782) says "Most days I am to be found at the cabins on the plantation. The main house and all it stood for are thankfully no more." Nothing says "behind". Fix: "Arthur finds her at the workers' cabins on the plantation."
8. **IMPRECISE (hedge lost).** Draft: "By 1907, according to the Red Dead Wiki, no Braithwaite is left at the manor." Wiki Braithwaite Manor: "the Braithwaite family seems to have died out". Fix: "By 1907, according to the Red Dead Wiki, the family seems to have died out at the manor."
9. **IMPRECISE + DUPLICATE (Personality).** Draft: "her sons draw their guns, and she tells them to stand down. She answers threats with a laugh and turns a sale she refuses into a paid errand. She knows who is behind the theft of her liquor before anyone tells her." Transcript l. 18396-18424: a son says "The alternative is you get shot". She laughs, says "Pay the man", and answers her son's "Momma!" with "Hush now". She never tells anyone to stand down. Hosea has already started to say "without orders from…" when she names Sheriff Gray. All three sentences also retell the Advertising paragraph of the Biography (ownership: biography owns events). Fix: replace them with one sentence: "Her sons back her with guns, but she settles the deal herself and silences her son's protest." Drop the other two.
10. **DUPLICATE.** Archibald's description appears twice. Origins has "an old cotton family that lost its fortune when the labor laws changed... expensive taste for thoroughbred horses", and Personality quotes "old cotton family" and "a rather... expensive interest in thoroughbred horses". Fix: in Origins keep only "The law keeps finding the family's moonshine stills all over Lemoyne, according to Archibald", and remove the line from C Quotes.
11. **DUPLICATE.** Origins: "During the raid on the manor, Catherine says the Braithwaites have lived in the house for 120 years." The same line is quoted in "In her own words". Fix: delete the Origins sentence, or cut it to "The family has lived in the house for 120 years, by Catherine's account."
12. **DUPLICATE.** Biography: "A guard tells them that the man of the house is a lady, Mrs. Catherine Braithwaite." Names: "The guard at the gate introduces her as "Mrs. Catherine Braithwaite"". Fix: Biography becomes "A guard lets them drive up to the house." Keep Names as it is.
13. **COPIED.** Draft: "Lucille Braithwaite is said to have shown Douglas Gray where her family's gold was hidden, and he took it, along with gold from his own family, to fund abolitionist groups." Wiki (Braithwaite Family): "gave the location of a significant sum of gold to Douglas Gray ... he stole this gold from the Braithwaites, along with additional gold from his own family, in order to fund abolitionist groups."
